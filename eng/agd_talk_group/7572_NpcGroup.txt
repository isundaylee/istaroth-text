# Talk Group: Sobolev (NpcGroup 7572)

## Talk 0

Sobolev: Things are already enough of a mess as it is. Tsaritsa preserve us, please don‘t let it snow now...

## Talk 1

Traveler: Might I ask your name?
Sobolev: You want mine? Sobolev. I’m the engineering supervisor here. Anything related to ice mining becomes my headache. That said, "supervisor" might be giving me too much credit. I'm really a slightly higher-ranking laborer...
Sobolev: Judging by the way you're dressed, you must be a traveler from out of town. If you need help, you can ask Nekrasov in town. Handling visitors and answering questions is his job.
Sobolev: Heh, though I doubt there's much that fellow will actually help you with. If you run into real trouble, you'll have to fend for yourself...

## Talk 2

Traveler: What happens if it snows?
Sobolev: Nothing good, that's for sure. The biggest problem is the drop in temperature. Not only does the ice-mining equipment start breaking down, but the workers can't take it either.
Sobolev: And if heavy snow blocks the roads, the trains will have to stop running. At that point, never mind delivering our cargo on time. We may not even be able to get the supplies and parts the town urgently needs.
Sobolev: Still, we’ve already made every preparation we can in advance. All that's left to do now... is pray, I suppose.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Oh, do you need a hand with anything?
Sobolev: Funny you should ask. I've got a shipment that needs to be loaded up and sent off to Snezhnograd soon. Problem is, three people from the escort team still haven't shown up.
Sobolev: We could probably manage without the three of them, but if anything goes wrong on the road and we suddenly find ourselves short-handed, that'll be a real headache.
Sobolev: My guess is those fools got the date wrong, and they're probably still down below with their heads buried in work. Sorry to trouble you, but could you make a trip over to their work area and look for them?

## Talk 4

Sobolev: Hmm. Looks like those three fools managed to make it onto the transport. Thank goodness... So long as they didn't hold anything up, giving them grief over it's more trouble than it's worth.
