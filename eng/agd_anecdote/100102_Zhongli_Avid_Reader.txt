# Zhongli: Avid Reader

A certain "consultant" is lost in thought by the *Wanwen Bookhouse's* stall...

You encounter Zhongli lost in thought by the Wanwen Bookhouse's stand. He tells you that he is there at the behest of Hu Tao, Director of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, to purchase some "light novels." After flipping through a couple of them, it seems that Zhongli himself has developed an interest in the genre.
