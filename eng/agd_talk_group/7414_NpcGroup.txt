# Talk Group: Grigory (NpcGroup 7414)

## Talk 0

Grigory: Check out our new stock! But take note that the transaction's done once you leave the counter... and we don't sell on credit. In principle, anyway!

## Talk 1

Traveler: Let's see what you've got...
Grigory: Feel free! It's all newly shipped in!

## Talk 2

Traveler: Can I put it on my tab?
Grigory: Haha, come now, dear customer, you hardly look poor. And besides, small outfits like ours need to run tight budgets, you know...
Grigory: But in all seriousness, when we say "credit," we mean helping villagers who struggle to make ends meet. People like you aren't who that service is for, you see.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Do a lot of people purchase on credit?
Grigory: ...In the past? Occasionally, but numbers are up nowadays.
Grigory: Everyone's got their fair share of problems, and our clientele here at Bratskaya are often nearby residents. And around these parts, there's always a cold chill of misfortune waiting around the corner to ruin someone's life...
Grigory: So though we call it selling on credit, it's more like doing someone a favor.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Not much I want to look at for the moment...
Grigory: That so? Well, come back when we've got a new shipment. Maybe something will catch your eye then!

## Talk 5
