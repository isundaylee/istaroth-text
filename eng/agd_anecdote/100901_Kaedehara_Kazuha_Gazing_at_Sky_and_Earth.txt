# Kaedehara Kazuha: Gazing at Sky and Earth

An itinerant swordsman is exploring his poetic expression on the outskirts of Liyue...

You come across Kazuha searching for his poetic muse in the mountains to the south of Guili Plains. A chance meeting with old friends is a delightful affair — is life not full of such fortuitous encounters?

## Talk 0

Kaedehara Kazuha: "The grass did spring and sprout anew, and heaven and earth both verdant grew"...
Kaedehara Kazuha: After the rain, the wind blows gently and the clouds are light. Looking out over the horizon, one's heart is opened wide.
Kaedehara Kazuha: And then, should one happen to meet an old friend... Well, there could be no better day than this, wouldn't you agree?
