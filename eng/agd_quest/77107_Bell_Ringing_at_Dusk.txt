## Talk 14 (alternative/additional)

(Talk to Pavlina)

Pavlina: ...
Aksinya: You took "his" power away from me, just for the sake of your people... But are they really worth it? Are they really worth betraying that world His Majesty has promised?
Aksinya: We should have had a better future.
Pavlina: Her people are my responsibility, my burden to shoulder...
Pavlina: A better future? It might have been so for you. But for us, that would just have become yet another prison.
Pavlina: Aksinya, you seek a "tomorrow," a place of eternal joy and flow, and a world of everlasting love. But what about us fae and those without love?
Aksinya: They... will be reborn in a new courtyard.
Pavlina: No, Aksinya, they won't. Both you and I know full well that the cycle has run its course and there will not be new civilizations henceforth.
Pavlina: There will be new creation no more. All that remains to us is the sifting and remaking.
Pavlina: The door is one that we will... never pass. It is but a pageant leading to the executioner's blade.
Aksinya: ...
Pavlina: ...Yet I must, for the sake of my friend who perished in pursuit of that absurd notion of "love" — for her sake alone... seek a way that even my people may find meaning in lives born of artifice.
Aksinya: But it appears that this isn't something all of your people can comprehend... Pavlina, you say that they are your responsibility and your burden. It may well be likewise for them — I'm speaking of the burden part.
Pavlina: ...The ones who love, and the ones who are loved, will always be burdens to each other.
Pavlina: But how about you, Aksinya? Have you ever given "him" a chance to choose "his" path?
Aksinya: ..."He" will come to understand.
Aksinya: Once upon a time... you told me that they do not know what is best for them. But I must say, you are no different than them.
Pavlina: Perhaps we are all so.
Pavlina: A price has to be paid for that "tomorrow" that should come. A price, and a sacrifice.
Pavlina: We have all chosen the paths that we thought were the best. As for where those will lead us to... is not something I can predict.
Pavlina: You have won this time, Aksinya... Go on. Claim the spoils of your victory...
Aksinya: I am aware of that. But I have never hoped for your loss... Pavlina.

## Talk 14 (variant 1)

(Talk to Pavlina)

Aksinya: Pavlina...
Paimon: She... disappeared!?
Aksinya: But she did not vanish entirely, for she has left this. The union ritual is complete. It's just that she did not manage to offer up that power...
Eye of Graeae: And that is why the parts of my body that were fragmentized and then fragmentized again re-crystallized after the ritual.
Aksinya: That is correct. It is fortunate that we managed to stop Pavlina. She... was so close to success.
Aksinya: Here, take this.
Traveler: What's this...
Aksinya: This is the Eye of Graeae... Or rather, what was originally a part of the Eye of Graeae's body.
Paimon: Are you sure you want to give this to us? Also... Hmm. Tell us, why did you want to stop that Snegurochka princess?
Aksinya: Because you and I share the same purpose.
Aksinya: Oh, that's right. Though I already know who you are, I suppose I have yet to formally introduce myself to you...
Aksinya: I am but a lowly "Court Jester" who once served His Majesty, the late Belyi Tsar. My name, insignificant as it is, is Aksinya.
Eye of Graeae: Aksinya...
Aksinya: Yes. Does that serve to refresh your memory?
Aksinya: Izmaylov always complains that my introductions are far too humble. But personally? I see it as a deliberate choice in style.
Eye of Graeae: "Aksinya"... That name sounds very familiar.
Aksinya: Well, that happens to be the name of the person who built the mechanism you're residing in right now, after all.
Traveler: You... are the one who made Goggles?
Aksinya: Yes, that is I.
Aksinya: Haha, so you see, "Goggles" belongs to me. Do be good and return it now, you little thieves!
Paimon: Huhhhhh????
Aksinya: I'm only teasing. Feel free to keep "him" with you.
Aksinya: In that grand design, it was also I who was supposed to obtain the Eye of Graeae along with the will inside it. But since "he" has made "his" choice...
Aksinya: I believe in the Eye of Graeae, and I also believe in you, the ones "he" has chosen.
Aksinya: That's enough of an introduction. Our goal is the same... to help the will inside the Eye of Graeae find its body and reclaim its power.
Eye of Graeae: So, what exactly is this "power"?
Aksinya: ...Have you forgotten even what it is you wish to reclaim? Do you remember nothing but the will to recover what is yours...? It is a thought that gives one pause. But I suppose it serves the purpose for which the plan was first devised.
Aksinya: Your power, your body, they were created so that "life may transcend life itself."
Aksinya: And that is also why Pavlina sought to steal your power... A number of the fae were originally servants created by the Hyperboreans. They were never perfect creations...
Aksinya: The Snegovik can only achieve completion and perfection through rituals. And what better than a ritual with an offering that would allow the Snegovik here to transcend as well?
Aksinya: That was what Pavlina was trying to do...
Aksinya: She sought to cast off the shackles placed upon her at the time of creation... The truth was, however, that because her existence was incomplete, she could only draw upon "his" power by performing one ritual after another, endlessly...
Aksinya: Though she is a Snegurochka princess, and thus among the mightiest of the fae, these ceaseless rituals nevertheless brought great pain to both her mind and will.
Paimon: And... you said that she was close to success regardless!?
Aksinya: Her success would have been the greatest defeat for the world.
Aksinya: The "tomorrow" she has in mind is a tomorrow where her people could become whole. This, perhaps, is how she loves them.
Aksinya: But for the rest of the world, using such power for a purpose like that would be nothing short of a waste... It is fortunate that we were able to recover it before she could do so.
Paimon: That's... Paimon feels a little bad for those Snegovik now.
Aksinya: ...Pity is a conceit born of arrogance and self-importance. Perhaps those Snegovik have no need to be pitied, nor for her so-called love.
Paimon: Uh... Paimon supposes you could be right...

Option 1:

> Traveler: But that doesn't mean that everything she's done is wrong...
> Aksinya: Perhaps. And her people certainly do not have the right to condemn her.
> Aksinya: But for the world... Her love is one confined to too small a circle.

Option 2:

> Traveler: She did all she could, and gave all she had...
> Aksinya: Yes. And that is precisely why this is the most sorrowful part of all for her — no, for all of us who dwell in this world. There comes a certain time when one realizes that...
> Aksinya: "You would do the same, had you possessed the means to save the world with your love." Yet no one possesses a love so great, nor so noble.
> Aksinya: Even one such as Pavlina can only bestow her "love" upon those within her reach — her people, her kin. Yet the love of the individual is far too limited.

Paimon: Tsk. All this sounds so complicated. Can love really be too great or too limited...?
Aksinya: Some would say yes, and others would say no. Yet this is what I believe: a love capable of saving more lives is a love all the greater, and all the more noble.
Eye of Graeae: So... What would I become if I recovered all my power? Would I then be... strong enough... to save everyone?
Aksinya: That would depend on the choice "you" make when the time comes.
Aksinya: Very well. Since we share the same purpose, I am certain we shall meet again... Until that day...
Aksinya: There is much that remains for us all to do.
Paimon: She's gone... How's it going with you, Goggles? Now that you've recovered some of your power, do you remember anything new?
Eye of Graeae: A few things. But they're... only vague impressions for now.
Paimon: Oh... Okay then.
Paimon: That's alright. She did say we'll see her again later anyway... So, when we do, we'll just make her tell us everything!
