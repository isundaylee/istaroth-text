Pekkani: Still, even with all this skill, I haven't caught a single fish lately. Makes no sense, right?
Pekkani: So obviously, the problem isn't with me, but with the environment. Just as it is with the cursed Eye of Kratti, there must be something underwater affecting the fish.
Paimon: Seems like quite the leap in logic...
Pekkani: Anyway, I was hoping you two could check the nearby waters, see what's scaring the fish off. Don't worry, you'll be properly compensated for your efforts.
Paimon: Well, since there's a reward involved, we'll take a look around... but no promises we'll actually find anything weird, okay?
