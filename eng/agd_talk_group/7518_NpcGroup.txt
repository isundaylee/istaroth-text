# Talk Group: Raissa (NpcGroup 7518)

## Talk 0

Raissa: I'm telling you, that terrifying cat-spirit stole the meat I spent days curing...
Agrafena: A cat-spirit? Oh dear, that's serious.
Raissa: I know what I saw! It had three enormous eyes, and every flap of its wings stirred up a gust of wind... I was sitting in my yard that night when it fixed me with its gaze. The next thing I knew, I'd been put under some sort of spell.
Raissa: When I opened my eyes again, it was already morning. The cat-spirit was gone, and so was all the meat on my drying rack.
Agrafena: Still, it doesn't sound like a bad kitty to me — you came out of it just fine, didn't you? If you ask me, it was probably just hungry...
Agrafena: Maybe so. But I should still warn everyone to be careful. At the very least, they ought to keep some treats at home in case it comes by.
