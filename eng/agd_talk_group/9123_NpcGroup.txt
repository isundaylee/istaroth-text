Unknown Role (None): Well, though this place is just as new and unusual...

Unknown Role (None): It's different from when I first arrived in Sumeru or Mondstadt. I don't feel that mix of excitement and unease. I feel safe and comfortable here.

Unknown Role (None): The bed is fine. It's just that I'm more used to sleeping in confined spaces. I feel safer that way.

Unknown Role (None): I've already found a few suitable places. The small sofa. And inside the wardrobe.

Unknown Role (None): Don't worry, my cats don't scare easily.

Unknown Role (None): They'll probably try to curl up beside you.

Unknown Role (None): They're more than welcome!

## Talk 2

Unknown Role (None): Would you like to have a chat with me?

Unknown Role (None): Of course I would! Opportunities such as this are rare.

Unknown Role (None): Based on my observations, it seems adventurers have very little free time... And you're probably busier than most.

Unknown Role (None): And how did you come by these observations?

Unknown Role (None): I like to stroll the streets of Mondstadt in my spare time, listening to people talk and watching people as they go about their day.

Unknown Role (None): Passing by the Adventurers' Guild, I've noticed that adventurers always run up to the counter and stop for only a moment to talk before hurrying on their way once more.

Unknown Role (None): Come to think of it, I suppose everyone has something they want or need to do.

Unknown Role (None): Albedo and Sucrose often get so caught up in their research that they forget to eat and sleep.

Unknown Role (None): As for Master Jean, well, need I say more? And then there are others who are busy practicing their swordwork, or singing, or even drinking and making merry...

Unknown Role (None): Compared to them, I sometimes feel like only my form is human, and I'm still out of place...

Unknown Role (None): Do you think I should find something to keep myself busy too?

Unknown Role (None): Do you have any life goals?

Unknown Role (None): Well, there are many things I'm interested in, but I wouldn't go so far as to say they're my "life goals."

Unknown Role (None): At least for me, I don't think you can call something your "life"-anything unless it's some kind of incredible achievement, right?

Unknown Role (None): The heroes in most stories all have momentous, earth-shattering lifetime accomplishments.

Unknown Role (None): You shouldn't compare yourself to others. Just be yourself.

Unknown Role (None): But... I want to grow a little faster too, to become someone everyone accepts.

Unknown Role (None): After all, the fact that my own "story" has come this far is the result of a lot of people's hard work.

Unknown Role (None): If the "ending" is anticlimactic, if it doesn't have some sort of positive impact on this world, then I'll have let everyone down...

Unknown Role (None): I'm sure Mother would want to see me become someone remarkable, too...

Unknown Role (None): I think she'd want to see a happy you.

Unknown Role (None): As long as you're happy, you won't have let anyone down.

Unknown Role (None): ...

Unknown Role (None): I think I'm starting to understand what you mean. Thank you.

Unknown Role (None): Uhm... I've still got a lot of difficult questions floating around my head. I still need to spend a lot of time thinking them over...

Unknown Role (None): Being busy thinking... Maybe that's the best kind of busy for me.
