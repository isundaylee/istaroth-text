## Talk 12

Sennedjem: Ashvapati?

Ashvapati: It's been a while, Sennedjem. Over a decade at least...

Sennedjem: How did you find this place?

Ashvapati: All these years, and the signal you use for the meet-up point still hasn't changed. All I had to do was pose as your point of contact, and fish a time and date out of that scout you sent.

Sennedjem: Useless bunch of idiots... Just wait until my backup gets here.

Ashvapati: Oh, I think that might take a while. The Fontaine and Liyue authorities have your men locked down tight... Every last one.

Sennedjem: No... There's no way the cops could have known we'd be here.

Ashvapati: Unless, of course, someone deliberately left a trail of clues leading them straight to the expo. That would be a different story, now wouldn't it?

Ashvapati: If they hadn't taken your agents out of the equation, I doubt I would've been able to lure you here.

Sennedjem: Hmph, so now what? You gonna turn me in too?

Ashvapati: No. The authorities can have your men. But you're mine.

Sennedjem: Is that Fatui tech? Looks like your skills are as good as ever.

Sennedjem: Listen, I know you're mad that I've been racking up Mora without you all these years. But it looks like you still got it. If you want back in, we can work something out.

Ashvapati: After everything that happened, what makes you think I would want back in?

Sennedjem: Because what's the alternative? ...You're not here for revenge, are you?

Sennedjem: After Khaemwaset took you in, you really convinced yourself you were one of them, huh...

Sennedjem: What you should have learned is that banking on "loyalty" and "belonging" is a losing hand. Better to cash out before you're in the hole.

Sennedjem: In the desert, my people live by one rule and one rule alone — the last laugh doesn't go to the righteous... it belongs to the one who knows how to cut their losses.

Sennedjem: Just look at me. When there's Mora to be earned in the desert, I'm a desert folk. When the opportunities in Fontaine are sweeter, I'm a Fontainian.

Ashvapati: You think everyone is as much of a lowlife as you?

Sennedjem: A lowlife? Heh, there's nothing Mora can't buy, Ashvapati... that includes prestige.

Sennedjem: You're talking to a renowned Fontainian philanthropist who lives in the Vasari Passage and spends his days going from one grand banquet to the next. Nobody who counts would dare call me a lowlife!

Ashvapati: Are you done?

Sennedjem: Tch... Clearly, there's no reasoning with you. Fine. Have it your way.

Sennedjem: But if you want me dead so bad, why'd you come to face me alone? That wasn't the wisest decision, now, was it?

Ashvapati: Oh, don't you worry — I brought plenty of firepower with me. You could've brought a whole army for all I care. These weapons will bury every last one of you!

Sennedjem: What the... Are you crazy!? If that thing fires, you're not getting out of here alive either!

Ashvapati: Yes, that's the plan. It's high time both of us paid the price for our sins!

Sennedjem: He's lost his mind... Get him! Start breaking his bones until he removes this barrier!

Chevreuse: Hold it right there, all of you! Otherwise, I've got enough firepower to go around too!

Sennedjem: The Maison Gardiennage...

Ashvapati: You got here sooner than I expected...

Paimon: Hey, what's that in the sky? Looks like bad news...

Chevreuse: Ashvapati, you have my word that these people will face the judgment of the Fontaine courts. Remove the barrier and come with us at once!

Ashvapati: Fontainian justice is too merciful for them... You should leave us.

Chevreuse: Stubborn fool...

Traveler: Ati, Neb, and Raya are waiting for you!

Ashvapati: I don't deserve them... I have to atone for the things I've done.

Yelan: What is it you feel you need to atone for? Weapons research?

Ashvapati: ...You're from Liyue, right? Your ancestors were the first people to master the recipe for gunpowder. And yet, they turned it into fireworks and firecrackers that even children could play with.

Ashvapati: Surely, it's choices like that which make Liyue what it is today... I hear that it's a very peaceful place to live for the most part.

Ashvapati: But me? I chose to use my research skills to create weapons of war that brought death to countless people and destroyed the only home those kids had... That's why I have to atone. Because I have blood on my hands.

Yelan: Sounds like you don't know as much as you think about fireworks. They might be harmless fun in this day and age, but they started out as weapons too, y'know.

Ashvapati: ...What?

Yelan: That's right. Fireworks and firecrackers were originally developed for use as weapons. They needed something a little more powerful than usual for purging evil forces and scaring off wild beasts.

Yelan: Over time, evil spirits learned to fear the sight of flames, and the beasts retreated into the mountains. That's when people started repurposing fireworks for entertainment purposes.

Yelan: The peace we enjoy today isn't because we chose to make toys instead of weapons.

Yelan: The weapons are what secured us the peace in the first place. Only then did we have the luxury of choosing to turn them into toys instead.

Yelan: It's people, not weapons, that commit acts of evil. And when people are as bent on destruction as the evil forces and beasts faced by our forebears, whatever tools they have at their disposal end up as weapons.

Ashvapati: ...Time is running out. Please, leave while you still can.

Yelan: Relax. Now that we have these guys in custody, I think we can find a way of repurposing these ballistas for the children's amusement.
