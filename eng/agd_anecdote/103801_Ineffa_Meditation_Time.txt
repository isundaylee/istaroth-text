# Ineffa: Meditation Time

At the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, Ineffa seems to be... daydreaming?

At the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, you encounter a "meditating" Ineffa. It turns out she's trying to help Jahoda with her insomnia. However... Ineffa's "meditation" seems an awful lot like "standby mode"...
