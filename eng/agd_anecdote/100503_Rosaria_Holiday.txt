# Rosaria: Holiday

There should be a nun "working" at the Cathedral... Or at least nearby...

You encounter Rosaria keeping watch outside The Cat's Tail. At the invitation of a certain bard, you join the festivities and enjoy a carefree moment clinking glasses in good company.
