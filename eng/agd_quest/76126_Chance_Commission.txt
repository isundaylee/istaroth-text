# Chance Commission


## Talk 1

Lyylikki: Jahoda? And... Mr. Zompaynco. What can I do for you?
Zompaynco: I... uhh... about that...
Jahoda: He wants to visit the Frostmoon Scions, but can't quite bring himself to ask, so he's hoping you'll help him out.
Zompaynco: Yes! Yes, that's exactly it!
Zompaynco: G—Greetings, Miss Lyylikki! It's such a pleasure to meet you. Uh... I brought this fossil specimen from Natlan along...
Lyylikki: Thank you, but Mr. Zompaynco... umm... We've already met, haven't we? You really don't need to be so formal.
Zompaynco: Huh? Then... how did you notice me?
Lyylikki: You've always been there when I was distributing meals, haven't you? Wandering around the line, circling back and forth, but never stepping in.
Lyylikki: I thought you might want a serving but were too shy to say so, so I quietly set a portion aside for you.
Lyylikki: But you've never came around to claim one. In fact, you hadn't shown up today, I was going to ask someone to check and see if you need help or something like that.
Zompaynco: Ah... I—I'm so sorry. I've been hesitating, trying to figure out how to say hello...
Zompaynco: So, Miss Lyylikki... would it be alright if I paid the Frostmoon Scions a visit?
Lyylikki: Of course. We've welcomed a few guests from outside before. But may I ask why?
Zompaynco: I'd like to take some photos and notes, look through some records, and if possible... ask someone about the local legends!
Zompaynco: I'm a member of Natlan's Saurian Relics Association. If I could find materials connected to my research, that would be invaluable.
Lyylikki: I see... so you're hoping to spend some time in the archives, then? Let me think...
Jahoda: Well, seeing how you seem to have hit it off, I'll consider this commission complete. Let me get back and report to my boss.
Zompaynco: Thank you, Miss Jahoda! But... I really must pay you for your trouble. It'd be wrong to have you come all the way here for nothing.
Zompaynco: And please be sure to convey my gratitude to Boss Nefer when you return.
Jahoda: Of course. I'll take my leave now, then. You two enjoy your chat.

## Talk 2

Zompaynco: Ah... Jahoda? What brings you here? Hmm... Is Boss Nefer available right now?
Jahoda: (Just as I thought, he wants to see Boss Nef... Hmm, I should be able to handle a small matter like this myself.)
Jahoda: No need to go to all that trouble. I'm actually here because my boss sent me to help you.
Zompaynco: Huh? Wh—What do you mean? Does that mean Boss Nefer has...
Jahoda: You've always wanted to visit the Frostmoon Scions, right?
Jahoda: So, the plan is to befriend Lyylikki first, then ask her to take you to the Frostmoon Scions?
Zompaynco: What!? Lady Nefer knew it all along? But I've kept it locked in my heart and barely told anyone...
Jahoda: (Now is the time to use that super cool line that Boss said! What was that line again...? Um... Oh!)
Jahoda: Hehe, Mr. Zompaynco, when you guard a secret, you must also learn to hide the fact that you're guarding one.
Jahoda: Otherwise, no matter how tall a wall you build in your heart, sooner or later the wind will find its cracks.
Zompaynco: *Sigh* I was never good at keeping my feelings bottled up, even back when I was still in my hometown.
Jahoda: (YES! I said it! All that practice paid off, heheh!)
Zompaynco: Well, now that you know, could you help me make an introduction? I'm an outsider, and I don't want to intrude on Miss Lyylikki.
Jahoda: Don't worry, Lyylikki's really kind. Whether you're a complete stranger or in a form not entirely human, she's always warm and welcoming.
Zompaynco: Oh? Is that so...?
Zompaynco: But still, calling on her unannounced is rather impolite... Um, but if you happen to know her...
Jahoda: Of course I do! They should be just about done handing out the meals now. Let's head over.
Jahoda: Oh! And by the way, Boss also said we won't be charging you this time.
Zompaynco: Oh, thank you so much! I'll go get my things right away.
