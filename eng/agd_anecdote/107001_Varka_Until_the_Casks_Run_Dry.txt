# Varka: Until the Casks Run Dry

In front of the Adventurers' Guild in Nod-Krai, you run into Varka, who's discussing a new shipment of alcohol with Katheryne. It looks like he's run dry — again...

## Talk 0

Varka: Any updates on the commission from last time? Should be done just about now, right?
Katheryne: Apologies, Mr. Varka. The wine merchants from Mondstadt say they'll need more time to prepare.
Katheryne: Also, they have asked me to pass this message on to you: "Have you seriously finished the last batch? That cart was huge!"
Varka: Well, it's not like I'm the only one drinking. That amount of wine is barely enough for two banquets. We'll need more.
Traveler: Are you trying to empty out Mondstadt's wine cellars...
Varka: Hah, come on, I timed this down to the second. The Dawn Winery should be re-opening their cellar of Dandelion Wine right around now.
Varka: As the Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius in Mondstadt, patronizing their business is but one of my duties.
Varka: Don't worry, I've left some for them. I didn't clean it all out, or Charles would have nothing to work with.
Varka: The trouble with long journeys, though, is that wine tends to lose its flavor in the process. *sigh* Next time I get back, I'll have Charles mix me the strongest drink he's got.
Varka: Now, who to drink with... Kaeya would definitely come if I called, and Diluc... Well, if I can catch him, I'll have a couple of drinks with him too.
Varka: Hmm... Might as well get those two to have a drink together, perhaps...
