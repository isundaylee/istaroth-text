# Talk Group: Kofitse (NpcGroup 7650)

## Talk 0

Kofitse: Don't underestimate the power of these hornshrooms, outlander. Just as your lamps can dispel the darkness, the pigment you get from grinding these shrooms can help ward off the Dark Calamity.

## Talk 1

Traveler: Let me see what you've got...
Kofitse: Certainly. I may not be a merchant, but I have heard your name carried on the night wind. Ah, our Tumai{RUBY#[S]hope}ni — if you wish to barter, please come forward.

## Talk 2

Traveler: And you are...?
Kofitse: I am Kofitse of Mict{RUBY#[D]Masters of the Night-Wind}lan, and I walk as a supplicant of the Wayob. It is they who have sent me to show the outlander warriors of Nephil{RUBY#[D]Nod-Krai}heim the way.
Kofitse: Yet the guard named Levonty referred to me as a traveling merchant and ordered me to "conduct my business" here.
Kofitse: But surely, words of wisdom cannot be spoken to the foolish? No matter — the night wind will eventually make them know the secret truths I impart... the truths revealed by the Wayob in ages past.

## Talk 3

Traveler: About your goods...
Kofitse: These aren't goods, and neither am I a merchant, our Tumai{RUBY#[S]hope}ni.
Kofitse: These wondrous hornshrooms are thousand-year relics from Mict{RUBY#[D]Masters of the Night-Wind}lan. Our ancestors learned the art of grinding them from the mistress of the hidden weave, a secret that has been passed down alongside countless other sacred rites.
Kofitse: Simply follow the passed-down steps to grind the herb, and you will obtain a faint glow, akin to that of Phlogiston, to form graffiti markings that can hold back the tides of darkness.
Kofitse: Just as the people of Nephil{RUBY#[D]Nod-Krai}heim drive back the darkness with their lamps, we too wield our glimmer to resist the Black Tide. There is no distinction between our two peoples.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Goodbye.
Kofitse: The night wind will bring us together again one day, our Tumai{RUBY#[S]hope}ni.
