Unknown Role (None): Regardless, the Offering Box is a most wondrous artifact. Perhaps repairing another shall attract yet more people?

Unknown Role (None): Uh, that's not how that works...

Unknown Role (None): Is that so? Human matters are so very bothersome.

Unknown Role (None): P—Paimon thinks this has got way more to do with the state of the shrine...

Unknown Role (None): And what of my shrine?

Unknown Role (None): It's a little messy...

Unknown Role (None): It could use some cleanup...

Unknown Role (None): Ho, is that so? Those little rascals quite enjoy playing amongst the piles of fallen leaves.

Unknown Role (None): Ah, but if one yet recalls clearly, Hibiki used to use a long stick with pointy ends to build a pile of leaves. Now, if she did it, it must be the right thing to do.

Unknown Role (None): What do you need swept?

Unknown Role (None): What exactly do you need done?

Unknown Role (None): What would I know about the intricacies of human deeds? Still... if it were Hibiki...

Unknown Role (None): Hibiki often took that stick and waved it back and forth before the sun had risen. I used to enjoy chasing that stick, yes.

Unknown Role (None): But now that I am the provisional head priestess, such churlish behavior shall not do.

Unknown Role (None): Domeki?

Unknown Role (None): Snicker-snack?

Unknown Role (None): Neko probably means trimming the branches.

Unknown Role (None): Hmph. That rascal always chopped off the same branches that I loved most. An infuriating fellow.

Unknown Role (None): From the sound of it, they were doing cleanup. Guess you could call it re-organization as well.

Unknown Role (None): Sweeping the floor, washing it with water, and trimming the branches...

Unknown Role (None): Such familiar work, eh...

Unknown Role (None): Ah, yes. You may forgo the snicker-snacking. I like the branches just the way they are.

Unknown Role (None): The long stick, leaves, and water will do. Have you understood my words?

Unknown Role (None): Don't you worry about it.

Unknown Role (None): Actually, could I ask who Hibiki was?

Unknown Role (None): Hibiki is Hibiki. She is the head priestess of Asase Shrine.

Unknown Role (None): And your name...

Unknown Role (None): My name is Neko — a name bestowed by Hibiki.

Unknown Role (None): It was she who often came out to receive guests at the shrine, and so I was not obligated to manage such affairs.

Unknown Role (None): I had neither name nor title then. I slept where she wished, and played with whoever she willed.

Unknown Role (None): One day, Hibiki said that she would call me "Neko," on account of my habit of sleeping.

Unknown Role (None): Hibiki is presently absent, but a shrine is no shrine without a head priestess, and so I have taken up that post.

Unknown Role (None): That is also why I hope to restore the shrine to its former liveliness before Hibiki returns.

Unknown Role (None): ...That's right! Once she returns, I can fall asleep in her bosom, and not have to wait for her to repair the Offering Box, or wait for her to use that long stick of hers to clear the leaves.

Unknown Role (None): Well, I shall leave those tasks to you. There are some sneaky bugs that have stolen into the shrine, and they must now be driven out.

Unknown Role (None): Meow! Yes, this work was given specifically to me by Hibiki. A vital task to be completed by no other.
