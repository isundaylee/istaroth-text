# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 1795

## Talk 0

Phonia: The selection is going pretty well... Ah, it's you, Honorary Knight. We meet again.

## Talk 1

Traveler: "Selection"? What is this for?

Phonia: We're looking for new Signals Officers.

Phonia: To ensure that we have the upper hand wherever we fight, and on when and where we fight, we must improve our ability to gather and act on battlefield intelligence.

Phonia: The role of Signals Officer was created fairly recently for this very purpose.

Phonia: Unlike the Outriders who move fast and light, Signal Officers stay back, hold position, and coordinate closely with the main troops.

Phonia: Their alchemical recon gear is complex and demanding, and in terms of mobility, they sit somewhere between the light infantry and armored knights. This also means that they are more dependent on teamwork and coordination.

Phonia: According to our plan, once the relevant equipment and protocols are fully in place, Signals Officers will be assigned to every company. This ensures that capabilities are enhanced evenly and across the board.

Traveler: That sounds like quite a challenging role...

Traveler: I'm guessing there's a lot to learn, then?

Phonia: Yes. Overall, a Signals Officer is certainly held to more stringent standards than a regular knight.

Phonia: Just a few years back, I was still concerned that the technical requirements were too tough and that few would be able to meet them...

Phonia: That's because only three individuals had managed to pass Leonore's initial screening, and in the end, I was the only one left standing by the time equipment testing was finally complete.

Phonia: And even though I've transitioned to the role of Intelligence Officer since, I'm still handling several matters related to the project.

Phonia: Fortunately, the knights coming back from the expedition have shown themselves to be more capable with new concepts and complex mechanisms than anticipated. The plan is well on track.

Phonia: Hopefully we can get the first group of Signals Officers in place before the next major battle. That will significantly increase our combat effectiveness.

## Talk 2

Traveler: How have you been lately?

Phonia: I'm... Work-wise, I'm doing great.

Phonia: I've been recovering well, and have returned to work at full strength.

Phonia: I've got the Acting Grand Master and Captain Albedo to thank for that. Only Ematol and the knights who escorted me back then know exactly what happened to me.

Phonia: Most of my fellow knights don't think less of me, and I'm able to get my work done without trouble.

Phonia: I've also finished the outline for my book, "The Knight's Guide to the Prevention and Management of Psychic Disruption," which uses my misstep as a case study. I'll do my best to complete the full manuscript as soon as possible.

Traveler: And what's going on in the rest of your life?

Phonia: Ah, thanks for asking...

Phonia: After my transfer here to Dornman Port, I rented out my house and moved into a new place here with Ematol.

Phonia: We have plenty of space in the living room for cat trees and cat toys, as well as for Dustball, the cat Ematol adopted.

Phonia: Thanks to the two of them, I've been in a rather good mood lately...

Phonia: Leonore also comes by to see us every now and then. It does feel like my life is becoming more interesting.

Phonia: Say, if you're ever around and have the time, do drop by for some tea too. I'd love to pick your brain about possible future directions for the Knights of Favonius.

## Talk 3

Traveler: I'll see you again some other time.

Phonia: Of course. I'll be here.
