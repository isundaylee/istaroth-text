> Bennett: And voilà, you've got protective gear that's even more shock-absorbing than the Prancing Mushrooms of Sumeru! They say with it on, you could climb the Great Volcano of Tollan twice in a day and your soles and joints would still be fine!
> Traveler: That sounds like a pretty straightforward process to me.
> Bennett: You know, if I were anywhere else, I'd probably find myself stuck at the cooking part...
> Bennett: Either something unknown gets mixed into the pot and the whole thing spoils halfway through, or the entire pot explodes and sends fragments flying everywhere...

> Bennett: So, I decided to get exactly what I need to finally make gear I'd be satisfied with during this trip to the resort...
