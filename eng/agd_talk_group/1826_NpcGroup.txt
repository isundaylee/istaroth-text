# Talk Group: Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira (NpcGroup 1826)

## Talk 0

Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: "In the night dark as raven jade, the blossoms bloom bright among the clouds. The body, like foam on the water, must but return to silence in an instant. As the stars depart and the rain disperses into the void..." Verily, what refined elegance...

## Talk 1

Traveler: Who are you?
Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: My name is Sumida Shigehira; a scion of the Soga lineage am I, and erstwhile lieutenant commander of the shogun's guards, left division, by the grace of Her Excellency, the Almighty Narukami Ogosho.
Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: The final memory I retain, before I awoke in this domain, is of my presence upon the isle of Yashiori, leading a nocturnal foray against the foe.
Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: The two hosts met in the van, and the Taishou was lost to all sight... Mine own retainers would surely have deemed that the witch Mouun, she who serveth under Akuou, had wrought some heretical art and thus caused me to be spirited away.
Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: While Her Excellency's divine name of course remains untarnished, and that be no sin, I fear that Touzannou shall once again be forced to endure the insult of groundless grievance.
Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: Though he be our enemy, to heap such shame upon him is by no means the conduct of a noble warrior.

## Talk 2

Traveler: About the fireworks from before...
Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: Such thunder and brilliance. I had presumed it to be the divine lightning of Her Excellency, the Almighty Narukami Ogosho, and never did I dream it to be but the exquisite Liyue craft known as "fireworks"...
Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: To think this was created not for the smiting of foes, but solely to adorn the night sky with flowers of fire... What madness this be, and yet, what an age of tranquil prosperity!
Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: If, one day, such elegance might also bloom above the scarlet cherry blossoms of my homeland... that would be a matter of most profound joy.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Goodbye.
Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: Goodbye. May the winds of victory be with you.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Do you like them?
Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: Indeed. A flash of a bloom, a fleeting fall... The heart of the noble warrior should be exactly thus.
Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira: ...Having said that, I now am unable to fulfill my wish, and find myself instead a prisoner of this stagnant world... How lamentable...
