# The Many Matters Learned in One's Travels

Unknown Role (None): #Ah, Traveler, you've come at just the right time — I have something for you.

Unknown Role (None): A letter for help? Or a clue to some treasure?

Unknown Role (None): An escort commission, or a search and rescue?

Unknown Role (None): ...You're certainly very familiar with the kind of work we do, but I'm afraid this has nothing to do with any of the above commissions.

Unknown Role (None): Please, take this brand-new Adventurers' Guild notebook.

Unknown Role (None): But I already have the Adventurer Handbook...

Unknown Role (None): Why are you giving me a new one?

Unknown Role (None): The Adventurer Handbook is for recording details of your completed commissions and intel on all sorts of monsters.

Unknown Role (None): Whereas this notebook is for recording the marvelous people and wonderful events you encounter on your travels.

Unknown Role (None): Oh...

Unknown Role (None): That actually sounds kind of useful.

Unknown Role (None): I hope it will be of help to you. Anyway, that's all for now, my "commission" is complete.

Unknown Role (None): #Safe travels, Traveler. Ad astra abyssosque!

Unknown Role (None): *Any news?*
