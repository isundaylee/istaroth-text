# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 7053

## Talk 0

Gaudenty: What in the... Even that guy with amnesia could find a job, and everyone says there's tons of work lately! So why's none of it coming to me?

## Talk 1

Traveler: Work doesn't come to you. You go to work.

Gaudenty: And what do you know, huh? Just like those legendary adventures always sweep up their heroes by happenstance, legendary work will surely do the same...!

Gaudenty: Hmph! Who'd take those cruddy jobs? Scrounging up the commissions yourself, sweating buckets, smiling till your face cracks... Only a spineless loser would sign up for that garbage!

Gaudenty: ...Curses. Could that woman be sabotaging me...?

## Talk 2

Traveler: Commissions that "find" you tend to be the dangerous ones...

Gaudenty: So what? Nobody ever made a pretty penny without getting their fingers dirty. The bigger the risk, the bigger the reward...

Gaudenty: Besides, clients who post those kinds of requests would never demand you "smile the whole blasted time" or whine, "Hey, what's with the attitude?!" — they only care about professional results.

Gaudenty: *sigh*... A lucrative job like that could keep me fed for months... Which makes the recent drought even more depressing. Where are all the good jobs...?

Gaudenty: You! Do you have any commissions for me!? If not, get lost.

## Talk 4

Gaudenty: I mean, come on! I helped my client dig up three whole lost boxes of Mora. It was tiring, I nearly lost my life, and, what, they still wanted me to kneel, smile, and hand over the loot with both hands!?

Gaudenty: "You smashed the box onto his face," yeah, right! Get a load of her, mouthing off like she was actually there... All I did was give it over a little forcefully...

Gaudenty: ...Curse it all. What rotten luck I have... Alright, don't stand in my way, or you might scare some potential clients away.
