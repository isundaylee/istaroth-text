# Talk Group: Akvilina (NpcGroup 7535)

## Talk 0

Traveler: What do you think about the current crop of actors?
Dorofeya: I must admit, there is a remarkable wealth of talent among them! If we exclude the veterans like Vodyanitsa entirely... the one who has really caught everyone's eye of late is Miss Odette. She truly is magnificent.
Akvilina: Violetta... Ah, that's Odette's mother. We worked together in the past. Mind you, while the lass certainly cannot compare to an almost monstrously gifted dancer like her mother...
Akvilina: When I watch Odette dance, I always see... Hmm, how should I put it? Yes, I detect an absolute passion for ballet in every move she makes. That characteristic, coupled with a certain stubbornness that compels her to go all out in every performance, makes her truly one-of-a-kind.
Akvilina: Everyone is trying their best to dazzle and shine on stage — how wonderful is that?

## Talk 1

Akvilina: It's not just Downtown — the Veche could use a thorough reckoning too...

## Talk 2

Traveler: You are...?
Akvilina: Akvilina Aristarkhovna Lebedyanskaya, Privy Councillor (3rd Class) and Officer of the Druzhna Guards. A blade ever prepared to strike and fall in Her Majesty's service.
Akvilina: Whatever rot festers within the Veche, Her Majesty's glory shall not be defiled by any man. Please keep that truth firmly in mind.

## Talk 3

Traveler: What's wrong with the Veche?
Akvilina: It is rotten, utterly corrupt.
Akvilina: Pardon my bluntness, but in the Veche of today, just how many men are truly loyal to Her Majesty?
Akvilina: So boundless is Her Majesty's grace, they have been permitted to voice their dissent. But that does not mean they may exploit this kindness to feign obedience while defying her orders.
Akvilina: Look closely at the current proposals from the aristocracy and the populace alike: are they really serving Her Majesty with absolute devotion, or are they ingratiating themselves with that Khaenri'ahn and his lackeys?
Akvilina: Me, I cannot say. But if it is the latter, my sword will uncover the truth.

## Talk 4

Traveler: You know, that reminds me. I think there are Vila mercenaries out in the wilds...
Akvilina: Ah... Those contemptible degenerates who have completely abandoned righteousness and honor. They stand as a permanent shame upon my people, and as fools who refuse to wake from their delusions.
Akvilina: During the Belyi Tsar's rule, it was His Majesty who granted us amnesty and sanctioned our life alongside the humans. It is thanks to him that we now share in the prosperity of today...
Akvilina: Yet some remain fixated on the privileges of the past, and have chosen to become bandits out in the tundra than submit to Her Majesty's boundless grace.
Akvilina: Should you encounter those traitors, show no restraint. I would never grant them even the opportunity to beg for mercy.

## Talk 5

Traveler: Farewell.
Akvilina: For the glory of the Tsaritsa.
