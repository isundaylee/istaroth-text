## Talk 3

Unknown Role (None): Anything you'd like to do?

Unknown Role (None): I... I have been sleepless of late.

Unknown Role (None): Oh, no, please don't misunderstand. The beds here are absolutely delightful, far more than I require.

Unknown Role (None): Besides, for someone of my particular habitation arrangements, I have never lost sleep on account of the outside environment.

Unknown Role (None): Then...?

Unknown Role (None): Lately, I've been longing for that time, long ago, when I slept alone in absolute darkness.

Unknown Role (None): It was a slumber without dreams, without consciousness, without joy, and without pain.

Unknown Role (None): It was a strange sensation. An absolute state of peace and emptiness.

Unknown Role (None): Perhaps it is difficult for most people to understand, but I was immersed in that slumber for a long time indeed.

Unknown Role (None): Compared to it, the dreamful sleep that normal humans experience every night feels almost... too noisy.

Unknown Role (None): I don't suppose you want to...

Unknown Role (None): ...To extinguish myself again? Hehehe, of course not.

Unknown Role (None): Thinking about my past experiences is merely a way for me to review my current judgments and position.

Unknown Role (None): To yearn for peace, and to forsake it for a new purpose — these paths do not conflict.

Unknown Role (None): I hope my prattling on and on does not bore you.

Unknown Role (None): I'm not bored at all.

Unknown Role (None): Then all is well. The fact I can share these trivial thoughts with you is proof of our friendship.

Unknown Role (None): I don't really understand...

Unknown Role (None): That is fine. There is always a difference between two individuals, and to fully bridge that gap of understanding is a nigh-insurmountable task.

Unknown Role (None): But it is precisely because we think differently that our conversations are meaningful.
