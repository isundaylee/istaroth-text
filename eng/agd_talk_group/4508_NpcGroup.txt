# Talk Group: Vitruvius (NpcGroup 4508)

## Talk 0

Vitruvius: Finally. Now, I don't have to wonder who that sand in my mouth used to be.

## Talk 1

Traveler: What was your era like?
Vitruvius: I suppose my era comes slightly after Josephus'. You're familiar with him, aren't you?
Vitruvius: Still, the history in between remains largely the same. To think that a whole generation could slip by without a single meaningful change...
Vitruvius: Looking at it that way, my era is nothing more than a Temple of Space, albeit one where everyone grows old.
Vitruvius: That said, my social standing is far superior to his. After all, I had a spot reserved for me in the cemetery of Irminsul trees.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Bye now!
Vitruvius: See you next time.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Do you have any legendary accomplishments?
Vitruvius: Actually, the golden ratio for the human figure in fine arts... I was the one who performed those calculations.
Vitruvius: Somewhere down the line, though, the "Vitruvian Form" became "Vitruvius's Form."
Vitruvius: Now, countless generations actually believe I was the naked model for that sketch. I've had painters from the later generations walk up to me, look me up and down, and say, "You know, your physique isn't all that impressive."
Vitruvius: To which I reply, "Because it was never meant to be me!"
