# Talk Group: Illuga - Serenitea Pot (NpcGroup 9127)

## Talk 0

Illuga: So, this your secret base?
Illuga: Pretty spacious here, but I get a sense of comfort too, somehow.
Illuga: Suppose it's because I don't sense any danger here.

## Talk 1

Traveler: Are you settling in okay?
Illuga: Yeah, don't worry about me, I'm very adaptable.
Illuga: Once, when my old man and I were pursuing the Wild Hunt to the northern lands, we accidentally stumbled into a Domain.
Illuga: There were countless monsters inside. The veteran Ratniki and I fought for three days and nights before we finally found the exit and retreated.
Illuga: And despite all that, I managed to sneak naps in between enemy attacks.
Illuga: My old man said he thought I was scared stiff, seeing as how I'd fallen asleep before my eyes even closed, haha.
Illuga: But here, I don't have to constantly keep an ear out for distant alarms, or be worried if the enemy will suddenly attack all night.
Illuga: I can get a good night's sleep.
Illuga: Only problem is... Well, you've put me out of a job.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Then get the rest you deserve.

Option 2:

> Traveler: Don't even think about work while you're here.

Illuga: Easy for you to say, but now that I can suddenly just relax, I feel strangely empty inside.
Illuga: And I can't help but wonder if I'll have trouble readjusting to the rhythm of a life of war.
Traveler: Don't you have anything you want to do?
Illuga: Hmm... Nope, not really.
Traveler: Eh?
Illuga: Now that I think about it, I hone my combat skills to protect everyone, and study cooking to make food for everyone.
Illuga: ...I can't think of a single thing I do just for myself...

Option 1:

> Traveler: Then let's start thinking about it now, eh?

Option 2:

> Traveler: Then let's go make some new friends, shall we?

Illuga: Yeah. Sounds like a good call.
Illuga: Well then, I'll treat your suggestion as my next "job".

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to chat?
Illuga: Of course, that's exactly what I was thinking. I just happened to be wondering where to find you, actually.
Traveler: Need anything?
Illuga: Nothing in particular, but friends should meet often, shouldn't they?
Illuga: Unlike my colleagues, who operate from fixed locations, adventurers like you come and go like the wind.
Illuga: See, if I want to visit Mr. Flins, all I need to do is visit the lighthouse during the day a few extra times, keep knocking, and I'll find him eventually.
Illuga: And if I want to contact Miss Nefer, even if I can't find her in person, I can leave a message at the Curatorium of Secrets to schedule a meeting.
Illuga: By comparison, finding you is much harder. After all, you're often out adventuring, and might not even be in Nod-Krai.
Traveler: I'll come by to see you.
Illuga: Haha, sounds like something I would say. Never thought I'd be on the receiving end.
Illuga: Thanks! Still, would be better if I had a way to contact you anytime.
Illuga: Could we get Miss Aino to invent some kind of instant messaging device? To be honest, we Ratniki could really use something like that...
Traveler: It might be faster to just post a commission.
Illuga: Huh. I didn't think of that. It does have "adventurer" written all over it...
Illuga: Right, wrote it down. You can look forward to receiving a commission to "guard a camp with the Ratniki."
Illuga: As for the commission itself... you'll spend the whole day playing cards with my colleagues and me.
Illuga: Though, I'm still not sure how good you are yet... Maybe we should have Mr. Flins join too...

Option 1:

> Traveler: No need to be so serious about it.
> Illuga: Haha, games are all about winning, though! I can't help but get serious.

Option 2:

> Traveler: I've got backup, too.
> Illuga: Haha, then call them all! Anyone you consider a friend is welcome amongst us Lightkeepers.

Illuga: Yes, the match will be one for the record books.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Anything you'd like to do?
Illuga: Well, what I have been doing is studying your secret base. It's every bit as mysterious as I expected, that's for sure.
Illuga: You'd think it'd be full of openings and weaknesses, but no, it's a sealed-in world unto itself.
Illuga: Even Aedon might have a hard time sneaking out.
Illuga: But then again, if monsters were to get into this teapot and bar the entrance...
Illuga: Wouldn't that put those inside in danger?

Option 1:

> Traveler: Monsters can't get in though, can they?
> Illuga: Probably... but that isn't a guarantee they won't attack, is it?
> Illuga: They could, for example, take the form of someone you know, then sneak in when you're not paying attention.

Option 2:

> Traveler: I won't let any monsters in.
> Illuga: And I trust you, of course, but you can't possibly anticipate everything.
> Illuga: Maybe monsters could take the form of someone you know, then sneak in when you're not paying attention.

Traveler: Is something like that even possible...?
Illuga: If something isn't 100% impossible, then there's a possibility it'll happen, right?
Illuga: And as they say, nothing's ever 100% impossible.
Traveler: And what do you think, Illuga?
Illuga: Well, I'd say that prevention beats a cure. After all, the latter might come too late.
Illuga: As such, I'm planning to build a barbican around the entrance here first.
Illuga: That way, even if an enemy does manage to sneak in, it will instantly realize they've fallen into my trap.
Illuga: Next, I can build several defensive towers around said barbican. Taking blind spots into account... eight watchtowers should do the trick.
Illuga: If we make full use of the materials, we could build a fortress that rivals the walls of Piramida. Just leave it to me.

Option 1:

> Traveler: It's good that you have things you want to do too.

Option 2:

> Traveler: You've really thought this through.

Illuga: Oh right! That reminds me, we need to build a granary, and stock it with half a year's worth of provisions for everyone.
Illuga: Dad said that when it comes to war, most are about which side can hold out longer.
Illuga: No way can we lose because of something like that!

Illuga: Good morning, sleep well last night?
Illuga: I just caught a fish. What would you say to some fish soup for today's breakfast?
