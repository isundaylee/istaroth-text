# Minor Commission

Dhabit: The publishing trade hardly makes a living these days. Maybe I should just focus on investments...

Traveler: Let me see the investment coupons.

Dhabit: Here you go. 2,000 Mora per coupon, with each investment tenure maturing weekly. Now then, into the pouch it goes...

Dhabit: ...Ugh!

Traveler: What's wrong?

Dhabit: Oh hello, Jahoda. What are you doing here? Does Lady Nefer...

Jahoda: Oh, no, no, I'm just out shopping with my friend.

Jahoda: Heh, looks like your investment business is doing really well, Boss Dhabit.

Dhabit: Oh, it's nothing, nothing, haha... just a small venture to make ends meet.

Dhabit: (Just look at her... She's being all sly and probing! I bet she's here on Nefer's orders. Tch.)

Traveler: Can this really make money?

Jahoda: Of course! Boss Nef says that Boss Dhabit is the most reputable AND the most capable businessman. Investing with him is definitely a safe bet.

Dhabit: Yes, yes... haha...

Dhabit: (Nefer! I knew it!)

Traveler: Then, when I can expect the payout on my investment...?

Dhabit: Today! Right this very day. You just bought our brand-new... short-term investment coupon! Profits and losses on this one are settled instantly!

Paimon: Kinda feels like you just made all that up...

Dhabit: Uh... Don't worry about it. A—Anyway, thanks for buying our short-term investment coupon!

Dhabit: Here's your investment return. Have a wonderful day!

Traveler: Well... making money is always a good thing, I suppose...

Traveler: Come on, Paimon, let's go buy some sugar sculptures.

Paimon: Really? Heehee, then Paimon wants one shaped like a little bird! That's Paimon's favorite!

Jahoda: Oh? I'll come with you two then. I can get one for Boss too.

Dhabit: M-Miss Jahoda, please wait!

Dhabit: Um... Do you happen to have a message for me from Lady Nefer by any chance...?

Jahoda: Oh. Boss said, "We've located what you're looking for."

Jahoda: "Do have patience, Mr. Dhabit. One step at a time — that's how proper business is done."

Dhabit: Yes, yes, of course. Lady Nefer is right... In that case, I'll be counting on you two...
