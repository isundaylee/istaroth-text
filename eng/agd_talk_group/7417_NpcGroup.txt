## Talk 2

Traveler: Do the Druzhna need to deal with the Shadow Realm...?
Yarovaya: You may as well ask if winter is cold. Our duty is to ensure that all lands the Tsaritsa surveys are at peace.
Yarovaya: The Shadow Realm is the antithesis of such, being a most unstable... disaster. We handle every report sent by the Energy Commission concerning this issue...

## Talk 3

Traveler: I won't disturb you then.
Yarovaya: Alright, be about your business, then. Ah. Right. If you discover a Shadow Realm, please notify us in good time.

## Talk 4

Traveler: The Energy Commission?
Yarovaya: We Druzhna are not at liberty to scramble this way and that. The Commission, which spends its days traveling across Snezhnaya to maintain the Kresnik's Torches, is more nimble in this regard...
Yarovaya: It does strike me as somewhat odd... It sometimes feels like they know something about the Shadow Realm...
Yarovaya: But so long as they detect the Realms in time, that's good enough for me.

## Talk 5

Traveler: How do you usually handle the issue?
Yarovaya: We organize search groups, and if they detect the Shadow Realm's energy core, they neutralize it. If not, they'll seal it off.
Yarovaya: Most Shadow Realms disappear naturally over time... so there's no great disaster, so long as no one enters them by mistake.
