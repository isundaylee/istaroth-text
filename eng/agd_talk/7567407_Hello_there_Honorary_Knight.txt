# Talk Dialog

Barbara: Hello there, Honorary Knight.
Barbara: I only found out upon arriving in Natlan how much everyone here respects you. I'd say it sometimes beats how much Mondstadters respect you as the Honorary Knight.

Option 1:

> Traveler: I just did what I could to help.
> Barbara: Speaking of which, I'd like to give you a bit of advice as a friend—
> Barbara: You can't always pour yourself into various commissions and focus solely on fulfilling the wishes of others.
> Barbara: You've gotta take some time for your body and mind, too. Don't let that fatigue build up.
> Barbara: You know... Like my sister does.
> Traveler: Thank you, Barbara.
> Barbara: This kind of relaxing gathering works great for that. You could definitely stand to organize similar events when you're with your friends in other nations.
> Barbara: I've never had the chance to see you dance or lead dances before today. So if you don't mind, I'll help myself to a seat in the audience.

Option 2:

> Traveler: I see you've made quite a few new friends, too.
> Barbara: Yeah! Everyone in Natlan has been such a pleasant surprise!
> Barbara: I didn't know that Miss Xilonen, who visited Mondstadt recently, was such a skilled craftswoman. I've heard that even the Archon goes to her to get tools made...
> Barbara: And to finally join the Dance-Off Stage for myself... Ah, it's truly marvelous.
> Barbara: The way the sound twists and pulses with each turn of the knobs, and those constantly flashing lights... It gave me chills, but that was a pleasant feeling.
> Barbara: Miss Xilonen also gave me a record of some unique music. She said that Fontainian devices can also play said record.
> Barbara: I'll make sure to listen closely to it once I get back.
> Traveler: Was Mualani the first to find you?
> Barbara: She was... and well, let's just say that I'm still getting my bearings on that one.
> Barbara: When I first received her invitation, my reaction was... Well... "Is this some new type of scam?"
> Traveler: "New type of scam?"
> Barbara: Fortunately, as I read on, I could feel her passion through the words written on paper.
> Barbara: I've heard my fair share of earnest prayers. Such sincerity, which comes from the heart, cannot be faked.
> Barbara: And sure enough, when our caravan arrived at this stop, I saw her rushing over on a surfboard. That's when I knew...
> Barbara: "This is the person I'm looking for. I'm sure we'll make great friends!"
> Barbara: After that, she dropped all the work she had on hand and took me all the way from the People of the Springs to where the Children of Echoes live.
> Barbara: We received some friends from Sumeru there, and then it was off to the Flower-Feather Clan to take a boat to this resort.
> Traveler: Sounds like a great time.
> Barbara: It was like a dream...
> Barbara: Of course, we've still got plenty of time, so I'll make sure to continue enjoying my trip.

Option 3:

> Traveler: How are things with Kachina?
> Barbara: To be honest, she reminded me...
> Barbara: ...Of me, when I'd just learned to dance.
> Barbara: Ah, I'm not talking about her skills, but more her state of mind.
> Barbara: She loves dancing, and she practices hard, but she still gets stage fright when she's in front of strangers.
> Traveler: Ah, you've noticed that as well.
> Barbara: Where dancing is concerned, her potential is well beyond her humble self-assessment.
> Barbara: Really, it'd break my heart if we couldn't polish that talent like a gem.
> Barbara: I'll share all that I've learned with her. Hopefully, that can help her overcome those little shortcomings of hers.

Option 4:

> Traveler: Have you spoken to Nilou?
> Barbara: Of course! She's a famous dancer from Sumeru, you know.
> Barbara: Even if I hadn't accepted this invitation, I'd have gone looking for her sooner or later — I am Mondstadt's idol, after all.
> Barbara: Dance knows no boundaries, and an idol must always push hers.
> Barbara: That said, we haven't gotten to know each other as quickly as I did with our Natlanese friends.
> Barbara: Too many fun places to visit, maybe? Either way, we haven't had time to have a proper chat.
> Barbara: Her dance steps, though, give you a sense of natural grace, like a flower bud in the midst of blooming.
> Barbara: Dance steps are the most authentic way to reflect someone's personality, so I would guess that she's usually a quieter person...?
> Barbara: I think perhaps a soft approach to getting along with her might work best, in that case.
> Traveler: I think you're easygoing to get along with, Barbara.
> Barbara: After all, she's a friend from Sumeru and a famous dancer, too. I'd think there might be some differences in our cultural customs and personal habits, no?
> Barbara: Jean reminded me to take note of that... Before coming here, I borrowed some books to learn the basics on Natlanese culture.
> Barbara: That said, I didn't have much time to learn about Sumeru in detail this time.
> Barbara: I should do my research over the next few days, maybe ask Mualani a few questions...

Option 5:

> Barbara: Jean felt that, as a Deaconess of the Church of Favonius, it would be best if I dressed formally for my first public appearance in Natlan.
> Barbara: As for Father, he reminded me that the sunlight here can be very intense, so I should watch for that.
> Barbara: That's how I came to this party in the same clothes I wear to church, despite the heat.
