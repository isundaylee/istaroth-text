## Talk 1

Traveler: About the Lightkeepers...
Nikita: The Lightkeepers were established to safeguard the freedom and peace of Nod-Krai. It was five hundred years ago that the Torchforger brought the first of the group together.
Nikita: That said, there were moments over the years when it seemed we might fade into history. Our troop strength has, in turn, been rebuilt time and again.
Nikita: But no matter what, as long as the light that symbolizes our will is not extinguished, we shall be reborn in flame anew.

## Talk 2

Traveler: About Piramida...
Nikita: Piramida is where we Lightkeepers are headquartered. Legend holds that it was founded by our first Starshyna, Solovei the Torchforger.
Nikita: And before that, this was once the palace of the fae governor of Nod-Krai. In fact, you can still see the coat of arms from that era on the stone walls of the foundations.
Nikita: If the stories are true, who knows, you might even uncover the fae governor's buried treasure.

## Talk 3

Traveler: I'd never have guessed that you're the Starshyna...
Nikita: Why, were you expecting an agile, fearless warrior instead of an old man like me?
Nikita: The Starshyna who came before me would have lived up to your expectations far better. I once fought by his side...
Nikita: But I do not possess the strength he had. The Lightkeepers are also no longer what they once were, which is why we must exercise extra caution in everything we do.
