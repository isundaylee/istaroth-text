Unknown Role (None): Is this the last stop for our first day? We're pretty familiar with this place, seeing how we've been here plenty of times.

Unknown Role (None): Hmph? If the Pale Floater is so confident, then why not swap roles with me? I will play the audience, you be the speaker.

Unknown Role (None): Yeah. Let's give it a try. Here comes Paimon.

Unknown Role (None): The place before us — covered with flowers and flowing water — is the Vourukasha Oasis. This place doesn't have... uh... Right! This place isn't infested by the demons of Dev, and is the home of the Pari.

Unknown Role (None): There are many Pari here. The best among them is one called Zurvan.

Unknown Role (None): Sorush, you were born here too, right? Though you were different from the other Pari...

Unknown Role (None): For example, while the other Pari only sound kinda weird when they speak, you sound *really* weird! Hmm. You all came from the same waters, but why is it that only you are so strange...

Unknown Role (None): Hey!

Unknown Role (None): Paimon's not lying. When you were speaking to the Order of Skeptics, your words were kinda weird. Well, the Skeptics themselves sounded weird too. So there's that.

Unknown Role (None): Whew! Paimon thought all the other Pari were going to be exactly like you. In fact, Paimon was worried about that until we finally got to the Vourukasha Oasis and met Zurvan.

Unknown Role (None): Then, we found out that only you are like... Uh, how should Paimon put this... you have a particular way of speaking to the Order of Skeptics, right?

Unknown Role (None): It seems the Order of Skeptics had too much of an influence on her.

Unknown Role (None): Uh... I will not deny that.

Unknown Role (None): Seeing as you have no objections, Paimon will keep going. There is a big stump in the Vourukasha Oasis—

Unknown Role (None): Pale Floater, you absolute...

Unknown Role (None): Uh, it's the "Harvisptokhm" in the Vourukasha Oasis! Man, these Pari names sure are a mouthful. And, it totally is a big stump, isn't it...?

Unknown Role (None): Paimon remembers you saying that the stump is also a god, and that the Pari have a deep relationship with it.

Unknown Role (None): Though these are indeed the facts... Letting the Pale Floater describe them would be too unflattering. I shall resume the role of the speaker.

Unknown Role (None): Alright... but Paimon thought she was making it easy to understand.

Unknown Role (None): Hmph... Regardless, five hundred years ago, our great god gave all to quell the disaster afflicting this place. As such, all the Amrita here is the last gift from the divine.

Unknown Role (None): Nourished by the Amrita, the Harvisptokhm and the Pari emerged. The eldest of the Pari, and my elder... is Zurvan.

Unknown Role (None): My birth came much later, so I was not a personal witness to those events. I heard my elder speak of it but...

Unknown Role (None): Elder Zurvan clearly associated with the Order of Skeptics when they were first established, but after my birth, all contact seemed to be lost between them.

Unknown Role (None): Though my elder has long stated the importance of Pari traditions, she rarely mentions the past in detail when she speaks with me. Instead, it was the Order of Skeptics...

Unknown Role (None): Who enthusiastically shared the results of their historical research with me, and approved of my wish to follow in the footsteps of the divine bird.

Unknown Role (None): Huh? So after that, you slowly drifted away from the other Pari and learned your strange way of speaking from the Order of Skeptics?

Unknown Role (None): Oh... I suppose that can be seen as correct. Objectively speaking.

Unknown Role (None): It sounds like a rebellious phase...

Unknown Role (None): A rebellious phase... I heard the humans in the Order of Skeptics mention something about this. They said the researchers in the forest experienced a "Satyavada Life" or something like that. Is this "rebellious phase" a similar thing that humans progress through?

Unknown Role (None): Yeah, that sounds pretty similar.

Unknown Role (None): Uh, no! They're not the same thing at all!

Unknown Role (None): Maybe you can think of it as a necessary process for growth...

Unknown Role (None): It's not entirely like the Satyavada Life thing that the researchers experience. In fact, Paimon's heard that plenty of human children experience it...

Unknown Role (None): Generally, kids going through a rebellious phase will resist the care of their parents and try to assert their independence. That's what Paimon's heard anyway.

Unknown Role (None): Hmm, just like you did.

Unknown Role (None): Hmph. My past has little to do with my present. Whatever can be said, I have now returned to the Vourukasha Oasis and earned the Khvarena's favor.

Unknown Role (None): You're amazing, Sorush!

Unknown Role (None): Never mind, Pale Floater. It means nothing to be patronized by one as immature as you.

Unknown Role (None): That's all for today. Yasnapati, do you or the Pale Floater wish to ask anything?

Unknown Role (None): Speaking of, are the Pari and Aranara related in any way?

Unknown Role (None): ...Why do you ask this?

Unknown Role (None): Because the Pari and Aranara are both small...

Unknown Role (None): But are still capable of great things.

Unknown Role (None): You describe your adventures with Arama and all the other Aranara to Sorush...

Unknown Role (None): Hmmm... How very important Festival Utsava appears to be. I heard my elder mention it once, but only thought of it as little more than a joyful gathering for the Aranara.

Unknown Role (None): But... if the Aranara have their own day, why do we Pari possess nothing of equivalence? I must mention this to my elder...

Unknown Role (None): Why do you want a Pari Festival? Is it because you can eat lots of tasty snacks during a festival?

Unknown Role (None): Of course not! The Pari Festival should... should have humans offering their odes and songs to us, while I get to choose the best singer of the lot.

Unknown Role (None): That doesn't sound like a festival at all. Well, not for the attending humans, anyway. It's more like a high-pressure competition.

Unknown Role (None): But it is an enjoyable celebration for me, yes? Hence, I believe my elder may also enjoy such an occasion.

Unknown Role (None): Perhaps... Zurvan is right. For Sorush to become a so-called Bloomguard, she might need a bit more training...
