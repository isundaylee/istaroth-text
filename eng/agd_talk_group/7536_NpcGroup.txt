# Talk Group: Vilora (NpcGroup 7536)

## Talk 0

Vilora: It... it's only a fitness test. I'll never give up no matter how many times I have to take it!

## Talk 1

Traveler: A fitness test, you say?
Vilora: That's right! You have to pass the fitness test to join the Druzhna...
Vilora: Ugh, you have no idea how demanding those in charge of the test are! They even make you run... tell me, in what world would a proper lady have learned to run before???
Vilora: My mum says if this were back in the day, all a Vila noblewoman needs to join the Druzhna would be a letter recommendation... And now they want to assess you first??? That's ridiculous!

## Talk 2

Traveler: Best of luck to you.
Vilora: Thanks! I will no doubt succeed this time... Well, most probably!

## Talk 3

Traveler: Why do you want to join the Druzhna?
Vilora: Because of Ves—— Ahem, no, to defend the glory of the Tsaritsa, of course!
Vilora: You see, the Druzhna is the very pillar of order of our nation. It's what we all look towards for authority and guidance! Haven't you seen the elegant Commander Vesna out and about on inspections?
Vilora: That close-fitting uniform, the exquisite ribbon, and that very cool sword... Wow...
Vilora: So, even if I have to—— no, no, there is no "even if" here! For the glory of the Tsaritsa, I must pass the test no matter what!
