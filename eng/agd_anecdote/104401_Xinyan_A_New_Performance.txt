# Xinyan: A New Performance

A passionate musician is visiting Nasha Town.

You encounter Xinyan in Nasha Town. She seems to have stumbled upon fresh inspiration — perhaps she's planning to arrange a show?

## Talk 0

Xinyan: #Hey, if it isn't Traveler!
Xinyan: Didn't expect to see you here! Hehe, this surprise meeting is pretty sick — I should spin it into a tune right here!

Option 1:

> Traveler: Hello Xinyan!

Option 2:

> Traveler: Are you having fun?

Xinyan: I sure am! Having a ball, to be honest. "Fun" is where the inspiration lies!
Xinyan: Most of my songs might be about rebellion and breaking down prejudices and all that, but that doesn't mean I have to beat a serious rhythm all the time.
Xinyan: Too much of a downer really ruins the feel of a song. You've gotta keep it bright sometimes, y'know?
Xinyan: You gotta relax and recharge, that way you'll be ready to kill it when it's time to perform.

Option 1:

> Traveler: That's a good point...

Option 2:

> Traveler: But there are countless places you could go to have fun.

Option 3:

> Traveler: Why choose Nod-Krai?

Xinyan: I guess I'm hopin' for... some sort of unexpected breakthrough?
Xinyan: I've been so focused on writing new material, but everything I come up with feels old and stale. I need something new.
Xinyan: So I decided to take a holiday, to try escaping this prison I've built for myself.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Very decisive.

Option 2:

> Traveler: Great idea.

Xinyan: My last stop was Fontaine, where I saw a magazine with some photos of Nod-Krai. It got my creative juices flowing, and I was itching to find out more.
Xinyan: Well, after looking into it, it turns out the whole atmosphere and way of thinking out here are like nothing I've encountered before. There's some group called the Frostmoon Scions who lean into some pretty far-out instrumentation...
Xinyan: So, I was thinking... maybe I could take some inspiration from the local folk scene and incorporate it into my own music...

Option 1:

> Traveler: Incorporate... far-out-ness...?

Option 2:

> Traveler: Folk... rock 'n' roll?

Xinyan: Sure! I wanna try adding a few flutes and keys, plus some of the more out-there melodic phrasing.
Xinyan: Obviously, I can't just flip genres completely — still gotta keep the core lyrical and musical themes intact.
Xinyan: Anyway, when I got to Nasha Town, I bumped my guitar case on some corrugated iron and it made this really funky sound.
Xinyan: I got this sudden flash of inspiration — I shouldn't just be looking for new instruments to improve my music, I should be trying to enhance my guitar's timbre too!

Option 1:

> Traveler: What kind of timbre are we talking about?

Option 2:

> Traveler: Can you hum it for us?

Xinyan: Bum-bum-ba... bum-bum-ba... Something like that...
Xinyan: Hah, it's kinda hard to replicate a guitar with your voice, y'know?
Xinyan: Basically, I'm goin' for something punchy, compact, clean — and absolutely savage!
Xinyan: If I can modify my guitar's acoustics, ramp up the compression, and add a second sound hole to boost the lower-end frequencies, I think I can get a sharper, more metallic sound...

Option 1:

> Traveler: I... have no idea what you just said, but it sounded very impressive!

Option 2:

> Traveler: I'm looking forward to the results!

Xinyan: Haha! Thanks for the support! Aw man, my motivation's like... a double bass drum going at 200 BPM right now!
Xinyan: Gimme a bit to play around with this and hammer out the kinks. I'll invite you to the official unveiling of my breakthrough sound soon enough!
