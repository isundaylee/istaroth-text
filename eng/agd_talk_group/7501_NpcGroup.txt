# Talk Group: Vzdykhalov (NpcGroup 7501)

## Talk 0

Balkonov: My radiant Yadova! By the fair moon shining in the heavens, I swear my love for you...
Yadova: Oh, Balkonov! Swear not by the moon. Even the crescent above is ever-changing... Moonlight hangs too far beyond our reach, and I fear your vows may prove just as fleeting in the eyes of the gods.
Balkonov: Then by what shall I swear? My family name? My very heart?
Balkonov: Though our parents wage war this very moment around the Council's round table, my heart shall belong to you, and you alone.
Yadova: Hush, my love! Speak softer. Should Father's spies overhear us, we'll each be locked away in our estates and made to drink the bitter poison of parting.
Balkonov: For you, I would gladly drink true poison and meet a hero's end.
Yadova: Oh, Balkonov, my sweet idiot! If you were to leave me behind, I'd gladly offer my heart to your blade. Let me be the first to fall, for—
Vzdykhalov: ...Ahem. Pardon the interruption, but the play's already begun. Do either of you actually have tickets?
Yadova: ...Do we?
Balkonov: ...No.
