# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 6553

## Talk 0

Tlapalli: I wonder how Atzilli is... She's just so immersed in her research. I should check on how she's been.

Tlapalli: And that "bartender"... Probably best to keep an eye on that matter too.

Tlapalli: Oh, it's you, adventurer. Thank you for helping us out with our Brewblossom trouble.

Traveler: Atzilli...?

Tlapalli: Curious about her? She's my little sister, and she's also one of Me & Dew's winemakers.

Tlapalli: She's been hiding herself away behind the mountain, working on making a new drink that doesn't need Aphids. After it's drunk, it's supposed to let the drinker see some amazing things.

Tlapalli: I really admire her spirit of research. If you're interested, just take a stroll around the mountain, I'm sure you'll bump into her.

Traveler: What about the bartender?

Tlapalli: Lately, we've had reports of some guy wearing an eyepatch. He's been standing at the entrance to Tete Isle and impersonating our bartenders.

Tlapalli: It's weird. Even though he's not one of our guys, he's always recommending our drinks to the guests. And he's doing this for free... Maybe he's just a fan?

Tlapalli: Anyway, there's no need for you to worry about that. I'll track down this kindly "bartender" and find out what his deal is.

Traveler: Is there anywhere suitable for adventurers around here?

Tlapalli: Haha, oh man, you're late by about twenty years.

Tlapalli: Back in the day, the Mare Jivari was THE location to spelunk and explore. It drew adventure teams from all over Teyvat. Unfortunately, it's gone now.

Tlapalli: If you'd been around back then, you'd have made loads of like-minded friends, and there'd be a million places to visit.

Traveler: Did the adventure teams find anything?

Tlapalli: Well... Nobody could crack the code of why the Mare Jivari disappeared, but I heard a few were able to enter some secret area that they thought was it.

Tlapalli: Apparently, there was more than one strange little pocket like this. People came to call them the "Mare Enthymema." They say it's like the Mare Jivari casting a shadow of itself into our world.

Tlapalli: But in the last few years, there haven't even been any sightings of the Mare Enthymema...

Traveler: Well, see you around.

Tlapalli: Goodbye, and enjoy your stay!
