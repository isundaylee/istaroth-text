# Talk Group: Bulgakov (NpcGroup 7822)

## Talk 0

Bulgakov: Had I known this was going to happen, I'd never have opted to become a rural doctor. All this travel... *Sigh*

## Talk 1

Traveler: What's wrong?
Bulgakov: N—nothing. Oh, you look like one of those noble-minded individuals who are willing to listen to others pour out their troubles...
Bulgakov: It's just that... I'm feeling a little vexed about my work. It took me so much effort to graduate from medical school with decent results. But now, I have no choice but to travel on these trains day in, day out to treat patients in far-off villages and towns...
Bulgakov: Not only are the living conditions in these places poor, sometimes, they can't even afford to pay me my fee. *Sigh* But what am I supposed to do, right? When there's a sickly patient lying right there?

## Talk 2

Traveler: Why don't you look for another job then?
Bulgakov: Another job... But all those years I've spent at the Royal Military Academy studying medicine... and all those years of practice... all of that would go to waste.
Bulgakov: I have my father to think of, too. *Sigh* He's always wanted me to be a doctor. It's a respectable-enough job, and brings in the Mora...
Bulgakov: What should I do...?

## Talk 3

Traveler: Goodbye.
Bulgakov: Goodbye... *Sigh* What do I do about my future...?
