Layla: Hmm, well, she's always been in a fragile state. If she knew that there was another "Layla" who's always taking care of everything...
Layla: She might feel like everything she's ever worked for is pointless, and that could lead to some demoralizing thoughts.
Layla: She's a resilient girl, but no matter how resilient she may be, no one can hold up to criticism and torment from themselves.

Layla: She's getting better, though, isn't she? It's apparent how hard she's working now.
Layla: She's my source of knowledge. I'm merely the representation of how she would be if she were rested and healthy.

Layla: Alright, I've written down all the possible questions that I think the advisers might ask. Next, I'll put together a few words of encouragement for her.
Layla: I'm not going to sign it, obviously. Oh, and don't forget about our agreement.
