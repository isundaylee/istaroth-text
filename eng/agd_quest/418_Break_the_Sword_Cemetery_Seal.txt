# Break the Sword Cemetery Seal

According to Dr. Livingstone, there are three hilichurl tribes surrounding the Sword Cemetery in *Dadaupa Gorge*. To the north, the Meaty tribe; to the south, the Sleeper tribe; and in the valley to the east, the Eclipse tribe. The key to breaking the seals on the Sword Cemetery may lie within their encampments...
