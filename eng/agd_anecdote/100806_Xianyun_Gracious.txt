# Xianyun: Gracious

A crowd of people gathers to express their gratitude outside Xianyun's residence...

Xianyun is greeted by a crowd of grateful townsfolk outside her residence. It turns out that Shenhe's deeds in defeating a villainous street gang have benefited a great many people. Setting a good example by graciously accepting their gratitude, Xianyun appears to be thoroughly enjoying herself, while in a quiet corner, Shenhe has her own thanks to give...
