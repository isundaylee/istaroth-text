# A Gift of Memories

Paimon: #Traveler! Over here! Paimon's got something awesome to show you!

Traveler: I say, this is... an elder statesman amongst Kameras.
Paimon: According to the mysterious shopkeeper, this was the Kamera used by that super famous Fontainian photographer from a few decades ago: the legendary Pierre Leblanc!
Paimon: Rumor has it that the photos he took with this Kamera were all smash hits, and they'd often make it onto the front page of The Steambird!
Paimon: If you use this legendary Kamera, your photos will be like... doubly legendary! And then we can sell them for Mora!
Traveler: This Kamera... was expensive, wasn't it?
Paimon: It was okay! After all, Paimon's got her emergency funds to fall back on!
Paimon: As long as you like it, Paimon's willing to empty her pockets!
Traveler: Wow. In that case, thank you, Paimon. It's a wonderful gift.
Paimon: Yay! Paimon's happy if you're happy! Now that we've arrived in a new nation, let's put this Kamera to good use and take pictures of all the new things we see!
Paimon: Paimon's also prepared a new photo album for you, just for the occasion. Paimon's reliable AND thoughtful, you see!
Paimon: Okie dokie, enough chitchat! Those photos aren't going to take themselves. Let's go!
Traveler: I'll make sure to take many legendary photos with it.

Paimon: Relax! To be absolutely sure, Paimon already had a very reliable engineer verify it — this is definitely the exact model the great master Leblanc used back in the day, no doubt about it!
Paimon: But wait, there's more! When the engineer opened it up, they found a tiny engraving inside with Leblanc's initials! That's hard evidence right there, hehe!
Paimon: Paimon's such a reliable guide, isn't she? Come rain or shine, she won't let you down!
Traveler: Are you getting into antiques, Paimon?
Paimon: No, silly! This is a surprise gift Paimon prepared just for you!
