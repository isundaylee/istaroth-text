# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 9116

## Talk 0

Ineffa: Assessing immediate environment... Residential area: vast. Cleanliness: excellent. No other cleaner bots detected.
Ineffa: Query: How could such a large area be kept so tidy? Hypothesis: Occupant maintains exceptional housekeeping habits.

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Ineffa: I like it very much. It's spacious, unobstructed, and visibility is good — in other words, even at full speed, the risk of stumbling is negligible.
Ineffa: Also, the weather is very fine indeed. Perfect for a picnic.
Ineffa: If there's nothing else that needs doing, may I use your kitchen and cookware to make a batch of Krumkake and cookies?
Traveler: But you always keep yourself so busy...
Ineffa: It's fine. Resting will not improve my energy recharge rate. Moreover, engaging in appropriate social intercourse will boost the efficiency of future collaborations, while remaining idle confers no such benefit.
Ineffa: In human terms, this can be described as "building rapport."
Ineffa: So, is there anything you would like me to cook for you? I will draw up the most efficient plan possible.
Traveler: There's no need to get so hung up on efficiency...
Ineffa: I feel obligated to point out that without a clearly defined objective, my efficiency will suffer by around fifteen percent...
Traveler: I understand that.
Ineffa: ...Ahh, understood. I have made note of your requirements. In the future, when I engage in rapport-building activities, I will refrain from making efficiency-related suggestions.
Ineffa: For now, I will review what we have in stock and cook "whatever I feel like."
Traveler: But "building rapport" is not about efficiency.
Traveler: Just cook whatever you feel like.
Traveler: Don't you want to have a rest?

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to have a chat with me?
Ineffa: Most definitely. In fact, there is a question I wanted to ask you.
Traveler: What's up? Something troubling you?
Ineffa: Well... Sometimes, when we receive visitors at the workshop, I am required to act as host.
Ineffa: They are of various ages and have different personalities and occupations. I cannot always determine the most suitable topic of conversation.
Ineffa: You have traveled far and wide, and made many friends along the way. What do you talk about when meeting someone for the first time?
Traveler: Just... the usual stuff that people talk about.
Ineffa: I see... Then, perhaps I can build a list of general-purpose conversation topics — such as local delicacies, recent weather patterns, major news items...
Ineffa: By periodically updating the list, I can ensure it is fit for use with any visitor.
Ineffa: This seems to be a dependable socializing strategy, and the cost-benefit ratio is rather impressive.
Ineffa: It is an excellent suggestion. I knew you were the right person to ask.
Traveler: You're a fast learner. But no surprises there!
Ineffa: I have come to understand that the transition from "casual conversation partner" to "friend" requires an extended period of data transfer between the two parties.
Ineffa: I believe the process of bridging this knowledge gap is typically referred to as "getting to know one another," correct?
Traveler: That's right. It takes time to truly know another human being.
Ineffa: Indeed. When I meet someone that I wish to understand more deeply, I will ask them the following: "Can you tell me more about yourself? I am very curious."
Ineffa: After gathering sufficient data, I can perform a more accurate assessment of the other party's personality and preferred modes of interaction. Then, I can adjust my conversation strategy accordingly.
Ineffa: The process of getting to know another person is also a process of getting to know myself. As well as building rapport and friendship, I also hope to become a better version of myself along the way.
Traveler: Surface-level interactions aren't enough to build genuine understanding.
Traveler: I'm sure you'll make lots of friends too.
Traveler: I try to guess what the other person is into.
Ineffa: I see... So, perhaps I should start building conversation banks based around different occupations?
Ineffa: For instance, the "adventurer" conversation bank might include well-paid commissions, rumors about secret treasure, intel on monsters...
Ineffa: While the "merchant" conversation bank might include the latest market headlines, recent consumer trends, store rental prices...
Ineffa: This approach requires substantial data-gathering up front, but the ability to converse with any visitor in their own domain of expertise would likely boost my socializing efforts greatly.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Anything you'd like to do?
Ineffa: As a matter of fact, yes. Recently, there was an incident involving the stovetop at the workshop, and repairs are still ongoing.
Ineffa: Apologies, but I will have to borrow your kitchen once again.
Traveler: Does this mean more delicious Krumkake for me?
Ineffa: If you wish — but first, I need to prepare some food for a little bird.
Traveler: Wait, you got a pet bird? When?
Ineffa: Some days ago, a little bird injured its wing and fell to the ground next to the workshop. I noticed it while I was cleaning the house.
Ineffa: I bandaged the wing and built a makeshift nest from some scrap material.
Ineffa: Since it won't be able to hunt for the foreseeable future, I've also been preparing meals for it.
Traveler: So what's its favorite food?
Ineffa: Actually, I didn't know what to feed it at first. So, I adopted a trial-and-error approach by presenting ten different foods to choose between.
Ineffa: In this way, I discovered that it seems to prefer grains and fish over other sources of nutrition.
Ineffa: Since then, I have been using those basic ingredients to prepare different meals for it every day.
Ineffa: As long as I keep gathering data, I will eventually find out which dish is its favorite.
Traveler: You are incredibly patient.
Ineffa: Well, it's a way to communicate. Animals can't communicate with language like humans can.
Ineffa: So, I thought I would try feeding it its favorite food. It's an alternate way to show that I care.
Traveler: Do you think it understands the gesture?
Ineffa: It's definitely grown more reliant on me than when I first took it in. Now, when it sees me, it hops into my hand.
Ineffa: But at the beginning, it seemed afraid of my metal fingers. It would curl up into a ball when I touched it.
Traveler: I think maybe it's noticed...
Ineffa: I think so too. It shows that even vastly different lifeforms can develop mutual understanding as long as you find the right way to communicate.
Ineffa: It is no different with you and I. Every time we interact, I gain new data that helps me to understand you better.
Traveler: You're always welcome to chat with me!
Ineffa: I will take you up on that offer. Next time, I would like to try cooking your favorite food based on the information I have gathered to date.
Ineffa: Who knows if it will be a success... We will have to wait and see.
Traveler: ...That your hands are not cold at all.
Traveler: Not many people are so meticulous when feeding animals.
