Ruler of Death: Blessed One of Wisdom, Mahakusaladhamma, are you certain that you wish to exercise your authority to destroy Irminsul?

Ruler of Death: It represents both your roots and your finest accomplishments. You truly intend to abandon the very thing you have always fought for?

Nahida: This is my decision. Irminsul has been corrupted beyond redemption.

Ruler of Time: Irminsul is the original tree of Teyvat. It is the means by which the Heavenly Principles maintains fate itself. The Seven Archons should honor and revere it, for their authority was bestowed upon them by the Heavenly Principles.

Nahida: It is precisely because I honor and revere it that I fight for the humanity it exists to protect.

Nahida: Wisdom is knowing how to weigh the options and make the right decision. If you truly care about the fortunes of this world, then it is me you should seek to preserve.

Ruler of Life: Mhmhm...

Ruler of Life: Planting a seed begins the chain of cause and effect. Since you have become a great tree yourself, why should the loss of another tree be anything to fear?

Ruler of Life: Till we meet again, Lord of Dendro.
