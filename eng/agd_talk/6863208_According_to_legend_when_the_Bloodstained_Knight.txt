Varka: To this day, their names are still recorded in the archives of the Knights of Favonius, though what happened after their departure remains unknown.
Varka: Is it because of the unique influence of the Ley Lines here, or was their longing and willpower so intense that these voices still linger...?
Varka: No matter what, the people of Mondstadt won't forget these heroes.
Varka: Though they had left the Knights, they fought bloody battles to uphold the chivalry in their hearts, until all that was left was one lone wolf...
Varka: No one can fault you for your deeds. Every action you took was truly befitting your knightly titles.
Varka: But it seems that the Bloodstained Knight could not return from the end of his path. It's an ending full of sorrow.
Varka: Don't worry — I've already found their grave. Their final wish has been entrusted to me.
Varka: Judging by your appearance, I can see you won't be returning home with me, but at least your long vigil is finally at an end.
Varka: So rest, heroic wolf... I'll carry your story back to your homeland.
