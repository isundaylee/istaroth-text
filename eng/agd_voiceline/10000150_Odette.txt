## About Odette: The Rokot
Since the implementation of Project Stuzha, the size of our audience has been dwindling. While most people are struggling to get by, corrupt leeches continue to prey on the public. I refuse to turn a blind eye. I know I can't turn the tide on my own, but I still want to do what I can.

## About Odette: Memory
The memories of my past are forever etched into my mind. Like a film, they play on repeat in the dead of night... Suffice to say, having a perfect memory isn't always a good thing.

## About Us: Trust
Relying on others for protection is like leaning on a thin layer of fresh ice. I don't pin my hopes on anyone but myself.

## About Us: Wounds
My sense of pain is quite dull. I often don't even notice I'm injured until the wound has already started to scab over. But, I prefer it that way. Whether it's dance rehearsal or Fatui special training, it's better if a trivial thing like my physical condition doesn't get in the way.

## About the Stellar Linchpin
Once, during an intense training session, I developed frostbite in my feet and legs due to the extreme cold, and almost lost consciousness entirely. When I came to, the first thing I noticed was a bright light emanating from the Stellar Linchpin in front of me. But, I've never heard of Stellar Linchpins being able to awaken people like that. So if that didn't wake me up... then maybe it was some instinctual desire to survive...?

## Something to Share
I treat every rehearsal as seriously as an actual performance.

## Interesting Things
You can bring out the sweetness of Rimecurrants by sprinkling them with a little table salt. Supposedly, the contrast helps amplify their inner sweetness. I heard that from a fan.

## About Rosalyne: The Past
I'm indebted to Rosalyne, but there was also a time when I really hated her. She was exacting in her demands and incredibly strict. Freezing from the cold, collapsing from exhaustion, getting injured, falling sick... Those were all common occurrences when I trained under her. I used to harbor this naive dream that I just needed to make it as a ballerina, earn enough to support my mother, and then, one day, I would leave her and the Fatui in the dust...

## About Rosalyne: Cor Ignis
After someone is gone, a lot of things are suddenly no longer worth saying. I could shatter this Cor Ignis in a heartbeat, yet that would make the pain and the misunderstandings it caused feel all the more ridiculous... Why is it only now, after all this time, that we finally understand each other?

## About the Tsaritsa
Her Majesty the Tsaritsa is Snezhnaya's pillar of faith. Her duties keep her quite occupied, so she rarely makes public appearances. In fact, I've only seen her on a handful of occasions, but she always strikes me as an incredibly regal and solemn figure. No matter where her gaze falls, it is as cold and unwavering as a sheet of ice, one that no amount of time could melt.

## About Lady Damselette
She walks with an almost feather-light grace that would be the envy of any dancer. According to Vodyanitsa, she also has a hauntingly beautiful singing voice. Although, I suppose what we see and envy is the persona she projects on the surface. Who knows what untold pain lies underneath...

## About Lady Arlecchino
The children of the House of the Hearth think of her as their "Father." Hopefully, that will spare them some of the bitter chill and raging storms that life brings. A father's role is to protect, after all.

## About Lady Marionette
Lady Marionette's tea parties always used to put Rosalyne in a better mood. She was just as strict, but her tone was a bit softer. It was quite a noticeable difference.

## About Vodyanitsa: Smile
The first time the Tsaritsa was coming to see one of the Korolevskiy Troupe's performances, Vodyanitsa was the one who told me. I replied with a simple nod of the head. She then proceeded to force a smile onto my face by pushing up both corners of my mouth, then made me repeat after her, "What. Great. News."
Perhaps it was practice for appearing in front of the Tsaritsa, but I still don't think Her Majesty appreciates that kind of smile.

## About Vodyanitsa: Getting Sick
Whenever Vodyanitsa gets sick, she always gets so clingy and practically latches herself to my side... She says it's because she's scared of getting a shot or having to take bitter medicine, so she needs something to hold onto, and I suppose my arm is her instrument of choice. I have a high pain tolerance, though, so there's no reason to shake her off.

## About Alyosha
He could easily make a living on hunting alone if all he cared about was himself, but he still takes on odd jobs around the theater so he can help others who are suffering... I'm not surprised he joined the Rokot.

## About Mitya
Hm... I suppose you could call him a "fan" of the theater. He comes to see me and Vodyanitsa perform often enough. But as soon as the show starts, he always buries his head in his notes, writing away about goodness knows what. It's hard to ignore, especially when the rest of the audience has their eyes glued to the stage. I wouldn't be surprised if there's more true appreciation for the arts in his scarf than his entire body.

## About Valeriy
Major Valeriy is a Fatui officer who's very strict about keeping things on schedule. I've heard he commands with a level-headed disposition that can also come off as quite terrifying... But we belong to different divisions, so we rarely have a chance to interact. Besides... I don't need anyone to remind me what I should be doing.

## About Vesna
Don't be thinking she's some dainty, harmless butterfly just because of those wings. People may flock to her, but she's the commander-in-chief of the Druzhna — one of the fiercest predators on the tundra. Keep your eyes wide open, and don't be fooled by her smile.

## More About Odette: I
You'll have to excuse me. My schedule for the day is quite packed, and I have rehearsal later, so if you want to chat, please keep it brief.

## More About Odette: II
My mother came from a family of ballet dancers, and my father served in the Fatui. They met after one of her performances and quickly fell in love. It's a sweet story that would bring a smile to anyone's face, I'm sure. When I was little, my days were spent dancing alongside my mother, clad in one of the many beautiful tutus my father would buy for me. I had no worries... except perhaps for which piece of candy to pick from the candy jar each day.

## More About Odette: III
Of course, before long, we received the tragic news that my father had died in the line of duty. My mother was devastated and ended up breaking her leg during a performance. Before this, life was sugary-sweet. But now, it felt like the candy jar was empty, and I was trapped inside.

## More About Odette: IV
After that, well... You know what happened. I've unpacked those memories so many times, they can no longer hurt me. Struggling against the hand that fate dealt me... It made me strong, and that strength is mine alone. I won't forget that.

## More About Odette: V
I wouldn't say any of this to my fans, so it's a good thing you're not one. I already have too many versions of myself to maintain, and I prefer for them not to bleed into each other... Of course, in my heart, I will forever keep dancing toward perfection.

## Odette's Hobbies
I've always loved ballet, and I do my utmost to give the best possible performance every time I'm on stage.

## Odette's Troubles
Honestly, I feel like small animals hate me. Vodyanitsa always makes me go with her to feed the stray cats around the theater, but as soon as I approach, they start sharpening their claws... Hm? That means they're excited to see me? Huh...

## Favorite Food
Right before closing, the salads at the dining hall are half-price, so I used to eat that a lot. Obviously, salads that have been left out all day don't taste as good as fresh ones. But something about the familiarity of it is quite comforting to me.

## Least Favorite Food
To be honest, I don't think there's any food I find particularly unpalatable. But, as a ballet dancer, I have to be strict about my diet, so all of my meals are carefully planned. I tend to avoid anything not included in that.
