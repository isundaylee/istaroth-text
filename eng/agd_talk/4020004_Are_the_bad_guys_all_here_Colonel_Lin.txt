# Talk Dialog

Roxane: Are the bad guys all here, Colonel Lin?
Sergeant Lin: Indeed. This is the final group of criminals to undergo interrogation. We've taken their statements. Now they're ready to join the other five groups on the ship.
Roxane: There's more of them than I expected... The worst lowlifes that Fontaine has to offer. We'll make sure they learn their lesson. By the time we're through with them, they won't dare do anything to disgrace the nation ever again.
Roxane: We appreciate your help, Colonel Lin!
Roxane: If you ever find yourself in Fontaine, I'll be sure to treat you to lunch at the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol's Melusine cafeteria. The buttered Tidalga is to die for!
Sergeant Lin: My sincerest thanks for the offer. Though my duties are set to keep me in Chenyu Vale for a good ten years, I hope that one day I can take you up on it.
Roxane: Ten years? That's hardly any time at all...
Sergeant Lin: Also, it really would be remiss of me not to mention that I am, in fact, a sergeant. Our system of ranks and responsibilities in the Millelith is quite different from those of the Maison Gardiennage and the Fontainian Navy of old.
Roxane: Don't worry, I quite understand. You Millelith are a modest bunch with a simple set of ranks. Nothing too fancy on the taxpayers' dime...
Roxane: But in my eyes, your responsibilities are worthy of the rank of colonel, and so that's what I plan to call you. Unless you deem it inappropriate, in which case I can call you sergeant after all.
Roxane: I hope one day we can establish a joint force — after all, it's been a pleasure working with you. We made light work of this little issue, I can only imagine what it would be like for our troops to unite against a horde of monsters!
Sergeant Lin: I'm sure you'd have achieved the same results without us, given a bit more time to get to grips with the situation.
