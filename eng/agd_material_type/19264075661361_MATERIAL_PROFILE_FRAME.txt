# Materials: Profile Frame

## Faint Glow

When stars first ascend from a corner of the sky, their colors are as subtle as a whisper of darkness.

## First Light

The first light is always faint, but the seed of its glow is already sown within, awaiting only the moment to flourish.

## Glimmering Glory

Rise in the dying night where no light remains, ignite hope in those who chase the dawn, and blaze through the darkness that the sun cannot reach.

## Brimming Spiritflow

It is light shining from above, and a gentle glow flowing from the heart. Lost children chase dreams of delicate moonlight. Amid the endless cycle of day and night, this place remains the only Elysium left in time.

## Keen Edge

Even after hundreds of hard-fought battles, it remains as keen as a freshly forged blade, its edge undiminished.

## Siege Breach

Like a heavy bullet, it punches through defenses and shatters the enemy's lines.

## Tempered Iron

Make adversity your crucible, forging the will to crush mighty foes and honing battle skills that none can withstand.

## First Snow

The endless snow falling with the light of dawn is nothing but drifting debris as the firmament breaks apart.

## Crystalline Frost

The north wind carries the breath of the first freeze, the eternal singing of the King of Winter Icelea's maidens. Let the world be held still in this moment forever! Only that wish was ever granted.

## Snowbound

Cold, silent streets and temples, layered corridors and palace halls, lie buried beneath a seal of ice that never melts. Under the reign of the King of Winter Icelea, the ever-frozen land quietly welcomes its end without end.

## Everwinter

The once grand and solemn ancient capital became a playground for the Jack Frost. All things lay sealed beneath the vast snow, as if for eternity. Only the sly Jack Frost knew that, when winter reaches its end, spring will return to the earth once more.
