# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 7007

## Talk 0

Urwa: Hehe, the weather today is nice and warm. Perfect for sorting out my goods, and perfect for telling stories to the young 'uns.

## Talk 1

Traveler: Hello. And you are...
Urwa: Me? Oh, I'm Urwa, and I am but a humble housekeeper. When Lord Sangemah Bay is not around, I handle matters on her behalf.
Urwa: The customs here are very different from those in the desert, but wherever you go, the kindness in human hearts remains the same.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Young 'uns?
Urwa: Hehe, I simply mean the children around these parts. Telling stories to them, watching their faces light up... it gives life to these old bones of mine.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Farewell.
Urwa: Farewell, young one. Take care.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Matters? As in business matters?
Urwa: That's right. Well, it's mostly about the flow and exchange of goods. Bringing Sumeru's specialties here, and sending Nod-Krai's wares back to Sumeru... Mutual benefit is always a welcome thing.
Urwa: As long as we can make Mora for Lord Sangemah Bay, and keep the back and forth journeys safe, I'm satisfied. Phew... "safe" is quite rare in these parts, after all.

## Talk 5

Traveler: You don't strike me as a desert-dweller...
Urwa: Is that so? Hehe, perhaps that side of me has been ground away by the sands of time, and I've been influenced by some other land in its stead.
Urwa: To an old fogey like me, doing what I can here and watching people live safely and peacefully... That's my greatest comfort.

## Talk 6

Traveler: About the stories you spoke of...
Urwa: These are old stories that the desert dwellers are familiar with. Like the adventures of Muzaffar Dey, and the treasure of the evil queen Makhaira. They're very popular with the children.
Urwa: Of course, there are also some stories about wisdom... and how to make the right choices.
Urwa: Stories always have heroes and villains. But this old man hopes they'll learn — even if they've stumbled onto the wrong path, they needn't keep walking it forever.
Urwa: The desert is a harsh and unforgiving place. Those who have survived have invariably learned some lessons, some of which were learned while young, albeit at a price...
Urwa: ...Aha, sorry. I'm afraid I've gotten carried away. You know how it is, us old dogs are always full of tales to tell...
