# Talk Group: Menshikov (NpcGroup 7431)

## Talk 0

Surikov: Lemme tell ya... Bachh in the day, I cooud drink shrree bottls o' fire-wahrr, an' bee jussh fine... Jushh fine! That old coot from Morpeshok... What was hissh name again?
Menshikov: Morepesok? Ah, you must mean Old Lugovskoy... Also, please, cut the drinking and get some water in your system.
Surikov: Aye, that ol' geezher, rayshed a bear, 'ee did... Shhaw my talents, said I wuzz... *burp* born to hunt!
