# Talk Group: Olenina (NpcGroup 7774)

## Talk 0

Olenina: Did you hear that? Miss Vodyanitsa's third aria just now was simply magnificent!

## Talk 1

Traveler: You listen to opera too? At your age?
Olenina: Don't get the wrong idea. I'm not the kind of fan who rushes to the theater just because a star is performing. I came to appreciate Snezhnaya's classical opera.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Yes, absolutely
Olenina: The orchestra after that, though... They weren't quite there. They came in half a beat late! That's practically a crime against art!

## Talk 3

Traveler: Really?
Olenina: *sigh* You wouldn't get it. Classical art is lost on most people these days. Places like this are the only reason they can still feel even a little of it.
Olenina: My family says the same sort of thing, always telling me to stop wasting my time and focus on "real" studies. But what do they know? Engineering is just what they put in textbooks. Literature and music are what a free spirit should be learning!
