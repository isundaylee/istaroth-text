# Talk Dialog

Zhongli: Hmm. I wonder how this tea will taste.
Zhongli: Ah, excellent timing. Sit.
Zhongli: I asked someone to buy a few bags of good tea leaves from Fontaine. Quite the pricey purchase, if I might say so.
Zhongli: I've long heard of the differences in water and soil makeup between Fontaine and Liyue, so I've been curious as to the flavors tea leaves from the former will produce.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Why don't you also get some water from there?

Option 2:

> Traveler: That might be a more interesting approach.

Zhongli: Hmm... You do have a point. Though as the ancients say, "living waters silt once drawn."
Zhongli: Were we to fetch water from Fontaine, the turbulent, tiring journey may rob the liquid of its flavor, rendering the resultant tea lesser as a result.
Traveler: Is there really that much to getting water?
Zhongli: To brew a good cup of tea is no less demanding a task than organizing an army for battle, along with all the elements that entails.
Zhongli: Buying good tea leaves is akin to elevating a wise and capable general to command.
Zhongli: Yet if the water and tea set are lacking, and if perhaps even the climate and weather not suitable...
Zhongli: Then no matter how outstanding the general, if burdened with incompetent officers and feckless soldiery, the battle will surely be lost.
Zhongli: As such, rather than chasing distant solutions, it would be better to simply brew this tea with some spring water from Liyue.
Zhongli: Perhaps the blend of two natures might produce a unique flavor all its own.
Zhongli: Ah. It should be about ready. Try some, if you would.
