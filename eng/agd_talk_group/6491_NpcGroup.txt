# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 6491

## Talk 0

"Teteltetl": An easy-breezy Natlan in an easy-breezy summer! Welcome to Easybreeze Market, friends!

## Talk 1

Traveler: And you are...?
"Teteltetl": I am your loyal friend, "Teteltetl"! "Teteltetl" was once the ceremonial greeter of old Tenochtzitoc. Now I'm the greeter of this modern-day resort!
Traveler: Your clothes look very unique.
"Teteltetl": Ohoho! This is a Nanas outfit! It was once a type of prickly pear native to the region... Unfortunately, no one's seen one in five hundred years.
"Teteltetl": If you keep walking, you'll eventually run into someone dressed as a Nochtli. That's another type of prickly pear.
"Teteltetl": If my memory serves me correctly, you're likely to find more of them in Villa Guiztli.

## Talk 2

Traveler: What can we do in the Easybreeze Market?
"Teteltetl": Rest, load up on wonderful local wares, enjoy great food, and admire the colorful streets... If you want to kick back even more, you can take it all in from the comfort of a couch.
"Teteltetl": Or, you could spend the whole day chatting with "Teteltetl"!
Traveler: Thanks. Maybe next time.
"Teteltetl": I ohoho-hope you enjoy your refreshing summer holiday!

## Talk 3

Traveler: Where else do you think we should visit?
"Teteltetl": If you head deeper, you'll find Wavey Bay, where warm waves caress golden sands, while to the east, you'll see Guiztli Ridge and Villa Guiztli — both of which are inhabited by some very special individuals.
"Teteltetl": To the south of Guiztli Ridge, children have painted the splendor of the world into Colorfall Cliffs.
"Teteltetl": Finally, to the far north, legend holds that a boundless trove of hilichurl treasures is buried in Paititi.
"Teteltetl": This whole place is full of mysteries, just waiting for you to uncover!
