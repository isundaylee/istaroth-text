# Test of Courage

The young thaumaturge Chongyun has obtained a great amount of (rather unreliable-sounding) information concerning evil spirits, but you decide not to expose this fact out of courtesy. The way Chongyun sees it, the most likely spot for evil spirits to show up is Wuwang Hill, where a Test of Courage is currently taking place. The test requires two people to go in together, so you accept his invitation and accompany him there...
