## Talk 2

Unknown Role (None): Are you someone who fell from... that, uh, from beyond the skies?

Unknown Role (None): Ah, how very intricate. Not only are the landforms accurately drawn, but even the paths of the mountains and rivers can be seen.

Unknown Role (None): Well, then. How large is this nation you call "Teyvat"? How does it measure up to Byakuyakoku?

## Talk 3

Unknown Role (None): *sigh* If only I had more time. I would become the greatest physician in the world...

Unknown Role (None): That way, even with this body of mine, I would surely be able to walk out of my room in good health and play to my heart's content.

## Talk 4

Unknown Role (None): Uh, Paimon can't just stop moving...

Unknown Role (None): Imagine asking Paimon not to move...

Unknown Role (None): Would you fall down, Paimon?

Unknown Role (None): They're not entirely aesthetically pleasing, but they are somewhat interesting.

Unknown Role (None): If you don't like 'em, why'd you even look?

Unknown Role (None): Seriously? Asking those lady retainers to model for me was hardly this difficult.

Unknown Role (None): That said, I've gotten quite sick of looking at the way our people dress.

Unknown Role (None): As for what you're wearing, now that's new.

Unknown Role (None): Hmm, I know. So that's how they put those clothes together... Wow, you outsiders really are shameless.

Unknown Role (None): I see, I see... So if the clothes are made like that, then the body shape should be like... this!

Unknown Role (None): What are you doing?

Unknown Role (None): Making a statue. I mix clay and water, and then I knead them into shape with both hands. It helps to calm me — it's one of the only things that does.

Unknown Role (None): And you know what definitely doesn't calm me? Sitting in regular meetings, reading aloud based on material pre-written by those adults.

Unknown Role (None): Well, whatever. I'm done with your forms as well.

Unknown Role (None): Now stop bothering me. Go do your own stuff.

## Talk 5

Unknown Role (None): I am Ion, after all. I never miss a reading.

Unknown Role (None): Really?

Unknown Role (None): You sound like a hustler.

Unknown Role (None): That said... Few of Byakuyakoku's people have happy fates awaiting them.

Unknown Role (None): So I often cheat them, telling them that their luck will turn.

Unknown Role (None): In the end, they said that I couldn't tell fortunes at all. Ah, how saddening.

Unknown Role (None): Is that how it is?

Unknown Role (None): Well, little one, I'll prove it to you, right now!

Unknown Role (None): O—Oh? Sure! Try Paimon on for size!

Unknown Role (None): Hmm, alright... Let me have a careful go at this... Huh. Why's there so much interference?

Unknown Role (None): Eh!?

Unknown Role (None): Wh—What's wrong?

Unknown Role (None): I... can't read your fortune.

Unknown Role (None): How could that be? I don't get it exactly right most of the time, but I usually don't fail to get a reading at all...

Unknown Role (None): Well, guess you ain't that good, huh?

Unknown Role (None): It does seem so. It seems that I must continue my studies in earnest.

## Talk 6

Unknown Role (None): Let's have some fruit juice together instead! Haha!
