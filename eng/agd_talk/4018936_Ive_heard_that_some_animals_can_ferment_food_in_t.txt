# Talk Dialog

Captain Ferment O'Juice: I've heard that some animals can ferment food in their stomachs to prepare it for eating. If I do the fermenting for them, then it should save them some effort. Genius idea, huh?
Traveler: You'd better have a proper theoretical basis for this...
Captain Ferment O'Juice: Haha, of course I don't! But a chef's intuition can't possibly be wrong — not when it comes to preparing food.
Captain Ferment O'Juice: And sure, I've been getting a lot of complaints because the flavor isn't quite right. But if you let fear of criticism stop you from trying new things, then you'll never make any progress.
Captain Ferment O'Juice: If I get it wrong this time, you can call me every name under the sun. I won't shy away from criticism!
Traveler: Just try not to produce any hazardous chemicals...
Captain Ferment O'Juice: Of course! I conduct all my experiments in a sealed environment. If they let off any noxious gases, I'll be the first to feel the effects...
Captain Ferment O'Juice: Once I come to, I'll make sure to destroy the lot. That way, there'll be no risk of wider contamination!
Traveler: You might need to consider the cost...
Captain Ferment O'Juice: Don't worry about that — when it comes to experiments like this, I pay out of my own pocket!
