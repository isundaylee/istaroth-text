# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 9129

## Talk 0

Lohen: Whoa, I never expected you to have your own secret base.

Lohen: It seems sharing secrets with friends can often help forge stronger bonds...

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?

Lohen: To tell you the truth, I'm finding it hard to adapt. When I take my afternoon naps, I'm used to hearing Varka harping on about something, or Jean sighing in response...

Lohen: The thud of Eula's boots stomping on the ground, or Mika's nervous footsteps...

Traveler: You know those sounds off by heart...?

Lohen: Of course. Each one is an alarm that calls me to work... Even if I really want to slack off, when the Acting Grand Master or the Grand Master himself shows up, I have to be up and at the door.

Lohen: Especially Varka, 'cause he's got his own key. Couldn't even lock him out if I wanted to.

Traveler: What about the other knights?

Lohen: Well... Eula always knocks first, then waits for an answer. If she doesn't get one, she usually just leaves.

Traveler: So couldn't you pretend to be out...?

Lohen: Hah! Nah, she's too smart for that sorta thing. She only leaves because it'd be rude to keep knocking.

Lohen: Besides, unless it's an emergency, someone as tuned in as her isn't gonna just stand there until you have no choice but to open the door.

Traveler: But what if it genuinely is an emergency?

Lohen: Then she wouldn't bother knocking. She'd walk right on in, whether or not the door was unlocked.

Lohen: And then there's Amber. Now she's a tricky customer — if the door doesn't open, she'll pop up by the window instead.

Lohen: ...Which brings us to Mika. I couldn't possibly mistake him.

Lohen: Whenever he's nervous, his footsteps get increasingly flustered. You can even hear them from the floor above.

Lohen: When he gets to the door, he usually marches on the spot for a while to calm his breathing. And when he finally gets it under control, he'll push the door open and enter.

Lohen: If you open the door just as he's taking his first deep breath, he'll break out in a fit of coughing as the shock disrupts his breathing.

Lohen: For the record, I should clarify that I've never done that myself — but I have seen Kaeya give him quite a scare.

Lohen: It's hard to say if Kaeya actually did it on purpose or not. I have a sneaking suspicion he was trying to interrupt a meeting that was going on at the time...

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to chat?

Lohen: Sure. I was just thinking about an interesting thing that happened a while ago. Why don't I tell you about it?

Lohen: I heard that Fontaine's Special Security and Surveillance Patrol was a hotbed for skilled sharpshooters, so I wondered if we might be able to organize a friendly exchange.

Lohen: After obtaining official consent from the Acting Grand Master — and the Grand Master, too — I sent a letter to the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol in the name of the Knights of Favonius...

Traveler: You requested approval from both of them?

Lohen: Not exactly. Normally, once Acting Grand Master Jean has approved an order, the Grand Master signs any necessary paperwork without reading it first.

Traveler: Why am I not surprised...

Lohen: Anyway, I took a few of my best knights from the Fifth Company to Fontaine for a friendly contest with the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol.

Lohen: I'd be the first to admit it — their accuracy with ranged weaponry is better than ours.

Lohen: But when it comes to strength and endurance, we have the upper hand.

Traveler: Well, you do wear a lot of armor...

Traveler: They only train in regular uniforms.

Lohen: Hahaha, guess that must be it. While we were there, we held a cross-country race with heavy packs, and I noticed a few of their members had to stop for a rest break partway through.

Lohen: But not their captain, Chevreuse. She seemed more than up to the task.

Traveler: She wasn't made captain for nothing...

Traveler: She knows her team's strengths and weaknesses like the back of her hand...

Lohen: Too right! She knew exactly what training to assign them after the contest was over.

Lohen: She even made sure to ask how heavy our armor is, and how we usually train...

Lohen: Then she said she was gonna start training with heavy sandbags to replicate the effect.

Traveler: I'm sure her team thanked you for that...

Lohen: Ahh, they'll be fine. Apparently, they're issued with muskets that are heavier than the standard model for use in their regular training drills.

Lohen: They're made to maintain their standard firing stance while carrying these heavier guns. The idea is to train their stability and endurance when shooting...

Traveler: So now you're planning to...

Lohen: Not just planning — I've already asked Sucrose to produce some weighted model weapons.

Lohen: In a few days, the best marksmen in the Fifth Company will be able to experience a little Fontaine-style training.

Traveler: Will you be training with them?

Lohen: Me? Not much point — I use a polearm.

Lohen: Oh, right. Once they're in the firing position, I plan to put a glass of Dandelion Wine on each knight's weapon.

Lohen: That way, if they can't hold their pose, they'll spill their wine — and then they'll have nothing left to drink after training...

## Talk 3

Traveler: Is there anything you'd like to do?

Lohen: If it's not too much trouble, could you help me find a few targets? Anything as tall as a person will do. I'd like to practice my fighting.

Lohen: This is a nice place to rest, but I'm not used to sitting around twiddling my thumbs. If I let myself do that, my spearwork will start getting rusty.

Lohen: Oh, and if you happen to know any spearmasters, I'd love to engage in some friendly sparring.

Lohen: And when I'm sparring, I promise I'll only use my spear — nothing else. My fighting style is as honorable as it gets.

Traveler: "When you're sparring"...

Traveler: And what about in battle...?

Lohen: Well, real combat's a different kettle of fish. Naturally, I use whatever I can get my hands on.

Lohen: Miniature firearms, poisoned crossbow bolts, daggers in boots, knives up sleeves, poisonous powders, alchemical solvents...

Lohen: And if all else fails, there's always a rock lying around to smash against your enemy's head. If you're really lucky, you might even find a broken bone — the splintered end can be quite sharp.

Lohen: If you're unlucky and there's nothing within reach, you can use your teeth, head, elbows... No matter what, you can always find a way to overcome your foe.

Traveler: Sounds like you've made it through some pretty tough scrapes...

Traveler: That must take its toll on a person.

Lohen: Hah, I don't feel any worse for wear. For me, coming up with ways to eliminate my enemies is a form of entertainment. I find it does wonders for both body and mind.

Lohen: But to reiterate what I said just now, when I'm sparring, my fighting style is entirely above board.

Lohen: And if my opponent happens to slip, cough, or sneeze... Well, that means the weather was too bad for a friendly spar to begin with.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Good morning, Lohen.

Lohen: Good morning. I must admit, I'm having a hard time getting used to how quiet it is around here.

Lohen: When I opened my eyes this morning to an unfamiliar ceiling, my first reaction was to reach for my knife.

Lohen: Old habits die hard, I guess. It's probably a holdover from being away on missions so much. I'm afraid you'll need to give me some time to adjust.

## Talk 5

Lohen: I'll keep watch here till you're asleep, then I'll hit the hay.
