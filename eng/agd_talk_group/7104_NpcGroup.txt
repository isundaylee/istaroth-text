# Talk Group: Rebane (NpcGroup 7104)

## Talk 0

Krodo: ...Miss Rebane, I'm afraid the price of these goods is still a little high.
Rebane: Mr. Krodo, this is the fairest price you'll find in all of Nasha Town! I do hope you're not mistaking me for one of those unscrupulous crooks?
Krodo: My apologies, it's not that I don't trust you, Miss Rebane, it's simply that it always pays to be careful when it comes to prices.
Krodo: After all, there are those who see us as easy targets. A few months ago, a lady named Hidimbi scammed Ehrnrooth for hundreds of thousands of Mora — she was from Nasha Town, too.
Krodo: The flesh and blood of the Lord of Stone is afforded no great value by my priestly sisters, but still... I can't accept this price — not unless you can offer me a more practical discount.
Rebane: Alright, fine, seeing as how you're a regular customer... But next time, there'll be no more such discounts. Any cheaper and I'll be losing money!
