# Talk Group: Zheleznov (NpcGroup 7593)

## Talk 0

Zheleznov: Dear friend, remember to nourish your soul at every moment. Ah, and if necessary, you may also fill your pack.

## Talk 1

Traveler: What do you sell here?
Zheleznov: Please, look this way. My goods are yours to choose from.

## Talk 2

Traveler: How does one nourish the soul...?
Zheleznov: There are many ways. Reading, for instance, or listening to music... Or simply contemplating in silence, holding a calm conversation with oneself...
Zheleznov: But I should mention that, in my view, "squandering money" and "chasing after wealth" are most certainly not among them.
Zheleznov: Excessive luxury will not bring satisfaction to the soul. On the contrary, once the indulgence has passed, our souls are instead filled with emptiness.
Zheleznov: As for chasing after wealth... Hahahahaha. A purse that bulges too much can sometimes crowd out the soul, leaving no room for one's true self.
Zheleznov: Take our dear Knyazev, for example. His life was once completely consumed by both of these things. And now... heh...

## Talk 3

Traveler: Goodbye.
Zheleznov: Farewell. I wish you a pleasant day.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Wait, you know him?
Zheleznov: I met him a few times at banquets long ago. Back then, he was full of drive, proud and confident, as though he were about to ascend to the throne of the world.
Zheleznov: And now, he is disheartened and dejected, as though he has been cast aside like an old shoe...
Zheleznov: But who can say whether a falcon that has fallen to the depths might not catch the wind once more and soar back into the sky?
Zheleznov: Knyazev's father entrusted him to my care, and instructed me that when his son finds his courage again, I'm to become his teacher and show him how to contend with the cunning serpents in the world of business.
Zheleznov: But, my friend... my young Knyazev. I do not wish to see your empty heart wholly occupied by petty calculations of profit and loss. Before that happens, it should have room for far more beautiful things.

## Talk 5

Traveler: Well... what are you planning to teach him?
Zheleznov: For now, I don't intend to teach him anything. In a few days, I plan to give him several books. Not the grand treatises on commerce he most wishes to read and imitate, of course, but a few novels.
Zheleznov: In those novels, there are those who willingly set foot upon a misguided path, those who cross blades with their own brothers, and those who pity all mankind, only to discover in the end that there is no one who can save them...
Zheleznov: Dear Knyazev, young Knyazev. Look upon them, and then go in search of yourself.
Zheleznov: ...Believe me. The most important thing in one's life is to find their true self.
