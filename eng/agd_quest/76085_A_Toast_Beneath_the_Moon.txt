## Talk 3

The Damselette: Hm, I've smelled something like this before.
Paimon: Oh, really? Where?
The Damselette: In Sandrone's room. She had a drink that smelled like this. The aroma caught my interest, and when she noticed... she just held on to the drink for ages without finishing it.
The Damselette: It was like... she was waiting for me to ask about it.
Traveler: So... did you?
The Damselette: Yes.
Paimon: What did you ask?
The Damselette: Shouldn't you be drinking engine oil instead?
Traveler: And... how did she respond?
The Damselette: Um...
The Damselette: She said something quite unpleasant. It doesn't bear repeating.
The Damselette: ...
Paimon: You lost interest after a single sip!?

## Talk 4

The Damselette: *glug*... *glug*...
Paimon: Wow, that was quick! Looks like she loves it!
The Damselette: Hm... completely flavorless.
Paimon: F—f—flavorless!?
The Damselette: Yes.
Traveler: Maybe you drank it too fast...
The Damselette: Oh... Thanks, but no need.
The Damselette: There was a time when I failed to understand why people felt such joy at harvest time. Then, one day, I reached out to touch a piece of ripe fruit hanging from a branch...
The Damselette: I sensed vitality in the very texture of its skin — the organic embodiment of sunlight, water, earth, and air.
The Damselette: With each bite, I felt a refreshing sweetness surge over me like waves of cool white foam.
The Damselette: And that's how I came to understand the irresistible power of aroma.
Paimon: Oh, that's interesting!
The Damselette: Then, we complete nature's cycle by taking that life force for ourselves.
Paimon: Uhh, that suddenly turned kinda dark...
The Damselette: That is why I prefer fresh fruit. It allows me to see what I took from this world, exactly as it was before I took it.
The Damselette: And reminds me of what I owe this world in return...
Traveler: Why don't I make you another one?

## Talk 7

The Damselette: Hmm...
Paimon: So, what do you think?
The Damselette: It reminds me of a time long, long ago, when I was living with the Frostmoon Scions...
The Damselette: The people used to make a kind of tea brewed with fresh mint leaves, spices, and dried berries.
The Damselette: Whenever I passed by their homes, there was always a subtle herbal aroma wafting from the windows...
Paimon: Did you ever get to try it?
The Damselette: I wanted to, but never got the chance.
Paimon: Oh, why not...?
The Damselette: When a "Goddess" arrives at your door and asks, "May I try your tea?"... No one dares to offer up the "rustic tea" thrown together for their own consumption...
Paimon: Ah... Yeah.
The Damselette: What I received in the end was more like an "offering" for the divine, complete with ornate packaging and all.
The Damselette: Not that it tasted bad, per se... But, it was missing that sense of... "home."
Traveler: Well, now we can fix that!
The Damselette: Mmm...
The Damselette: The flavor is delightful.
The Damselette: ...Though I must say, the idea of adding milk seems quite unorthodox to me.
Paimon: Ah, well this is a Mondstadt recipe. People love adding milk, cream, and sugar to their tea over there.
The Damselette: It's novel... but in a good way.
Paimon: Phew, glad you like it!
The Damselette: Mm... Thank you.
The Damselette: It seems moonlight is not the only medium through which one can glimpse the past. Even something as simple as the aroma of a drink can bring back long-forgotten memories...
