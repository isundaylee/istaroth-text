# Talk Group: Zarechnaya (NpcGroup 7520)

## Talk 0

Zarechnaya: Strange place. The paperwork is all in order, but I can't shake the feeling that something's off... Ah, well.

## Talk 1

Traveler: Who are you?
Zarechnaya: I'm the Chairperson of the Royal Energy Commission of Snezhnaya... Well, the Acting Chair, anyway. I was temporarily assigned here by the Zapolyarny Palace.
Zarechnaya: It's said that the previous Chairperson passed away from a sudden illness not long ago. Since the Energy Commission is vital to people's livelihoods across Snezhnaya, the position couldn't be left vacant, so they sent me.
Zarechnaya: Hopefully, they'll find a permanent Chairperson soon. Then I can report back to the Palace and be on my way.

## Talk 2

Traveler: What's bothering you?
Zarechnaya: ...Perhaps it's just my imagination. The former Chairperson passed away, I was parachuted into the position, and all the handover documents seemed perfectly in order.
Zarechnaya: And yet, I can't help feeling that certain things within the Commission have been very carefully... hidden. A secret second ledger, evidence of embezzlement, that sort of thing.
Zarechnaya: Haha, but that's probably just me letting my imagination run wild. Even if such things did exist, they wouldn't really concern me. What's important is that the Commission keeps functioning.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Goodbye.
Zarechnaya: ...Goodbye! Now then, where did today's paperwork get to...?
