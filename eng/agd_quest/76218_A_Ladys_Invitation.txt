## Talk 4

(Go to the agreed-upon location to take part in Sandrone's tea party)

Marionette: Oh? You're here.
Paimon: Hiya, Sandrone!

Option 1:

> Traveler: Thanks for the invitation.

Option 2:

> Traveler: Hope we didn't keep you waiting.

Paimon: Huh? Didn't your letter say we were having a tea party? How come there's nothing here? Don't tell Paimon this is a snack-free get-together!?
Marionette: Hah, I bet you would love that, Paimon? You really think I'd forget the food?
Paimon: Whoa! Did... Did that table just appear out of thin air!?
Marionette: Hehe, impressive, right? This is my newly developed portable tea table.
Marionette: It's easy to carry and store, and even equipped with a hover device, just like Fagio.
Marionette: What's even more impressive is... Oh, uhm, never mind, I don't want to show off and make this sound like a sales pitch. You can check it out for yourselves.
Marionette: Here, I've prepared some tea and snacks.
Traveler: I'll help myself then.
Paimon: Mmm...! These are so delicious! You really are a great cook!
Marionette: Naturally.
Paimon: But what inspired you to make something like this?
Marionette: I wanted to be able to enjoy pleasant tea parties with friends wherever they are, of course.
Marionette: I've said this before. Holding on to what you already have isn't the only way to preserve what's precious.
Marionette: Lately, I've found myself wanting to go on walks and visit my friends more often.
Marionette: But inviting myself to others' homes and expecting them to prepare tea and treats, then letting them clear the table afterward... It's hardly proper etiquette for a lady.
Paimon: Hehe, Paimon doesn't think your friends would mind.
Marionette: I mind. Besides, a simple invention like this is no trouble at all for me.
Marionette: I can see you like it. Don't worry, I can make one for you, too.
Paimon: Huh!? Are you sure that's alright?
Marionette: It's perfectly fine. Like I told you, I can whip up something like this in no time.
Traveler: In that case, thank you very much...
Marionette: Don't mention it. You helped me out a lot during the Fontinalia Festival. Consider this as thanks.
