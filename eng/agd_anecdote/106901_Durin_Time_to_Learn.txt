# Durin: Time to Learn

In Nod-Krai, you run into Durin, who is learning alchemy from Albedo. Hopefully, it'll all go well...

## Talk 0

Albedo: ...And that's the alchemical transmutation process. What do you think? Would you like to try it yourself?
Durin: I... still can't remember the formula from just now. If I damage the crafting bench...
Albedo: For small-scale work like this, failure will only result in the loss of materials.
Albedo: Besides, losing materials is an unavoidable part of learning alchemy.
Traveler: Teaching him, are we?
Albedo: Yes. After all, "alchemy" counts as "common sense" in this world.
Albedo: Durin happens to be interested in this, too. Although the... environment here is somewhat rudimentary, it's good enough to teach some basic formulas.
Durin: Hmm, I wrote down all the formulas Albedo taught me. There are so many numbers and symbols, it feels like I'm trudging through mud...
Albedo: When it comes to learning, how fast you go isn't what matters. What matters most is laying a solid foundation every step of the way.
Albedo: ...It looks like we've used up most of our materials. For now, let's head back and retrieve some more.
