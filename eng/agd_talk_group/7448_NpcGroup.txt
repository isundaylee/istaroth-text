# Talk Group: Sylvan (NpcGroup 7448)

## Talk 0

Clement: This dratted weather... How nice would it be to have a shot or two to warm ourselves right up now... Excuse me, miss, what I mean to say is, we were originally thinking of viewing the most renowned artworks here in this city...
Rada: Oh, do you wish to visit the most famous art gallery in our city?
Rada: I can take the two of you there! And I also know where the opera house and bookstore are... In other words, you can talk to me about anything about art.
Sylvan: No, no, ma'am, there's some misunderstanding here. We're just looking for a cozy little tavern right now.
Clement: "A cozy little tavern"... That reminds me of the pictorial art of Fontaine. Have you ever seen the painting titled "A Bar at The Rusty Rudder"?
Rada: No. But opera can be considered a form of pictorial art too, for the bar scenes depicted by the singers are equally vivid... I can more or less imagine what the work you're referring to looks like.
Clement: Alright then. So, we're looking for... the sort of tavern depicted in this artwork... or like you said, in the operas. But the drink doesn't have to be that good, you hear? Atmosphere, ambiance. What you want is ambiance.
Rada: I know a small, hole-in-the-wall place, it's called Kvasnik. The drinks there are cheap... And the owner's a pretty interesting fellow, too. I'm guessing you'd like the ambiance of any conversation you strike up with him.
Clement: But allow me to say upfront that I'm not talking about just any old ambiance, but rather, I'm looking for a certain aesthetic ambiance. The kind—— ACHOO!
Sylvan: You must have caught a chill there, my dear uncle! You must put on some warmer clothes now, which I have thankfully prepared for you beforehand.
Sylvan: And please, stop going on and on about "art," "aesthetics," "the figurative," whatever! Nobody knows what you're talking about! And let's not keep this officer from her duties any longer.
Rada: ...I beg to differ. You're not keeping me from my duties at all, mister. This is also part of my work.
Clement: You hear that, lad? ...ACHOO!
Clement: So, dear miss, you do seem to be a lady of culture and breeding. What is your name? And which department do you work for? I must write a letter to commend you to the proper authorities... ACHOO!!!
Sylvan: That's enough, uncle! Aren't you cold??? We'd better find ourselves somewhere warm soon. All that sneezing isn't befitting of a gentleman either.
Sylvan: ...Sorry to trouble you again, ma'am, but I will be grateful if you could point us the way to the tavern... Any tavern would do at this point. You don't have to bring us there.
