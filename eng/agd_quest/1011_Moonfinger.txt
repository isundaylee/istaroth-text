Unknown Role (None): Welcome to the Jade Mystery, my good friends! Would you like to try your luck betting on jade? This could be your lucky day.

Unknown Role (None): It's cheap and it's fun, and who knows, you just might strike it rich!

Unknown Role (None): Betting? No, no, we're here for... um, what was it again?

Unknown Role (None): Noctilucous Jade, of radiant grade at the very least.

Unknown Role (None): Radiant grade Noctilucous Jade? I see, you're not a tourist. My apologies. I have some here for your perusal...

Unknown Role (None): (Fade to black. Shitou fetches goods.)

Unknown Role (None): What do you think? The Jade Mystery is an old name in the jade business. Just look at that wonderful quality! Rex Lapis doesn't often bless us with such finery. Go on, pick whichever one you like!

Unknown Role (None): These three pieces really do look pretty, not like the ones you usually dig up...

Unknown Role (None): But how do we pick? Should we just grab one and go?

Unknown Role (None): A man who makes his living off betting on stones can't be trusted so lightly.

Unknown Role (None): Very good. It seems you have learned some tricks of the trade.

Unknown Role (None): What do you think, Mr. Zhongli? How should we pick from these pieces of Noctilucous Jade?

Unknown Role (None): What do you think, Mr. Zhongli?

Unknown Role (None): Oh? You want me to decide? That is fine as well.

Unknown Role (None): If it were me, the answer would be simple.

Unknown Role (None): Oh? And that would be...?

Unknown Role (None): I'll take them all, boss.

Unknown Role (None): Oh, you act with such panache, good sir! I always knew you were not a man of ordinary caliber!

Unknown Role (None): Wait! Wait! Boss, that one didn't count! We need to discuss it again!

Unknown Role (None): (Fade to black while Zhongli, protagonist, and Paimon step to one side to talk amongst themselves)

Unknown Role (None): Hey! If we only need one for the ritual, aren't we wasting three times the Mora if we buy them all!?

Unknown Role (None): That's true.

Unknown Role (None): Actually, it's two times more Mora.

Unknown Role (None): Oh, Mora. Hmm...

Unknown Role (None): It is as you say. I suppose I overlooked this particular aspect of the transaction.

Unknown Role (None): Huh? How do you not think about Mora when buying things!?

Unknown Role (None): If one must always consider Mora before acting, then in all things one is bound by Mora.

Unknown Role (None): Ahh... What?

Unknown Role (None): All Mora is currency, but not all currency is Mora.

Unknown Role (None): ???

Unknown Role (None): He's talking about economics.

Unknown Role (None): Is this how the rich live?

Unknown Role (None): Well, he knows a lot about big money, but not a lot about big savings.

Unknown Role (None): Sounds like he's loaded.

Unknown Role (None): So loaded that he doesn't even bother hunting for a bargain.

Unknown Role (None): No need to waver. Even when I am constrained by Mora, I have ways of working around my limitations.

Unknown Role (None): Evaluating the quality of Noctilucous Jade is indeed very tricky. As crude ore, there is little difference in texture, lustrousness, and internal pattern between good and bad Jade.

Unknown Role (None): Only after the item made using Noctilucous Jade has taken shape, will you be able to see whether it is up to par or not.

Unknown Role (None): If you return to those crafty merchants to quibble, they will counter by saying that your crafting bench is to blame, or that your heat control was poor.

Unknown Role (None): Whoa! To think it's that easy to get cheated...

Unknown Role (None): But there is a way to truly evaluate this jade, and a true insider would know it.

Unknown Role (None): "A fool sees the pointer and misses the moon."

Unknown Role (None): What does that mean?

Unknown Role (None): If you point at the moon with your finger, a wise man knows that you are pointing at the moon, while a fool will only see the finger.

Unknown Role (None): The patterns, the facade, these are all the "finger." Noctilucous Jade is a mystical stone used to light up the darkness, and so its brightness is the important thing — it is the "moon."

Unknown Role (None): Noctilucous Jade of excellent quality would have superior Pyro affinity. In other words, the bluer and brighter the luster of the ore under high temperature, the higher its quality.

Unknown Role (None): I didn't realize you were such an expert...

Unknown Role (None): Is this normal life for a high-class gentleman?

Unknown Role (None): I have imparted the priceless secrets of the jade trade to you. Now all that's left is to put it into practice.

Unknown Role (None): Priceless, huh... Paimon's just sad that we might never be able to use it again...

Unknown Role (None): (Fade to black while Zhongli, protagonist and Paimon return to Shitou's store)

Unknown Role (None): We're back to buy some rocks, boss. But can you let us burn them first?

Unknown Role (None): B—Burn them? You can't do that, my friends! If you were to do so, what would I have to sell?

Unknown Role (None): Then, can we get some small samples?

Unknown Role (None): That... Well, fine. As you wish, then.

Unknown Role (None): Then I'm not buying any.

Unknown Role (None): Not buying...? Ah, come, come now, my good friends... we can still discuss this. Negotiation is key to trade, wouldn't you say?

Unknown Role (None): How about this? I can take a small sample of all three. I'll take a bit of a loss — we'll count it as a friendly gesture.

Unknown Role (None): Don't worry, I know the rules. As long as we can prove that it is good jade, you will not take a loss.

Unknown Role (None): (Fade to black. Shitou obtains samples.)

Unknown Role (None): Alright. Take these as samples. I've carved them off with a knife and tagged them to boot.

Unknown Role (None): These samples...

Unknown Role (None): So thin...

Unknown Role (None): ...Aren't these too thin?

Unknown Role (None): Even paper's thicker. No, even a bug's wings are thicker! These are almost see-through!

Unknown Role (None): How stingy...

Unknown Role (None): Stingy? I've already been very generous. Any more, and you may as well have my neck!

Unknown Role (None): This is impressive handiwork.

Unknown Role (None): Hahaha. You flatter me, but I have to be gentle with these rocks. They are my pride and joy!

Unknown Role (None): If I'd taken off even a bit more, it would have killed me.

Unknown Role (None): But wouldn't something this thin go poof if we held it to the fire?

Unknown Role (None): It can't be helped. Trying to deprive a merchant of his profits would be like forcing a ravenous wolf to vomit up the food in its stomach. Nonetheless, under the right conditions, these thin slices will serve.

Unknown Role (None): What sort of conditions?

Unknown Role (None): While we add the high temperatures using Pyro, we can use Hydro to reinforce it from within. This way, the samples will not disintegrate immediately.

Unknown Role (None): Oh? Oh, sir, to think you were this learned... Thank you for your understanding!

Unknown Role (None): Strictly speaking, asking for samples when we have not yet agreed to purchase the goods is unfair. Trade in Liyue must be based upon fairness.

Unknown Role (None): Well, guess we just need to find a place to try this out...

Unknown Role (None): I remember this spot in the Dadaupa Gorge...

Unknown Role (None): How about the kitchen at Xiangling's place...

Unknown Role (None): You mean at Wanmin Restaurant? As long as we do it when she's not in...

Unknown Role (None): That would not do. It is a place where people come and go. They would be endangered if the elemental reaction were to get out of control... We cannot take such a risk.

Unknown Role (None): Ooh! Paimon remembers! We once saw this *biiiiig pot* down at the *Dadaupa Gorge*, in the camp of the hilichurls from the Meaty Tribe! It's real sturdy, and should be able to take the elemental reactions!

Unknown Role (None): #Traveler, now, let's pack those samples up and make a move!
