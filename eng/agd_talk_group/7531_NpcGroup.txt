# Talk Group: Lopukhin (NpcGroup 7531)

## Talk 0

Traveler: Has something happened?
Ovsyannikov: We're talking about how every single tavern in Snezhnograd is diluting proper fire-water with fruit juice these days!
Ovsyannikov: How ridiculous is that? There used to be a proper way to drink fire-water: you gulp it down in one go, then you chase it with a slice of pickle and a piece of black bread. That's the right way to do it.
Ovsyannikov: That's exactly how my elders did it when they were out on the Belyi Tsar's campaigns!
Ovsyannikov: And look at what we're stuck with now. We've completely lost the old ways, I'm telling you. Everything is just frills and gimmicks these days.

## Talk 1

Lopukhin: How exquisite... Now this can truly be called art!

## Talk 2

Traveler: What do you mean by "art"?
Lopukhin: Can't you feel the pure artistic vibe in the air around here?
Lopukhin: Thank goodness there is no vulgar caterwauling from merchants peddling low-class "art," nor any tedious rabble debating those mediocre essays in Vestnik. No trace of the human offends the senses here. How truly exquisite this is!
Lopukhin: This is true high society! The deep, millennia-old heritage of the fae aristocracy is, as one would expect, utterly incomparable to those parvenu humans.

## Talk 3

Traveler: I'll leave you to it, then.
Lopukhin: Vulgar, so vulgar!

## Talk 4

Traveler: But aren't you human yourself?
Lopukhin: That may be so, but I have long since transcended my human form in matters of taste. Intellectually, I now occupy the exact same lofty plane as the fae nobility.
Lopukhin: Oh, the sheer misery of it! Pity my poor spirit, condemned to be confined inside a physical form so utterly mundane and mortal.

## Talk 5

Traveler: I'm not sure one born fae necessarily understands "art"...
Lopukhin: That is completely immaterial. The most important thing here is the noble aura inherent in their bloodline, that flawless fusion of composure and the maniacal!
Lopukhin: You might actually grasp this fact if your taste were elevated to my particular caliber.
