# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 1790

## Talk 0

Wernher: No drinking on duty days! That applies to everyone, knights and civilians alike!

## Talk 1

Traveler: I completely agree with you on that.
Wernher: Thank you! If you've got some time to spare, would you mind dropping a few "suggestions" into the Knights of Favonius mailbox? For instance, how alcoholics have become a nuisance in your daily life, or how they've gotten in the way at work...
Wernher: Look, I'm very much aware that a bit of drinking can boost morale and soothe the homesickness of those on the frontlines...
Wernher: And how it can also dull one's fear and take a little of the edge off, allowing us to face the enemy head-on.
Wernher: I, too, drank when I was a greenhorn myself. It was hard to face those grotesque abominations without a few sips of liquid courage...
Wernher: But now that we're back in Dornman Port, the situation is completely different! While a tipple may lift the spirits, there's also a real risk to one's health and good judgment.
Wernher: It is my fervent hope that the matter will finally receive the attention of the Grand Master that it deserves soon...

## Talk 2

Traveler: Who's in charge of these things, usually?
Wernher: Great question! As disciplinary officer of the Garrison Squad, I keep an eye on the day-to-day behavior of the knights.
Wernher: From their conduct while in uniform to how they carry themselves among civilians on their days off... There are over 200 rules that every knight has to abide by.
Traveler: That's way too many!
Wernher: The first code of conduct wasn't that detailed, to be honest. We were fairly relaxed at the beginning.
Wernher: But exchanges with the Millelith gave us many insights into about disciplinary structures. That gave us the idea to thoroughly expand our code of conduct...
Wernher: Strict adherence to military discipline can make the squad more effective in combat!

## Talk 3

Traveler: How does it feel to be stationed in Dornman Port?
Wernher: Hmm... This may sound a little ridiculous to you, Honorary Knight...
Wernher: But I miss Mondstadt greatly. I miss the food at Good Hunter, and the times I spent listening to the bards with Aramis while drinking like there was no tomorrow...
Wernher: Ahem... That's beside the point, since I've quit drinking now. I'll stick to fruit juice when I get back.
Wernher: Although there's nothing to complain about here in Dornman Port, most people hold a very deep affection for the place where they grew up, don't they?
Wernher: I might be able to request a transfer back to Mondstadt once I put in another two years or so of duty here.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Yes, yes, that's right. I'll see you around!
Wernher: Even if you're not much of a drinker, remember to stay sober and keep your head clear!
