# The Meaning of Meaningless Waiting

Unknown Role (None): But now, all of a sudden, he says he plans to leave us... We can't help but wonder — was it something we did?

## Talk 3

Unknown Role (None): *sigh* ...

Unknown Role (None): So why the change?

Unknown Role (None): If you want my opinion, something to do with the Vision Hunt Decree.

Unknown Role (None): It's clear that Tejima had done nothing wrong. And still they confiscated his Vision. After that... he became a completely different person.

Unknown Role (None): I can't claim to fully understand it, but I could tell that he'd lost something very important to him. He went off on a walk, alone, circled the village a few times, and then out of the blue, he announced that he was going to leave and become a wanderer.

Unknown Role (None): Truth be told, we aren't sure whether trying to keep him here is the right thing to do. But equally... it doesn't feel right to let him leave when he's in this state. He's a lost soul.

Unknown Role (None): Seems like he's a well-respected guy around here.

## Talk 4

Unknown Role (None): You must be Tejima? So, what's made you wanna up and leave all of a sudden?

Unknown Role (None): Me...? I... It's not a question of why I want to leave... but a question of... what reason I would have to stay.

Unknown Role (None): You've lived here for a long time.

Unknown Role (None): Everyone depends on you.

Unknown Role (None): True... but that's not why I chose to stay here...

Unknown Role (None): ...and what made me want to come here 30 years ago? And why have I never wanted to leave in all that time?

Unknown Role (None): *sigh* I don't have answers to those questions, because I can't remember anymore. Ever since they took my Vision away, it's like... a slice of my memory is gone.

Unknown Role (None): In the past, I knew I wanted to stay here... but whatever resolve I had then, it's gone now. So I thought, what's to stop me from moving around instead? The emptiness inside me will be there either way.

Unknown Role (None): Okay... Well, in that case, if we help you rediscover the reason you chose to stay, you won't need to leave anymore, right?

Unknown Role (None): Hmm... But if you can't remember anything, it's not gonna be easy.

Unknown Role (None): Oh! Maybe if you just try a little harder to remember, then... it'll all come flooding back?

Unknown Role (None): Oh, that reminds me... Last time I brought Tejima some fruit, I do believe I saw him writing in a diary.

Unknown Role (None): ...I keep a diary? If you say so... I honestly can't seem to remember.

Unknown Role (None): Yes, yes, you do! And what's more, I remember you saying at the time that you wanted to make a note of a few interesting things — things which would prove very important at a later date.

Unknown Role (None): Perfect! So, if we wanna keep Tejima from leaving, we just need to find his diary. It must be around here somewhere, let's take a look.

Unknown Role (None): If you don't mind, we will leave you to find the diary... we should head back to the village to inform the others of Tejima's situation.

Unknown Role (None): The soil looks like it's been disturbed... Maybe Tejima buried something precious here — something that's kept him in the village all these years?

Unknown Role (None): Must be something pretty amazing if it made him stick around for 30 years. Let's dig it up and take a look~!

Unknown Role (None): It looks like... a letter? The paper's gone yellow — must have been written a really long time ago.

Unknown Role (None): "Konda Village"... Sounds so familiar — where is that place, again?

Unknown Role (None): It's here.

Unknown Role (None): Huh? So the reason Tejima came here was to wait for someone?

Unknown Role (None): But he's been here for 30 years... Aw, guess they didn't show up in the end, huh...

Unknown Role (None): Well, let's go give Tejima his stuff back and take it from there.

## Talk 8

Unknown Role (None): Well, I'll be... That's certainly my handwriting... and I guess the omamori and letter belong to me too.

Unknown Role (None): ...But I have no memory of anything that's written in this diary.

Unknown Role (None): Still... it's clear that I was waiting for someone here, and that I chose to wait for 30 years.

Unknown Role (None): Over the years, I must have made a note of anything interesting... anything that I could share with her when we were finally reunited.

Unknown Role (None): And just look at all the things that did happen over the years... The time has flown by so quickly. 30 years feels like the blink of an eye.

Unknown Role (None): How could I have forgotten something so important to me? Hmm... Now that I think about it, when my Vision was taken from me, it felt like I'd suddenly been... hollowed out.

Unknown Role (None): Love, regret... everything I felt for her... it's all disappeared.

Unknown Role (None): Does that make you feel sad?

Unknown Role (None): No... not especially.

Unknown Role (None): After all, I've forgotten who she was. Her face, her voice, the things we experienced together... I barely recall any of it.

Unknown Role (None): It's as if she'd never been in my life to begin with... as if, all these years have been nothing but a hazy dream.

Unknown Role (None): So, do you still plan on leaving?

Unknown Role (None): Mm... I think... maybe not. If this is something I waited most of my life for, I suppose I should carry on waiting.

Unknown Role (None): Although, what if she were to turn up eventually, only to find I didn't remember so much as her name... Wouldn't that be upsetting for her?

Unknown Role (None): When I think about it like that... I do feel a slight tinge of sadness in my heart.

Unknown Role (None): How curious... Why am I thinking like this when I don't even remember who she is? It's just like that feeling of emptiness, the feeling that something is... missing...

Unknown Role (None): *sigh* Thank you both for helping me reconnect with my reason for staying here.

Unknown Role (None): I shall remain here, and keep waiting for her.

Unknown Role (None): Tejima seems to be dealing... okay, but still... it makes Paimon really sad...

Unknown Role (None): Yeah, me too...

Unknown Role (None): Losing a reason to feel sad is maybe the saddest thing of all.

Unknown Role (None): Seems it's just like Ayaka and Thoma were saying. If you lose your Vision, you lose all your hopes and dreams, too...

Unknown Role (None): That certainly explains the state Tejima was in earlier.

Unknown Role (None): At least we were able to help him... weren't we?

Unknown Role (None): *sigh* ...Well, let's go find the next person.
