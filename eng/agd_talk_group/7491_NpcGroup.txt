# Talk Group: Fyodor (NpcGroup 7491)

## Talk 0

Fyodor: I am, I am! I've got a post with the Veche now. Not a seat of my own, but it's still enough for me to know the right people and be known by them.
Fyodor: In fact, I'm here today to see off one of the deputies on an inspection trip. Personally, no less. That should tell you they see me as someone dependable. Give it a few more years, and who knows? They might just promote me straight to the Veche...
Fyodor: But listen to me, going on about myself. How have you been, Ilya?
Fyodor: You look like you just came back from somewhere. Planning to find a post here in Snezhnograd? Well, if there's anything your old classmate here can help with, don't be shy. Just say the word!

Fyodor: Oh, no, no, old friend... no, sir, please. You're far too modest. I've always known you were different than the rest of us, even back then. You always had a certain air about you!
Fyodor: To think I'd run into you here, what luck! Sir, when you have a moment, I'd be grateful for any guidance you could spare...
