# Talk Group: Kuzmichi (NpcGroup 7516)

## Talk 0

Lodygina: I heard Torchlight Books has new releases again... I wish there were stories like this.
Lodygina: "A beautiful Domovoy princess fled from her homeland and found refuge in a distant, impoverished kingdom, where seven handsome human princes welcomed her with open arms..."
Lodygina: "As she gazed upon her unfamiliar surroundings, she let out a wistful sigh. 'Oh, what strange land is this? Where are the silken carpets, the lavish banquets, the sweetest wines, and the glittering dances that fill the night with joy?'"
Lodygina: "At that moment, an unexpected incident occurs — the Domovoy princess's stomach let out an embarrassingly loud growl..."
Kuzmichi: Yes! At this point, one of the princes takes her to the Podvorye Restaurant for a meal. Deeply moved, she gifts him all her precious jewels.
Lodygina: No! I hate where this story is going! If I were the author...
Lodygina: "The first prince had hair as golden as the morning sun. He offered the princess a gentle smile, and her embarrassment melted away at once..."
Lodygina: "The second prince was dark-eyed and quiet, yet thoughtful beyond measure. Long before the princess arrived, he had prepared the palace's finest tableware and its most exquisite delicacies..."
Lodygina: "And the third prince..."
Kuzmichi: Alright, alright. So tell me, dear, how does your version end?
Lodygina: Of course, the Domovoy princess becomes queen of the kingdom and takes all seven handsome human princes as her royal consorts! Then they march against the kingdom she once fled from...
Kuzmichi: That's somehow even worse than I imagined... Though, dear author, if this masterpiece of yours were ever published, it would surely become every young Domovoy maiden's favorite bedtime story.
Kuzmichi: Now come on — let's head to the Podvorye Restaurant and do something about my poor starving stomach. These stairs are endless...
