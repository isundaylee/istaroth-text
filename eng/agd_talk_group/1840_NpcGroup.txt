## Talk 1

Traveler: Tell me about your era.
Yu Huangyi: I do not believe there is anything particularly exceptional about my time.
Yu Huangyi: At that time, everyone situated themselves around Mt. Tianheng. Those who had the strength engaged in blacksmithing, while the others agile of body would venture into the hills to hunt and forage.
Yu Huangyi: Morax — heh, he was neither Rex Lapis nor the Geo Lord then — called himself Mr. Mo.
Yu Huangyi: "The divine artifact remains, and chaos has been kept at bay. The world is at peace. Dwell I shall for now in this corner of the mortal world."
Yu Huangyi: I have been apprised of what happened after — the world became topsy-turvy, and Mr. Mo's "corner of the world" subsequently became much bigger.

## Talk 2

Yu Huangyi: Why ever not?
Yu Huangyi: We have the time and the knowledge to do so, that's all.
