Unknown Role (None): Obscured portions? You mean the bits Paimon can't read?

Unknown Role (None): And since I've been the Abyss Order's enemy for so long...

Unknown Role (None): They've made me the target around which they can rally their troops and unite their strength.

Unknown Role (None): Wow... Seems like it takes a lot of brains to be a superhero, huh?

Unknown Role (None): I understand my enemy well is all. As the saying goes, heroes and villains are two sides of the same coin.

Unknown Role (None): So what're we gonna do about the intel that's written in gibberish then? How do we... ungibberishify it?

Unknown Role (None): Simple. When you don't understand something, you ask.

Unknown Role (None): I've already tracked down one of their *strongholds*... If you're interested, feel free to come along.
