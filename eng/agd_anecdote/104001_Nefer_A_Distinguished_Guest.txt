# Nefer: A Distinguished Guest

Today, The Flagship is welcoming two very special guests...

Inside The Flagship, you see Nefer discussing business with Yelan. Though their words remain polite, the atmosphere is tense. Maybe it's best to keep your distance for now...

## Talk 0

Nefer: A base of Dandelion Wine — aged for 25 years — one-seventh part Bittersweet Liqueur, filled halfway with Fizzy Zaytun Peach Pop...
Yelan: All three of those ingredients are fairly sugary. It'll have a lovely sweet aftertaste, even a little too cloying...
Yelan: A spoonful of salt and half a spoon of powdered Summer Orchid Leaf should cut through the sweetness in a way that's clear but not overpowering.
Nefer: Hmm... I can imagine it now — a delightfully layered flavor.
Yelan: It's all down to the seasonings you sent me, Lady Nefer. Otherwise, I'd have no idea how to mix up a cocktail that so befits Nod-Krai.
Nefer: Just a little gift, I'm glad you liked it. I'll be happy to send more when you run out.
Yelan: Many thanks, though I think one taste is enough where these seasonings are concerned. Besides, you sent so much, I could hardly finish it by my lonesome.
Yelan: And my colleagues aren't accustomed to these flavors, so I can't even share around. I'm afraid they'll end up sitting on a shelf, going to waste.
Nefer: Can't say I'm surprised your colleagues at Liyue's Ministry of Civil Affairs aren't too keen. I wouldn't expect the flavors of Nod-Krai to be their cup of tea...
Nefer: Though from what I've seen, might I presume that we've won you over?
Yelan: I wouldn't go that far, though it's not like I dislike the food here. It's good to try new things occasionally.
