## Talk 1

Da Xi'er: Hello there, Honorary Knight Tumaini! What a surprise, running into you here.

Option 1:

> Traveler: What are you doing?
> Da Xi'er: It's called "circulating qi to ward off the cold."
> Da Xi'er: Or, to put it simply, tempering the will.
> Da Xi'er: Enduring extreme cold in only thin clothes is a classic martial arts discipline, and I'm just about starting to master it!
> Da Xi'er: Of course, compared to Master Huo in his prime, I still have a long way to go...
> Traveler: Master Huo...? Who's that?
> Da Xi'er: I've only heard about his fame from my master's stories.
> Da Xi'er: Master Huo was a renowned swordsman who also knew the art of forging weapons.
> Da Xi'er: His treasured blade was called "Unseen Razor," and with it, he struck down many evils. Even now, the martial world still tells stories of his deeds.
> Da Xi'er: Lately, though, people have been telling a different version of the story. They say Master Huo's growing fame made him realize his heart had become restless, so he went looking for somewhere cold and secluded, far away from worldly noise.
> Da Xi'er: He kept heading north across Teyvat, and in the end, vanished into Snezhnaya's snowstorms...
> Traveler: Sounds pretty mysterious.
> Da Xi'er: Though, I should probably add that Master Huo's younger sister came forward a couple of weeks ago to clear the air. She said the story had been exaggerated far too much, and asked everyone to stop spreading it around.
> Da Xi'er: And since my master lost to her ten years ago — and she's coming to Snezhnaya soon herself — I'd rather not say any more.
> Da Xi'er: I'll just focus on training, steady my mind, and keep improving myself, like a proper martial artist should...

Option 2:

> Traveler: You look like you're from Liyue. Why call me by all those titles?
> Da Xi'er: Well, my little sister lived in Mondstadt for years. Whenever we all got together, she'd go on and on about the food there, the Knights of Favonius, and all kinds of stories from Mondstadt.
> Da Xi'er: Then I even caught a glimpse of you from afar during the Dragonspine International Cross Country Challenge, though I'm sure you didn't notice me.
> Da Xi'er: Later, after I bought several cartloads of fruits and Grainfruit from Bold Bang Bouldern, she invited me to Natlan, where I heard a whole new batch of heroic tales about you.
> Da Xi'er: So with two grand titles like that, why not combine them? It makes you sound even more impressive — exactly the kind of title a legendary martial-world master deserves.
> Traveler: So you haven't told Chichikova?
> Da Xi'er: Well, wouldn't it be more interesting to let her see a legendary martial-world master's skills firsthand, instead of just hearing about them from someone else?
> Da Xi'er: Besides, Chichikova might not know all the legends from other lands. Even people who've traveled somewhere can miss a tale or two.
> Da Xi'er: Take my master, for example. Have you ever heard of Huangshan, Demon-Slayer of the Flying Leaf?
> Traveler: I can't say I have... Who's that?
> Da Xi'er: Exactly! Teyvat is vast, and there are skilled masters in every corner of it. No one could possibly know them all.
