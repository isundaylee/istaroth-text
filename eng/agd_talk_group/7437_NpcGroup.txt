# Talk Group: Shakhovskaya (NpcGroup 7437)

## Talk 0

Shakhovskaya: Look at you, Praporshchik Rozhdestvensky. Your bearing is barely distinguishable from that of a dissolute sellsword. You are a Druzhna, Tsaritsa's sake.
Rozhdestvensky: Oh, come now, Your Grace. What would you have me do? Emulate some of our colleagues and strut in circles, dressed in my parade best, taking military theater for license to feign blindness to crime in this city?
Rozhdestvensky: If you want to catch the scum who skulk in the shadows and foment chaos, you're going to find yourself in dark, dank corners the higher-ups would never give a second look...
Rozhdestvensky: In which case, this outfit will serve far better than uniforms bedecked with medals.
Shakhovskaya: I can't believe you! Why, I'm starting to suspect you've never read a single page of the Druzhna drill manual!
Shakhovskaya: Solemn bearings, majestic formations, those are the blades by which we drive evil back, not by scurrying through the sewers like rats!
Malinina: Oh, come now, let's not get all worked up...
Malinina: A visit from Rozhdestvensky's hard come by, y'know? I've got freshly brewed tea and some snacks Lady Vesna sent us. Let's have ourselves a seat and a chat, hmm?
Rozhdestvensky: Much appreciated, Miss Malinina, but I believe I shan't be staying here for too long.
Shakhovskaya: Running with your tail between your legs, are we? You see? That's what you get for abandoning honor and pride. Next thing I know, you're going to be as hopeless as those Fatui.
Rozhdestvensky: Alright, alright. Are you quite done, dear lady? These lectures of yours never fail to magically transport me to solitary confinement in some cloistered school for noble daughters...
Malinina: Come now, Lady Shakhovskaya means well! I mean, where would our merry lot of disparate fae be without order and discipline?
Malinina: And she's saying this because she's concerned about you, you know.
Shakhovskaya: Concer— Yes, indeed. Of course. This is the duty that those at the top must take on, after all.
Malinina: See? There you have it. And I'm sure Her Ladyship will not protest so much, Rozhdestvensky, should you be willing to join us for evening exercises.
Rozhdestvensky: Okay, sure. Since you've been so generous, Miss Malinina, I suppose I'll have to dust off that outdated uniform of ours.
