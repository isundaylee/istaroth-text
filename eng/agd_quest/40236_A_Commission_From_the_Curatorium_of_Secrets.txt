# A Commission From the Curatorium of Secrets


## Talk 3

Paimon: Hello? Anyone here?

Nefer: Ah, you got here even earlier than I expected. That should give us just enough time to go over some details before the opening ceremony.

Traveler: So, what did you need us to do?

Nefer: How about a cup of tea first? I'm sure it was a long trip.

Nefer: #Now tell me, Traveler and Paimon... Have you heard of the "Nod-Krai Trade Expo"?

Paimon: Uh, nope...

Nefer: A long time ago, back before Nasha Town even had that name, this place was essentially one giant black market.

Nefer: With no administrative oversight, trade that was prohibited elsewhere was fair game here. So, this place began to attract business from all over Teyvat.

Nefer: Over time, more and more people settled here, and what was once a black market became what is now Nasha Town.

Nefer: The spirit of free trade has been a local staple ever since. Hence, the "Nod-Krai Trade Expo" was born.

Paimon: So... is it like a business convention?

Nefer: In a sense. It's an annual tradition once the shipping routes open up. The expo offers a chance to trade without restrictions.

Nefer: In other words, it's the biggest "legal" black market in all of Teyvat.

Traveler: So, is that why you asked me to come?

Nefer: Yes. The Voynich Guild is organizing the event, and that comes with a lot of responsibilities.

Nefer: Normally, I would let Jahoda handle most of the work. But this year, it might prove too much for her to manage on her own.

Nefer: Something tells me we'll have a lot of big names in attendance this time. Working with VIP clients takes someone with the right experience and connections.

Nefer: Hence why I think Jahoda might need a helping hand.

Paimon: #Oh, so that's where Traveler comes in!

Nefer: Exactly. I was hoping you could help her with participant coordination.

Traveler: It's nice of you to look out for Jahoda.

Nefer: I don't know if I'd put it quite like that. I'm just interested in playing to people's strengths.

Nefer: In addition, I think you'll find the compensation to be quite to your satisfaction. So, are you in?

Traveler: Yeah, should be a piece of cake.

Nefer: Confident, I like it. This is exactly why I chose everyone's favorite globe-trotting hero for this job.

Traveler: Sure. But what's the catch?

Nefer: Hah, good instincts.

Nefer: I do have one special request, as the one offering this commission.

Nefer: In principle, the Nod-Krai Trade Expo is a platform for commerce with no restrictions. That said, the Guild does have one line that is not to be crossed.

Nefer: I'll need you to help make sure all participants are clear on this unspoken rule. As long as everyone complies, the expo should go off without a hitch.

Traveler: So what's the unspoken rule?

Nefer: No violence at the expo. The Guild has pledged to ensure the safety of all sellers and buyers in attendance.

Paimon: Violence? Whoa, that wasn't even on Paimon's radar... Just because it's legal doesn't change the fact that this is still a black market, huh...

Nefer: If that line is crossed, the event will descend into chaos.

Traveler: Understood.

Nefer: Of course, mediating such situations might prove complicated. So, if you ever want my judgment on the matter, just come find me.

Nefer: The expo is being held on the shoreline to the southeast of Nasha Town. Jahoda is working on setting up the booths as we speak. She'll be able to provide details on the participants as well.

Nefer: Alright, I suppose I'll leave you to it, then.

Paimon: Yep, we've got this!

## Talk 10

Paimon: Wonder how Beidou, Navia, and Ineffa's goods are selling? Let's go check!

Ineffa: Excuse me, according to the expo announcement, you are in charge of mediating disputes between participants, correct?

Traveler: Yeah, did something happen?

Ineffa: Just now, as Aino was about to return to the workshop, she got dragged into a conversation with some individuals who appear to have unofficial ties to the expo.

Ineffa: My database does not have relevant information on how to address this situation, so I came to you for help.

Paimon: Unofficial ties, huh? Sounds a little shady...

Traveler: Let's go check it out.

Ineffa: Come with me.

Yelan: A device that uses pneuma and ousia energies in battle... With that kind of tech, a bandit group could rob a caravan without even showing their faces.

Chevreuse: And what about this thing... Remote guidance, precision bombing at long-distance... If a group of thugs got their hands on it, their enemies might as well surrender on the spot.

Aino: Uh...

Yelan: The Fontaine Research Institute made the right call going through the Spina di Rosula rather than selling it themselves.

Chevreuse: The same is true for the Ministry of Civil Affairs, I'm sure. A merc captain who spends most of her time at sea is far less conspicuous.

Aino: Huh?

Yelan: What about this girl? She seems to be trading in military-grade weapons as well...

Chevreuse: The Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop does take on a lot of orders for dangerous creations, but I think in this case...

Yelan: I'm overthinking it?

Chevreuse: I think so...

Paimon: Yelan? Chevreuse? What are you doing here?

Yelan: #Traveler? Paimon? How did you find this place?

Ineffa: Although this location is indeed rather secluded, Aino has equipped me with a special sensor module. With it, I was able to detect that you had pulled her aside for questioning.

Chevreuse: The rumors are true, then... She's quite the little engineer.

Aino: Can you tell me what this is about already?

Chevreuse: Apologies, but it's a matter of utmost importance, so I had to be sure. After our conversation, however, I can confirm that Aino is not involved in this case.

Yelan: #Traveler, could you help us clear up this misunderstanding?

Traveler: Ineffa, why don't you get Aino out of here?

Traveler: They're no danger to her.

Ineffa: Understood.

Chevreuse: Based on our conversation just now, I'm guessing neither of us is the target the other is searching for.

Yelan: I second that. And, since our strategies were so similar, I'd wager we're after the same person.

Traveler: When we talked earlier...

Traveler: It seemed like you both were hiding something.

Paimon: Yeah! Paimon thought so too!

Yelan: Well, I wasn't planning on dragging you into all this, but now that I appear to have gained an unexpected partner, I suppose I might as well explain...

Yelan: #I trust you, Traveler, and Miss Chevreuse seems to do the same. With you here, I'm hoping we can avoid further misunderstandings.

Chevreuse: Hmm... Agreed.

Traveler: I'm all ears.

Yelan: Miss Chevreuse and I seem to be here in Nod-Krai to reel in the same fish.

Chevreuse: That's right, and the product we brought to trade is the bait. Of course, if I worked the booth, it might tip people off, so that's where Navia comes in. I assume it's the same situation with Captain Beidou?

Paimon: So, does that mean your target is gonna be at the expo?

Yelan: Yes. There's a good chance it's one of the buyers.

Paimon: But, um... What about Nefer's golden rule?

Traveler: Yeah...

Traveler: We're gonna need to take you to the Curatorium.

Chevreuse: What? No. I've done some digging into the head of the Curatorium and where she stands. We can't let her know what we're doing.

Paimon: Well...

Yelan: #Heh... I wouldn't be surprised if she got Traveler involved because she anticipated this from the start.

Yelan: #If Traveler has someone to answer to, then we should go.

Chevreuse: Hmm... Fine.
