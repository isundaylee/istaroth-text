Flins: Hello there, friend. A pleasure to meet you again amidst the graves.
Black Screen Text: You describe the "Oath Lantern" you saw and the incomprehensible words inscribed upon it.
Flins: "The winds awaken, the leaves whirl round"... This is the oath those who join the Lightkeepers swear.
Flins: Usually, each of us will carry our own lantern, only abandoning our charge to care for it when we have no other choice.
Flins: Each masterless lantern, therefore, is a marker of misfortune, a tragedy that fills me with sorrow.
Flins: But they knew that such would be their inevitable fate from the moment they made their oaths.
Flins: If there was anything else we could do for them, it would be remembering the oaths they made.
Flins: To bear and defend "lanterns alight with eternal hope"... That was their dearest wish.
