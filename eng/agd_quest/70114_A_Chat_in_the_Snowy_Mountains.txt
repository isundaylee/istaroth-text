## Talk 3

(Enjoy teatime)

Albedo: It's a rare occasion indeed when Miss Sandrone finds the time to visit Mondstadt.
Marionette: You provided such invaluable help when I was researching the numerological array. I wanted to thank you in person.
Albedo: You're too kind. But such things lie well outside my area of expertise. The credit for completing it should really be all yours.
Marionette: It was a team effort. Hmm... Come to think of it, where's Durin? Seeing as you're practically his guardian, I thought he would be unable to stray too far from your side.
Albedo: You're not entirely wrong.
Albedo: But recently, he's been making headway with his social skills, so I've been trying to encourage him to be a bit more independent.
Marionette: Aren't you worried about him?
Albedo: A little. In the past, if he was on his own and interacting with someone whose personality was... let's say "strong," he tended to...
Marionette: Pick up bizarre habits? Mimic unusual verbal tics?
Albedo: Precisely. I take it you've experienced something similar, Miss Sandrone?
Marionette: Hmph, do you even need to ask? I'm practically an expert...
Albedo: Typically, after a meeting with Grand Master Varka, I'll take Durin to visit Sucrose and help with her research. It allows him to find his focus.
Albedo: But if he's spent too long with Acting Grand Master Jean, we'll pay a visit to Klee so he can rediscover how to relax.
Marionette: Sounds like a lot of work...
Albedo: Just looking after my family.
Albedo: How would you handle such situations?
Marionette: Me? I don't have the energy for that sort of mess...
Marionette: But it seems to me that if someone's good by nature, no matter how much they're influenced, they'll always return to their true self.
Albedo: Hmm... An excellent observation. Perhaps I can adopt this way of thinking.
