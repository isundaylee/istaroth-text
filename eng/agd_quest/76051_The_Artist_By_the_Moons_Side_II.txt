## Talk 3

Paimon: Well, let's see here. Oh! Another drawing of Columbina. No surprise there!
Traveler: It's beautiful.
Paimon: Is that... a forest somewhere in Nod-Krai? There are so many little animals!
The Damselette: I'm afraid... you would have to go back to the time before I left the Frostmoon Scions to see a scene like this.
Paimon: Wow, Paimon's so jealous! Did cute little animals really gather around you like that?
The Damselette: Yes, most of them were drawn to the kuuvahki around me. They felt safe curling up next to me like that.
Paimon: That's so awesome! You could pet them all you wanted without them running away!
The Damselette: Hm... I would say the chic badgers were the nicest to pet. They're the ones with the big, fluffy tails.
The Damselette: The fur on a rimehorn deer's neck is quite soft as well. It makes a very comfortable pillow.
The Damselette: Which do you prefer?
Paimon: Um, we don't really have the same kind of experience to go off of...
Paimon: But, Paimon would probably choose to pet a chic badger's tail.
The Damselette: Chic badgers can be quite timid. If you want to pet them, they need to feel safe first.
The Damselette: Ah... But I would exercise caution, Paimon.
Paimon: Why? Do they... bite, or something? P—Paimon promises to be super gentle...
The Damselette: No. I'm referring to danger from above.
Paimon: Danger from above...?
The Damselette: Yes... Longplume ibises often hunt little animals like these. I had to issue them a warning to get them to stop going after the kuuhenki.
Paimon: You're just telling Paimon this now!? It's a miracle she hasn't been carried off yet!
The Damselette: Ah... Then, the next time I see one, I'll advise them that you are off limits as well.
Paimon: Next time? No, you need to find one right now, or Paimon'll be too scared to even fly out that door!
The Damselette: Ah, I see. In that case, I will be sure to tell them that Paimon is...
Traveler: The best companion ever.
Paimon: Exactly! They can't just go around snatching up people's travel companions!
Maalaus: !!!
The Damselette: The little one... seems strangely curious about the idea of Paimon being carried off by a longplume ibis.
Paimon: Don't even think about painting a picture like that...
Traveler: The greatest travel guide in Teyvat.
Paimon: Uh... Paimon's glad you see it that way, but... They probably don't even know what a "travel guide" is.
Traveler: A food source that would just upset their stomachs.
Paimon: No, don't even put that thought in their heads! What if they try their luck!?
Traveler: Just looking at it makes me feel happy.
