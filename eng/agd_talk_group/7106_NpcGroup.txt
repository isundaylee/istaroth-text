# Talk Group: Rahetar (NpcGroup 7106)

## Talk 0

Rahetar: Children shouldn't be exposed to such nonsensical stories...

## Talk 1

Traveler: Nonsensical stories?
Rahetar: You know what I mean... love and romance, fighting and death, especially those tall tales about the thief Reed Miller. It just leads them astray.
Rahetar: Luckily, my Kasha is a clever child. She'd never read any of that brain-rotting nonsense. But if other children were to read it, who knows how much damage it could do?

## Talk 2

Traveler: Goodbye.
Rahetar: May the moon light your path.

## Talk 3

Traveler: About Reed Miller...
Rahetar: Hmph, he's just a trouble-making thief from hundreds of years ago. I have no idea why anyone would write stories about someone like that.
Rahetar: Stealing from the governor's palace, falling in love with the fae governor's adopted daughter, and even some utter claptrap about some holy lady of the Frostmoon Scions falling madly in love with him...

## Talk 4

Traveler: You seem familiar...
Rahetar: F—Familiar?! H—How could I possibly be familiar with such an absurd thing?
Rahetar: "O mysterious maiden, even the greatest treasures of the Frostmoon Scions pale in comparison to your beauty and warmth"... "Now, be so kind as to return every last coin of that ill-gotten wealth"...
Rahetar: ...Obviously, I've never even had such ridiculous things said to me!
Rahetar: In... In any case, my dear Kasha can't be exposed to such frivolities! Children should focus on developing their virtues, until they become as pure as the moonlight!
