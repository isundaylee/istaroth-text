# Dodoco's Bomb-Rally Ruckus

Klee has picked out her favorite toy...


## Talk 1

(Talk to Klee)

Paimon: Find any good toys to bring, Klee?
Klee: Yeah! Mom made me this magic toy so that Dodoco and me can play with bombs together. I'm gonna bring it to the tea party so all the little witches can join in too!
Paimon: You want everyone to... play with bombs together!? That's way too dangerous for a tea party!
Klee: It's not dangerous! The toy makes it so that Jumpy Dumpty doesn't explode, so I can use it all I want without getting sent to confinement. That's what Mom said!
Klee: You guys should come play too! Dodoco came to Inazuma with me and really wants to meet you!
Paimon: Huh... But, you usually carry Dodoco with you, don't you? We met ages ago, remember?
Klee: No, not that Dodoco! The Dodo Clan has lots of Dodocos. Some are like me, and like going on exciting adventures in new places.
Klee: But a lot of Dodocos live in a different place most of the time, so you haven't met them yet.
Paimon: A different place?
Klee: Yeah! Back in Mondstadt, Prune showed me a picture book about it. She said that place is a... made-up world for kids, and Dodoco is my imaginary friend.
Klee: But no matter where Dodocos come from, Mom always finds a way to bring them here to play. Like this time, she put Dodoco in a magic toy!
Paimon: Yeah, we've seen all kinds of Dodocos at this point, but it still feels amazing every time...
Traveler: Nothing like a bit of magic, huh...
Klee: So, you gonna play bombs with us? Please, please!
