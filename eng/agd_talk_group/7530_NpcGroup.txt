# Talk Group: Lyubimova (NpcGroup 7530)

## Talk 0

Traveler: Has something happened?
Ovsyannikov: We're talking about how every single tavern in Snezhnograd is diluting proper fire-water with fruit juice these days!
Ovsyannikov: How ridiculous is that? There used to be a proper way to drink fire-water: you gulp it down in one go, then you chase it with a slice of pickle and a piece of black bread. That's the right way to do it.
Ovsyannikov: That's exactly how my elders did it when they were out on the Belyi Tsar's campaigns!
Ovsyannikov: And look at what we're stuck with now. We've completely lost the old ways, I'm telling you. Everything is just frills and gimmicks these days.

## Talk 1

Lyubimova: "Project Stuzha," my foot! Someone must have offended Chernabog again or something...

## Talk 2

Traveler: Who is this "Chernabog" you're talking about?
Lyubimova: You young'uns wouldn't know a thing about it. Chernabog's a terribly wicked fiend, a real monster, and it was because of its curse that the old Tsar bit the dust back in the day...
Lyubimova: Lately, Snezhnograd has been all in an uproar, a real madhouse! I say it's because Chernabog's back again!

## Talk 3

Traveler: Didn't they shelve Project Stuzha already?
Lyubimova: Huh? They did?
Lyubimova: Hmph! I don't believe that for a second. That was just a terribly cheap lie when people started complaining. I say they're still doing it on the sly!
Lyubimova: I'm telling you, that's how it always happens, every single time! Won't be the first time I am seeing it either.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Farewell.
Lyubimova: You be careful now!

## Talk 5

Traveler: That sounds a little farfetched, to be honest...
Lyubimova: Not at all! You've heard of Northland Bank, but do you honestly believe it's just a place for coin? I'm telling you, that bank wasn't even a thing when the old Tsar was still around!
Lyubimova: That bank exists because Chernabog once marked every Mora in existence. The Tsaritsa ordered it established to give them a terribly strict washing, a real cleaning, before they came back out.
Lyubimova: You'd better believe it! I heard this from a personal attendant of the Tsaritsa's, so it must be true.

## Talk 6

Traveler: That's quite something indeed!
Lyubimova: Isn't that so? So, we must all be careful, and keep from heading out of doors if we can.
Lyubimova: But if we must leave the house, stop every three steps. Just stand still for a moment. That is the only way to avoid bumping into the unclean.
