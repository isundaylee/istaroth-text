# Talk Group: Pauper (NpcGroup 7840)

## Talk 0

Pauper ("Basement Dweller" and "Pauper"): (I wonder why there are a hedgehog and a cat on the train. Are they a passenger's pets...?)

## Talk 1

Traveler: (Maybe I should stay away.)

## Talk 2

Traveler: Excuse me, you two...
Pauper ("Basement Dweller" and "Pauper"): (The hedgehog and the cat don't even look at you.)
Pauper ("Basement Dweller" and "Pauper"): (The most terrible suffering lies precisely here — in the clear knowledge that there is no hope of an answer, no hope of love. Indifference is the pain of no longer being able to love.)
Pauper ("Basement Dweller" and "Pauper"): (They are so arrogant... so cold and detached, as if they simply cannot understand you.)
Pauper ("Basement Dweller" and "Pauper"): (Though, in truth, they can't. The hedgehog and the cat don't know what you're saying. They are just a hedgehog and a cat.)

## Talk 3

Traveler: (Leave quietly.)

## Talk 4

Traveler: Dear hedgehog, could you please tell me...
Basement Dweller: (The hedgehog remains motionless, curled up on the soft cushion.)
Basement Dweller: (It might be a sick hedgehog. A malicious hedgehog. An unremarkable hedgehog. Bitterly and futilely occupying a seat that does not belong to it.)
Basement Dweller: (This is not its seat, nor will it ever be. The cushion is dying, and the hedgehog knows it. That is why it despises itself — almost to the point of hatred.)
Basement Dweller: (Too much awareness is also a kind of illness. A true, complete illness.)

## Talk 5

Traveler: (Leave like a hedgehog — coldly, without looking back.)

## Talk 6

Traveler: And you? What do you think?
Pauper: (The cat lets out a soft meow, but also ignores you.)
Pauper: (It does not have a seat of its own. Poor cats are always like this, prone to overthinking. They observe the world with different eyes, watching passersby.)
Pauper: (The poor have no right to refuse true suffering — pain — yet still long for a dignity that was never meant to be theirs.)

## Talk 7

Traveler: (Leave hopelessly like a cat.)

## Talk 8

Traveler: Please forgive me. I meant no harm to your dignity.
Pauper: (The cat does not answer. Perhaps out of anger, wounded pride, or a sense of insult. Not a lack of morality, but a very human reaction.)
Pauper: (Or perhaps simply because it is only a cat.)

## Talk 9

Traveler: (Leave hopelessly like a cat.)
