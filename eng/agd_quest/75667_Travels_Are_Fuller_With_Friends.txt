# Travels Are Fuller With Friends

Huilu: #Hello there, Traveler! Do you have a moment? I've got a story I want to share...
Traveler: Ooh, what's it about?
Huilu: It's about traveling, friends, and family.
Huilu: In the People of the Springs, there was once a famous traveler by the name of Akamai.
Huilu: Together with his Koholasaurus companion, Kau Kau, he embarked on a long journey. Their quest: To traverse all of Natlan and feast on all its delicacies.
Huilu: Whenever they arrived somewhere new, Akamai and Kau Kau would search for the most beautiful stone around to keep as a memento.
Huilu: Their culinary journey brought them to countless places, filled their backpacks with innumerable beautiful stones, and stuffed their bellies with myriad delectable dishes.
Huilu: Legend has it that by the time they made it back to the People of the Springs, Akamai was as portly as yours truly.
Huilu: But that's all by the by. The crux of this preamble is that the duo encountered many wonderful tales on their travels.
Huilu: #Traveler, if your ears are ready to listen, I'd be more than happy to share all these happenings with you!
Huilu: I assure you, you won't regret taking the time to stop and listen!
