## Talk 3

Traveler: Considering the muddled history of the desert, that's hardly a surprise.

Makani Khagan: To think that such a glorious reign was but a passing mist, forgotten by the world... unlike those frail lords of Tulaytullah, who have poets to sing their praises even now.
