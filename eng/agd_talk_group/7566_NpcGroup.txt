# Talk Group: Lugovskoy (NpcGroup 7566)

## Talk 0

Lugovskoy: Careful, now... Come on over slowly. Wouldn't want to upset my friend over here...

## Talk 1

Traveler: Did you tame this bear?
Lugovskoy: Ah... The bond between caregiver and creature isn't that simple, little one. In the eyes of many, I am the one who raises this beast...
Lugovskoy: But perhaps to him, he is the one looking after me... After all, when dealing with scoundrels, he proves far more reliable than any hound.

## Talk 2

Traveler: So you are...
Lugovskoy: A hunter... Back then, I was tracking a bear deep in the woods. I fought bitterly to bring the beast down, but when I approached the body, I found she was a mother. And her cub...
Lugovskoy: Well, look at him now. He was just a tiny thing back then.
Lugovskoy: It's our tradition to bring him back. I took his mother's life, after all... So perhaps when I grow a bit older, when I'm too frail to even pull a trigger...
Lugovskoy: He will turn on me and devour me. Just like the old saying goes... "He who hunts the bear shall meet his end in its jaws."

## Talk 3

Traveler: See you later!
Lugovskoy: See you. Watch your step, though. You don't want to step on his paws...

## Talk 4

Lugovskoy: Hah, Ajax, back at last, eh? What say we go hunting together?

## Talk 5

Traveler: Next time, old fella.
Lugovskoy: Hmph, I knew it. Our Lord Harbinger's mighty busy, he is. Squeezing every last brain cell he was born with on the Tsaritsa's business, he is...
Lugovskoy: Too busy to be going out hunting amongst the white birches with this old man nowadays, he is...

## Talk 6

Traveler: You should get more rest...
Lugovskoy: Me? Please. Free time's all I've got nowadays, sitting in this chair.
Lugovskoy: I miss the days when I'd take you out bear hunting... Back then, my legs were quick, and my eyes were sharp.
Lugovskoy: *sigh* Ach, I've gotten old...

## Talk 7

Traveler: Next time for sure.
Lugovskoy: For sure, eh? Well, there we go. A deal it is. Remember to come back in one piece. There's an old codger waiting here to go hunting with you, alright?
