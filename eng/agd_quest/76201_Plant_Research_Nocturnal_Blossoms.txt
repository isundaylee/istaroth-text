## Talk 4

(Talk to Vesa)

Vesa: You're being mean, Metta! I just wanted you to have fun on your adventure... Why do you have to say things like that, hmph...
Metta: If you don't stop acting on every little whim, others are going to get frustrated with you too.
Vesa: I was not acting on a whim! I—I have my own plans too...
Metta: Wow, plans, huh?
Vesa: Ugh... you're dismissing me again. I'm not talking to you anymore! Even if you regret it later, it'll be too late!
Vesa: ...Ah, Linnea! Metta was being—
Black Screen Text: As Vesa fumbles through the story of what happened, it becomes clear that it was just a typical little quarrel among kids...
Metta: No matter how I look at it, I wasn't the one at fault, right?
Vesa: Then... I wasn't wrong either! Big Sis Linnea...
Linnea: Hmm... sometimes these things just happen. No one's really at fault, but things still don't turn out the way we want them to.
Linnea: Oh, it's like the story of the two sisters among the Frostmoon Scions that the Nocturnal Blossom represents.
Vesa: Huh? The story of two sisters?
Linnea: Yes. I've heard that there were once two sisters among the Frostmoon Scions. They were very different in personality, like the Nocturnal Blossom — one red, one blue.
Linnea: Both sisters did what they believed was right, but because of that, they went their separate ways.
Linnea: It's said the younger sister never forgave her elder sister for leaving her behind, even though the elder went on to save countless lives... In the end, up until they died, the two never saw each other again.
Metta: Oh... I didn't know the Frostmoon Scions had stories like that...
Vesa: Me neither...
Linnea: Hmm... I heard it from a traveling bard. Maybe there's some embellishment in it...
Metta: Ah, so it's a made-up story.
Linnea: Well, even if the story isn't entirely true, the lesson it carries is still sound, right?
Linnea: Neither of them thought they were in the wrong. Both were certain their own choice was the right one, but their choices meant they lost each other forever.
Linnea: If proving you're right means losing a friend... what would you choose?
Vesa: ...
Metta: ...
Metta: Ah... actually, a few spontaneous little detours on an adventure aren't so bad...
Vesa: ...Yeah! Come to think of it, the time Metta spends adventuring with me is precious. If I keep changing my mind, we might not get to enjoy anything at all...
Vesa: I... I'll plan things better next time!
Linnea: Hehe, I'm glad to see you two getting along again.
Metta: Next time Vesa and I go on an adventure, we'll bring any unusual stones we find back to Speranza.
Linnea: Sounds good! Thanks in advance, then.
