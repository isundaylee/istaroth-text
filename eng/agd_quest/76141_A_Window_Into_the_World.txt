# A Window Into the World


## Talk 1

Nicole: Welcome back.

Nicole: But it's not time for a drawn-out, deeply emotional, tearful goodbye just yet... After all, you haven't finished your tour of my little lodge.

Paimon: But Paimon could swear we've been in all the rooms already?

Nicole: Well, I never actually said there were no secret hidden rooms, did I? Us mages like our little fancies, you know — you should be used to this by now!

Nicole: This way, if you please.

## Talk 2

Nicole: You want to open another chest? I'm afraid you've already claimed everything I have on hand.

Nicole: If you really want more treasure, I should be able to scrape a few things together... How about you give me a few hundred years? Don't worry, you can wait that long if you try — you just have to believe in yourselves.

## Talk 3

Nicole: As one of the fixed observation points for changes in the world, I require it to have excellent visibility. Just as a sculptor needs a chisel, a writer needs both pen and ink...

Nicole: My achievements in those two fields of art may well be up for debate, but when it comes to my main line of work, I take things very seriously indeed.

Nicole: #The "fate" of Teyvat has been shaken more and more frequently as of late. I'm sure you've felt it too, Traveler.

Nicole: Sometimes I can't help but think, if I still had the form, power, and abilities I once possessed, I'd be able to do so much more than I can right now.

Nicole: But I soon shake those thoughts from my head. If things were how they were before, I'd have so many more restrictions placed on me by the heavens.

Nicole: The thought of having that many things I can't even talk about makes it seem like a terrible trade-off. I mean, how would I tell stories without the freedom to say what I want?

Nicole: That's why I've never once regretted the price I paid, or any of the choices I've taken.

Nicole: I wanted to become human, to live like a regular person... but such a life is a luxury. I can't forget that I am an angel.

Nicole: Even if I were stuck being an angel in silence, I'd still fulfill my duties — defending civilization, defending life...

Nicole: Oh dear, have I started talking too seriously again? Perhaps it's because of where we are...

Nicole: I'm actually quite happy with my angelic side. You could even say I'm proud of it.

Paimon: So you should be! You don't get involved much, but when you do, we all know we're in safe hands.

Traveler: You deserve to be proud.

Nicole: You really feel that way? Hehe, as long as you see me as someone to rely on, then I know I'm doing a fantastic job!

Nicole: It's getting late. Here, take my blessings and continue on your adventure.

Nicole: Never be afraid of losing your way, and never forget you have an angel watching over you.
