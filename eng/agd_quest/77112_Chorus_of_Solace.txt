Paimon: Nastya!
Nastya: Don't worry about me. Actually, I should say that I feel very good right now, because I saw Doctor Moreau...
Nastya: I saw him, and he even praised me. He said he could see that I am working really hard to get home with help from both of you.
Nastya: He said I was amazing, and that I could turn into part of the Perfect Tree too. And I... I could feel mama too... Even though her hug felt a bit different...
Nastya: But I know that mama was there.
Paimon: Huh? Doctor Moreau? Nastya's mom? But there's no one here except Nastya!
Nastya: You can't see them, Paimonka. You have to be family to feel them...
Nastya: Because... Ah, because... The Perfect Tree is a life that transcends all time and all life. It is... eternity.
Nastya: Thank you. *Nastya is no longer alone*... Nastya is "us." And now, *or perhaps then too*, Nastya has always been part of "us."
Nastya: It is only *at this very moment * that she has returned *to our embrace*. On the level of the eternal, all is connected. Thank you for your help, especially you...
Nastya: #*Descender*, the fourth, one half of the twins, *and now the one who stands unique in his position*.

Option 1:

> Traveler: You are not Nastya, are you?

Option 2:

> Traveler: ...What are you?

Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): *We* have had countless names, though words *like "before" and "past"* no longer hold any meaning for us. But I am the thing you seek *or pursue*. The thing. *That person*.
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): For your ease of understanding, you may call me Koshchei *or Doctor Moreau*. There is no real difference between the two. *Especially now, less than ever.*
Paimon: Oh, so you're the baddie in this story!
Paimon: We want Goggles' power back! And Nastya, too! You're not allowed to use Nastya's body like that!
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): ...The Eye of Graeae, or "Primordial Human." The return of its power *was a thing that was bound to happen eventually*. But fate will take a turn. I, *or we*, had originally thought...
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): That the one who would appear here would be someone else — Aksinya. But now, you have been chosen to take her place and do what she was meant to do.
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): Take it. *The ritual is already complete*. I, *or we*, have no use for that power anymore, for we are already eternal. *We are a life that is, in concept, infinitely close to eternity*.
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): Even this brief moment of speech between us will not matter. Across all eternity, all words will lose their meaning. *Everything will lose its meaning*.
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): Ah, but... O everlasting dream, everlasting dream. A courtyard devoid of meaning and therefore entirely without suffering... For what reason did I seek eternity? Was it for... for...
Traveler: You have forgotten what you wanted.
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): ...Because wanting has lost all meaning.
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): O Eternity, that cold and merciless far shore, where even the fiercest love cannot reach.
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): I, *we*, shall return that power.
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): But... all of this shall still end in a way no one wishes to see...
Eye of Graeae: What do you mean?
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): You shall come to understand. At the very end, *in the very beginning*, it was your design, your choice. You chose the Descender. Is this be your final struggle, *or merely a coincidence of fate*...?
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): Such brief suffering. I shall return, return to among "us." Nastya, you still have a little time before you become "us."
Nastya (Thing That Used to be Nastya): Go say goodbye to them. Though in the end, *or at the end of time*, after annihilation, we shall all become the same thing in any case.
Nastya: Um... Okay. I'll take a minute to do that.
Paimon: Nastya?
Nastya: #Paimonka, Big Brother Traveler, thank you for bringing me home.
Nastya: Now I can become part of the Perfect Tree. I can be just like everybody else, and be with my mama again...
Paimon: But Nastya, if you turn into the Perfect Tree, we won't be able to play together anymore. Or, uh, run around in the woods, or eat yummy food together.
Nastya: That's okay, Paimonka. It'd be so awesome if I could do all that, yes, but there's something more important I have to do.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Is this... really what you want?

Option 2:

> Traveler: All you have to do is say no...

Paimon: That's right, Nastya. There are so, so many fun things to do outside, and so many wonderful things to see too. Just come with us.
Paimon: And, and, that ritual thing Koshchei set up... Goggles! Goggles must have a way to fix it, right?
Eye of Graeae: Yes... The ritual can be dismantled. While what has already been changed cannot be reversed, once the ritual stops, life's form will not continue to transform.
Paimon: #Nastya, you do know that you can still live a wonderful life without turning into the Perfect Tree, right...? And you can leave the rest to Traveler and me. We can keep you safe!
Nastya: #Yeah... I know all that, Paimonka. I know what you and Big brother Traveler can do for me.
Nastya: Aunt LePuy, "No-Nose" Ove, and Furiosa, they have all told me about the world outside... It sounds so interesting that I want to go on adventures out there too.
Nastya: But what made me happiest was coming home every day and telling Mama, who lay in bed, about the day I had... About how I played with my friends, and all the fun stories I heard...
Nastya: And when I talked, mama always looked at me with those eyes that made me feel all warm and nice inside.
Nastya: I just want to see those eyes just one more time...
Paimon: But...
Traveler: ...Nastya has made her choice.
Nastya: Heh, I know. Sometimes, when the two of you look at me, it makes me feel all nice and warm inside too.
Nastya: I'm sure there are lots of people in the outside world with the same kind of eyes. But mama is mama...
Nastya: I'm so happy I met the two of you before I turn into the Perfect Tree. When I see Mama, I'll definitely tell her about you.
Paimon: Okay then... Goodbye, Nastya. Goodbye!
Nastya: Goodbye! Don't worry about me, Paimonka. We'll see each other again in the world beyond eternity!
Black Screen Text: From the girl's waving hand comes a light, and a disintegration, that then spread to the rest of her being.
The light grows, then dims, transforming into that glowing blue algae you have seen earlier along the way.
Her body is changing faster than expected. That brief confusion in her eyes turns into joy.
Black Screen Text: The girl who is now glowing from top to toe appears to be taking a step toward you... Or perhaps that was just a momentary loss of balance on her part.
Her body falls to the ground in an almost weightless manner, and by the time she touches the ground, she has already become blue algae.
...And it looks just like all that blue algae you have seen along the way here.
Paimon: Nastya...
Eye of Graeae: This is the final form of the Perfect Tree... The eternal form of life Koshchei seeks to create.
Eye of Graeae: It cannot move, cannot speak, and even its very form of life has been transformed, stripping everything of meaning. All that remains is eternity, and a life that no longer knows either joy or love...
Eye of Graeae: Ah, I sense that all the power from the grand ritual in this area has been recovered... and it is now in that enormous egg. The egg that is... a part of my body.
