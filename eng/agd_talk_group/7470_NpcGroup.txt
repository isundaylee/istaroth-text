# Talk Group: Vedomek (NpcGroup 7470)

## Talk 0

Vedomek: This is a really good book... What a shame...

## Talk 1

Traveler: What's a shame?
Vedomek: The book itself is excellent. Once you start reading, it draws you in completely. If I were at home, that would be no problem, but unfortunately, this is a train station...
Vedomek: To finish this chapter, I have already missed two trains I was supposed to take.

## Talk 2

Traveler: I'll leave you to your book, then.
Vedomek: No trouble, no trouble... Now, let me see. Where was I again...

## Talk 3

Traveler: Maybe you should stop reading for now!
Vedomek: *sigh* You do have a fair point, but when a good book is right there in your hands, it's really hard not to read it.
Vedomek: Ok, one more chapter. I will read just one more chapter, then wait for the train in peace.

## Talk 4

Traveler: What kind of book is that good?
Vedomek: It's a mystery novel about the grudges, passions, and tangled history between the descendants of ancient elves and the founding leader of the Luchkin gang.
Vedomek: The plot is thrilling, the descriptions vivid. It's simply impossible to put down.

## Talk 5

Traveler: Maybe try a collection of love poems instead...
Vedomek: Ah, what is there to read in love poems? No, no, not for me.
