## Talk 1

(Head to the designated location)

Shuvalov: Hello, everyone. It is my great pleasure to have you here for Theater Open Day! Please join me in giving a warm welcome to the prima ballerina of the Korolevskiy Troupe, Odette!
Paimon (Audience Members): (Applause)
Odette: Hello.
Paimon: Whoa, she's not smiling, even at a time like this.
Passionate Fan: Ah, but you see, that's where Miss Odette's charm lies!
Shuvalov: Thank you for that warm reception. Now then, straight to business...
Shuvalov: Let's talk about the upcoming ballet I'm sure we're all eagerly anticipating — the "Black Snow Swan"!
Shuvalov: It is, as you may know, an adaptation of a literary classic.
Shuvalov: Through the story of an extraordinary black snow swan, the ballet explores the relationship between the individual and the collective as well as the eternal question of fate.
Shuvalov: I'd like to take this opportunity to ask you, Miss Odette, on behalf of our audience... What are your thoughts on this work?
Odette: It's a fantastic work. Interpreting it as a ballet has proved difficult...
Odette: ...But it's worth it.
Shuvalov: A most concise insight, Miss Odette.
Shuvalov: I'm sure everyone's curious about the effort that lies behind this seemingly understated conclusion. Could you share a bit more?
Odette: ...
Odette: Everyone in the company gives their all to each performance. I'm no more hardworking than them.
Odette: But the Black Snow Swan does hold a special meaning to me personally, so I will make sure to do my very best.
Shuvalov: Oh, splendid! We'll leave the remaining time for questions from the audience...
Shuvalov: The lady in blue over there — your question, please.
Professional Fan: I heard you added a lot of difficult steps to the choreography, and even intend to challenge the record for the most consecutive fouettes performed onstage... Is that true?
Odette: Yes.
Odette: I believe it's well within my current capabilities.
Stern-Faced Fan: What Snezhnaya needs is revitalization. Instead of promoting these performances, shouldn't celebrities lead by example and do some actual work instead?
Paimon: Huh? What kind of question is that...? Is he trying to pick a fight?
Odette: As a prima ballerina, I respect our artistic traditions, and I use ballet to express my love and care for our land.
Odette: I also hope those who watch my performances can find the strength to defy fate through art. That includes you, sir.
Stern-Faced Fan: ...
Delighted Fan: Miss Odette! What are the key characteristics of the Black Snow Swan to you?
Odette: Solitude, nobility... Even at the cost of her own life, she refuses to bow down to fate.
Shuvalov: Thank you, Miss Odette. Are there any other questions from the audience?
Passionate Fan: Miss Odette! How many hours a day do you spend training, and what does that entail?
Gentle Fan: Miss Odette! What kind of music do you listen to during dance practice?
Black Screen Text: The passionate questions continue, causing the event to end thirty minutes past schedule.
Odette: Phew...
Paimon: Something wrong, Odette? Paimon thought your answers were great. Your fans are so passionate!
Odette: It was tiring, but...
Paimon: But?
Odette: Seeing how excited and passionate they all were, even after the event ended... I'm even more determined to make this performance a good one.
Traveler: (Looks like she really meant what she said at the meet-and-greet...)
Paimon: Well, we believe in you! We're done for the day, so you should head back early and get some rest!
Odette: No need. I'm going to head to the studio to do some extra practice. I'll leave once I'm tired.
