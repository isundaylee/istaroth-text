# Talk Group: Leanid (NpcGroup 7770)

## Talk 0

Marat: My dear friend, there is nothing wrong with your performance. If anything, the scene itself has drifted away from the feeling the author was trying to create. I would revise it like this...
Marat: "The ghosts close their hands around the faithless man's throat and drag him toward death. Then, at that very moment, the woman he betrayed appears before him — sword in hand."
Leanid: Ah, now that is a clever turn... Before, all the woman could do was weep and mourn. Her love felt too delicate, too helpless. But now? Now she stands like a warrior. So, has she come to save the very man who betrayed her?
Leanid: "'Even if you do not love me, I still love you.' The woman raises her sword high. This is her vow and her declaration of war against the vengeful ghosts."
Leanid: Ah, now that is love in its purest form! My dear friend, I'll make sure the playwright hears your idea.
Leanid: And I, the faithless man she comes to save, will be moved by the sincerity of her heart. Her love will give me the strength to tear myself free from the ghosts' clutches, and become, at long last, a man who can shoulder his responsibilities.
Marat: Hmm... Yes, that is moving. But perhaps there is an even finer way to play it.
Marat: "The woman raises her sword high. The faithless man believes she has come to grant him salvation. Hope and gratitude begin to shine in his eyes."
Marat: "'My dear,' he says, 'I can feel your love at last.' The woman smiles beautifully, as if she has only just discovered what love tastes like."
Marat: "And then, by her own hand, she kills him. Her revenge was never something she needed to leave to the ghosts."
Leanid: Hmm... I'll have to think that one over. After all, my dear friend, I am the one playing the faithless man here. With every new idea, his fate grows a little more tragic.
Marat: Haha, no need to get that deep into character... The more tragic he is, the more sympathy he'll get. With a storyline like that, people will remember your name in no time!
