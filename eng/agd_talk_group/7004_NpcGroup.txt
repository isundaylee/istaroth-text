# Talk Group: Limppu (NpcGroup 7004)

## Talk 0

Limppu: Hey there, want some pastries? Our shredded carrot-stuffed ones are the most popular, and our meat-stuffed ones are great too!

## Talk 1

Traveler: Let me see what you've got...
Limppu: Sure thing! Have a gander!

## Talk 2

Traveler: Wow, are they that popular?
Limppu: Hehe, don't just take my word for it! Just ask any of the kids around here, they can't keep our pastries — or praise for them — out of their mouths!
Limppu: But the treasure hunters are the ones who buy the most. I mean, you can tell why, right? They're nice and big, fill your belly, and don't put a scratch in your Mora pouch. Just buy a bag of them and pop two in your mouth when you're feeling peckish.
Limppu: All in all, those who don't have the time to sit down for a full meal at Speranza come straight to us.

## Talk 3

Traveler: I'm fine at the moment, thanks.
Limppu: Sure thing! If you ever change your mind, you know where you can get Nod-Krai's most value-for-Mora food!

## Talk 4

Traveler: And if you have time to go to Speranza...?
Limppu: Well, if you've got a moment and the appetite, Speranza's meals are great. I ain't ashamed to admit my pastries can't measure up to a full dinner from that restaurant, haha.
Limppu: I mean, if I wasn't running this stall, and I had some time to kill, I'd head over there too!
