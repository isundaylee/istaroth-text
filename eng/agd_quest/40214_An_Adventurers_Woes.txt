# An Adventurer's Woes

To provide assistance to Nod-Krai's adventurers, the Adventurers' Guild has also placed an order with Aino...


## Talk 1

(Talk to Sarin)

Sarin: My name is Sarin, and I'm the Branch Master of Nod-Krai's Adventurers' Guild. Just think about your reputation in the Guild... If I didn't know you, it would be a serious dereliction of my duties, wouldn't it?
Paimon: Huh, that makes sense!
Aino: Ah, so you know each other! But lemme just confirm, which order's yours again?

Sarin: Well... You know how it is. An adventurer's biggest obstacle out in the wild are the various enemies that stand in their way.
Sarin: In other regions, the Guild can collaborate with local authorities to provide some protection for adventurers. But in a place like Nod-Krai, we can only rely on ourselves.
Sarin: So recently, I've come up with an idea: To design a self-defense device that any adventurer can carry around. That way, even less-skilled adventurers can fend off powerful foes!
Sarin: But... Haha, well, let's just say my attempt ended in failure. And since I lack the talent to get the job done, I may as well hire a professional to help me out.
Aino: Oh yeah, that one! It's a good idea, but the design? Yeesh...
Sarin: And that is why we have asked you, Aino... Ah, I mean Miss Aino, for her invaluable assistance!
Aino: The hard part about this one, Sarin, is that the device you've designed uses tech from elsewhere... It can't adapt to Nod-Krai's kuuvahki-rich environments, y'see.
Aino: I mean, what we need to do isn't complicated. We just need to use his device, find some bad guys, beat them up for testing, then tweak the design as needed.
Aino: And when testing, I'll be able to gather some data about kuuvahki-rich environments! So it's a win-win order.
Aino: Buuut, I'm not helping out with the fighting. If the device breaks down when you're surrounded, you'll only have your fists to rely on!
