Roxane: It's part of my job. Don't be nervous.

Roxane: A lot of people say it leaves a buzzing between their ears, but once you get used to it, I think it's exciting. It's like... the metallic clang of a hammer shaping iron. There's something uniquely pleasing about it.
Roxane: Turenna gave me earplugs to wear, but I never use them.
Traveler: Was your life not quiet before?
Roxane: I'd say it was very fulfilling!

Traveler: It's good to relax, just make sure you don't neglect your work.
