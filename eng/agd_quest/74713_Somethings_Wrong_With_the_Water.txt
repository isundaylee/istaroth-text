# Something's Wrong With the Water

Freminet seems to have something to tell us...


## Talk 1

(Talk to Freminet)

Freminet: #Traveler, Paimon, it's been such a long time...
Paimon: Freminet? Why are you wearing your diving helmet?
Traveler: Have you just come out of the water?
Freminet: Mmhmm... Things down there have been a bit hectic lately.
Freminet: There are so many more divers right now, all in a frenzy to salvage sunken cargo. All I can do is try my best to lend a hand...
Freminet: The sooner we finish cleaning up, the sooner these extra divers can go back to their day jobs, and the sooner the seas can go back to their usual peace and quiet.
Paimon: I see... It must be really hard for you to adapt to all the commotion, huh?
Freminet: Thanks for your concern, Paimon. It's not all that bad, though. I've actually made a few extra finds — things like unclaimed jewelry and antiques...
Freminet: "Father" said that if I salvage any pretty or valuable things, I should bring them here. She said you'd be able to find a use for them.
Paimon: Huh. Arlecchino sure is thoughtful! If you've got any pretty-looking ornaments that nobody wants anymore, we could use them to decorate the aquarium.
Paimon: Or even better, why don't we go salvaging with you?
Freminet: Are you sure? I wouldn't want to waste your precious time...
Paimon: Don't you worry about that! You already said we can keep any ownerless goodies you find.
Paimon: But how could we let you do all the work yourself? Taking the fruits of someone else's labor while we sit here twiddling our thumbs doesn't sit well with Paimon!

Option 1:

> Traveler: Besides, finding things yourself is half the fun!

Option 2:

> Traveler: We like hunting for underwater treasure, too!

Freminet: In that case... I'll share everything the other divers have told me. Hopefully, that'll help narrow down your search...
Freminet: If you find anything that takes your fancy, feel free to make good use of it... However, some things are wanted by their owners, so if you find one of those, you'll need to return it.
Paimon: Understood!
Freminet: Please stay safe, and don't stray into unfamiliar waters. If you run into danger, make sure to surface as quickly as it's safe to do so.
