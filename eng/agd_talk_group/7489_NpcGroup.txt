# Talk Group: Aryna (NpcGroup 7489)

## Talk 0

Traveler: What's happened?
Gerard: You're not going to believe this, my friend. I'm just visiting from Fontaine, and for some unknown reason they pulled me aside for a security check. Apparently, it's "for the safety and security of Snezhnaya"!
Gerard: So naturally, I asked, "Tell me, what is it about me that could possibly be such a threat to Snezhnaya?"
Gerard: And they told me, "You have a bag. We need to confirm there are no weapons, Kristall, foreign organisms, or any other suspicious items inside."
Gerard: So I said, "What about that Snezhnayan woman who just dashed right past? She looked all kinds of jumpy, and her bag is way bigger than mine! Why didn't you stop her for a check???"
Gerard: And all they said was, "If you have any questions about our screening criteria, please feel free to direct them to the Transport Bureau."

## Talk 1

Aryna: What's the matter, dear? Do you need help with the train schedules, a lost item, or are you here to post a missing-person notice? There's no need to rush. Take a deep breath first, then tell me what you need.

## Talk 2

Traveler: About the train schedules...
Aryna: Of course, dear. A traveler's train time is no small thing. Rather like the weather in Snezhnaya, I'd say. Everyone keeps an eye on it all the time.
Aryna: Now, let me think... Oh, yes. You should talk to Voskresensky at the ticket window. He handles the schedules, so he should be able to answer any questions you have.

## Talk 3

Traveler: About a lost item...
Aryna: Oh no, dear... something's gone missing, hasn't it? You must be worried sick. I understand, truly.
Aryna: But take a deep breath first, dear. Let's think this through... Hmm. First, are you certain you lost it here at the station?
Aryna: Do you want to look through your things one more time, just to be sure? And if you still can't find it, try asking Chagina out on the platform. I'm sure she'll help you find it.

## Talk 4

Traveler: About posting a missing-person notice...
Aryna: Oh dear, did you lose your companion? That must be so worrying...
Aryna: Let's see what we can do... Hmm, we could post a notice, yes, but sitting here and waiting may not be the best use of your time. Why not go search for them yourself? Better to hold the reins in your own hands than leave your heart at the mercy of chance, wouldn't you say?
Aryna: So, dear, listen to me — why don't you ask the attendants on the platform? I'm sure they'll be glad to help you look. I think Iliana is on duty today... or is it Sochikov?
Aryna: Well, either way, they'll be in uniform. So go on, dear. Take a look on the platform. I promise you'll spot them right away.
