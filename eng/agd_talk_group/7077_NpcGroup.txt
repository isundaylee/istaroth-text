# Talk Group: Hjerson (NpcGroup 7077)

## Talk 0

Hjerson: If I'm not tailing some husband who's having an affair, I'm looking for earrings that someone lost on the road... If this is all a detective does, I'd be better off changing careers...

## Talk 1

Traveler: Is work not going so well?
Hjerson: Real gift for understatement you've got. There's so little of it going around that "work" is turning into an occasional pastime.
Hjerson: *sigh* Around here, even if a big case does occur, people would rather just round up all the suspects and "interrogate" them with the butt of a weapon than get a real detective to investigate.
Hjerson: You'd be amazed how many criminals they end up catching — a decent chunk more than the number of people who actually commit any crimes, I'd wager.

## Talk 2

Traveler: I take it you haven't gotten any big cases lately?
Hjerson: Well, I mean, I guess, if you count... Not long ago, I helped a merchant from Sumeru find their lost Sumpter Beast. It fell asleep curled up down an alleyway.
Hjerson: The Sumpter Beast was pretty big... So, I guess you could say that means the case was big too?

## Talk 3

Traveler: Why do you want to change careers?
Hjerson: Detectives aren't very popular in Nod-Krai. The only cases I get are for very minor problems.
Hjerson: I often dream of cracking a really big case — pinpointing the one tiny hole in an otherwise airtight act of criminality... But now I'm starting to think that instead of dreaming about it, I should be writing it all down and turning it into a detective novel!
Hjerson: At least in Nod-Krai, being a crime novelist sounds a lot more appealing than being a detective.
Hjerson: In fact, I already have some ideas. You look like you've seen a few things. Why not be my first test audience?

## Talk 4

Traveler: Goodbye, and good luck.
Hjerson: Goodbye, and good luck to you, too! That was the most touching blessing I've heard in quite some time. *sigh* I do hope that things start going well...

## Talk 5

Traveler: Sorry, I'm more of a non-fiction fan...
Hjerson: Really? Well, that's a shame...

## Talk 6

Traveler: Sure thing. I'm all ears.
Hjerson: Alright, first up is "The Rifthound of the Bashkirovs."
Hjerson: The central conceit is this — a family who raises Rifthounds uses them to open rifts to a secret room where they can carry out murders without leaving a trace!

## Talk 7

Traveler: Why not just let the Rifthounds do the murdering?
Hjerson: Erm... That's a good question... Maybe I was too focused on explaining how the family could open the secret room... Alright, how about this one — "The Bakufu Food Taster Murders"
Hjerson: A food taster for the Yashiro Commission uses their position to put poison in some Tricolor Dango personally prepared by the Shogun in an attempt to assassinate the head of the Commission...

## Talk 8

Traveler: Maybe we can skip this one...
Hjerson: Really? Hmm... Is the concept not exciting enough? I could always refine it a little...

## Talk 9

Traveler: I don't think they'd be able to taste the food...
Hjerson: Oh? I think I see what you're getting at... There's no way a food taster would be allowed to touch something made by the Shogun themselves, as that would be seen as disrespecting the latter, correct? I guess I need to brush up on Inazuman culture.
Hjerson: I'm amazed how much you know about these things! Alright, this one's a surefire hit — "The Curious Case of the Liyue Bulle Fruit"!

## Talk 10

Traveler: Bulle Fruit don't grow in Liyue...
Hjerson: Oh, really? I always thought Bulle Fruit were a Liyue specialty...
Hjerson: *sigh* I thought my fantasies would make for exciting detective novels, but it looks like becoming a crime writer isn't any easier than being an actual detective...

## Talk 11

Traveler: Maybe we can skip this one...
Hjerson: Really? *sigh* It looks like becoming a crime writer isn't any easier than being an actual detective...
