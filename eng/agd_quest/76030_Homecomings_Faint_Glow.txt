# Homecoming's Faint Glow

Paimon: You mean... the kuuhenki?
The Damselette: Yes. These stones emit a faint light upon absorbing kuuvahki. The kuuhenki are quite fond of them.
Paimon: So they just... put them on the ground and play with them?
The Damselette: Not exactly. Come over here and look.
Paimon: Huh...?
Paimon: Ooh! It forms a picture when you look at it from above!
Traveler: Looks like we're a chunk short, though...

The Damselette: Sure.
Paimon: Alright, then let's get collecting! We wanna see what the finished picture looks like, too!
