# Talk Dialog

Consigliere's Ex-Left Hand: As the Demoiselle's partner and esteemed benefactor of the Spina di Rosula, you, too, are my superior. Please, what would you have me do?
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Just make sure you do a good job.
        Consigliere's Ex-Left Hand: That is a matter of course. I will treat every animal in this manor with the same care and attention as I did when serving Monsieur Consigliere.
        Consigliere's Ex-Left Hand: I merely thought that you might have some additional requirements. For example... That I assist our guests in conversing with the animals, so that they may better understand their viewpoints...
        Consigliere's Ex-Left Hand: Though I may be busy, if you were to have such a requirement, I have... brothers I can call. You wouldn't need to pay another Mora more.
    Option 2:
        Traveler: What wonderful attire!
        Consigliere's Ex-Left Hand: Why, thank you for the compliment!
        Consigliere's Ex-Left Hand: You have a certain coldness to you, you stand tall and straight — perhaps you, too, might suit... the more solemn attire that we favor in the Spina di Rosula.
        Consigliere's Ex-Left Hand: Were you to dress all in black, with a pair of dark glasses, and conspicuously display your formidable approach throughout the land... I'm sure you could gather your own group of loyal disciples.
    Option 3:
        Traveler: I'll be going. Stay focused on the job at hand.
        Consigliere's Ex-Left Hand: As you ordain. I won't take up any more of your time!
