## Talk 1 (variant 1)

Traveler: (Perhaps *Lauma* might like to hear about the Feast of the Moon...)
Lyylikki: Hello, would you like to— Whoa! Lady Lauma, what brings you here?
Lauma: Thank you for your work, Lyylikki. Has the distribution of the "Feast of the Moon" gone well today?
Lyylikki: Yes, well indeed! Both cooking and handing out the meals went just perfectly!
Lyylikki: We haven't had any more troublemakers or line-cutters since Miss Jahoda came to help out for a few days. Everyone lines up nicely for their meals now!
Lyylikki: Before that, there was a big guy who kept causing problems — cutting the queue and grabbing multiple portions... Poor Mr. Kantor tried to stop him, and was hit a few times for his trouble...
Lyylikki: That day, Miss Jahoda happened to be there getting her meal. When she saw that thug causing trouble, she jumped up and kicked him so hard he fell!
Lyylikki: Having knocked him flat, she even caught the unopened meal midair — not a thing was spilled! Then she pointed at the man lying on the ground and said...
Lyylikki: "If you have no respect for your benefactors or your food, then you can go hungry today. Now outta the way — or was one kick not enough for you?"
Lyylikki: After that, she helped me distribute meals for many days... I made her lots of delicious food as thanks for that.
Lauma: An act that deserves gratitude indeed. Perhaps... I really should hire a guard to assist you, like I mentioned before.
Lyylikki: Eh? No, there's no need for that! We're already short on manpower. We should just open another distribution point instead.
Lyylikki: Besides, things are really well organized nowadays. Miss Jahoda still stops by from time to time, and between her and Mr. Kantor, I doubt anyone would stir up further trouble.
Lauma: Let us hope so. In that case, I'll have some friends take turns coming over. If you need help, just tell them.
Lyylikki: Friends...? They live on the island too, I assume. Do I know them?
Lauma: Hehe, I am sure you do.
Lauma: Well then, I shall leave you to you work. See you again, Lyylikki. May the Moon Goddess guard your path.
Lyylikki: Goodbye, Lady Lauma!
