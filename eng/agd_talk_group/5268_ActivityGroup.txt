## Talk 0

Vigee: Hey there, fellow traveler! Want to chat with me while you're enjoying your game?

Traveler: About this "interactive woven scroll"...

Vigee: Oooh, are you interested in this sort of emerging technology as well?

Vigee: In truth, many artists and filmmakers are researching similar works. Their only goal: to breathe life into these still drawings...

Vigee: What makes mine special is that they're inspired by woven scroll comics, a popular medium that's widely known throughout all of Natlan.

Vigee: Natlanese woven scroll comics are, in themselves, the product of combining local legends with Inazuman comics. So you could say my creation is inspired by three nations' cultures...

Vigee: I really hope it can bring smiles to people's faces!

Traveler: Tell me a bit about yourself...

Vigee: Me? Well, I'm flattered that you want to know more about me... I'll start with my experiences.

Vigee: In truth, my main profession is that of a painter. I often painted portraits of noblewomen when I was back in Fontaine, and my family bragged to be part of "high society."

Vigee: But after visiting the Fleuve Cendre and the countryside to collect materials, I got to meet many ordinary people who I found to be passionate and sincere.

Vigee: Unlike so-called "high society," which is riddled with unspoken rules, these people had an air about them that was easygoing and genuine.

Vigee: I believe that creating something for the everyman to enjoy has its own kind of unique significance. That's why I wanted to step out of my comfort zone and try new methods of creation.

Vigee: And this interactive woven scroll is one of these new methods! Moving forward, I'll be studying different forms of art in other nations.

Traveler: I'll leave you to it, then.

Vigee: Sure thing! I'll be here with the children if you have any questions. Come back anytime!
