        Ilmari: I heard talk at the tavern, too, from sailors who escaped their sinking ship. Said it happened on a moonless night, yet something was glowing among the towering waves.
        Ilmari: They swore it was one-eyed Kratti, a monster that could swallow the moon. It was fighting a giant sea serpent for the right to rule its territory.
        Ilmari: For a long time after that, this whole stretch of ocean stayed as dark as ink. No ships dared pass through — they all feared the lost souls beneath the waves would drag them down.
        Ilmari: People claim those lost souls still wander nearby, but I say it's all just stories meant to frighten kids. I don't buy that nonsense.
    Option 3:
        Traveler: About this tree...
        Ilmari: It's cursed. There used to be a whirlpool here — the sailors called it the Eye of Kratti. Then one day, this tree just started growing right out of it.
        Ilmari: And that wasn't the only strange thing that happened around that time. Tracing it back, it all started with those rocks falling from the sky... Who knows? Maybe this tree's from the same place.
        Ilmari: Then there are those little shiny things that appear every now and then, rare as you like, those are. I figured there might be new kinds of fish, too. That's why I came out here — to try my luck.
        Ilmari: I used to have a fishing buddy who came out here, too, but he said he kept hearing strange singing. It spooked him so bad, he never came back. Hah, only half a Frostmoon Scion that one, but he inherited every one of their odd little habits.
