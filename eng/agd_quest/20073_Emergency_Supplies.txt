# Emergency Supplies


## Talk 1

Unknown Role (None): Traveler! Wonderful timing. Could you do me a favor?

Unknown Role (None): What do you need?

Unknown Role (None): As you've no doubt seen, the conditions up here are horrendous. Even experienced adventurers would not dare to claim that they can handle all that Dragonspine can throw at them.

Unknown Role (None): I have orders from the Acting Grand Master to stand guard here and warn all citizens not to go up the mountain.

Unknown Role (None): However, while I have been able to persuade many citizens not to do so, those adventurers...

Unknown Role (None): I'm sure you know what I mean when I say that there's no use trying to convince them. They just want to go up that mountain.

Unknown Role (None): I've lost count of the number of matters I've had to resolve involving the adventurers... But they just keep coming anyway, and their ranks never thin.

Unknown Role (None): Trying to manage them all is an impossible task... As such, I've decided to prepare some supplies for them in designated spots on Dragonspine.

Unknown Role (None): In this way, those adventurers who have suffered a reverse and are preparing to turn back will have more opportunities to extricate themselves from the mountain.

Unknown Role (None): However, if some reckless fool drunk on their good fortune were to get too far up, then they'd only have themselves to rely on...

Unknown Role (None): So, could you help bring these supplies up the mountain?

Unknown Role (None): Leave it up to me.

Unknown Role (None): I shall be counting on you, then. *sigh* If only those adventurers had as good a sword-arm and as sensible a head on their shoulders as you do...

Unknown Role (None): Will they be of any use?

Unknown Role (None): Whether they will or not... It's better to have them than not.

Unknown Role (None): In such terrible conditions as the ones we have on this mountain, the more supplies we have, the better. All told, I'm counting on your help.

Unknown Role (None): I've marked three locations out on your map. You simply have to place *one serving of Ham* at each place.

Unknown Role (None): *sigh* I wish those adventurers had a little more self-awareness...

## Talk 2

Unknown Role (None): Have you placed all the supplies?

Unknown Role (None): They're all where they need to be.

Unknown Role (None): That's wonderful. I can rest easy now.

Unknown Role (None): Still, I hope that those who go up the mountain will come sufficiently prepared and that they shan't have to use these emergency foodstuffs.

Unknown Role (None): Come to think of it, your clothes seem a little thin, too. Is this the latest fashion in Mondstadt or something?

Unknown Role (None): Whatever the case, please take these as a token of my thanks, and go put on some extra layers.
