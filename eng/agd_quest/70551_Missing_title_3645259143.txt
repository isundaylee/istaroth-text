Emilie: The various parts of the Windwheel Aster plant can be ground into a powder to extract their essences. I then blended the scents from its petals and leaves. The result carries the intoxicating sweetness of mead, while still emphasizing the fresh notes contained within the flower's stems and leaves.

Emilie: I just took it for a little trial run at The Cat's Tail, and the cats seemed to really like it, while the clients themselves found it comforting and invigorating.
