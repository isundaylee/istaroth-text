# Just a 30% Cut!

Sandrone seems to be looking for us...


## Talk 1

(Talk to Sandrone)

Marionette: Hold up... these accounts... are you sure you haven't miscalculated?

Marionette: ...Have you really made this much profit?
Traveler: Having second thoughts about your 30% cut?

Marionette: Hmph. Maybe a little.
Paimon: Only a little?
Traveler: Must be because she settled for 30%...
Marionette: Ugh, I told you, I don't care about that!
