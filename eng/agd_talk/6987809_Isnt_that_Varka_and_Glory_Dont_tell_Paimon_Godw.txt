# Talk Dialog

Paimon: Isn't that Varka and Glory? Don't tell Paimon Godwin's run away again?

Varka: Miss Glory, in accordance with Godwin's personal wishes and the need to keep a small force stationed in Nod-Krai, I'm afraid he'll be away for some time longer.

Varka: The reason I've come to see you today is to inform you of the situation and to read this letter on Godwin's behalf.

Varka: Ahem... "My Lady Glory, at this very moment, I am writing to you over a glass of fine wine."

Glory: My Lady...?

Varka: "Although the Knights of Favonius's expedition has ended, my personal journey continues still..."

Paimon: Uhm... This letter from "Godwin"...

Traveler: Shh, let's see where this is going.

Varka: "...Therefore, I have decided to stand fast in Nod-Krai, and to continue fighting for the defense of my beloved Mondstadt!"

Varka: "I understand this decision may hurt you, and no apology can undo the pain I cause... But please, believe me when I say, my love runs deep and true."

Glory: ..."Deep and true"... Thank you, Grand Master Varka, for bringing me this letter. Godwin's resolve... I can feel it clearly.

Glory: Though being apart may be painful, if this is his decision, I'll wait as long as it takes... May the thousand winds protect you, Godwin...

Varka: Haha, don't worry... The situation there is safe now. I'm sure that Godwin will be back before you know it.

Varka: Well then, Miss Glory, I have other matters to attend to. Farewell for now.

Paimon: What's the deal with that letter? Did Godwin really go to Nod-Krai?

Varka: He did indeed... *sigh* Where should I start? After my little "refresher course," I gave him one day to make his final decision...

Godwin: Grand Master, I think... I think I've made up my mind... I can't keep lying to her...

Varka: Is that so? Good. I may have said that the Knights of Favonius do not interfere with personal matters — so long as the decisions you make do not impugn the honor of the Knights...

Varka: But I must say... Deep down, I was hoping this would be the path you chose. You don't look so good, though — rough night, huh?

Godwin: You can say that again... Even though I made my decision, I wasn't sure of the best way to tell her. In the end, I stayed up all night working on this letter.

Varka: Sure didn't skimp on the prep, huh? C'mon, let me take a look...

Varka: "I'm sorry... Glory... I must confess my sins..." Haha... Well, I guess that's passable.

Varka: "A hypocrite like myself deserves no forgiveness... much less the honor to remain by your side..." ...Huh? What's all this...?

Varka: "I don't deserve your forgiveness... Perhaps you deserve someone better..." What the heck is this nonsense!? Is this your attempt at taking responsibility?

Godwin: G—Grand Master! Please let me explain! I... I've carefully thought this through...

Godwin: If I tell her more lies — even if she never finds out the truth — I would only grow to despise myself more... Could someone like that truly make her happy?

Godwin: But if I confess everything... I'm sure she'll forgive me and accept me, as despicable as I've been... Because she's so gentle, so understanding...

Godwin: But I can't bring myself to accept her kindness in such a shameful way! I wouldn't be able to forgive myself!

Godwin: I've thought it all through — what decision to make, what the consequences will be, and... I think I could bear it all...

Godwin: But even then, no matter what I choose... I'd never be able to forgive myself! I—I... *sob*...

Varka: *sigh* In this state, even if I forced you to face her, I doubt it would do either of you much good.

Varka: ...After all these years of patrolling and fighting on the outskirts of Mondstadt, you've covered no less ground than the expedition itself. C'mon, how has your resolve gotten so weak?

Godwin: Perhaps... I was beyond saving from the moment I told that first lie to avoid the shame...

Varka: Hah... Well, if you can't forgive yourself for your lies, then why not turn them into truths? Embark on your own "expedition"!

Godwin: My own... expedition?

Varka: Go to Nod-Krai and temper your resolve. They're short-handed there at the moment anyway, so consider this your punishment for not returning to duty on time.

Godwin: Nod-Krai... the expedition's final destination?

Varka: Go and learn from the expedition forces there what it truly means to be an upstanding knight! Naturally, before your character-building begins, you'll need to resolve your affairs in Mondstadt first.

Varka: Go fetch a pen and paper and write a new letter. This time, I'll dictate, and you transcribe. I don't wanna read any more of this spineless drivel.

Varka: Ahem... "My Lady Glory, at this very moment, I am writing to you over a glass of fine wine."

Godwin: Uhm... Grand Master, we don't usually address each other like this... And if I'm going to write about wine... Uhm... Well... How about, "The scent of wine on the wind reminds me of you, no matter how far apart—"

Varka: There's no need for you to add anything — just write exactly what I say! Now, "Although the Knights of Favonius's expedition has ended, my personal journey continues still..."

Godwin: ...Uhm... A—Alright... I've written so many letters, maybe it's time I took notes from someone else...

Varka: "...to continue fighting for the defense of my beloved Mondstadt!" Haha, I really outdid myself with that ending!

Godwin: Y—Yes, indeed! Haha... B—But... Uhm... I still want to apologize to Glory, at least in some way.

Varka: Huh? Alright, let me think... You could add a postscript...

Paimon: Paimon knew it! Something felt off about that whole letter — except the last bit... But isn't this just a different kind of lie?

Varka: Hmm? You think so? The letter genuinely was "written" by Godwin himself, and he really has gone to Nod-Krai now. As for getting separated from the expedition... Well, the letter didn't mention anything about that.

Paimon: Ugh! So Paimon guesses you didn't "technically" lie...

Varka: Besides, if Godwin were to face Glory now, it would leave an indelible mark on both their hearts... They'd never be able to truly move on.

Varka: He made an undeniable mistake, one for which he must make amends through his actions — both for the Knights of Favonius and for Glory.

Varka: Godwin has promised me that once his "expedition" is over, he will personally confess everything to Glory.

Varka: ...As for me, I pray he won't waste this opportunity to become a true knight once again. But that's an understanding we have as fellow Knights of Favonius.

Paimon: *sigh* Well, at least he's moving in the right direction...

Traveler: (Though... if even we can tell something's off with that letter, surely Glory will know exactly who wrote each line too...)

Glory: ...Don't forget to keep your promise, Godwin.
