# Talk Group: Eteri (NpcGroup 7772)

## Talk 0

Eteri: *sigh* If only I'd been born in a romantic, noble time like that...

## Talk 1

Traveler: What time?
Eteri: Hmph... What other time could I mean? The romantic time of His Majesty the Belyi Tsar, naturally.
Eteri: I, Eteri Vsevolodovna Strivozha, descendant of Saint Strivozha, the Pure One, am duty-bound to remember that ancient glory!
Eteri: Snezhnaya was nothing like this back then... not dull and lifeless like it is now.
Eteri: A messenger of His Majesty the Belyi Tsar crossed whole continents, even the land of the Nether, all to meet the witch from the east who stole his heart at first sight...
Eteri: The Prinzessin der Verurteilung shattered the divine halberd forged by His Majesty himself, all for some nameless thief who had stolen a national treasure...
Eteri: And a knight from the kingdom at the heart of the earth went to face an evil dragon alone, just to win a smile from a golden-haired outlander...
Eteri: ...Ah, even the cold winds over the ice fields must have carried the fragrance of roses and the grace of proper etiquette! What an enchanting time that must have been...
