# A Little Raid


## Talk 1

Unknown Role (None): These Treasure Hoarders are running more and more rampant...

Unknown Role (None): You were looking for me?

Unknown Role (None): Ah, yes. I have a matter to entrust to you. This Bao'er you mentioned previously... After thorough investigation, I have ascertained that she is a Treasure Hoarder.

Unknown Role (None): As for the reason why she acted alone... Perhaps she wanted to take all the treasure for herself, or perhaps she was always meant to be a vanguard for the main group of Treasure Hoarders.

Unknown Role (None): Judging by how she was able to fool even such an experienced adventurer as yourself, she seems adept at subterfuge as well.

Unknown Role (None): If we leave her be, then we also leave the surrounding Treasure Hoarders to run amok. This will put the cultural relics nearby at risk.

Unknown Role (None): Therefore, I'd like you to stop them. Even if we cannot rein them in entirely, rescuing some relics from their clutches will serve.

Unknown Role (None): Understood.

Unknown Role (None): I'll leave it to you, then.

Unknown Role (None): How do you know where they are?

Unknown Role (None): If I'm not mistaken, this group of Treasure Hoarders has been following me.

Unknown Role (None): These greedy scoundrels haven't simply been content to have me lead them to some treasure.

Unknown Role (None): As long as it's someplace I've been, they will start digging into the ground, thinking to uncover a little something.

Unknown Role (None): I know where they'll show up, it just takes a bit of thought. But I'm just a scholar. Such matters of logic are simple for me...

Unknown Role (None): But combat is something that I'll have to leave to experienced adventurers like you.

Unknown Role (None): So please, take those relics back from the Treasure Hoarders!

Unknown Role (None): I'll clean them up.

Unknown Role (None): Please wait a moment. While I did say you should stop them, I don't mean for you to have a showdown with Bao'er right away.

Unknown Role (None): The Treasure Hoarders have a very loose organization, and it's hard to get ahold of their trail. Now that you've gotten tangled up with them, we have the chance of a lifetime.

Unknown Role (None): I believe that there's a bigger scheme behind their presence here in the Guili Plains. If you stay near Bao'er, you might be able to uncover those plans.

Unknown Role (None): So don't reveal yourself just yet. I trust in your ability to find a hidden point to strike, and to... well, end things quickly.

Unknown Role (None): Remember, get the relics and get out.
