# Moonlight Sonata


## Talk 1

The Damselette: Greetings.

Paimon: Huh? Are you heading out, Columbina?

The Damselette: Yes. I need to get a lyre repaired.

Paimon: ...What?

The Damselette: One of the kuuhenki's lyres is broken. She greatly values this lyre, so I wish to have it restored.

Paimon: Man, kuuhenki sure are multi-talented...

The Damselette: Of course. This little one often performs around here, and I sometimes sing along.

Paimon: Y'know, Paimon's been thinking this for a while, but you sure do spoil the kuuhenki.

The Damselette: Well, it's only right. They have kept me company all this time, and often bring me amusing objects as well.

Traveler: (Lauma told me about this before — the natural instinct of kuuhenki to make the Moon Goddesses happy.)

Paimon: If you don't mind Paimon asking, how do you plan to fix this lyre? Didn't you say you didn't want to be seen by others?

The Damselette: Hmm... In the past, small animals or kuuhenki would help deliver broken objects to an artisan's doorstep for me.

The Damselette: All I had to do was wait for the artisan to find it. Of course, sometimes, that would take a few days.

Paimon: Seems like a roundabout way of doing things...

Traveler: Why don't you let us handle it?

The Damselette: ...

Paimon: Yeah, just leave it to us. Who knows, we might even be able to get this done today.

The Damselette: ...Very well. Thank you.

Paimon: Our pleasure! By the way, what artisan do you usually bring your stuff to?

The Damselette: I don't know her name, but there's a large mechanical hand by her door, and she lives in a round, conch-like home.

Traveler: (Wait, she must mean...)

The Damselette: The aroma of krumkakes often wafts from within as well.

Paimon: Yep, that clears it up for sure...
