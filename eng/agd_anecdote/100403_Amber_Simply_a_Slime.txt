# Amber: Simply a Slime

The Outrider's work continues on the banks of Starfell Lake...

You bump into Amber as she is completing a mission on the banks of Starfell Lake. A measly slime is no match for an Outrider.

## Talk 0

Amber: Phew... And another one bites the dust. Just where did all these Slimes pop out from...
Traveler: Great shooting, Amber!
Amber: Oh, it was just a Slime! Every Knight can do that.
Amber: I don't know why, but their numbers out here in the wild have been skyrocketing recently. They've even started attacking travelers on the roads.
Amber: In order to ensure the safety of the area, both the Adventurers' Guild and the Knights have had to send people out on patrol and to eliminate the monsters.
Amber: Between ensuring that the city's well-guarded and pursuing monsters beyond its walls nonstop... Everyone's been run ragged! People are so tired they can barely deploy their wind gliders.
Amber: But before Albedo comes up with some sort of solution, someone's gotta get rid of the monsters, right?
Traveler: Aren't you exhausted too?
Amber: Me? I'm... I'm doing fine. After all, I'm used to fighting out here, so I know how to conserve my strength and choose the best way to engage!
Amber: #We can talk more later, I've got another area to patrol. You should head back to Mondstadt for now. I'll see you around, Traveler!
