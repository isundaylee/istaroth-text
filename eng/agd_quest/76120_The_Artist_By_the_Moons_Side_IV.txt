## Talk 1

The Damselette (Columbina): ...Here. This is for you. That's my share done, yes?
Paimon: Hey, it's a drawing canvas. Wait, that means...!
The Damselette (Columbina): Ah. You're just in time.
Maalaus: ~!
Paimon: Well, if it isn't Maalaus, our Great Painter! Long time no see!
Traveler: So, what are we "just in time" for?
The Damselette (Columbina): I just took Maalaus to the moon for a bit.
Paimon: Paimon's sooo jealous of how you just... said that, like you were visiting the garden next door or something.
Paimon: Still, what did you go there for? Looking for inspiration?
The Damselette (Columbina): That's half right. We also selected a location for an "exhibition." Do you recall?
Paimon: Paimon does! Maalaus's Moon Goddess exhibition! Don't forget to invite us when the time comes, okay?
The Damselette (Columbina): Yes, of course. In addition, Maalaus seems to be able to sense subtle changes around her.
The Damselette (Columbina): She's told me that I have changed a lot since returning from the moon.
The Damselette (Columbina): That's why she wanted me to take her there, to see if there was something special about it.
Paimon: Heh! Now that's an artist for you, alright...
Traveler: So, this special thing...
The Damselette (Columbina): I'm not sure either. I took Maalaus to many places, but none of them satisfied her.
The Damselette (Columbina): That is, until I brought her close to your ship.

Traveler: And that story isn't over yet.
Paimon: Yup! And Paimon wants to see more of our great painter's work, anyway.
Maalaus: !!!
Paimon: Oh, done already? Quick as ever, huh.
Paimon: Let Paimon see... Oohh! This is so pretty!
Paimon: Paimon had no idea Teyvat looked so beautiful from the moon...
The Damselette (Columbina): Hah... Now I can understand why Maalaus says that I have changed.
Paimon: Hmm? Why's that?
The Damselette (Columbina): When I see this painting, I remember...
The Damselette (Columbina): That as I sat on the moon, watching Teyvat, I thought about how I'd always been looking for a "home" of my own.
The Damselette (Columbina): But now, I suddenly have two homes, both of them places of such beauty.
The Damselette (Columbina): It was then that I realized that perhaps "home" is wherever the things and people you cherish are.
Traveler: A lovely thought.
The Damselette (Columbina): Thank you. I think that after this, I'd like to take the kuuhenki to the moon more often.
The Damselette (Columbina): You're welcome to come visit my "other home" as well.
