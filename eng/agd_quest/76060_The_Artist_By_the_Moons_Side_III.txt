## Talk 3

Paimon: Alright, these should do. What do you think, Maalaus?

Maalaus: ~!

Paimon: Yep, looks like a happy kuuhenki to Paimon! Hehe, wonder what kind of painting we'll get this time...

Maalaus: ...

Paimon: Oh, is it done already?

Maalaus: ...

Paimon: Maalaus seems a little sad. Is it because of the painting? Let's take a look...

Paimon: Okay so, it's Columbina... taking a walk under the moonlight and gazing up at the night sky.

The Damselette: Ah... I must be looking at the true moon beyond the false sky. I used to take midnight walks like that all the time.

Paimon: Used to...? Oh, right...

Traveler: (Columbina is pretty much confined to this garden, now... even though Nod-Krai is where she was born...)

The Damselette: Yes, I would gaze at the sky and wonder... What really lies up there on the true Frost Moon?

The Damselette: Perhaps it's just like here, filled with lots of little animals...

The Damselette: Or perhaps... there's nothing there at all.

Paimon: Oh...

Traveler: You could always take a few up there with you?

The Damselette: Ha... An interesting thought.

The Damselette: But... Since the moon lies beyond the false sky, that's one adventure I would rather not risk taking them on...

Traveler: There could be other cute creatures up there...

The Damselette: Mm, I hope so.

The Damselette: And I hope... if there are, the creatures would welcome the sudden and unexpected return of their "Goddess."

Traveler: (One is a "home" where she isn't even free to move around... the other is a "home" she knows nothing about...)

Traveler: (...)

Maalaus: ...

Paimon: Maalaus and the other kuuhenki are really gonna miss you...

The Damselette: And I will miss them too.

The Damselette: But in the end, this is a journey that only I can make... and the answer at the end of it is mine alone to seek.

The Damselette: And who knows? Whatever that answer turns out to be... Perhaps it will be something beautiful.

Paimon: W—Well... We'll just have to come visit you, then! With Maalaus!

The Damselette: Alright. We could hold that art exhibition on the moon, perhaps.

Paimon: Yeah, great idea! It's a promise!

The Damselette: Mm...

The Damselette: I hope so, I truly do...
