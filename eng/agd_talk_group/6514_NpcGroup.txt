# Talk Group: Nahuatlalli (NpcGroup 6514)

## Talk 0

Nahuatlalli: Welcome, champion of the Tour of the Return of the Sacred Flame, you have earned our respect.
Traveler: Is it not called the Ayar Heroes' Cup?
Nahuatlalli: Well, you know how it is, people have real names and nicknames, and so do competitions. The Ayar Heroes' Cup is its official name, and the Tour is its nickname — Ipacura likes to call it that.
Nahuatlalli: Ipacura's parents took part in the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame alongside us. Unfortunately, they fell in battle during the last Abyss invasion.
Nahuatlalli: That's why Ipacura likes to call this contest the Tour of the Return of the Sacred Flame.
Traveler: So where does "Ayar" come from...?
Nahuatlalli: You might have guessed by now, but his full name is Ipacura Ayar.
Nahuatlalli: Before his parents passed, they entrusted Ipacura to us. That's why we set up this contest — we like to say it was to give him a happy summer... but the truth is...
Nahuatlalli: One day, he'll become a hero just like his parents. Isn't that right?
