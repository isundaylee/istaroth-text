# A Commission From "That Guy"


## Talk 1

Unknown Role (None): Why, we meet again. How do you fare?

Unknown Role (None): Oh, yes, and one thing. Thus far, Jilliana has neither done too well nor too poorly. The good news is that she has yet to leave Mondstadt...

Unknown Role (None): But the bad news is that she has already confessed her situation to Sister Victoria, who hasn't done much about it so far.

Unknown Role (None): We can only wait and see how this whole affair will end.

Unknown Role (None): ...What are you planning?

Unknown Role (None): Oh, come now. Don't look at me like that. I'm not some sort of villain. As a matter of fact, am I not giving you pointers on how to help her?

Unknown Role (None): Right, let's get down to business. The reason I'm here are the "loose ends" we left behind from that previous incident.

Unknown Role (None): Simply put, you defeated the Treasure Hoarders, yes. But at the same time, you've also blown Jilliana's cover.

Unknown Role (None): The Treasure Hoarders are not lenient to traitors. As far as I know, we've already caught wind of quite a few of their tracks, and we've never seen any of this lot before.

Unknown Role (None): Chances are that they're out to get Jilliana, and also to avenge their losses in the meantime. At this moment, we can only ask you to beat them back.

Unknown Role (None): Isn't this all your doing?

Unknown Role (None): Oh, please, I too have my orders. If it wasn't for that gentleman's insistence that you be the one to handle this, I wouldn't have come looking for you.

Unknown Role (None): Go do it yourself!

Unknown Role (None): Don't say that. You know I don't have your strength.

Unknown Role (None): This is a matter for the Knights.

Unknown Role (None): While that is true, that certain gentleman judges that under the present circumstances, the fewer people know of Jilliana's affairs the better.

Unknown Role (None): Besides, that gentleman does not wish for the Knights to get involved. After all, the Knights are too used to acting in the open, and often go overboard in the process.

Unknown Role (None): Take our ever-reliable Huffman, for example. Why, if he were to hear that there were Treasure Hoarders abroad near Mondstadt, he would raise the alarm within the hour.

Unknown Role (None): And should the Knights march out in full muster, the Treasure Hoarders would surely scurry off in a flash, rendering our plan to get rid of them for good moot once again.

Unknown Role (None): So, we ask you to fly solo and take them out efficiently, only because we must.

Unknown Role (None): Still, that band of Treasure Hoarders has actually gotten so near to Mondstadt that the Knights of Favonius may have already caught wind of them. The Knights may even be prepared to make a move already...

Unknown Role (None): But this is beyond the scope of our consideration.

Unknown Role (None): Don't worry, I have ensured that the rewards this time will be substantial.

Unknown Role (None): This time, our dear gentleman has furnished me with ample Mora, unlike the previous time, when I had to pay you out of pocket... Ah yes, I should also mark the location of those Treasure Hoarders on your map.

Unknown Role (None): Well, then. If for nothing else, please protect Jilliana, Rudolf, Lily, and Mondstadt for the Mora. It's all up you now, so do your best.

## Talk 2

Unknown Role (None): You're back already? Why, aren't you efficient? I think I know why that gentleman likes you so much.

Unknown Role (None): The money. Give it here.

Unknown Role (None): How cold. I mean, I do have the bounty right here. But pray, spare a thought for a fellow errand-runner's feelings!

Unknown Role (None): Why, it's an honor.

Unknown Role (None): Oh no, the honor is all mine. Please take this reward... And pleasure doing business with you.

## Talk 3

Unknown Role (None): ...Those Treasure Hoarders... Oh, I'm sorry! Is something the matter?

Unknown Role (None): There are Treasure Hoarders active around Mondstadt.

Unknown Role (None): Treasure Hoarders? We're on the trail of a group of them ourselves. How dare those crooks skulk so near to Mondstadt. Not to mention that we don't know what their intentions are at all...

Unknown Role (None): No matter what the reason, they're definitely up to no good. We've redirected a lot of manpower to dealing with them, so I'm afraid we don't have the manpower to deal with your report right now.

Unknown Role (None): Nonetheless, I'll note your report down. Once we are done with those Treasure Hoarders, I will pass it upwards. Again, thanks for reporting the matter to us.

## Talk 4

Unknown Role (None): Still preparing? That's fine. Just head out once you're ready.

Unknown Role (None): Who's this gentleman, anyway?

Unknown Role (None): I won't tell you no matter how you ask. That's par for course in our line of work.

Unknown Role (None): Even when we do reveal any intelligence, we do so because we must.

Unknown Role (None): Well, you can give this a thought: which of the things I just said falls under "things I revealed because I must"?

Unknown Role (None): What's your goal?

Unknown Role (None): That gentleman's goals... I couldn't guess them. But I believe that he also means to protect Mondstadt.

Unknown Role (None): Or should I say that even he probably wouldn't do things like this just for the fun of it?

Unknown Role (None): Hmm, no, no, I shouldn't be speculating about such things...
