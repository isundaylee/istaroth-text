Diotima: Back then, I was a boatman, responsible for sending wandering souls onto the silver shining ships, bound for the Moon Capital above the clouds.

Diotima: Come to think of it, it's not much different from what I do now.

## Talk 3

Traveler: The "Moon Capital"? What is that?

Diotima: Ah... I suppose people nowadays wouldn't know. In my time, three bright moons still hung in the sky, and upon each resided many lunar envoys.

Diotima: Back then, noble souls did not head for the depths of the ancient tree. Instead, they followed the trail of the envoys to sleep eternally in the cities upon the moons.

Diotima: I never actually visited the moons myself, but it must be a place as peaceful as it is here.

## Talk 4

Traveler: And what do you do now?

Diotima: I wouldn't call it a "job," it's more like work I found to feed myself. As an old-timer who's lived here so long, I provide guidance to the newcomers who have just arrived.

Diotima: But once you've settled into life here, you're unlikely to run into any real trouble. After all, whether you are wealthy or poor, it makes no difference here.
