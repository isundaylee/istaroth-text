## Talk 0

Traveler: Are you waiting for someone?

Flins: Hmm... Is no one here?

Flins: Well, hello. This is certainly a most joyful encounter.

Flins: Seeing you lifts my spirits after a somewhat disheartening trip.

Traveler: Is something the matter?

Traveler: Do you need our help?

Flins: I wouldn't say anything's the matter, merely that I am dealing with some... unexpected circumstances.

Flins: I recently learned of a collector in Nasha Town who, due to financial concerns, has been obliged to offer a pair of ancient coins for sale.

Flins: I'm told she's a little eccentric and doesn't reply to letters, so I came out here myself to see if I could arrange a deal.

Flins: I needed to come to town anyway to pick up some supplies, so I thought I'd pay her a visit on the way. Unfortunately, she doesn't appear to be home... More's the pity.

Traveler: You collect ancient coins?

Flins: It's just a little hobby of mine. Everyone has something that tickles their fancy, am I right? For me, it's old coins and gemstones.

Flins: Time is the best filter. Anything that can make it through the sieve of time is almost guaranteed to be of good quality.

Flins: The style, pattern, and condition of a coin convey a lot of information. They can tell you, for instance, how many moons it has seen before finding its way into my hands.

Flins: Think about it. A coin may have passed through 10,000 people's hands, brought them happiness or sorrow, and witnessed a multitude of moments in their lives.

Flins: The weight of time it contains is far heavier than its appearance would suggest. It is a witness to the past and something that has value in the present day, which makes it more than worth collecting.

Flins: These coins have been stuck in my mind these last few days.

Flins: What do they look like? What stories can they tell? If I can't meet this collector, I fear all desire to return home shall desert me.

Flins: ...Look at me, rambling on about coins without a thought for the friends standing by my side.

Flins: Haha, let me think of a more interesting topic to make up for boring you just now.

Flins: Ah, yes! Recently, I purchased a beautiful azure gemstone from a Fontainian merchant. It had long slumbered at the bottom of the ocean until being recovered just some days ago.

Flins: I was told it was once embedded in the sword of a champion duelist at the Court of Fontaine, while another story claims it was a pirate crew's prized treasure...

Traveler: I'm not sure which story is less believable...

Traveler: You don't suppose they were lying to you?

Flins: Haha, that's exactly what I thought! Whenever a merchant starts waxing lyrical and weaving tales, it's usually a sign that they don't have faith in their wares.

Flins: Fortunately, I'm very good at appraising a gemstone's true worth — even if its owner hasn't the faintest idea what they're holding.

Flins: Please allow me to invite you to my residence once more. I have a feeling you'll appreciate the gem itself, and you may well find my valuation of it quite interesting, too.

Traveler: Alright, I'll be sure to drop by.

Traveler: I can't wait!

Flins: I'll be waiting for you.

Traveler: Are you looking for something?
