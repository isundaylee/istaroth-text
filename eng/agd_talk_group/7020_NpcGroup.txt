# Talk Group: Thianges (NpcGroup 7020)

## Talk 0

Thianges: Blast it all, the entry procedures here are such a far cry from Fontaine's...

## Talk 1

Traveler: What's wrong?
Thianges: What's wrong? What do you think!? Just look at that endless queue, not to mention the mountain of forms to fill out once you get through it! It's even bigger than a stack of application forms from the Fontaine Research Institute!
Thianges: Every step has to be checked over and over again. It's like these people are terrified of registering people or goods that shouldn't be allowed in or something...

## Talk 2

Traveler: Goodbye.
Thianges: Bye, and you'd best get going... Best not to disturb the others in the queue.

## Talk 3

Traveler: People that shouldn't be allowed in?
Thianges: I—It was just an analogy, okay?
Thianges: Like... smugglers, or rulebreakers... You know the sort, right?! Anyway, nothing to do with me, no sirree, nothing at all. Do I even look like a villain to you?!
Thianges: I'm just an ordinary sailor who wants to get by... I don't know why they're so stringent with security when it comes to normal, innocent folk like us...

## Talk 4

Traveler: You seem anxious.
Thianges: A—Anxious?! Me?! H—Haha, dream on! I'm just a little... seasick, yeah! I'm just a little dizzy! I just want to get back to the city — my city — and find a place to rest!
Thianges: After all, who doesn't want to settle down after a long journey, am I right? Especially for... people who want to live a peaceful life. Like me!
