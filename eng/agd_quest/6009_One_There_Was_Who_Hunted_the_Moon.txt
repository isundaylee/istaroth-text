Flins: Fortunate, then, that Abyssal energy was only leaking from the fragment in miniscule amounts when we first met, or I might have been in for a real interrogation.

Paimon: Yeah... Hard to say how that would've played out. We've had a whole bunch of unpleasant run-ins with the Abyss...

Traveler: Rerir seems fixated on you...

Traveler: ...And you, on him.

Flins: You mean to ask why I'm going this far to oppose him? Well, as a Lightkeeper, I'd say I'm simply doing my job.

Traveler: Come on, don't play games with me.

Paimon: Yeah! You're covered in more mysteries than Paimon could shake a skewer at. You can't just get out of explaining yourself by making lame excuses!

Flins: Hmm, alright. I suppose I do owe you some honesty, whether out of gratitude or the need to build trust.

Flins: Your deductions have been quite correct. Should one wish to conceal oneself within a lantern, elemental power alone is not enough.

Flins: You know humans well, and naturally, you understand their limits.

Flins: But some things that humans would consider difficult are quite normal in the eyes of non-humans.

Paimon: So you mean you're one of the latter?

Flins: Have you heard of how Snezhnaya was once the realm of the fae?

Flins: This race is no longer of any importance, but they still exist — as evidenced by the fact that I am standing before you.

Paimon: We've heard some scattered legends, but... that's all Paimon thought there was to it.

Flins: I once turned my heart toward humanity, and thus did I experience loss. I once labored and did much, and put an end to my tale with my own hands.

Flins: When you realize you are floating in the middle of a vast ocean, you cannot help but feel that swimming in any direction would be equally meaningless.

Flins: Would it not be better, then, to simply sink?

Flins: But before I could sink to truly lightless depths, chance had it that I should be awakened by a band of Ratniki.

Flins: They were facing a horde of Abyssal monsters, and could not last against such a disparity in strength, yet none fled.

Flins: Those who dwell in darkness for long enough become exceedingly sensitive to all light — and the first light I saw upon awakening was that which shone from their eyes.

Paimon: Is that why you chose to help them?

Flins: Perhaps I was moved by their valor. Perhaps I simply saw my past self in them, and was moved to action for the first time in a long, long while.

Flins: But I was freshly awoken, and my powers were limited. I could not prevent casualties altogether.

Flins: The survivors and I buried the fallen heroes... That was the origin of the "Final Night Cemetery."

Traveler: And that's why you chose to become a Lightkeeper...

Flins: Well, not exactly. I was just more familiar with them, so joining up seemed the most convenient way to experience human life.

Paimon: Doesn't sound too believable...

Flins: Well, I believe it. Besides, I'm well past the age when one might believe in "things that must be done."

Flins: Nonetheless, there are still some things I will not allow to happen. The chaos that Rerir has been stirring up is one of them.

Flins: Tomorrow, I shall go and check our records to see if I can find any connection between the Wild Hunt, Rerir, and the cannon.

Paimon: Will you need us to get you where you need to be?

Flins: Oh, I'll be fine. I keep a backup copy at my residence in case of emergencies.

Paimon: Can... you even go back there right now? Won't there be Ratniki watching it?

Flins: Thanks for the reminder. I believe they'll have checked my place, but they won't have stayed. All things are ranked according to their priority, and we lack the manpower for such an open-ended stakeout.

Flins: And even if someone is waiting, I have ways around that. I am the master of my own house, after all.

Paimon: Alright then, we'll trust you. *yawn* We sure ended up chatting for a while, huh? Paimon's super tired now, but... Y'know, feels like we can breathe easier, now that we know you better.

Flins: Haha. I didn't take you for the cautious sort.

Flins: Anyway, I hope you'll help me keep this a secret. I'd prefer others not to know about my past unless strictly necessary, nor do I want to attract more trouble on account of being a fae.

Traveler: Don't worry, I won't say a thing.

Paimon: Paimon's lips are sealed too!

Flins: Alright. Thanks again for your help. Good night, you two.
