# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 7737

## Talk 0

Vindia: Watch your step, dear. This place is teeming with books. It'd be quite a headache if you accidentally tumbled into one of the stranger books.

## Talk 1

Traveler: Who are you?

Vindia: My name is Vindogeneta de Uxellobrogi.

Vindia: Hard to pronounce, isn't it? It's so long that even I can't be bothered to say it. You can just go by what the Remurians do and call me "Vindia."

Vindia: Mr. Castiglione mentioned that the descendants of the High Sea would call me the "Madam Snow-Winged Goose"... but there's no need for you to use that. It makes me sound like some strange old crone.

Vindia: I was once the Queen of Uxellobrogi. As for now? I'm just an ordinary fairy tale writer.

## Talk 2

Traveler: What's that book over there?

Vindia: As you can see, it's just a "storybook" that's been knocked over.

Vindia: A never-ending tea party, a clock that runs backward, talking penguins, and a princess of flowers... In short, nothing but useless fairy{RUBY#[D]fantasies}-tale.

Vindia: After all, fairy tales are written for children who refuse to go to sleep. And the children here, my dear friend, have been awake for far too long.

## Talk 3

Traveler: I'm leaving now.

Vindia: Goodbye, dear. Watch your step, don't fall into the rabbit holes.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Queen...

Vindia: Honestly, there wasn't much of a difference. When I was Queen, my only job was to smile and nod. Having nothing better to do, I spent my time penning fairy tales in secret.

Vindia: Whether it was surrendering to the Remurians or to the High King{RUBY#[D]Errighenth} of Aremorica, those were decisions made by the regency elders.

Vindia: So, you see, even when that deity brought me to this place, no one actually cared.

Vindia: Besides, this way, I won't be forced to go to Aremorica to pay my respects, put on a fake smile, and listen to those barbarians who don't even bathe...

Vindia: Sorry, I meant my venerable compatriots. Anyway, I'm spared from listening to them and that woman bickering endlessly over how many people they should kill. Honestly, she deserves to stay single for the rest of her life. A wonderful ending, wouldn't you say?

## Talk 5

Traveler: What's that room over there?

Vindia: Oh, my dear, it would be best not to disturb him. Mr. Bickerstaff has quite the temper. Writers who wrestle with satire but can't quite finish the novel often end up like that.

Vindia: Her Majesty the High King {RUBY#[D]Errighenth} of Aremorica has more than a few such fellows in her service as well. In a sense, I suppose you could call it a kind of knightly spirit.
