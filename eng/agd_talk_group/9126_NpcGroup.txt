# Talk Group: Zibai - Serenitea Pot (NpcGroup 9126)

## Talk 0

Zibai: So, this is an "abode" created by the Adepti of Liyue.
Zibai: "Mighty and illuminated" indeed...

## Talk 1

Traveler: Are you settling in okay?
Zibai: The environs here are most comfortable. The air is pure and peaceful, free of taint or pollution.
Zibai: I am certain that your honest spirit was what allowed such an abode to be made manifest.
Traveler: Wait, that matters?
Zibai: But of course. All things here are fashioned from your every thought. As your thoughts fly, so shall they be.
Traveler: Couldn't we use this as a mood detector, then...?
Zibai: Indeed, you are most discerning. That assessment is correct.
Zibai: But should you wish to use this as a way to control your thoughts and avoid treading the path of evil, that seems too...
Traveler: Rigid?
Zibai: Indeed.
Zibai: Mortal hearts are filled with thoughts beyond count, complex and cluttered...
Zibai: "Rage" may drive people to commit acts of violence, yet may also imbue them with courage.
Zibai: Unpleasant though "Envy" may sound, so too may it push people to strive harder.
Zibai: "Fear" is despised by all, yet should one so utterly lack it that they pursue only their own pleasure in all things, without regard for right or wrong, how would they be any different from a base beast?
Zibai: Such emotions whisper evil into mortal hearts, yet they can also guide them towards good. The same holds true for the sword in your hand. You choose whether it shall be used to ward off evil or scourge the innocent.
Zibai: Hehe... But I imagine one such as yourself must have long learned how to control your thoughts.
Traveler: I can't always control them...
Zibai: Oh? And when can you not?
Traveler: Right now. I'm getting a bit sleepy listening...
Zibai: Hahaha. Apologies, 'twas I who spoke too much. I was for many years a teacher, and could not help but launch into a lecture.
Zibai: I shall steep a pot of tea. Let us enjoy it together and refresh ourselves.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to chat?
Zibai: Certainly. Are you interested in mathematics? I often used that to pass the time while I dwelt on the moon.
Zibai: I have not laid my arithmetic tomes down after arriving in Liyue either. I sometimes discuss the study of the mathematical sciences with the one you call Cloud Retainer.
Zibai: That said... our perspectives on it seem to differ somewhat.
Zibai: Most of Cloud Retainer's research in the field centers around studying the mechanical arts. She believes that mathematics should have practical applications, and not remain purely theoretical.
Zibai: However, I believe that it is wondrous in itself. To study mathematics solely for the purpose of creating mechanical devices would inevitably destroy its inherent beauty.
Traveler: The... the beauty of numbers and mathematics?
Zibai: That's right. For example... have you ever wondered why one finch plus another finch equals two finches?

Option 1:

> Traveler: Uh... what now? Of course it's two...

Option 2:

> Traveler: H—How many else could it be?

Zibai: That's right. This is a natural law, one that everyone knows.
Zibai: So, were we to express our observations as an equation, that is, write down "one plus one equals two"...
Zibai: Would you not think to ask, "Why does one plus one equals two?"

Option 1:

> Traveler: Uh... come again? It's still two...

Option 2:

> Traveler: What else could it be, three?

Zibai: As I see it, though it is widely accepted that "one plus one equals two," it still lacks... a more objective and rigorous logical grounding.
Zibai: I have conferred with Cloud Retainer several times already on this point alone.
Zibai: She initially thought that such discourse was "nonsense," but after hearing my explanation, she, too, became interested in this type of theoretical research.
Zibai: We spoke until midnight, and were still hungry to explore the subject more deeply, but as we both had matters to attend to, we could only agree to adjourn and continue the discussion another day.
Traveler: I heard the Sumeru Akademiya is also researching mathematics...
Zibai: You mean the Darshan they call "Rtawahist," I presume? Hehe, I did once visit them during my previous wanderings.
Zibai: The research being conducted there seems incredibly profound. I gained tremendous insight from reading but a few of their books.
Zibai: That is why I'm preparing to apply to the Akademiya to further my studies.

Option 1:

> Traveler: An adeptus of Liyue...

Option 2:

> Traveler: Going to study at the Akademiya...

Zibai: I see nothing wrong with that. When one encounters a great teacher, it's only natural to seek their guidance.
Zibai: Besides, the Akademiya must be a gathering place of sages. I do not doubt they uphold the principle of education for all, and will not easily reject those who seek to study there.
Traveler: Y—Yeah, probably...
Zibai: Hmm... It seems I shall learn a great many new stories on my journey to Sumeru.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Anything you'd like to do?
Zibai: In a place so tranquil as this, the best thing to do is relax your body and mind, and let your spirit wander freely.
Zibai: I believe that mortals would call this...
Traveler: Spacing out?
Zibai: Indeed. Spacing out.
Traveler: Is there some special technique behind adepti spacing out?
Zibai: Were I to force the issue through adeptal arts, it could hardly be called "spacing out," now could it?
Zibai: Indeed, few can achieve this seemingly ordinary and straightforward emptiness of mind.
Zibai: Most will find that though they close their eyes, hold their breath, and force themselves to relax, their thoughts still boil and churn, playing out countless fantasies...
Zibai: And they then open their eyes to greater exhaustion than before they closed them.
Zibai: Perhaps this is why they say, "peace of mind is hard to find"...
