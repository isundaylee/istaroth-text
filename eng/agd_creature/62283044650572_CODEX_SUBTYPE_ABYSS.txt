# The Abyss (Monsters)

## Pyro Abyss Mage
Erambert
Also known as: Pyrochanter

Abyss creatures who command the power of Pyro in battle.
For unknown reasons, Abyss Mages are able to communicate with the hilichurls, thus enabling them to manipulate their minds easily. They say that Abyss Mages utter a long-forgotten language from which they draw magical power.

## Hydro Abyss Mage
Erambert
Also known as: Hydrochanter

Abyss creatures that utilize Hydro energy in battle.
Stories circulate among adventurers that Abyss Mages are at the lowest rung of the Abyss Order's hierarchy and that they roam the various lands to scheme fell deeds of destruction. Though they also often make fun of these Abyss Mages, saying that the most harm they can do is to cause rheumatism or a common cold, their power is not to be underestimated.

## Electro Abyss Mage
Erambert
Also known as: Electrochanter

Abyss creatures that utilize Electro in battles.
While studying at the Akademiya, researcher Alva Nikola once conducted a systematic study of the methods by which Electro Abyss Mages manipulate Electro and came up with a theoretical weapon known as the Nikola Coil. However, it has yet to be put into practice. Some say that his research materials were eaten during a fungal infestation.

## Cryo Abyss Mage
Erambert
Also known as: Cryochanter

Abyss creatures that call upon Cryo in battle.
Though they dabble with great power and can even create icicles by condensing and freezing water vapor in the atmosphere, their bodies are very vulnerable in and of themselves. Once their protective Ward has been broken, they are at your mercy.

## Abyss Herald: Wicked Torrents
Francobert
Also known as: Dark Usher of Wicked Torrents

Servants of the Abyss Order that use Hydro in combat.
These silent figures are the vanguard of the Abyss, brandishing Tidal Blades that can engulf everything in an instant, crumpling armor and obliterating heretics. They shall bring preordained damnation upon the foolish masses.

## Abyss Herald: Frost Fall
Francobert
Also known as: Emissary of Frigid Blades

A monster from the Abyss Order that wields frigid, icy blades in battle.
Just as there are saints who will spread and glorify the profound gospel, there are envoys who must correct any twisted strains and remove any dissonance.
People often say that steel feels like ice as it pierces the body, but this dark frost is said to freeze even the soul.

## Abyss Lector: Fathomless Flames
Octric
Also known as: The Devouring Benthic Flame

Wielders of abyssal fire who pursue the meaning behind texts and scriptures. Is a member of the Abyss Order.
The surface people have a slightly distorted understanding of what their name means, calling them "Lectors," when their name can also mean "exegete." Reading the word of grace grants them great power.

## Abyss Lector: Violet Lightning
Octric
Also known as: Evangelist of Striking Thunder

A monster who serves the Abyss Order and commands lightning while singing the praises of the darkness.
These are the evangelists and the scholars of the Abyss. Their violent lightning strikes the hearts of unbelievers with dark wisdom, warped by the shadowy depths of an eternal night, its violet glow proclaiming the existence of a great power that corrodes human intellect.

## Iniquitous Baptist
The Guide to the Dark Paradise

A monster from the Abyss that wields multiple types of Elemental Power and has gained extra limbs from its "evolution."

However, even the elements of gods can be entrapped and distorted before they are subsumed to become tools of the abyssal will. ## Thundercraven Rifthound Whelp

## Rockfond Rifthound
Gap-Infiltrating Aliens
Also known as: Rifthound

A beast with monstrous blood that is capable of eroding the boundaries of the world. They were created by "Gold."
They do display certain biological behaviors similar to those of real wolves. Perhaps they feel jealousy towards these their "next of kin," and dream of replacing them someday.

## Thundercraven Rifthound
Gap-Infiltrating Aliens
Also known as: Rifthound

A beast with monstrous blood that is capable of eroding the boundaries of the world. They were created by "Gold."
Once upon a time, they ran riot across the continent, but they went extinct for a time due to resistance and mass hunts. Recently, however, they have re-emerged. The pack of black wolves that now threaten Springvale and Wolvendom are such creatures.

## Golden Wolflord
Nameless King of the Rift

Hailing from a dark world, this ruler of the black wolves has the power to call upon its followers, its hidden claws, to dissolve space itself and forge a rift through which it may enter.
The Wolflord has no name, for it was but one of "Gold"'s unintentional creations, and that is precisely why it is obsessed with invading worlds that do not belong to it and making a name for itself.
That said, the wolf pack is far from unintelligent. After suffering a defeat in its previous invasion, a deserted island where no humans or protectors dwell has been selected for them to prepare for their lord's descent.

## Shadowy Husk: Standard Bearer
Also known as: The Black Serpent Knights' End

A pitch-dark soldier. Once a proud warrior of an ancient underground nation, it has now lost any awareness of memory.
But even so, it holds the banner of the royal house aloft into battle. Though it has forgotten its comrades or any royal blessing they might have been given, it will never lay this banner down.

## Shadowy Husk: Line Breaker
Also known as: The Black Serpent Knights' End

A pitch-dark soldier. Once a proud warrior of an ancient underground nation, it no longer has any "intelligence" that it may still be called "human."
Yet the desire to destroy the foe and plunge into battle to defend others is etched into this empty suit of armor, even if no one remains to tell it where the enemy is or who or what exactly must be defended.

## Shadowy Husk: Defender
Also known as: The Black Serpent Knights' End

A pitch-dark soldier. Once a proud warrior of an ancient underground nation, it is now nothing but a husk that only bears memories of battle.
All shall decay in the end, and this is all that is left of the Black Serpent Knights amidst the merciless march of their sins, curses, and time itself.

Aldric

A guard of some standing in the court, sundering the foes of the nation's rulers using a sword art known as "Truthseeker."

## Black Serpent Knight: Rockbreaker Ax
Aldric

A guard of some standing in the court, sundering the foes of the nation's rulers using a sword art known as "Truthseeker."
Though Truthseeker is, strictly speaking, a sword art, its teachings can also be used with various other weapons — and in the realm's darkest hour, those who had to protect their homeland had little room to be fussy anyway.

## Large Shatterstone Breacher Primus
Otherworldly Agglomerated Organism

A strange life form that mostly appears in Elynas.
Melusines perceive Breacher Primuses as harmless creatures, and many even find them completely adorable.
The visual perception of Melusines is very different from humans.

## Shatterstone Breacher Primus
Otherworldly Agglomerated Organism

A strange life form that mostly appears in Elynas.
Receives a powerful shock from encountering ousia or pneuma energy, and thus Breacher Primuses can be driven away using this relatively nonaggressive and noninvasive means.

## Large Overgrown Breacher Primus
Otherworldly Agglomerated Organism

A strange life form that mostly appears in Elynas.
The nature of a Breacher Primus defies known categories: they are not animals, plants, Shroom-Kin, nor Elementals. The debate among scholars of Sumeru never formally reached a consensus on just what it is, until it was finally banned as a research topic. The reason is that based on the current system of knowledge, this problem is like "the definitions prescribed by language and the true nature of things," and it is impossible to have a meaningful, final answer.

## Overgrown Breacher Primus
Otherworldly Agglomerated Organism

A strange life form that mostly appears in Elynas.
In Fontaine, the earliest recorded observations of a Breacher Primus appeared shortly after the cataclysm. Records from the time show instances of Breacher Primuses both attacking and protecting humans.

## Tenebrous Mimiflora
Void Obliterator Bubble

Abyssal monsters that have descended to this world from the deep, dark depths of a twisted and alien world, they possess a plant-like appearance.
It is said that where the corrosive waves of the dark tide lap, such plant-like dark monsters will grow. But they only bear a superficial resemblance to plants, for it is a form they only adopted to get closer to the tree-like world, that they might extract the necessary concepts from it, and analyze and imitate them.
Some raving lunatics who have lost their minds say that those things that extend out like leaves are actually the beginnings of gates to other worlds.

## Tenebrous Papilla: Type I
Void Fantasy Plant
Also known as: Tenebrous Papilla

Abyssal creatures with plant-like forms that have descended from a deep, dark, unfathomable, contorted otherworld.
These twisted monstrosities are capable of infiltrating deep down into the bowels of the earth, extracting memories to imitate and manipulate. Through this process of distorting the past's memories into more abstruse and sordid forms, they gradually draw closer to Irminsul's core, threatening to undermine its very existence.
Some say that these aberrations work constantly to corrupt the many colors of light, leaving only pitch-black information in their stead. Such a hypothesis seems to be confirmed by the branches they extend outward, as if to pierce the void itself...

## Wilderness Exile
Also known as: Shadow of the Wild Hunt

A ghoul that stalks the frozen wilderness when the pitch-dark mist rolls in. Their emergence presages worse calamities to come.
All who dwell beneath the moon set out on journeys with their own goals and wishes in mind, but not all such wishes receive favor. Spirits that cannot rest due to engendered resentment, partings with loved ones, or desires that go unfulfilled, if corrupted by the Abyss, may transform into ghouls, forever lost in the thick mists of the Wild Hunt.

## Wilderness Hunter
Also known as: Shadow of the Wild Hunt

A ghoul that stalks the frozen wilderness when the pitch-dark mist rolls in. Their emergence presages worse calamities to come.
Many great champions of a lost ancient kingdom lie buried beneath the frozen northlands. Though the remnants of the past have been long wiped clean, these noble spirits yet defend their golden homeland. Awakened by Abyssal taint, these warriors now see all who trespass as prey.

## Fisher of Hidden Depths
Also known as: Eyrie Beast of Another World

An Abyssal creature that slips through rifts in the hidden depths, crossing over from beyond this world.
Long ago, an ancient philosopher described humans as "featherless bipeds," using that simple phrase to distinguish mankind from animals and assert human superiority. In some sense, these deathless wanderers of the silent cosmic void, scaled instead of feathered, and using their hooked beaks to draw in biomass from world to world, might actually also match that same simplistic definition of "human."

## Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares
Also known as: Faceless, Boundless Overlord

An Abyssal creature that creeps through rifts in the hidden depths, quietly watching this world. Its eerie, faceless form is said to be nothing more than a projection, for its true body exists only as a formless mass of pure Abyssal energy.
Even invaders who seek to claim this world must obey its rules to some extent. Unless the heavens split open and the earth's defenses collapse, these star-born outsiders can do little more than linger at the world's fringes, whispering their madness into this realm, their attempts to seep inside amounting to nothing... But how long can this stability and order truly last?
