# Talk Group: Hannelore (NpcGroup 1788)

## Talk 0

Hannelore: Lord Barbatos, please hear my wishes on the wind...

## Talk 1

Hannelore: What's that pleasant aroma... Huh? It's perfume, isn't it?

## Talk 2

Traveler: What are you doing here?
Hannelore: I'm listening to the sound of wind... and the voices of the people.
Hannelore: With so many merchants and sailors coming and going, it's natural for them to bring more than just goods, but also secrets and sins buried deep within their hearts.
Hannelore: Lending an ear to their confessions and soothing their souls — that is the duty of the Church of Favonius.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Goodbye.
Hannelore: Goodbye. May the Anemo Archon protect you.

## Talk 4

Traveler: I don't see a church around here...
Hannelore: Of course. This is Dornman Port, after all.
Hannelore: Long before the Anemo Archon toppled the rule of the tower's tyrant, this land was already a fief of the Gunnhildr Clan, and it was here that they established Mondstadt's very first sacred sanctuary.
Hannelore: But during the dark age of the old aristocracy, the Church gradually devolved into a mere puppet of the aristocracy.
Hannelore: To signal a total break from the increasingly tyrannical Imunlaukr Clan, the Gunnhildrs of the time seized the bishop sent from Mondstadt, hurled him from the window of the sanctuary's high tower, and subsequently tore it down.
Hannelore: To the Church of Favonius, the Gunnhildr Clan's actions were the true act of piety — a refusal to coexist with those who commit evil in the name of faith.
Hannelore: That's why the sanctuary was never rebuilt ever since... and why this place remains roofless to this very day.

## Talk 5

Traveler: So the Dornman Port belongs to the Gunnhildr Clan?
Hannelore: No. After Vennessa's uprising, the Gunnhildr Clan led a push to abolish all hereditary noble fiefdoms, returning both the land and their freedom to the people of Mondstadt.
Hannelore: Look at the present day. The once-mighty Imunlaukr Clan has seen its bloodline vanish, and their accomplices have long since lost their former prominence.
Hannelore: Time eventually erodes all mighty powers with no partiality, and only the wind continues to blow as it always has. This is also a blessing from Lord Barbatos.

## Talk 6

Traveler: About fragrances...
Hannelore: Hmm... your fragrance is quite distinctive. Fresh, but not overly sweet. Like the breeze that drifts across Starsnatch Cliff in early spring.
Hannelore: Many people wear perfume before coming to confession. Much like prayer, confession is a solemn sacrament. And as Millhaven nearby happens to produce so many fragrances, it's only natural...
Hannelore: But yours feels gentler. There's something about it that settles the heart.
Hannelore: If a fragrance like this lingered here while they listened to confessions... perhaps those who come weighed down with sorrow might find their spirits loosening, if only a little.

## Talk 7

Hannelore: Mr. Lohen, I feel we must talk about the matter of confession...
Lohen: Hmm? Oh? Have I done something that requires confession? You'll have to forgive me, I can't seem to recall anything.
Hannelore: Do you remember... Struwell? And the others?
Lohen: Ah, the three noisy brats. I remember them.
Hannelore: A few days ago, they came to me in floods of tears, practically begging to confess to some pranks.
Lohen: Oh? Interesting. What did they do?
Hannelore: ...Sneak into Granny Marthe's house with the grand plan of smearing cream onto her face.
Hannelore: But when Moricztill was keeping watch, a gust of wind rang the bell above his head, scaring him so much that he fled immediately.
Hannelore: Then, just as Max was planning to sneak into the room, another gust of wind knocked over a honey jar by his feet. That woke Granny Marthe up immediately.
Hannelore: Finally, when Struwell was making his frantic escape, a sudden gust of wind brushed his back. Only when he reached his friends did he find a slip of paper clinging to him...
Lohen: —Let me guess... was it, "The Anemo Archon is always watching"?
Lohen: Heh. Isn't it a wonderful trade? The world loses three little troublemakers and gains three devout believers in the Anemo Archon... though I'd give them two days, at most.
Hannelore: *spluttering* Mr. Lohen! Regardless of your motives, there's a limit to how much you should be terrorizing children. Dragging the Anemo Archon's name into it is equally uncalled for!
