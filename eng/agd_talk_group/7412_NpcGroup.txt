# Talk Group: Buturlina (NpcGroup 7412)

## Talk 0

Buturlina: Welcome, welcome to Podvorye Restaurant. If you should like anything, don't hesitate to speak to me.

## Talk 1

Traveler: Let me take a look at the menu...
Buturlina: Of course! Please. Anytime you're ready to order, just come to me.

## Talk 2

Traveler: So, Podvorye, the "homestead." How stead-y has this homey house been going?
Buturlina: Oh, very! Consider, after all, the employees here are all long-lived fae... My, even I cannot say how long this place has been trucking along.
Buturlina: If you harbor any concerns that you might not be tasting authentic flavors, worry not! Our current boss... has been head chef here for a very, very long time now.
Buturlina: Why, when he took up the mantle, I was just a furball rolling around the snowy plains.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Do you take reservations around here?
Buturlina: Not by our house rules, no. But if you really, really need it, you can tell me in advance...
Buturlina: You see, there will sometimes be tables we've forgotten to assign guests to, which can then have a most fortuitous encounter with you. A joyous arrangement for all, I say.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Do you have any recommendations?
Buturlina: Every dish here comes highly recommended! Anything that has stood the test of time and stayed on the menu has more than earned its spot there.
Buturlina: Whether you like it or not depends on your preferences, and my suggestion to that end is... try more dishes!

## Talk 5

Traveler: Hmm. I don't need anything right now...
Buturlina: Then we await your next visit, dear customer.
