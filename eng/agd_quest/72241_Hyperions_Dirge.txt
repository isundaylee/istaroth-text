## Talk 3

Unknown Role (None): Ah, Traveler from another land, it is you.

Unknown Role (None): Thanks to you, my soul has become whole, and I now recall everything. Incredible.

Unknown Role (None): Perhaps you already know that in the distant past, there was only a great darkness in Byakuyakoku, and that those who lived there dwelt in fear of the Dragonheirs of the Depths.

Unknown Role (None): Back then, it was I who organized the construction of the Dainichi Mikoshi, and thus did Byakuyakoku obtain a cyclical night and day, and thus did the name "Byakuya" come about.

Unknown Role (None): It was said that: "Now that the Dainichi Mikoshi has been built, though this land is in everlasting night, we can at least tell black from white."

Unknown Role (None): How mortifying then, that before the shreds of my soul were reunited, I could not even remember that day when the people crowded around the completed Dainichi Mikoshi.

Unknown Role (None): It should have been my fondest memory.

Unknown Role (None): But alas...

Unknown Role (None): Alas what?

Unknown Role (None): When Dainichi Mikoshi was built, the Dragonheirs were defeated, repelled by a light that they were unused to. Thus did the people regain peace of mind.

Unknown Role (None): But the moment peace began to reign... Ah, the human heart is hard to comprehend. Who knew then that they would have begun to nurse such evil intent?

Unknown Role (None): O Traveler from afar, I am certain that you know how voracious villains will seize for themselves riches and power if given the chance... more than they ever deserved!

Unknown Role (None): And so they used the people, leading them unto the worship of the Dainichi Mikoshi, leading them into the folly of believing in such vain and dead objects.

Unknown Role (None): In the end, they even proclaimed the rule of the "Sunc{RUBY#[S]Phaethon}hild" over the land.

Unknown Role (None): The Sunchild?

Unknown Role (None): Indeed. There will always be a young infant chosen to be the Sunchild.

Unknown Role (None): Of course, such children would not have been of age to understand the world. They would not have been fit to rule. And so they served as puppets, going along with those who pulled their strings in the background.

Unknown Role (None): I wanted to oppose this, but I was accused of high treason by the Sunchild under their control and imprisoned here.

Unknown Role (None): Ah, the Sunchild...

Unknown Role (None): But wouldn't the Sunchild eventually realize that they were being manipulated?

Unknown Role (None): And that is why no Sunchild has ever reached maturity. For it is as you say: grown people are not so easily swayed by villains.

Unknown Role (None): "The hallowed Sunchild, their birth-rite begun, on Pegasus shall ride to the feast of the sun."

Unknown Role (None): Wait, surely you don't mean...

Unknown Role (None): So, on one of their birthdays...

Unknown Role (None): Yes. They would be taken to the inner sanctum of the Dainichi Mikoshi, dressed up as those attending birth rites and in high spirits...

Unknown Role (None): ...The thing hidden here is related to them.

Unknown Role (None): This bridle is the proof that one is indeed a child of the sun. I hid it here, thinking to someday hand it over and alert the people... But I suppose that such a deed is meaningless now.

Unknown Role (None): Traveler from another land... I was neither able to protect those children nor this nation.

Unknown Role (None): My name is...

Unknown Role (None): Aberaku, Helios-Forger!

Unknown Role (None): I organized the construction of the Dainichi Mikoshi. 'Twas I who hung a bright mirror on high amidst the ever-night, and I thought myself a genius.

Unknown Role (None): And such talent I had! Such ability!

Unknown Role (None): I dreamed of using the Dainichi Mikoshi to wash away the darkness, but I did not then know that it was the greed and desire within human hearts that was the all-devouring night everlasting.

Unknown Role (None): Guest from afar, I now give the bridle into your hands.

Unknown Role (None): This Divine Bridle is a testament to our supremely laughable history...

Unknown Role (None): Keep it well... Nay, do with it whatever you wish.

Unknown Role (None): This is too precious a gift...

Unknown Role (None): Hah! Nay, it has already been rendered meaningless. That I have been restored to wholeness and become able to hand it to you is fate. And for this thing, it shall be considered a fine death.

Unknown Role (None): Ah... All the talent, all the ability...
