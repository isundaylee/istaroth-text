Euphrosiniya: When Solovei the Torchforger asked the Tsaritsa to grant the borderlands autonomy, Grand Duke Brahe also appeared before the throne. There, he willingly gave up any claim to the land and all it held.
Euphrosiniya: He then entrusted the governor's palace to the Lightkeepers, who turned it into a military fortress. And that is how the Nod-Krai we know today came to be.
