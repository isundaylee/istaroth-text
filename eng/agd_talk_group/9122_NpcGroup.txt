## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to have a chat with me?
Nefer: Of course. What do you want to talk about? The wanted criminals in Nod-Krai with the highest bounties? The secret lingo of the black market? Or maybe... Oh! Did you need to get your hands on some under-the-table armaments?
Traveler: Um... how about something more normal? Like your daily life...
Nefer: Huh... that's not a "normal" topic for me. Not a lot of people come to me for a casual chat, so... I'm not sure what to say.
Traveler: Well, what do you and Jahoda usually talk about?
Nefer: The commissions she messes up, how to salvage the commissions she messes up... what sort of commissions she'll mess up in the future.
Nefer: And other things, of course, like... her paycheck, who needs to watch the storefront, who's going to feed Ashru...
Nefer: Ah, but I'm tabling that last one for now. Jahoda's too generous with the food, and Ashru's been getting chubby.
Nefer: I'm thinking about ordering a cat-sized hamster wheel from Aino to help Ashru slim back down.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Aside from Jahoda and Ashru...

Option 2:

> Traveler: Do you have other friends in Nod-Krai?

Nefer: Friends? Hah, I've got plenty of those. Why, interested in meeting some of these "friends" of mine? I can introduce you to them sometime...
Traveler: Real friends, I mean...
Nefer: I am talking about real friends. These ladies and gentlemen are esteemed guests of the Curatorium. Our friendship has inspired many a generous financial donation on their part.
Nefer: It's a friendship that will remain unbreakable... Until they run out of Mora, at least.

Option 1:

> Traveler: That just makes them your customers!

Option 2:

> Traveler: You called them "esteemed guests"...

Nefer: At the Curatorium, the line between "guests" and "friends" is a thin one indeed.
Nefer: After all, we deal in goods that cannot be seen or touched — there are no guarantees in a business like that.
Nefer: Without a solid friendship and the level of trust that comes with it, how could I convince my guests to part with their precious Mora?
Traveler: So how does one establish that sort of "friendship"?
Nefer: How indeed. Hmm... I'll skip over the tricks of the trade. The most important thing is to find "common ground."
Nefer: Shared hobbies or habits. A common friend... or a common enemy. Similarities, you see, are the source from which friendship flows. And, with a few nudges in the right direction, it can be nurtured into a rushing river.
Nefer: Of course, if you alone control the "source"... you alone control the entire river of friendship.
Nefer: And that means... whether the river remains clear or turns muddy, flows freely or dries up... is all in your hands.

Option 1:

> Traveler: I don't want to control my friendships.

Option 2:

> Traveler: I prefer friendships to happen naturally.

Nefer: Yes, I can see that... I'm sure that's part of what people find so endearing about you.
Nefer: Still, it's best to be careful. I've seen people bare their soul... only for it to be sold to the highest bidder.
Nefer: If you ever meet someone you can't read, feel free to ask me. I'll help you separate the loyal ones from the snakes waiting to strike while your back is turned.
Nefer: But don't worry. No matter how well-hidden they think they are, they'll never escape my gaze.
