## Talk 3

Traveler: You have another daughter?
Latkin: Yes... When that couple took Yashina with them, they entrusted their daughter to us. Her name is Linnea.
Latkin: For some reason... that girl could only grow up in human society. Otherwise, she would have suffered unimaginable pain.
Latkin: I used to be a naturalist, so I happen to know a thing or two about these matters. An "exchange of lives" like this was never meant to be known by humans. Otherwise, the original arrangement would lose its purpose.
Latkin: It wasn't until a few years ago that we finally realized she was a switched child.
Latkin: At the time, all we could think about was getting our own daughter back, which is why we were completely blinded to the deeper truth of it all. And on the other hand, Linnea would feel deeply uncomfortable the moment she got anywhere near the tundra...
Latkin: We knew she would only hold us back if we tried to move faster, so we left her alone back in Nod-Krai.
Latkin: It was only later that we realized how selfish, how incredibly foolish we had been.
Latkin: Even if we aren't bound by blood, she is still our daughter, who loved and trusted us unconditionally. She did nothing wrong, yet we...
Latkin: That is why... I want to cure Yashina as soon as possible. Once she's fully recovered, we're going back to Nod-Krai to find Linnea. Then, we can try to make up for our mistake... though we might never truly do that.
