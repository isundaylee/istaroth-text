## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to chat?

Varka: ...

    Option 1:
        Traveler: You look a little lost.
    Option 2:
        Traveler: Aren't you usually an avid talker?
Varka: I'm fine. As an experienced Knight of Favonius, I can quickly adjust to even the most unusual of situations...
Varka: My expectations were a little... misaligned. Alright, let's go! Let's get this "conference" on the roaad.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Um... conference?
    Option 2:
        Traveler: That sounds a bit formal...
Varka: Every conversation without drinks is a "conference."
Varka: I think my last one was probably when I had tea with the lovely ladies of the Hexenzirkel... Oh, actually, I may have attended a few during the expedition...
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Well then, since you brought it up...
    Option 2:
        Traveler: How did you break the ice with the Hexenzirkel?
Varka: Ha, great question. First, find something you have in common, or bring up people or things they care about. For example...
Varka: "Oh my, your child is adorable! A real handful, you say? Haha, well, that's just how kids are — never a moment's peace. Take my two for example..."
Varka: "Looking for somewhere to settle in Teyvat? Well, it simply has to be Mondstadt! And just let me tell you why..."
Varka: Then, once the conversation gets going, look for a new opening...
Varka: "Since the young 'uns are in Mondstadt, why don't you join us too?"
Varka: "Oh, I don't mean sign up to the Knights of Favonius. Let me think... would 'external support' be the right term?"
Varka: Then, while they're still uncertain, go all in to assuage their concerns — and seal the deal!
Varka: "Don't worry! All sorts of people call Mondstadt home. I suppose you've heard that even Lupus Boreas was once a wanderer from a faraway land!"
Varka: "You don't need to do anything! We can handle most situations ourselves, and if we do need your help... I believe it'll be in everyone's interests."
Traveler: You've got quite the silver tongue.
Varka: Haha, thanks! In return for your kind words, let me treat you to a drink!
Traveler: So you still want a proper "conversation"!
