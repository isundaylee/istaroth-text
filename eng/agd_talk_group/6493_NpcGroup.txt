# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 6493

## Talk 0

Lawaia: An easy-breezy Natlan in an easy-breezy summer! Welcome to the Shoal's Haven! Ah, here comes someone with an aroma of freshness...

## Talk 1

Traveler: An aroma of freshness?

Lawaia: Absolutely. My nose is sharp enough to tell the difference between today's catch and yesterday's catch, as well as tell fresh customers apart from overnight people.

## Talk 2

Traveler: What exactly is an "overnight person"?

Lawaia: They're like a fish that was caught the night before — they smell a little off. Take Teuciztli, for example...

Lawaia: He'll keep leftover seafood from the day before and continue to sell it. But just one day is enough to pollute the freshness with a little bit of decay.

Lawaia: Even though eating it won't make you sick, we take pride in selling fresh food every day. So, just like his fish, Teuciztli is very much "overnight".

Lawaia: But as long as I'm here, you can rest assured that I'll give you only the freshest fish. I can't stand the stench of cooking with anything less, and that's why I throw out all the old fish on the sly.

## Talk 3

Traveler: You're not scared the boss'll find out?

Lawaia: He won't find out if you don't snitch. You're fresh, people like you don't mix with his type.

Lawaia: (Lawaia gives you a playful wink.)

## Talk 4

Traveler: How's business today?

Lawaia: It's not bad at all. What's strange, though, is today's catch was suddenly much fresher.

Lawaia: The boss even came to tell me that I'd been working hard lately, and he gave me some Mora as thanks. Talk about a turnaround...

Lawaia: I'm starting to suspect something... Did he hear about the things I said?

Lawaia: Bah, whatever. It's not every day that free Mora just falls into your lap. Here, have some — consider it a token of good fortune.

## Talk 5
