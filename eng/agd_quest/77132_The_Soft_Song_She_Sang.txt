## Talk 5

(Talk to Paimon)

Paimon: ...That big guy was chained up back there... In the end, this lighthouse is where he always wanted to be, huh?
Paimon: But that young lady, that shadow... She was only human, so she shouldn't have been able to live this long...
Paimon: So what was that shadow, really?

Option 1:

> Traveler: The wishes of that young lady herself gave it form, perhaps...
> Paimon: Hmm, maybe this is one of those cases where you think about something hard enough and your body actually moves in the direction you're thinking about without you noticing.
> Paimon: Right. Maybe that shadow... was also able to remain here to this day because she held on to her desire to see this big guy...

Option 2:

> Traveler: Perhaps it was comprised of the Snegovik's wishes...
> Paimon: The big guy's? You mean... That the girl never managed to wait for him, and he never did meet her...
> Paimon: And that the shadow was just a figment of his imagination?
> Paimon: Well... But even so, at least he did gain the world in the end, if only in his imagination... Right?

Paimon: The big guy... He's walking beneath the waves... Paimon has a feeling we'll never see him again.
Paimon: Seeya, Big Guy!
Paimon: ...Well, not that Paimon knows if he can hear us.
