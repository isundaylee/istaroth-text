## Talk 1

Kuzmichev: Oh, Alyosha! You came at just the right time. The hunting traps you asked me to make are finally ready.
Alyosha: Thank you, Mr. Kuzmichev. I'll take these traps with me. Could you repair these ones for me?
Kuzmichev: Good heavens... How did you even manage to break these? I used nothing but the finest materials.
Alyosha: A few days ago, I came across a tough Chimeric Monster. While chasing it, I realized that we were both trying to lure each other into traps. I had no choice but to use everything I had on hand...
Kuzmichev: Boy, you're still alive. That means you won.
Alyosha: ...
Alyosha: Nature showed me mercy. The monster, blinded by its own rage, plunged into the ice chasm.
Kuzmichev: It's dead for good?
Alyosha: Can't say. I never saw the body.
Kuzmichev: Hmph. Leave those traps with me, boy. I'll make 'em sturdier this time. Something tells me you and that beast aren't finished with each other yet.
Kuzmichev: Next time you cross paths, finish the job. I've got a feeling it won't slip away from you again.
Alyosha: Very well, Mr. Kuzmichev. If you're right, I'll bring you its sharpest fang.
