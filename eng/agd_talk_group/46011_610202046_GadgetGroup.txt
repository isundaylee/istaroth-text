# Talk Group: Ancient Terminal (GadgetGroup 46011_610202046)

## Talk 0

Ancient Terminal: (A terminal located in the mysterious ruins, waiting for visitors to arrive.)

Option 1:

> Traveler: Records Regarding the "Blackbody Matter"
> Ancient Terminal: The black, iridescent filament-like ejecta found on the outer hull of the "Moongazer" possessed a unique "reflective" property. This likely explained why the spaceship remained concealed...
> Ancient Terminal: After Professor Ungien fused with the "Stellar Blackbody," his body also began secreting the same substance across its surface... Professor Maagees named it "Blackbody Matter."
> Ancient Terminal: Experiments have revealed that Blackbody Matter can mutually stimulate with Archaeolune Energy, and can also fuse with plants and animals. In some fused specimens, coloration shifts from blue to black.
> Ancient Terminal: What an astonishing property, but it may prove useful — I have always believed that transmitting signals into deep space is far too risky.
> Ancient Terminal: There is no evidence to suggest that whatever receives those signals would necessarily bear us goodwill. On the contrary, it may invite disaster.
> Ancient Terminal: Even so, Professor Maagees now needs support more than ever. This is her decision...
> Ancient Terminal: I must therefore devise a safeguard, and the key lies in the properties of the Blackbody Matter...

Option 2:

> Traveler: Records Regarding the "Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment"
> Ancient Terminal: The ecological research team has cultivated thousands of Moonshade Trees. From among them, Professor Maagees's team selected compatible specimens with a high compatibility for integration with the "Blackbody Matter."
> Ancient Terminal: After being stimulated by a certain intensity of Archaeolune Energy, this individual released diffusive waves with reflective properties.
> Ancient Terminal: By increasing the intensity of stimulation, the specimens will continue emitting diffusive waves until a stable curtain-form containment is formed, with a radius extending beyond the entire laboratory.
> Ancient Terminal: This effect has already exceeded our initial expectations. The next phase of research should focus on how to further expand the range of these waves...

Option 3:

> Traveler: Records Regarding "Project Lunar Veil: Phase I"
> Ancient Terminal: Researcher Nuur suggested that we conduct project trials on a larger scale within the biosphere.
> Ancient Terminal: I had thought he took no interest in anything other than stones and the dead.
> Ancient Terminal: Still, Professor Maagees agreed, so we proceeded with it. When she asked me for a project name, I suggested "Project Lunar Veil" — a veil that could obscure the moon.
> Ancient Terminal: It was a fitting name. But no one would ever guess that it actually came from Ninnin.
> Ancient Terminal: It is truly astonishing. When we were testing the "Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment," Ninnin could barely even speak.
> Ancient Terminal: Yet when the indistinct words "Lunar Veil" tumbled from her lips, it was as though those innocent eyes had already understood what we were doing. It felt as if she, too, had come for this very purpose.
> Ancient Terminal: Perhaps in the future, she would become a remarkable researcher in her own right.

Option 4:

> Traveler: Records Regarding "Project Lunar Veil: Phase II"
> Ancient Terminal: Phase I was a success. We created a miniature Lunar Veil to shroud the biosphere, but it only made me more anxious.
> Ancient Terminal: Professor Maagees's condition continues to decline. The incident inflicted severe psychological trauma, leaving her mental state increasingly unstable. Her ongoing work is rapidly depleting her vitality, and she no longer has the luxury of time.
> Ancient Terminal: The research team from the Eye of Ibni-Belum carried out a signal transmission for this purpose. I could neither bring myself to, nor truly had the right to, stop Professor Maagees's final wish — even if that wish did not truly come from her.
> Ancient Terminal: I remain deeply uneasy about this transmission. The only thing left for me to do is pray to our King who ventured into the depths of the stars, and hope that this transmission does not invite evil or disaster...
> Ancient Terminal: Next, I had to finish the full-scale Veil immediately, even though it required an energy supply several orders of magnitude greater than Phase I...
> Ancient Terminal: I didn't know if the Moon Goddesses were willing to shed their mercy upon me, but they were the only ones I had left to turn to...
