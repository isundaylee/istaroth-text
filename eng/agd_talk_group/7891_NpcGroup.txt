# Talk Group: Gryaznov (NpcGroup 7891)

## Talk 0

Gryaznov: Ha ha! To think, after waiting all this time, this old man would finally have his day! Now, the one thing that is at stake, and which must be considered carefully, is the future of Sretomorozsk...

## Talk 1

Traveler: And what "day" would that be?
Gryaznov: Heh heh, my young friend. To be honest with you, even this old man wasn't expecting this...
Gryaznov: I have ridden the shifting tides of public service for over 30 years, and had been due to retire next year.
Gryaznov: Little did I think this little drama would land the mayor's mantle right on my shoulders. And now, once the public notice period is through, this old man here will have to take office...
Gryaznov: What was this old man to do? I had no intention of seeking further advancement at all. I was going to find myself a scenic little town where I could pass the days drinking and going ice fishing, heh...
Gryaznov: Unfortunately, it seems retirement must wait a while longer for this old man... But it is a burden one must shoulder for the future of Sretomorozsk. Don't you agree, my young friend?

## Talk 2

Traveler: "For the future of Sretomorozsk"...?
Gryaznov: Heh heh... My young friend, I wonder if you've heard about what happened here in Sretomorozsk earlier?
Gryaznov: To think that such scoundrels were found... right here among the ranks of public servants in Sretomorozsk... in our very own Snezhnaya!
Gryaznov: But now that the truth has come to light, that wicked mayor and liaison officer have been stripped of their posts. Even so, let me tell you, the townsfolk still need more reassurance...
Gryaznov: As the incoming mayor, I'll have to do something about this... Heh, our very first order of business must be to commission a new official stamp — one with this old man's own name on it...
Gryaznov: ...Ahem. At any rate, every action we take must be for the future of Sretomorozsk. Don't you agree, my young friend?

## Talk 3

Gryaznov: Goodbye, my young friend.
