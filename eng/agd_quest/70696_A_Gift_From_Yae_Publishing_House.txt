# A Gift From Yae Publishing House


## Talk 1

Yae Miko: Hello there, little one. What a lovely surprise. Where have your travels taken you recently?
Black Screen Text: You tell Miko about Minnie, the Legion of Soulblazers, and the Dragonspine Cross Country Challenge.
Yae Miko: Aww. They all sound quite adorable.
Yae Miko: It must have been quite an adventure for them, coming from far and wide, crossing national boundaries to meet with kindred spirits... Ah, it's a tale filled with the romance of youth.
Yae Miko: The Soulblazer Saga seems to have quite a strong following. Maybe it's time to run another round of reprints...
Yae Miko: With such a dedicated fanbase, it's only right to throw a token of gratitude their way.
Yae Miko: How about some free comic books? Hmm... There's quite a large group of you, I'll need to make sure there's enough to go around.
Traveler: Thanks.
Yae Miko: These are first-edition prints — you won't find them for sale anywhere. Take good care of them, now.
Traveler: I'll be sure to share them with the group.
