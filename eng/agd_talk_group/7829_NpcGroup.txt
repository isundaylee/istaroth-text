# Talk Group: Tsvetaeva (NpcGroup 7829)

## Talk 0

Tsvetaeva: Hmph! Can't they go any faster? Oh my goodness, why are these peasants all so slow...

## Talk 1

Traveler: Take it easy. Just take your seat and you'll be there soon.
Tsvetaeva: Pfft... That's easy for you to say. Would I not be sitting down if there's actually a seat for me?
Tsvetaeva: What can I do if a seat is too expensive for me? ...Tell me, what is someone like me to do? Someone who can only afford a standing-room ticket???

## Talk 2

Traveler: Just wait a little while. A seat might open up.
Tsvetaeva: *Sigh* You have no idea. The old days were so good. So, so good...

## Talk 3

Traveler: Huh? Are you still talking to me?
Tsvetaeva: You should know that I received my education at a school for daughters of the nobility in Snezhnograd. The kind of school where you dance a shawl dance at the graduation dinner, in front of the mayor and other important people.
Tsvetaeva: I even received a gold medal and a certificate of commendation for that, mind you. Look at me, I am a descendant of Koshkar. That's as close to nobility as you can get. And now, now, here I am, standing on a moving train!
Tsvetaeva: You may not know this, but this dress I have on now, I have three of those lying around at home. That's the same kind of dress that the couturiers sent to Pale Crown Palace back in the day... But now... This is simply... simply...

## Talk 4

Traveler: Um... Why don't you sell a couple of those for cash then?
Tsvetaeva: Oh my... What in the world do you think you're saying, oh kind stranger?
Tsvetaeva: Please tell me, how would anyone know my noble station if I were to sell all my dresses off?
Tsvetaeva: What a ridiculous notion! Simply unthinkable! You just don't understand — it's one thing to have to stand on the train, and another to look the part as well.
