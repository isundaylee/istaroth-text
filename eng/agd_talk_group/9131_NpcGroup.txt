# Talk Group: Nicole - Serenitea Pot (NpcGroup 9131)

## Talk 0

Nicole: Aha! If it isn't a certain someone's Folded-Dimensional Quadrant Shift Technology...
Nicole: I must say, combined with your artistic vision, the results truly are sparkling!

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Nicole: It feels like I've returned home — to Celestia, I mean. It, too, has an unusual relationship between its inner and outer dimensions, as I'm sure you can imagine.
Nicole: Though... am I allowed to be discussing this?
Nicole: Hmm, it should be fine. After all, these are just my memories, so it has nothing to do with my access to the knowledge base that I lost long ago.
Traveler: Knowledge base? Access...?
Nicole: Ah, well, I can't tell you much about that, I'm afraid. Just think of it as having the keys to a library, and then having them confiscated.
Nicole: Though, plenty of my sisters have secret copies of books they failed to return. There might just be one who kept a key... or maybe even two?
Nicole: The best students are always so calculating. It's better to be like me and be content with what you have.
Nicole: Not that I didn't keep any knowledge for myself! But that was more of a case of me keeping certain books by default, because I still had them the day that the library closed.
Traveler: You'd probably better not borrow any books in Mondstadt...
Nicole: Why not? Oh, I know. Alice did mention that, despite her languorous looks, the librarian there is a real bloodhound when it comes to hunting down overdue books.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to chat?
Nicole: I'd love nothing more! I'm not sure if you know this, but I do enjoy a good chat. Not that I have as many friends to chat with as I'd like — it's not easy when you have to keep up the pretense of being mysterious and dignified.
Nicole: What would you like to talk about?
Nicole: Astronomy, geography, bad-mouthing Alice... I'm an expert on all sorts of topics.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Bad-mouthing Alice.
> Nicole: Haha, I was only joking. How could I possibly talk bad of her behind her back?
> Nicole: This is a principle Anya taught me about how to conduct myself. Even if you're trying to be diplomatic and get along with everyone, you have to set limits.
> Nicole: The bottom line is, you should never betray your friends — even if it's only taking a few minor verbal swings when they're not around.
> Nicole: Alright, alright... Don't look at me like that, it's not like I'm afraid of her. Let me just think of something she won't mind me sharing.
> Nicole: The introductory magic that Alice designed for Rhinedottir used a ton of different templates. The whole thing is an affront to aesthetics.

Option 2:

> Traveler: Tell me something you normally couldn't...
> Nicole: That's a fantastic idea! This realm may well be cut off entirely from the outside world. Anything that's said within is probably impervious to prying ears.
> Nicole: However, a powerful retracing spell might still prove effective. For instance, if you mentioned a revised version of history in here, you wouldn't be able to remember anything upon leaving. In other words, there would be no loophole to exploit.
> Nicole: It may seem somewhat abstract, but that abstraction is necessary to ensure the rigor of the design.
> Nicole: I still think it's safer not to talk about such things. But... since it's you, maybe it's fine.


## Talk 3

Traveler: Is there anything you'd like to do?
Nicole: Seeing as there's hardly any living things nearby, I'd like to try screaming as loud as I can. Alice measured the volume last time, and it turns out that at my loudest, I can penetrate the minds of every living thing within a one-hundred-meter radius.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Please don't... I have other friends in here.
> Nicole: Oh, of course I'm not the only one you've invited!

Option 2:

> Traveler: ...Even Tubby wouldn't be safe.
> Nicole: Fair point. She really is an adorable little teapot spirit — I'd hate to disturb her.

Nicole: Perhaps no shouting for today, then. Let me think... It's not every day I get to visit realms like this. I should find something suitably fantastical to do.
Traveler: Try thinking of this place as a regular courtyard.
Nicole: Hm... Perhaps I've been too fixated on its metaphysical essence, and completely ignored its actual appearance. This might be a transcendental realm, but more importantly, it's your home.
Nicole: I needn't treat this place like some sort of testing ground. I should think of it as though I'm simply paying you a visit.
Nicole: In which case... I'd like to bury some treasure chests and design puzzles for you to find them.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Wouldn't you be better off taking part in Octavia's plans?
> Nicole: Huh, that's a good idea! Though I don't see why I can't do both.

Option 2:

> Traveler: Where do you plan to start?

Nicole: First, let's figure out how to make a fun maze using walls.
Traveler: Can you provide the treasure chests?
Nicole: I most certainly can! For an angel like me, the chests are more important than their contents. I'm sure I can get my hands on some pretty fantastic examples.
Nicole: As for what to put inside... I'm sure some Mora, and perhaps a few cabbages, will suffice.
