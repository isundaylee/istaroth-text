## Talk 0

## Talk 1

Traveler: Tenochtzitoc...?
Popocani: That's the name of this island we're on.
Popocani: In the local language, it translates to "cavern of stone and prickly pears."
Popocani: But, yeah... Prickly pears don't grow here anymore — Nochtli and Nanas both... Maybe it's because the nutrients in the soil aren't what they used to be.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Easybreeze Holiday Resort...?
Popocani: Huh? Didn't you come here after receiving an invitation from the resort? Uhm, okay, let me give you a brief overview...
Popocani: The Easybreeze Holiday Resort is a large summer resort located on the coast and divided into several different regions. It was decided to build the resort here in Tenochtzitoc, in the far west of Natlan.
Popocani: Now, all you need to do is walk that way along the beach to get to the reception. Make sure to pick up your commemorative album from the resort. After that, you're free to enjoy your holiday.
