# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 7679

## Talk 0

Viliana: Never thought I'd meet a fae like this...

## Talk 1

Traveler: And you are...
Viliana: Ah, hello. My name is Viliana Velesovna Belozerskaya. I'm an adventurer from Novokitezhgrad...
Viliana: ...I haven't officially joined the Adventurers' Guild, though. Traveling like this is the absolute most my mother can tolerate. If I took it a step further and joined the Guild, she'd practically melt with rage.
Viliana: Anyway, I'm trying to gather inspiration for my book, Night Murmurs from the Far Border, while traveling. It's a collection of stories about all kinds of fae friends!

## Talk 2

Traveler: Tell me a little about the fae...

Viliana: Hmm... How should I put this... Snezhnaya has such a massive diversity of fae that, despite being one of them, I still have to consult ancient books just to recognize certain names.

Viliana: Take Miss Linnea as an example. Before coming to Nod-Krai, I never dreamed of encountering a live Augury Bird in the flesh... Ahhh, I'm so lucky...

Viliana: So, I'm planning to spend a few more days here. Who knows, I might even run into some other fae friends!

## Talk 3

Traveler: You're... a Snegurochka?

Viliana: Huh? Hmm... Judging by your expression, you haven't been to Snezhnaya yet, have you?

Viliana: Hehe, you're right! Compared to Augury Birds like Miss Linnea, whom you'd only ever read about in books, we Snegurochka are among the most numerous fae out there.

Viliana: It's just that... as you can probably guess, our kind generally loathe the heat. So we seldom leave the northern snowfields.

Viliana: For my mother, a titled lord honored by Her Majesty the Tsaritsa, letting me travel solo like this is an incredible concession. Meeting you is truly a privilege made possible by her rare leniency.

Viliana: My distant aunt, on the other hand, the Grand Duchess Pavlina of the House of Hesperides, has completely sealed off her territory, refusing to let a single one of her subjects venture out into the world...

Viliana: Though... I suppose she has her reasons. Anyway, once you visit our beautiful city, you won't be surprised by Snegurochka anymore!

## Talk 4
