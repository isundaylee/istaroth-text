# Talk Group: GadgetGroup - 37002

## Talk 0

Unknown Role (None): Phobos has guided you here, golden-haired outlander.

Unknown Role (None): And you are...

Unknown Role (None): Lucius Patricius am I, a musician of our empire.

Unknown Role (None): Be not alarmed, outlander. Unlike my kin who have been inundated by their dreams, I still maintain the tranquility of my melody.

Unknown Role (None): What is this place?

Unknown Role (None): This was, once upon a time, Portus Anticus. The most prosperous port in the empire. Today, it is our sepulcher.

Unknown Role (None): If you seek the bronze bell that once dispersed the boundless ocean fog, then continue upward. Mayhap time and rust have not utterly devoured her.

Unknown Role (None): You seem pretty calm about all this...

Unknown Role (None): The fall of the empire, the death of our God King... All these things were predestined. Getting emotional about events long-fated is unwise.

Unknown Role (None): Why should one agonize over a matter that is merely a part of life? In truth, every moment in life is worthy of our tears.

Unknown Role (None): Moreover, of what benefit is sorrow and resentment, if punishment cannot wipe away sin?

Unknown Role (None): See you.

Unknown Role (None): Farewell, golden-haired outlander. May your music be tranquil.
