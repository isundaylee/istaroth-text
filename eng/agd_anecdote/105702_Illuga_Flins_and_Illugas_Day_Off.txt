# Illuga: Flins and Illuga's Day Off

At the Cliffwatch Camp, you run into Illuga once again. He is in the middle of a conversation with Flins...

At Cliffwatch Camp, you run into Illuga and Flins once again. According to them, Aino is heading to the Ratnik headquarters to conduct a class. For these two Ratniki, the top priority right now is figuring out a way to get out of studying without raising suspicion...

## Talk 0

Illuga: Well, Mr. Flins, now we're completely out of options.
Illuga: Who on earth came up with the idea to have Miss Aino attend the big meeting at headquarters? It couldn't possibly have been her request, could it?
Flins: It was me.
Illuga: You!?
Illuga: Please tell me this is part of some new "trick."
Flins: It certainly is. When a solitary leaf takes to the breeze, it cannot fail but draw the eye. Yet, when a multitude of leaves depart in unison, the singular form can no longer be distinguished.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Ah, I get it.

Option 2:

> Traveler: The more people there are, the easier it is to skip class.

Flins: Sharp as ever, I see. Still, I must express my great regret for being unable to participate in such an enlightening assembly.
Illuga: Be that as it may, Mr. Flins, look at all the "autumn leaves" in this building. I imagine all of them would love to fly away quickly...
Illuga: I can already imagine all their excuses: Stomachaches, headaches, outstanding assignments, emergency deployments—

Option 1:

> Traveler: How are you so familiar with these?

Option 2:

> Traveler: You've skipped your fair share too, haven't you?

Illuga: Nope. They've used up all the excuses and left none for me.
Illuga: I mean, good heavens, we once had twenty Ratniki down with stomachaches, fifteen who hurt their legs in a fall, six struck by fevers, and eight with urgent matters to attend to.
Illuga: If an outsider saw this, they'd think our headquarters had been raided by the Wild Hunt!
Flins: In all fairness to them, if this place were genuinely to face a sudden incursion of the Wild Hunt, I am confident that they'd be here in full force.
Illuga: Ahaha.. ha. Very funny, sir, but I have no intention of attending a meeting with the Wild Hunt.
Flins: And neither do I. Yet as we both know, the Wild Hunt seem to have a special fondness for playing the part of uninvited guests...
Illuga: Anyhow, I'm usually the only one left in the entire meeting room. And then, I still have to deliver the meeting minutes to the absentees after it's done.
Flins: Then I must extend my sincere gratitude for your efforts.
Illuga: Could you come by yourself next time, then?
Flins: As I said before, such "frustrations" likewise possess an uncanny propensity to show up without invitation.
Illuga: *sigh* When can this poor leaf finally drift away...
Flins: No need to drift away completely... just a little further than the nearest leaf.
Illuga: Thank you, but I'm the leaf nearest to you, aren't I?
Flins: Rest assured, Young Master, we'll all make landfall together. Besides, I'm inclined to believe that no autumn leaf ever falls where it shouldn't.

## Talk 1
