# The Unbowed One's Gratitude

With these materials in hand, Odette should be able to repair her music box...


## Talk 1

(Go to the Korolevskiy Theater)

Odette: That should do it...
Odette: Let's see...
Traveler: You did it.
Odette: Close your eyes.
Traveler: Hm?
Odette: I've done all I could, but I'm by no means a professional.
Odette: If there are still flaws in the work, I don't want you to see them... So, please. Close your eyes.
Traveler: ...
Traveler: ...!
Traveler: (Seems like a success to me...)
Traveler: Can I open my eyes now?
Odette: ...
Traveler: ...Odette?

Option 1:

> Traveler: (Quietly open your eyes...)
> Odette: ...
> Odette: Sorry, I got a little... emotional just now.

Option 2:

> Traveler: (Wait for Odette to reply...)
> Odette: ...
> Odette: Sorry, I got a little emotional just now. You can open your eyes.

Odette: I suppose I wasn't expecting to hear such a beautiful melody from it after all this time.
Traveler: (Ah... So she asked me to close my eyes earlier because she didn't want me to see her getting emotional...)
Traveler: Want to talk about it?
Odette: ...Alright, if you can keep a secret.
Odette: I mentioned earlier that this was a birthday present. In truth, it's also the last birthday present I received from my mother.
Odette: It's... complicated. After that birthday, our family started to struggle financially, and life got busier and busier...
Odette: But most of all, my mother and I gradually drifted apart.
Odette: She always said that my dancing lacked emotional warmth, but I kept myself closed-off, and refused to listen to her guidance.
Odette: Now, I realize how childish that was. So, when I found this music box in the attic, it felt like a symbol of happier times.
Odette: But in a twist of irony, the melody it played was harsh and distorted... Almost like it was reminding me how much I've neglected it and my family all these years.
Odette: And now, I can't help but wonder...
Odette: If I can pluck a music box free from the grasp of time, might I be able to restore a relationship I wore away with my own hands?

Option 1:

> Traveler: That's a hard question to answer.

Option 2:

> Traveler: But it never hurts to try.

Odette: I will.
Odette: In fact, I plan on doing so tonight.
Odette: But I should thank you first. While I originally had no intention of relying on help, the repairs only went so smoothly thanks to the materials you provided.
Odette: Besides, you've decorated this place so very elegantly. It really suits my taste.
Odette: Just a pity it won't scare off nosy theater-goers anymore.
Odette: I'll be sure to save you a ticket for my future shows.
