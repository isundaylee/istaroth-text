Talalikhin: #You seem to be in good shape, Traveler. Shall we begin the Shadow Realm combat simulation?

Option 1:

> Traveler: Let's get started.

Option 2:

> Traveler: Let's talk about you.
> Talalikhin: Me? My name is Talalikhin Gogol. I serve as an ordinary clerk at the Adventurers' Guild headquarters...
> Talalikhin: If you still remember the recent Adventurers' Guild Development Conference and Adventurers' Tournament... I had the honor of assisting with some of the arrangements during the event.
> Traveler: An ordinary clerk... investigating the Shadow Realm?
> Talalikhin: It is only a personal interest of mine... together with a few rather unfortunate experiences. They have given me a somewhat deeper understanding of the Shadow Realm than the average person...
> Talalikhin: I, too, wish to do what I can to help reduce the harm adventurers suffer because of it.
> Talalikhin: According to the cases recorded by the Guild, the probability of surviving the Shadow Realm while retaining one's basic sanity is less than nine in ten thousand...
> Talalikhin: However, that does not mean we should assume that falling into the Shadow Realm is certain death. It is always better to do all one can than to give up outright.
> Talalikhin: If one can hone their firearm skills and use of fortifications to a truly masterful level, perhaps they may yet find a slim chance of survival.
> Traveler: You think more like a soldier than a clerk.
> Talalikhin: Yes... Perhaps I was influenced by my uncle and his closest friends.
> Talalikhin: When I was young, I could never quite hide my fascination with firearms. Uncle Gordienko's hunting rifle, in particular, was something I would study again and again.
> Talalikhin: Whenever Uncle Sidorenko, Nomokonov, and Vasiliy invited him to go hunting, my uncle would take me along.
> Talalikhin: They taught me how to bring down the fiercest beasts from three hundred paces with a single shot, and how to conceal my tracks in the snow...
> Talalikhin: After my fifth hunt, they even let me confront some monsters on my own, to test whether I had learned to turn distance into a means of protection.
> Traveler: That's quite an experience.
> Talalikhin: Regrettably, my uncle and the three of them later went their separate ways, and saw less and less of one another...
> Talalikhin: If even one of them had still been in Snezhnaya, Uncle Gordienko might never have let his guard down and been caught up in the Shadow Realm incident...
> Talalikhin: Ah... My apologies, I seem to have rambled a little. We should not lose ourselves in memories and what-ifs. Let us look ahead instead.

Option 3:

> Traveler: Has anyone else taken part in the simulation?
> Talalikhin: Before the adventurer you just met, twelve others had attempted it. None of them performed as well as she did, however, and not one managed to complete the first simulation.

> Talalikhin: In terms of overall ability, I believe she is comparable to young Chichikova. That would put her at roughly one-tenth... perhaps even one-fifth of Mr. Alyosha's level. She has tremendous potential.
> Traveler: You know Alyosha?
> Talalikhin: I have benefited from his help indirectly. And, out of professional habit, I once made a rough assessment of his abilities based on scattered pieces of information. The conclusion was rather astonishing...

> Talalikhin: As for the Guild, Lesnova's fiance, Radomir, has also returned to Snezhnograd, and she has been in exceptionally high spirits ever since.
> Talalikhin: That said, Lesnova is not especially good at planning or managing her time. She often misses scheduled meeting points, and on quite a few occasions, her team has had to proceed shorthanded because of it...
