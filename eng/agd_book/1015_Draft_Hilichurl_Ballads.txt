# Draft Hilichurl Ballads

## Hilichurl Ballad: Recital Draft

*Draft Hilichurl Ballads — Volume 1 of 3*

Olah! Olah!
Yoyo mosi mita!
Nye, nye mosi mita,
Yeye mosi gusha!
Mosi gusha, mosi tiga,
Yeye kucha kucha!

## Hilichurl Ballad: Narrative Draft

*Draft Hilichurl Ballads — Volume 2 of 3*

Biadam! Ye dada nesina,
Ye dada nesina nunu,
Nunu shato!
Shato celi, shato lata,
Ye dada nesina,
Nini zido!
Nini zido!

## Hilichurl Ballad: Lyrical Draft

*Draft Hilichurl Ballads — Volume 3 of 3*

Gusha, gusha,
Gusha muhe kucha.
Kucha mita pupu,
Pupu si kucha mita.
Mi mosi mita,
Ye mosi gusha,
Kucha.
