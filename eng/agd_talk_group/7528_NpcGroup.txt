# Talk Group: Dorofeya (NpcGroup 7528)

## Talk 0

Dorofeya: Ha! Just look at the children these days! See how magnificent and beautiful they are... Even I cannot help but be completely mesmerized by them!

## Talk 1

Traveler: You are...?
Dorofeya: Oh dear... If you are posing a question like that to me, my star must have truly faded.
Dorofeya: My name is Dorofeya. I was a singer with the Korolevskiy Troupe for a few years.
Dorofeya: I suppose you can say I exited the spotlight early. These days, I am but a leisurely observer whiling my days away, courtesy of my husband's land holdings and a rather tiny income.
Dorofeya: It is also a distinct blessing to be here, far from the stage, lending my applause to those who have come after me like this.

## Talk 2

Traveler: See you later.
Dorofeya: Goodbye. Do come by the theater when you can!

## Talk 3

Traveler: So, you used to be an actor?
Dorofeya: Hehe... Indeed. You might not be able to tell looking at me now, but back in the day, I was the reigning star for quite a few years too. The bouquets showered upon me were simply countless.
Dorofeya: But... as you can see, youth and beauty are but fleeting illusions.
Dorofeya: The moment the singer loses her brilliant luster, all the nobles and rich men who had worshiped her so will simply vanish in an instant.
Dorofeya: For what they adore is that flawless illusion on stage, and not the person behind it. This is something I know intimately, as someone who wandered and begged in the streets of Zolnyy as a child.
Dorofeya: This is why I chose to make my departure at the absolute zenith of my career. Fortunately, my husband is a nobleman who, dull as he may be, has a heart that is entirely true... As you well know, noblemen like that are truly hard to find.

## Talk 4

Traveler: What do you think about the current crop of actors?
Dorofeya: I must admit, there is a remarkable wealth of talent among them! If we exclude the veterans like Vodyanitsa entirely... the one who has really caught everyone's eye of late is Miss Odette. She truly is magnificent.
Akvilina: Violetta... Ah, that's Odette's mother. We worked together in the past. Mind you, while the lass certainly cannot compare to an almost monstrously gifted dancer like her mother...
Akvilina: When I watch Odette dance, I always see... Hmm, how should I put it? Yes, I detect an absolute passion for ballet in every move she makes. That characteristic, coupled with a certain stubbornness that compels her to go all out in every performance, makes her truly one-of-a-kind.
Akvilina: Everyone is trying their best to dazzle and shine on stage — how wonderful is that?
