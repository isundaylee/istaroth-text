# Talk Group: Ignatov (NpcGroup 7784)

## Talk 0

Ignatov: "Those skilled at deceiving themselves live the happiest lives..."

## Talk 1

Traveler: ...What?
Ignatov: Ah...! My apologies. Did I interrupt your meal? I must have read another line from my book out loud...
Ignatov: How may I help you? Would you like to order, or should I clear the table?

## Talk 2

Traveler: See you.
Ignatov: Ah... yes, of course. Goodbye...

## Talk 3

Traveler: No, nothing like that... What book were you reading?
Ignatov: Ah... It's Punishment. Mr. Zheleznov gave it to me. Please don't tell Tolstaya, or she'll sigh at me again...
Ignatov: But please don't get the wrong idea. She is my best friend. If she hadn't convinced my parents, I wouldn't be allowed to work here...
Ignatov: My parents never would've let me work here otherwise... B-But they're not bad people. They've always treated me well...

## Talk 4

Traveler: Somehow, everyone in this story ends up being nice...
Ignatov: ...My parents only worry because I've always been frail. They don't want me working and getting sick.
Ignatov: And Tolstaya wants me to read something else because she thinks... heh, these books are making me gloomy.
Ignatov: I know they care about me. They've always protected me, and I'm grateful for that. But... I'm sorry. Maybe I really am strange...
Ignatov: I keep wanting to go outside — to step out of this warm, gentle spring and into the freezing ice pit myself.
Ignatov: Mr. Zheleznov said it meant I was looking for a kind of growth that was mine and mine alone. Then he handed me a huge stack of books and said maybe I'd find the answer in them...
Ignatov: And then... hehe, Tolstaya came over with her hands on her hips and showed him out. He even made a face at me on his way out.
Ignatov: ...Ah! I-I really shouldn't be standing here chatting. I need to get back to work. I'm sorry. If you need anything, please just call me.
