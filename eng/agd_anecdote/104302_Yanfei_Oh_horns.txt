# Yanfei: Oh, horns...

A legal adviser is troubled by something...

On Hiisi Island, you meet Yanfei once more. She has apparently revealed her adeptus blood to Lauma, who takes the news rather calmly — even allowing her access to the library of the Frostmoon Scions. It looks like everything's going smoothly.
