## Talk 2

Unknown Role (None): What sort of technical conundrum is this?

Unknown Role (None): One once developed a supreme housekeeping machine to perform domestic chores such as sweeping and tidying.

Unknown Role (None): To enhance its functionality, one added a sound response module so that it could be operated with voice commands.

Unknown Role (None): ...Very well, we may consider this conundrum resolved. Now, back to our conversation.
