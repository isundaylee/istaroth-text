# The Commission's Commission


## Talk 1

Unknown Role (None): Honored guest, a moment, if you please. I trust you are the Traveler of whom Miss Kamisato has previously spoken?

Unknown Role (None): Indeed I am.

Unknown Role (None): Then please forgive my intrusion. I am Madarame Hyakubei, a retainer of the Kamisato Clan.

Unknown Role (None): I have heard tell of your great deeds, and Miss Kamisato thinks extremely highly of you. No doubt you are one of great honor, and worthy of our trust.

Unknown Role (None): I have long since wished for the pleasure of making your acquaintance. Unfortunately, I have been unable to extricate myself from my duties recently.

Unknown Role (None): Too kind, too kind.

Unknown Role (None): On the contrary, it is you who are too modest. Even the Yashiro Commissioner Kamisato Ayato himself has mentioned you before. He, too, believes that you are worthy of our trust.

Unknown Role (None): What do you want?

Unknown Role (None): Haha, you are every bit as forthright as the stories claim. Please excuse my habitual meandering.

Unknown Role (None): I'll get to the point. Since the Yashiro Commission oversees worship at the shrine, we often have the occasion to hear about people's earnest ambitions.

Unknown Role (None): Miss Kamisato, being as kind and thoughtful as she is, convinced the Yashiro Commissioner to allocate resources to help people realize their ambitions.

Unknown Role (None): After some deliberation, he decided to establish a public commission system as the means of achieving this. I am the one he assigned to organize it.

Unknown Role (None): The remuneration is more than ample, but the problem is that there are too many commissions and not enough people to handle them... If you were willing to lend your assistance, that would be wonderful.

Unknown Role (None): If you are unable to start right away, that's no problem. You can simply find me in a few days' time when your schedule is freer.

Unknown Role (None): I have compiled the details of the commissions that we've received so far, if you wish to take a closer look.
