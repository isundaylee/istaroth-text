## Talk 1 (variant 1)

Lauma: How about... "Lost Tales of the Moonchanters"?
Ehrnrooth: Ah! L—L—Lady Lauma! I—I, uh, I— I didn't mean to... Well, I guess I did mean to...
Ehrnrooth: S—S—Sorry, I'll stop with the nonsense now!
Lauma: Pfft. You misunderstand. I'm not angry. I was serious about that name.
Lauma: I had this library built precisely to better record our history, such that those who come after us have records they may consult.
Lauma: And if one were to tell our history, it would be remiss of us to not speak of the Moonchanters.
Lauma: Yet... the texts that tell their tales are either challenging and cryptic, or as hefty as they are humdrum.
Lauma: Never mind you. Even I... *chuckle* Let's just say I've had to exercise much patience and perseverance in trudging through them.
Ehrnrooth: W—W—Wait, so you heard everything I said? I, well, they're actually not that hard to read. Just a couple pages a day, and boom, you're done before you know it. I mean, I've done it. Honest...
Lauma: Even so, it might be a tall order to expect people to read in this way and still retain the information for any length of time.
Lauma: You said that you wanted to write down those stories about the Moonchanters into a compilation. I think that's a good idea.
Lauma: A book that presents those tales in an interesting and humorous manner could go a long way to inspiring interest in history amongst the children.
Lauma: And this interest may then guide them to undertake more serious historical research later down the line.
Lauma: Still, this task will be far from easy, Ehrnrooth. Would you be willing to help me?
Ehrnrooth: O—O—Of course I can... Well, err, I might...?
Lauma: I have faith in you. You have not spent much time as a priestess, but you are no one's inferior in wisdom or texts studied.
Lauma: I believe you are more than up to the task of composing such a text.
Ehrnrooth: I... I'll give it a try.
Lauma: I'll leave it to you, then. Hmm... As the current Moonchanter, allow me to share a story with you as well.
Ehrnrooth: W—Would you really? Err, um, pl—please wait one moment, Lady Lauma! I haven't any paper or pen on hand, and I'm n—not mentally prepared either! Could you... tell me the story later...?
Lauma: Of course. Just come to see me once your preparations are complete. I'll make myself available.
Ehrnrooth: Yes, Lady Lauma!
