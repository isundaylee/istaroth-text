Varesa: I guess, but this place is different!
Varesa: I feel so... at ease. It's hard to find this feeling anywhere else.

Traveler: Well, you'd be right, because I did arrange this place just for you!
Varesa: R—Really? Oh, wow. I... I...
Varesa: I can't believe a great hero did all this just for me! That's an honor even famous people never experience. It's like a real life power-up!

Varesa: C'mon, gimme a fist bump, hero!
Traveler: Guess we have a similar style, then!
Varesa: R—Really? Oh, wow. I... I...

Traveler: You can find happiness anywhere!

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to have a chat with me?
Varesa: Sure! Let's chat the day away. I have nothing else to do...
Varesa: Oh, but chit-chat is always better with a few snacks a drinks... A full course meal works too, hehe!

Traveler: Do you like cooking, Varesa?
Varesa: Oh, it's just a hobby! Honestly, I'm not very good, hehe... It takes me a while to get the hang of new recipes.
Varesa: Like, when I first tried making Puff Pops. I kind of... blew up the kitchen. The pan flew so far, it hit Master Iansan in the head...
Varesa: ...*sigh* That pan has never been the same since.
Varesa: Now, I'm not allowed to cook in the house. So, I'm stuck with whatever I can make outside with a campfire.
Varesa: It usually doesn't turn out great, but making something myself always brings a certain sense of accomplishment, and that's something you can't get from a restaurant.
Traveler: I agree! There's something special about homemade dishes.
Varesa: Next time, let's make something together! You can bring the veggies and condiments, and I'll be in charge of fruit and protein! We'll come up with something amazing!

Varesa: Although, that name gives us a lot to live up to... We'd need to add a lot of intense flavor and roast the meat on super high heat...
Varesa: Which also means... We should find somewhere with less... safety hazards, considering my history with cooking accidents.
Traveler: It's a deal!

Varesa: Hehe, cool! Bring it on!
Traveler: I've had my fair share of kitchen mishaps, too.
Traveler: You must be quite the chef!
