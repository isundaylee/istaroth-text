## Talk 5

(Talk to Auni)

Viljami: During my years at sea, I once heard a call coming from nowhere. The others didn't react at all, but I was captivated almost instantly.

Auni: It must have been a voice from another world. I've heard it too.

Viljami: Only later did I realize... That call carried with it every dream that never made it to the far shore. You might even call it a gathering of regrets.

Auni: You should bring an Icy Pebble with you. Look through the hole at its center, and you'll see who's been calling out to you.

Viljami: But back then, I was still a hot-headed young fool. Even danger looked like an opportunity to me. So I charged straight in without a second thought.

Auni: They say the hole at the center of the Icy Pebble is a gateway to another world... But how exactly are you supposed to cross through it?

Viljami: And after that... the sea and its raging winds took everything from me — my wealth, my honor, and even my family.

Viljami: Adventures driven by ambition and desire never end well.

Auni: Ah, it's stuck...

Linnea: Let me help you get that off. And next time, try not to do that again, okay?

Auni: Linnea! What brings you back here?

Linnea: I've been collecting Icy Pebbles nearby. I saw you chatting and thought I'd come say hello.

Auni: Oh! Maybe you could really use an Icy Pebble to open a gateway to another world, Linnea! If anyone could pull it off, it would be you...

Linnea: Even I can't do that. Didn't I tell you before? That's just a folk tale people stitched together. There's no solid evidence behind it.

Viljami: Ugh... Looks like this isn't gonna work.

Linnea: ...Oh. So you were listening.

Viljami: Ahem... no. I'm not the person I used to be anymore. These days, I'm content with what I have, and I want nothing else.

Auni: What a pity. Then I'll help you collect Icy Pebbles too, Linnea.

Linnea: That'd be great!
