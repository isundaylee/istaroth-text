# A Small Venture


## Talk 1

Dhabit: The publishing trade hardly makes a living these days. Maybe I should just focus on investments...

Traveler: Let me see the investment coupons.

Dhabit: Here you go. 2,000 Mora per coupon, with each investment tenure maturing weekly. Now then, into the pouch it goes...

Dhabit: ...Ugh!

Traveler: What's wrong?

Dhabit: N—No, nothing at all. I just didn't expect you to stop by, Lady Nefer. Are you... perhaps interested in buying an investment coupon too?

Nefer: Oh, no, I'll pass. I'm just here with my friend to have a look around.

Nefer: I've heard your investments have been thriving, Mr. Dhabit. Seeing it with my own eyes, I can see the rumor holds true.

Dhabit: Oh, it's nothing, nothing, haha... just a small venture to make ends meet.

Traveler: Can this really make money?

Nefer: Of course. Mr. Dhabit is an excellent businessman — follow his lead, and you won't lose. Right, Mr. Dhabit?

Dhabit: Yes, yes... haha...

Traveler: Well then, let's see how my luck holds today.

Dhabit: T—Today, you say... W—Well, funny that you should say that! What you've bought this fine day is our brand-new... short-term investment coupon! And here's the thing — profits and losses on this one are to be settled immediately!

Paimon: Kinda feels like you just made all that up...

Dhabit: Uh... Don't worry about it. A—Anyway, thanks for buying our short-term investment coupon!

Dhabit: Here's your investment return. Have a wonderful day!

Traveler: Well... making money is always a good thing, I suppose...

Traveler: Come on, Paimon, let's go buy some sugar sculptures.

Paimon: Really? Heehee, then Paimon wants one shaped like a little bird! That's Paimon's favorite!

Nefer: Then I'll take my leave as well. Like I said, Mr. Dhabit, you've truly lived up to your reputation — an honest merchant through and through. May your business prosper.

Dhabit: Thank you for the kind words, Lady Nefer...

Dhabit: And, about that matter I asked your help with last time...

Nefer: We've found what you're looking for, but...

Nefer: Do have patience, Mr. Dhabit. One step at a time — that's how proper business is done.

Dhabit: O—Oh yes, you're right, of course... I'll leave it in your capable hands...
