# Talk Group: Nadyushenka (NpcGroup 7541)

## Talk 0

Nadyushenka: There are just too many risks with this trip. I'd better think of a perfect plan before I set out.

## Talk 1

Traveler: "Risks"...?
Nadyushenka: Yes, risks... And who are you!? Gave me a real fright back there.
Nadyushenka: You look like an experienced adventurer to me, so you must understand my concerns.
Nadyushenka: I mean, what if I get pegged as a swindler? Just because the customs at the border are different from home?
Nadyushenka: Or what if the train I'm on derails, and I'm seated far, far away from the emergency exit? How am I supposed to get out then?
Nadyushenka: Or what if I have to spend the night out in the wilderness for some reason? With the air so cold, am I likely to suffocate in my sleep???
Nadyushenka: Besides... what if a Weasel Thief robs me blind in my sleep? Or what if someone takes my map from me after hearing that I'm out on a long trip for the very first time?
Nadyushenka: Adventures can be so tough... Maybe I should think of a perfect plan first before I set out.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Hope all goes well.
Nadyushenka: Thanks. Seeya!

## Talk 3

Traveler: It's not as hard as you think. You just have to be determined!
Nadyushenka: Hmm... Maybe you're right.
Nadyushenka: But what if... What if I lose my Mora to a cheat? Hmm. I suppose I could go be a part-timer for money somewhere then.
Nadyushenka: But what if the mine wouldn't hire me? I guess I could be a kvass promoter at a restaurant too.
Nadyushenka: And if the restaurant doesn't sell kvass, I can... I can...
Nadyushenka: *Sigh* Adventures really are tough. I'd better think some more. Yes, I should think some more before I set out for sure.
