# Talk Group: Avreliya (NpcGroup 7453)

## Talk 0

Avreliya: Now where has Rozh run off to this time...? Ah, hello there, outlander. Is there anything I can help you with?

## Talk 1

Traveler: Who're you?
Avreliya: Avreliya, officer of the Druzhna, at your service. I'm currently carrying out patrol and supervisory duties.
Avreliya: If you run into any trouble, such as being harassed by certain heavy-handed individuals, please feel free to ask me for help.
Avreliya: As Her Majesty's most loyal sword and shield, the Druzhna will not just stand by. This is also a matter of preserving Snezhnaya's dignity. After all, our Fatui colleagues have never cared much for such things.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Rozh?
Avreliya: *sigh* That was embarrassing. Praporshchik Rozhdestvensky is my fi— a friend I have known since childhood.
Avreliya: He was born into a noble family, and even joined the Druzhna on the strength of his own talent. And yet he spends all day neglecting his duties, ignoring discipline, and idling about...!
Avreliya: Take your eyes off him for a moment, and he will find some new ways to cause trouble! If he's not off flirting with young ladies, he's drinking with that lot outside the city...
Avreliya: If I hadn't been keeping an eye on him, who knows how far he would drag the Druzhna's discipline through the mud...!
Avreliya: Ah... My apologies. I should not be burdening you with complaints. Just... please pretend you heard none of that.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Goodbye.
Avreliya: Goodbye. If you run into any trouble, please come to me at any time.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Do you have something against the Fatui?
Avreliya: Not at all. As fellow servants of Her Majesty, we should work together as one. And now with a crisis looming in the horizon, this is no time to dwell on old grievances.
Avreliya: Whether our Fatui colleagues understand that, however, is not for me to decide. As you can see, all I can do is govern myself, and remain ever loyal to Her Majesty.
