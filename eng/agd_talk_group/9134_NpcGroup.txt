# Talk Group: Odette - Serenitea Pot (NpcGroup 9134)

## Talk 0

Odette: It's nice and quiet here.
Odette: Thank you for the invitation.

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Odette: This place is completely different from Snezhnaya. It's so warm that it almost feels a bit alien.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Does that mean you don't like it?
> Odette: No.

Option 2:

> Traveler: So you like it here, then?
> Odette: Yes.

Odette: The breeze and the sun are gentle and cozy... a rare sight in my hometown.
Odette: It's as though my body, which was just immersed in cold water, has suddenly been plunged into hot water instead. I like the feeling, but it takes some getting used to.
Odette: Perhaps I'll need to take it slow.
Traveler: Sure. You're welcome to visit whenever.
Odette: Thanks.
Odette: If there's still time after my daily training and ballet rehearsals, I'll visit more often.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to chat?
Odette: Sure.
Odette: ...
Odette: What about?
Traveler: Not used to starting conversations yourself, huh?
Odette: Yeah. Or rather... I'm more used to settings like fan meet-and-greets or theater open houses.
Odette: People ask, I answer.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Then let's talk about how you've been lately...
> Odette: Things are a bit busy right now, but it's not entirely unmanageable.
> Odette: I don't want to let others down, much less compromise on my own goals, so being a little busy is neither here nor there.
> Odette: Most of the time, it's just a bit of mental fatigue. But my physical sensations are manageable. Pain has no real effect on me.
> Traveler: That's not okay, right?
> Odette: It's perfectly fine. My schedule includes breaks, too.
> Traveler: When do you normally take them?
> Odette: When I sleep at night.
> Traveler: ...That's exactly the problem.
> Odette: And right now, chatting with you also counts as a break, too.

Option 2:

> Traveler: Why don't we... talk about something interesting you've experienced?
> Odette: Something interesting?
> Odette: Well, there was one thing that happened when I was a child.
> Odette: My father secretly took me to see my mother's performance, and after the curtain call, he led me to the dressing room to surprise her.
> Odette: I loved the smile she had on her face, and I loved that my father would prepare such special surprises for her.
> Odette: So, I followed his example and secretly began preparing a gift for my mother, too.
> Odette: I found a film recording of a ballet at home, and while they were out, I followed along, learning the moves bit by bit.
> Odette: Finally, one day, I felt I could almost complete the routine, so I called my parents over to witness that moment.
> Odette: Hehe. What I didn't know at the time was that the film was mirrored, so I learned the moves completely backwards.
> Traveler: Were they happy?
> Odette: Very much so. It was an even bigger success than my father's surprise operation that same day.
> Odette: Those truly were happy days.


## Talk 3

Traveler: Anything you'd like to do?
Odette: I'd like you to critique my dancing. Please be completely honest.
Traveler: But you're the professional...
Odette: Ah, sorry, I didn't make myself clear.
Odette: What I want is feedback from the people around me, rather than fans. From ordinary audience members, rather than professional dancers.
Odette: Listening only to the applause from below the stage can cloud your judgment. At the same time, a professional's perspective, like my mother's, often focuses more on the form and difficulty of the action.
Odette: I need to hear more diverse opinions to broaden my horizons. Your feedback will be closer to an actual viewing experience, and...
Odette: ...Your suggestions will be based on your own emotions and aesthetic standards, reflecting your personal ideals.
Odette: I admit to being a bit curious about those.

Option 1:

> Traveler: What if I compliment you too?
> Odette: You've seen a lot, so I trust your judgment. I'll take whatever you say as motivation to keep pushing on.

Option 2:

> Traveler: And if I don't like it?
> Odette: That's okay. I'll reflect on your comments and aim to impress you next time.

Traveler: Looks like I can't refuse.
Odette: Of course.
Odette: Let me know when you're ready. I can start anytime.
