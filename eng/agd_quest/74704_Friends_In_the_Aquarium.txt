# Friends... In the Aquarium?

Columbina seems to be admiring the aquarium...


## Talk 1

(Head near the aquarium tank)

Paimon: Hey, Columbina! Are you admiring the fish in our aquarium?
The Damselette (Columbina): Mmhmm. You've done an amazing job with this place. There are so many fish I've never even seen before.
Paimon: Have you been to an aquarium before?
The Damselette (Columbina): No, though I saw a few fish tanks in Snezhnaya. But my eyes weren't truly open back then, so I never really appreciated things properly.
Traveler: What do you think now?
The Damselette (Columbina): It's beautiful. I never knew the underwater world was so vibrant and colorful. It's like... living in eternal springtime.
The Damselette (Columbina): Oh, look at that fish. Don't you think it looks just like Sandrone? And those two over there, one following behind the other... They're like the two of you!
Paimon: Hey, you're right! Now that you mention it, they really do look like us.
The Damselette (Columbina): That busy-looking one over there reminds me of Lauma. And that one in the coral seems to be quietly observing everything, just like Lady Nefer.
Paimon: Uhm... Paimon gets the feeling you like matching all your friends to fish...
The Damselette (Columbina): Not exactly. I like my friends, so I naturally find myself thinking about them. Even when I'm looking at fish.
Paimon: Wow! Paimon's really happy to hear you say that! So, are there any other fish that remind you of someone?
The Damselette (Columbina): Hmm... I've made a lot of new friends lately. I'm afraid I can't find a fish to match every single one of them.
Paimon: That makes sense. Anyway, we'll keep adding more fishies to the aquarium! You should come back from time to time — maybe you'll be able to find even more of your friends next time you're here!
The Damselette (Columbina): ...You're going to put my friends in the aquarium?
Paimon: Of course not! Paimon's talking about fishie lookalike friends, you big silly!
