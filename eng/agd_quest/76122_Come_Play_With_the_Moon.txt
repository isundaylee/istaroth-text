# Come Play With the Moon

Columbina seems to have something she wants to do in the Silvermoon Hall...


## Talk 1

(Meet Columbina)

Paimon: Columbina?
Paimon: Huh. That's odd. Paimon's positive she heard Columbina's voice...
The Damselette (Columbina): ...Hyah!
Paimon: Aaaah!
The Damselette (Columbina): Greetings.
Paimon: Another jumpscare? Not cool! If you were here, you should've just said so!
The Damselette (Columbina): I thought that since you were already so familiar with this game, I should increase the difficulty this round...
Paimon: Hmph! Paimon's gonna get you next time, just you wait!
The Damselette (Columbina): Great! I look forward to it.
Traveler: What happened to your eye mask, Columbina?
The Damselette (Columbina): Oh. I guess the kuuhenki must've swiped it when we were playing earlier.
The Damselette (Columbina): I don't usually need to use my eyes, so the mask makes little difference. Let them play with it if they like.
Paimon: Oh! On that note, don't you want to go out more often?
Traveler: You no longer need to hide from anyone, right?
The Damselette (Columbina): Yes... I have plenty of time now. I can go where I wish, when I wish.
The Damselette (Columbina): However, I was concerned that you might worry if you came looking for me but found that I had left without saying goodbye.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: In this kind of situation...
    Option 2:
        Traveler: You can just leave a note.
Paimon: Yeah! After all, we only need to know that you're okay.
The Damselette (Columbina): I see. I will consider it, then.
Paimon: You'll only "consider" it...?
Traveler: Or maybe this is just where you want to be.
The Damselette (Columbina): Everything that has happened here... I find them to be happy memories.
The Damselette (Columbina): That said, I do have another reason for waiting for you here.
Paimon: And what's that?
The Damselette (Columbina): To give you a pleasant surprise.

## Objective 2

(Head to where the surprise is)


## Talk 3

(Talk to Columbina)

Paimon: Wow! This must be the place you were talking about, right? Paimon wasn't there last time, so she didn't get to see it.
Traveler: It truly is lovely.
Paimon: Can Paimon fly around and check it out?
The Damselette (Columbina): Of course.
The Damselette (Columbina): If it pleases you, I could bring you back again someday.
Paimon: So, this is the "pleasant surprise," then?
The Damselette (Columbina): Oh? So this is enough to pass as a "pleasant surprise" in your eyes? No, the real surprise is yet to come.
Traveler: There's more?
The Damselette (Columbina): Come. Let us paint.
Paimon: Wait. Here? But Paimon doesn't see canvas, brushes, or paints anywhere.
The Damselette (Columbina): Oh. That's simple.
The Damselette (Columbina): The water's surface is our canvas, the moonlight our brushes, and the paths we take shall serve as the brushstrokes.
The Damselette: With the moon's reflection to guide us, pure thought shall flow beneath our feet.

## Objective 4

(Follow the moon's guide and paint the pattern)


## Talk 5
