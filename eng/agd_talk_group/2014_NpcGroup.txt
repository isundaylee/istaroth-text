## Talk 1

Unknown Role (None): I live in the countryside. I visit the city once a year at this time to burn incense as an offering to the Geo Lord.

Unknown Role (None): Everyone in Liyue is into things that bring good luck. I'm no different.

Unknown Role (None): Were it not for the Geo Lord's power, Liyue would not be as prosperous as it is today. That is why I come here every year to pay my respects at this temple, no matter how long the journey is and no matter what other pressing business there is to attend to.

Unknown Role (None): As the saying goes, "None shall learn what the immortals have in store for them until he first does that which is within his power as a mortal."

Unknown Role (None): About Rex Lapis' protection...

Unknown Role (None): Huh. Judging from your tone, you're not a believer, are you?

Unknown Role (None): Well, just a short while back, I was set upon by bandits on the road to Liyue Harbor. It was only by the intervention of a young hero named "Xingqiu of the Guhua Clan" that I was saved.

Unknown Role (None): This must have been the protection of Rex Lapis! He must have known that I was coming to offer incense, and thus stepped in to rescue me!

Unknown Role (None): Were it not for Rex Lapis' divine arrangement, how could a long-lost clan like Guhua suddenly return out of nowhere?

Unknown Role (None): However you want to put it, I thank Rex Lapis. I shall also offer incense on behalf of that young hero of the Guhua Clan. I will pray for his fortune and safety, as repayment for his deeds.
