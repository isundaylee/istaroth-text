## Talk 1 (variant 1)

Lauma: You've been hard at work, Kukkia.
Kukkia: Lady Lauma? What brings you here? Also, I was just cleaning up on the side. It was no trouble at all.
Lauma: But you do often come by to maintain the statue as well, don't you?
Kukkia: Yes, occasionally. Did the birds tell you? Or did the Moon Goddess?
Lauma: Haha, our Goddess did indeed guide me with her light.
Lauma: It was by that guiding radiance that I noticed you switching out the flowers in front of her statue for new ones, and cleaning the statue's base.
Lauma: Still, please take care yourself, your devotion notwithstanding.
Kukkia: ...I will. Thank you, Lady Lauma.
Lauma: I should be thanking you. Please, come a little closer, Kukkia.
Lauma: May the Moon Goddess guard your path...
Lauma: ...And that of us all.
