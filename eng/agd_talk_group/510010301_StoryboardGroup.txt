# Talk Group: StoryboardGroup - 510010301

## Talk 0

Zhongli: Sir Yun's opera performances are truly extraordinary. Well worth the trip to come see it today.

Traveler: Hey there, Zhongli. You're here too, huh?

Zhongli: Indeed. A pleasure to see you.

Zhongli: With little to do at the Funeral Parlor these past few days, I asked the Director for some time off, during which I've been traveling around a bit, and visiting some old friends in passing.

Zhongli: I just happened to pass by as Sir Yun was performing, and I could not help but sit down and watch the entire performance.

Zhongli: She exercises wondrous command of this folk art. So absorbed was I in the performance that I forgot to drink even a single cup of the tea I had ordered.

Zhongli: To drink tea of such refinement cold, when much of its flavor has been lost... A shame indeed.

Traveler: Do you have friends around here as well?

Zhongli: An ancient adage says, "As lush mountains may be found everywhere, so too may friends be met."

Zhongli: But my dear associates are busy at the moment, and I don't wish to disturb them.

Zhongli: Checking on them from afar and sending them letters at suitable times will suffice.

Zhongli: *sigh* Truly, I have done this fine pot of tea a disservice...
