# Talk Group: Trapeznikov (NpcGroup 7580)

## Talk 0

Trapeznikov: Just how many technological artifacts were excavated from the Shadow Realm during the reign of the Belyi Tsar?

## Talk 1

Traveler: About the access card...
Trapeznikov: I don't completely understand how it works myself. What I do know is that it's an indispensable tool across similar ruins, whether you're activating mechanisms or disabling barriers.
Trapeznikov: What's more, you can use materials gathered from the Shadow Realm to elevate its access level. Naturally, unlocking its full potential requires a much higher level of clearance.

## Talk 2

Traveler: The Belyi Tsar?
Trapeznikov: Ah, it's a famous rumor. During his reign, he tasked a mysterious organization with excavating technological artifacts from the Shadow Realm and putting them to use.
Trapeznikov: Word is, those excavations haven't stopped to this day. The only difference is that it's now driven entirely by the ambition of certain rogue factions.

## Talk 3

Traveler: About you...
Trapeznikov: Well, I'm mostly known as a bit of a bookworm. I actually spent all my Kristall funds just to get my hands on some research data... If the Rokot hadn't helped me through it, I probably would've been turned into a human icicle by now!
Trapeznikov: But even after all that, I just can't shake my obsession with Shadow Realm tech. I'm really hoping my curiosity can actually be of some use to everyone here.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Thank you for your help.
Trapeznikov: Not at all. I should be the one thanking you!
Trapeznikov: A chance to gather first-hand data like this doesn't come around every day.
