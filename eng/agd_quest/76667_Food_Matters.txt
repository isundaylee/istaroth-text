# Food Matters


## Talk 1

Lyylikki: Ugh... I should have brought more Lakkaberries...

Traveler: (When Lauma was here last time...)

Traveler: (It seems they were handing out food...)

Lyylikki: #Hello, Traveler, are you here for a Feast of the Moon? I'm so sorry, but... you might have to wait a little while.

Traveler: What is a Feast of the Moon?

Lyylikki: Huh? Didn't you get one last time...? Oh, right, Lady Lauma organized that one... and I guess she didn't mention the meal's official name.

Lyylikki: Simply put, a Feast of the Moon is a free meal we Frostmoon Scions distribute to those in need.

Lyylikki: Helping such people has ever been one of our traditions, though it used to be limited to the area around Hiisi Island...

Lyylikki: But under Lady Lauma's leadership and with everyone's help, we've gradually expanded our reach.

Lyylikki: Lady Lauma says this is part of spreading the Moon Goddess's Favor to all. There's a saying that goes... Um...

Lyylikki: "The first fruit of the harvest must be placed upon the Moonlight Altar — not as an offering to the gods, but as a spark for..."

Lyylikki: "A spark for..." Umm... What was the rest of it again...

Lyylikki: *Ahem*, anyway! This shop is one of the main distribution points for the meal. Hehe, and I manage almost everything here by myself! Pretty impressive, right?

Lyylikki: We've had a lot of trouble getting the meals out in Nasha Town, but I believe in what Lady Lauma says — we really can help more people this way!

Lyylikki: However... when I was preparing the ingredients yesterday, I forgot to close the box lid tightly, and when I woke up this morning, many fruits had been pecked open by birds and were completely unusable.

Lyylikki: I can barely make any Feasts of the Moon with the remaining Lakkaberries... *sigh*... What should I do now...
