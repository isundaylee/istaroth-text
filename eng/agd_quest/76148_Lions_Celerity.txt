# Lion's Celerity


## Talk 8

Istoria: Once more, we return to this place...

Istoria: The last time I came, by human lifespans, it must have been... many, many, many... generations ago.

Istoria: Looks like we've all been cast back into the Apathic Interval.

Traveler: So how are we supposed to get out?

Istoria: The usual way is through meditation — learning to master your mind and thoughts, and then letting go of every desire.

Istoria: In short, we must maintain a state of complete emptiness.

Istoria: Simply enough said. But depending on a person's nature and the strength of their desires, it can take anywhere from a few years to several centuries.

Istoria: For someone like you who carries the fate of the world on your shoulders, your desires probably can't be cast aside... nor should they be.

Traveler: Then what do we do?

Istoria: Let's find Zeitlind, and figure it out from there.

Istoria: In that case, there may yet be a chance to turn this around.

Istoria: Since Dex is also a Watcher, it might be able to use the power contained within these fragments to force open a path.

Istoria: We're going to need to give these fragments a name.

Istoria: I've heard of these overwrought coffins. They were set up by one of the temple mistress's sisters... Maranasati Caskets, I believe they were called.

Istoria: In which case, let's call the fragments Deathmuse Shards.
