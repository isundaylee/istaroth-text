# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 9132

## Talk 0

Prune: Is this the legendary Serenitea Pot? I've read about it, but I never imagined the inside would look like this...
Prune: And it's not just the Serenitea Pot, I've read about loads of other artifacts and mechanisms, too.

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Prune: Don't worry about me, I'm not a child anymore. Moving to a new environment isn't going to throw me out of whack.
Prune: Even when I was traveling with the caravan and living life on the road, I never had any difficulty adapting.
Traveler: That reminds me, whereabouts in Mondstadt do you live, Prune?
Prune: I'm staying with a relative. She's my grandfather's cousin's son's...
Prune: Uhm, you know what? The details aren't important. The point is, we're related.
Prune: But because Mom and Dad sent them a letter saying to "keep a close eye on me," they've given me a curfew at night... Honestly, it's not like SHE'S my mom...
Traveler: Are you allowed to be here?
Prune: Oh, it's fine. I already told her I was coming.
Prune: Which reminds me, are there any rules I need to abide by? Curfews, off-limit areas, things I shouldn't touch...
Traveler: None at all. Only adults come up with rules like that.
Prune: Really!? Oh, that's amazing!
Prune: This is such a great vacation spot. Everyone must love your home!
Prune: And I do, too!

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to chat?
Prune: Since you asked, I'd quite like to talk about my arch nemesis, Alice.
Traveler: I consider you both friends...
Prune: I'm not asking you to take sides. Witches have all sorts of tricks up their sleeves to beguile and ensnare the unwary, so I don't blame you for falling under her spell.
Prune: But you're smart, so I trust your judgment. Before long, the spell will fade, and you'll see her for who she really is!
Prune: What I really wanted to say is that lately I've been wondering... Why is it that every time I think I've won, things always just seem to end up going the way she planned?
Prune: Ugh, it's so frustrating! I need someone who understands witches to help me work it out...
Prune: What's the logic behind Alice's schemes?
Prune: C'mon, you're smart. Help me figure out a way to turn the tables. I wanna give her a taste of what it's like to miscalculate for once!
Traveler: Have you considered that maybe Alice's schemes don't follow any normal logic?
Prune: ...!
Prune: That's it! That wicked witch does whatever she wants! Acting without regard for others in pursuit of her own ideas of fun!
Prune: So then, what's the best way to deal with her?
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Throw a tantrum and roll around on the floor...?
    Option 2:
        Traveler: Fight magic with magic!
Prune: Why would I do that? Screaming and shouting and annoying everyone nearby... People would hate me.
Prune: Wait a second... Something's wrong here. You're not the Traveler at all, are you? You're a witch in disguise!
Prune: Reveal yourself! I know it's you!
    Option 1:
        Traveler: What I was trying to say is... Stop overthinking everything.
    Option 2:
        Traveler: Why not let yourself be a child for once and do whatever you want?
Prune: Hmm... I don't think that's something I can do so easily...
Prune: But I can try to learn a thing or two from Klee and the others...

## Talk 3

Traveler: Is there anything you'd like to do?
Prune: I'm glad you asked. I've decided to take your advice — I'm going to conduct an experiment!
Prune: It's just a trial run, really... I'm going to try being a carefree, regular child for a day.
Prune: A whole day!
Traveler: I guess you have to start somewhere.
Prune: I even did a "warm-up" last night in preparation.
Prune: I closed all my books, put away my stationery, and kept reminding myself that there's no need to arrive at any conclusions.
Traveler: And then...?
Prune: Well, I was thinking about this as I lay in bed. What would a regular kid be doing right now? Listening to a story? Drawing? Drinking some warm milk before bed? Or maybe they'd already be asleep!
Prune: I stayed up so late thinking about it that this morning, I almost missed the optimal time to wake up naturally.
Traveler: You're still overthinking things...
Prune: Mhm... I realized that, too. So, to remove all thought from the equation, I decided to make a "To-Do List for Kids."
Traveler: So what's on your list?
Prune: Item one: toss and turn in bed (immediately after waking up). Then, get told to get up (do not get out of bed unprompted). Since there's no one to prompt me, I just pretend to be someone else and tell myself to get up.
Prune: It isn't ideal, but I've decided it counts.
Prune: Item two: be a fussy eater. I tried to find something to complain about, but I just couldn't bring myself to see it through.
Prune: That's why I've written "item failed" on the list.
Prune: Item three: snack uncontrollably. This one was the easiest, and my favorite.
Prune: ...But halfway through, I realized I'd made a mistake.
Prune: In the real world, no adult would ever actually let their child eat as much junk food as they wanted.
Prune: Item three: play meaningless games. This one was hard!
Prune: I ended up focusing all my attention on trying to find loopholes in the rules...
Traveler: Sounds like things didn't go to plan...
Prune: Correct. So now I'm going to throw a tantrum!
Prune: I'm going to put away my to-do list and declare my experiment a huge failure! Then, I'm not going to make any effort whatsoever to learn from my mistakes!
Prune: I don't want to reflect on why I failed, and I don't want to listen to reason. I... just... don't... care!
Traveler: Is "throwing a tantrum" also on your list?
Prune: Eh!? How did you know?
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Because children are allowed to be unreasonable. It's in the job description.
    Option 2:
        Traveler: So maybe your experiment was successful after all!
Prune: Hmph.
