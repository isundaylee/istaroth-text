## Talk 4

Traveler: Do you have any songs about flowers?
Linus: Ah... whether it's the lilacs of the early spring wastelands, hope slumbering beneath the frozen soil, or the summer blooms dancing in the warm breeze, flowers have always been timeless muses. Let me pick one...
Linus: "...When radiant May comes with the lavish gifts she brings... Joy to hearts, dispelling every woe. Behold, Spring returns where feathered choirs sing... and flowers in meadows, vying, aglow."
Linus: "...When drowsy insects from their slumber rise, and birds in concert sing a joyful tune... The woods and peaks resound with happy cries; a verdant bliss beneath the sun and moon. Yet I remember winter's icy hand, and silent now, my song falls back to slumber."

## Talk 5

Traveler: Do you have any songs about spring?
Linus: There are many songs of spring. Even in the heart of winter, a traveler's longing waits for the carriage that carries them, swaying toward the warmth of spring. Let me choose one...
Linus: "...The flock has fled the meadow's gentle green, yet blooms shall soon grace the verdant scene. When spring reveals her colors bright and fair, the land shall shine as paradise laid bare."
Linus: "...In gardens bright, the daffodils shine; in ordered rows, their golden heads sway. Perhaps the kindest hearts among us will know, they're waiting for someone to come their way..."

## Talk 6

Traveler: I'd like to hear your favorite song, please.
Linus: My favorite song... hmm. I suppose it would be a poem about love. Sunsets and books of verse, the scent of salt and lemon on a midsummer breeze...
Linus: Pity that Miss Elsie can't even grasp metaphors like that.
Linus: "I shall no longer sing as she has sung. If quiet peace may answer my plea, if kind solace can, from any soul, be wrung..."
Linus: "This is my grief, which knows no earthly end. Day by day, I keep my silence bound. Ah, let me linger in your lovely gaze! One glance of love, a fleeting moment's grace, frees me from these mortal chains..."
