## Talk 2

Traveler: What do you make of this tooth?

Unknown Role (None): A tooth? Ugh, don't tell me you've been foraging around on the mountain for junk...

Unknown Role (None): ...Huh, although, as teeth go...

Traveler: What? What is it?

Unknown Role (None): This is a rather fine specimen. Tough as nails, too...

Unknown Role (None): And these contours... Looks an awful lot like Dragontooth to me.

Traveler: What do you mean, "Dragontooth"?

Unknown Role (None): As in, the tooth of a dragon. Weapons made from Dragontooth are supposed to be extraordinarily strong and sharp... and even possess some sort of strange power.

Unknown Role (None): It's hardly the sort of thing you come across every day, though... In all the stories you hear, it's only ever the most famous heroes who manage to acquire Dragontooth and craft weapons from it.

Unknown Role (None): As they say, if you plan on slaying the dragon to pinch his teeth... you'd better be sure to have a dragon-slayer of a weapon handy, or he might just bite back!

Traveler: Then... is this really a dragon's tooth, or not?

Unknown Role (None): Honestly, I've never dealt with Dragontooth firsthand, so I can't give a definite answer.

Unknown Role (None): I do think it has to be the tooth of some enormous beast or other...

Unknown Role (None): Anyway, since you've gone to all this effort... let's just treat it as if it really is a genuine, precious piece of Dragontooth, shall we?

Unknown Role (None): Hmm, but that said — it's not enough to make a weapon from.

Unknown Role (None): How about you go find, say, three more pieces of Dragontooth, and in the meantime, I'll do some reading and find out how to work with this stuff.

Unknown Role (None): Oh — and *Dragontooth* aside, we'll want to supplement it with some good ore...

Unknown Role (None): In which case, while you're up on the mountain, grab 50 pieces of *Starsilver*.

Unknown Role (None): Once you've got all of that, come and see me again.

Unknown Role (None): Ah yes, and as well as all that... I'm also going to need a *Northlander Polearm Billet*...

## Talk 3

Traveler: I found some more Dragontooth...

Unknown Role (None): Ah, I was hoping you'd swing by. Listen, before you go gathering all that Dragontooth... Oh. I see you've already finished.

Unknown Role (None): My profuse apologies... but I've hit a slight snag. No matter what I hit this thing with, I simply cannot take so much as a sliver off of it.

Unknown Role (None): It's almost as if it really were Dragontooth... If so, then based on what I read, there are several rather specific methods to work with this material.

Unknown Role (None): One of them is to have the dragon's tooth absorb some sort of... dragon-related "vitality."

Unknown Role (None): Then, use the dragon's tooth infused with dragon vitality to work the other pieces of Dragontooth into shape.

Unknown Role (None): But how to go about infusing something with dragon vitality... I haven't the foggiest idea.

Unknown Role (None): Any thoughts, Traveler?

Traveler: I'll, um... see what I can do.

Unknown Role (None): Great! Here — you take this Dragontooth, then, and I'll leave that whole side of things to you.

Unknown Role (None): Huh, if just the teeth cause this much hassle, whole dragons must be seriously complicated creatures...
