# Talk Group: Dementieva (NpcGroup 7833)

## Talk 0

Firsova: *sigh*... This is already the fifth time this month. What on earth is going wrong?

Firsova: But... our conversations in the dining car always seem to go well. Why is it that after drinking the tea you serve them, they suddenly turn pale and flee the train?
Dementieva: Indeed. Why is that? I've been trying to figure it out myself.
Dementieva: Look, you're drinking the very same tea right now...

Dementieva: Exactly. Which means that man was simply far too frail. How could someone like that ever be worthy of someone as perfect as you?

Dementieva: There's no need to worry, milady. Even if every dull, disappointing man in all of Snezhnaya lets you down, I will always remain by your side and take care of you.

Dementieva: Hehe, hearing you say that is the greatest honor I could ask for.
