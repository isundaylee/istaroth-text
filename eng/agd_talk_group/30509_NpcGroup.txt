# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 30509

## Talk 0

Frejus: So, what did you think of the cannon?
Traveler: It could still do with a little work...
Frejus: What do you mean?
Traveler: Well, it's stuck in one place...
Frejus: I wasn't expecting a request like that... I guess I should have known someone as exceptional as you would have extraordinary ideas.
Frejus: But if I'm going to make it mobile, I'll need to do something about the recoil first.
Frejus: It is a naval cannon, after all. Of course, if it were you carrying it, I'm fairly confident you could withstand the force...
Frejus: But for a regular member of the Gardes... the recoil would probably kill them stone dead. I'll need to invent a... "Miniature Terrestrial Naval-Grade Platform"! Something mobile that can support the cannon's weight.
Frejus: Obviously, for something this big, I'll need to consider its defensive capabilities — armor plating would make it too heavy. Then, I need to consider propulsion and mobility. There's much to consider.
Frejus: The engineering designs are going to get extremely complicated. I wonder, can I really do this? I was rejected three times by the Fontaine Research Institute, and...
Traveler: I'd rather be able to pick it up and fire from different angles...
Traveler: You look familiar...
Frejus: So you still remember me? That's wonderful!
Frejus: It was a good while ago now, but you helped me test the original prototype for this cannon.
Frejus: Back then, I was still lying to Chenee about being a genius...
Traveler: So, she finally figured you out?
Frejus: Haha, as it happens, the answer is neither — the lie evolved!
Frejus: Thanks to you, my cannon performed outstandingly, and the Maison Gardiennage rewarded me with financial support. They even introduced me to a few engineers.
Frejus: With guidance from those old hands — and after getting to grips with a fair few abandoned projects from the Fontaine Research Institute — I managed to produce some noteworthy inventions...
Frejus: Such as my "Shallow-Earth Mining Drill" and "Variant Antoine Roger Aircraft"... which I should really call the "Frejus Flyer."
Frejus: Of course, Chenee's favorite is the "Automatic Breakfast Oven." She uses it every day.
Traveler: So, in the end, your lie became the truth?
Frejus: Not at all. Everything I've invented was based on the work of others. I'm more of an improver than an inventor. If you were feeling particularly unkind, you might say all I do is "embellish" others' ideas.
Frejus: A real genius wouldn't need to borrow inspiration from anyone else.
Frejus: That said, I'm no longer that old run-of-the-mill everyman who couldn't make something valuable to save his life either.
Frejus: So I've come to feel that my "lie" has gradually evolved into more of a... a "promise," perhaps?
Frejus: It's not quite so far from the truth anymore, but at the same time, I've still got some ways to go.
Traveler: Keep up the good work!
Frejus: I'll try my hardest! Your encouragement has given me a little more confidence.
Traveler: Forge ahead, intrepid inventor!
Traveler: Perhaps you really were a genius all along?
Traveler: Does that mean you came clean?
Traveler: I need some more time to test it out.
Frejus: Be my guest! The barrel's extremely durable, so fire away!
