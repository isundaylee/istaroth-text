Ryabov: And most importantly: this liquor right here, my friend, is made with a special artisanal method passed down by a fae family for at least a century.
Ryabov: It's made with handpicked ingredients by a century-old fae artisan who painstakingly handles the fermentation, the distillation, the blending...
Ryabov: It may cost a few more Mora, my friend, but this, this is the true taste of heritage you won't get anywhere else in Snezhnaya. How about you have a taste?
