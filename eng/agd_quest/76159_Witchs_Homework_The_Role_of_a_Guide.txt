## Talk 6

Traveler: But why would she give you a Seelie Court...?

Nicole: It symbolizes "the completion of guidance," "the end of a journey," and "the fulfillment of prophecy."

Nicole: You've followed Seelie before, right? Only when a Seelie returns to its Seelie Court does its guidance come to an end.

Nicole: So, to give this unwelcome homework a clear conclusion, Barbie and Alice must have built a Seelie Court right here.

Nicole: It's like writing a novel. As long as the main outline is there, there'll always be a way to flesh out the details so the pieces fall into place.

Nicole: As long as I take my seat, this homework of mine will be officially over...
