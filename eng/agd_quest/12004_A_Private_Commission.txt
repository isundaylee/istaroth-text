Unknown Role (None): Something has come up, and I would like to request your assistance.

Unknown Role (None): But you ought not be nervous. This is not coming from the Yashiro Commission or the Kamisato Clan. It is my own individual request.

Unknown Role (None): Individual request? You mean... personal stuff?

Unknown Role (None): Correct. It concerns my mother.

Unknown Role (None): This may seem a little sudden... but in my view, you are the only person I can entrust this to.

Unknown Role (None): Can you give me any more details?

Unknown Role (None): A few days ago, I was sorting through some old family belongings when I found a notebook that belonged to my mother.

Unknown Role (None): She wrote about all kinds of things, mostly of little consequence. But what drew my attention was a name — it is one that I have never heard before, so the fact that she mentions it so frequently... it makes it difficult for me to ignore...

Unknown Role (None): Probably your mom's best buddy, right?

Unknown Role (None): You'd really never heard the name before? Not once?

Unknown Role (None): Never. And although my mother and father passed away many years ago, both my brother and I are familiar with the vast majority of their acquaintances.

Unknown Role (None): So... I am very curious about this mysterious individual. I would very much like to meet them.

Unknown Role (None): According to the notebook, their name is "Tsubaki." This seems to have been someone very important to my mother. Someone with a lot of personality, who liked to have fun.

Unknown Role (None): Hmm... Paimon thinks it sounds like a girl's name.

Unknown Role (None): That's what I thought too.

Unknown Role (None): So, what do you want us to do?

Unknown Role (None): Do you need us to find her for you?

Unknown Role (None): No, no, her address is given in the notebook — we can head straight there. I wanted you to assist me with the preparation of greeting gifts...

Unknown Role (None): ...Please keep everything I have told you in strict confidence.

Unknown Role (None): The proper course of action would be to involve my brother and visit her together, but he is terribly busy at present, and... on a more selfish note, I don't wish to involve anybody else from Inazuma in this matter.

Unknown Role (None): Hence why you came to me, an outlander.

Unknown Role (None): Exactly. That, and you are also a kind, well-mannered person with a genuine concern for other people... not to mention trustworthy. I would not feel comfortable with anyone else in this situation.

Unknown Role (None): You can count on us! We've done greeting gifts before, so we should be able to help. In terms of payment... just treat us to some delicious food, and we'll be more than happy!

Unknown Role (None): Wonderful — in fact, I was hoping to take this opportunity to show you both around. If you don't mind, of course?

Unknown Role (None): #Oh, we don't mind! We don't mind at all! Do we, Traveler?

Unknown Role (None): Not in the slightest.

Unknown Role (None): Thank you kindly! Shall we head off, then? Ogura Textiles & Kimonos is our first stop.

Unknown Role (None): Alright! Sounds great!
