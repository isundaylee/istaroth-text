# Aino: Time to Say Farewell

Aino's troubles remain unresolved...

Despite her reluctance, Aino decides to let the fawn go, and Lauma offers to help find its family. Before it leaves, there may still be time to say goodbye...

## Talk 0

Aino: Take these snacks with you. And this hat I made with Ineffa... And, uh, right! Your horns need some protective oil... Gimme a minute...
Ineffa: The protective oil is with me, Aino. Let me assist.
Rimehorn Deer: ...
Lauma: Hehe. It says, "I'm so lucky to be friends with you."

## Talk 1

Ineffa: Hello, Miss Lauma.
Aino: Hi...
Lauma: Hello, you two. Please allow me to inspect the little Rimehorn Deer once more.
Lauma: ...Mm, yes. It's doing a lot better now. I believe it can take care of itself.
Aino: Wait, does that mean... You're gonna take it home? Right now?
Aino: I mean, its leg just got better... I made a perfect protective brace for it, which I haven't gotten the deer to try on yet... But once we get that installed, it'll be able to jump higher than any others in its herd!
Aino: And there's food, too! Ineffa and I made lots of yummy food for the deer to take home, because you're not gonna find anything as nice as Ineffa's cooking in the forest. I guarantee it!
Aino: And... we also...
Ineffa: Aino...
Aino: *sniff* Can't it stay here...? Please...
Rimehorn Deer: ...
Lauma: ...Of course. I'll let them know.
Lauma: It says, "Thank you so much, Aino and Ineffa."
Aino: Huh?
Lauma: "Please don't be sad, or I'll cry too. Actually, I'm almost about to cry, but deer eyes get uncomfortable when we do."
Aino: Then... could you maybe not leave? I'll make you some super comfortable bedding, and get you all the food you could ever want...
Ineffa: ...And I will take responsibility.
Rimehorn Deer: ...
Lauma: ...It's asking you to go over and brush its fur.
Aino: ...
Ineffa: Miss Lauma. Are you certain you can find its herd?
Lauma: If this deer had truly fallen off a cliff, as you say, then its family must have been working hard to find it.
Lauma: After I take it back, I'll first thoroughly investigate its past movement patterns, as well as the physical characteristics of the herd's leader.
Lauma: Although Nod-Krai's deer population is large and scattered, this simply makes success a question of time and effort, and of following the many intertwined clues to locate the herd.
Ineffa: Thank you. We will compile all the information we know and send it to you to aid in your search.
Aino: Then... Will it be staying at the Frostmoon Scions for a while? Can I visit?
Lauma: Of course. The doors of the Frostmoon Scions are forever open to you.
Aino: But... It's still going to go home someday.
Aino: I know how deer are. They don't stay in one spot for long, which means... Pretty soon, I won't be able to find it again...
Lauma: ...Yes. It is best for the children of the wild to return to the wild.
Rimehorn Deer: ...
Lauma: However... It will take some time to locate its herd. While it stays with the Scions, you may feel free to visit at any time.
Lauma: I'm sure the little one has many, many more things it wants to say to you.
Aino: Then... Then I'll visit the Scions every Tuesday.
Aino: On Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays, I've got to do maintenance on Ineffa, and on Thursdays, I have to sort out the circuitry around here... so I'm only free on Tuesdays.
Lauma: Of course. Feel free to visit whenever the urge strikes you. Unless anything out of the blue occurs, you may always find me near the Frostmoon Scions' library.
Aino: Alright, it's a promise! Let me think of what else I need to tell you... Ah, right, I'd better write down everything you said...
Aino: Urgh, but there's so much I WANT to say, and so little time...!
Lauma: I believe there will be enough time to say your farewells.
