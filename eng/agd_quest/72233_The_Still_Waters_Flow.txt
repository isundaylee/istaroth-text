## Talk 2

Unknown Role (None): We call this a "Key Sigil." It was once heraldry used throughout Enkanomiya. It only resurfaces when the road is about to be opened.

Unknown Role (None): This is said to be a second set of keys that are sent to the people of Watatsumi Island when Enkanomiya awakens, and our records show that there are a total of five types of Key Sigils.

Unknown Role (None): At the moment, we have found three of the five on Watatsumi Island. We can then operate them in this manner to return them to their proper places and release the seal.

Unknown Role (None): We removed the three we found from their keyholes and placed them in other locations throughout Watatsumi Island. This way, the barrier can be restored.

Unknown Role (None): The purpose behind doing that, of course, was to prevent children, Fatui spies, Narukami Islanders or outlanders from sneaking inside.

Unknown Role (None): Um, you're not in that "outlanders" category, don't worry. Ahaha...

Unknown Role (None): We are presently unable to establish contact with the Adventurers' Guild, so no adventurers are allowed down there either.

Unknown Role (None): Did you ever let them go down there anyway?

Unknown Role (None): There are such historical records. We were looking for a win-win arrangement, you understand. It is always good to have people whose strong suit is exploration around — yourself included.

Unknown Role (None): Oho, so this means that no one's stolen a march on us this time, right?

Unknown Role (None): Whoa, Paimon, manners...

Unknown Role (None): Well, how should I put this? I doubt that our temporary barrier restoration was completely foolproof. Some time did pass between our investigating the Key Sigils' locations and removing them from the keyholes, so some rats may have scurried in during the interim.

Unknown Role (None): We are short on manpower, after all. In short, if you encounter any Fatui, Treasure Hoarders, Kairagi, or any of their ilk... Feel free to teach them a lesson.

Unknown Role (None): Wait a minute. If they got down there so easily, what was the point of us getting that Moon-Caked-Whatever again?

Unknown Role (None): Enkanomiya will only awaken if we retrieve the Key of the Moon-Bathed Deep. Similarly, only that Key can activate the trial system within.

Unknown Role (None): And if there are any who did not enter Enkanomiya to perform the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri... Well, there might be some other twisted, winding way to get in. Who knows?

Unknown Role (None): Either way, please retrieve the other Key Sigils on the island with all haste.

Unknown Role (None): The Key Sigils have been placed at the Fin and Heart of Watatsumi. As for their two altars, they can be found at the line that connects either shrine and Sangonomiya Shrine, which intersects with the second mountain wall that surrounds the Moon-Bathed Deep.

Unknown Role (None): Well then, I'll wait for news from you at the usual place.
