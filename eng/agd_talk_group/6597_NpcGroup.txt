Navaratra: Natlan's gemstones are unique. They appear very lively, but they tend to be quite rough.

Navaratra: No, it's got nothing to do with craftsmanship. In a tribe called the Children of Echoes, I once saw Honey Pearls worked with breathtaking skill. They've just never really made a splash on the international market.

Navaratra: The way I see it, it's a matter of choice. Natlan has no shortage of skilled craftsmen, but they choose to sell products that are simple.
Navaratra: Perhaps they prefer raw, unfiltered wildness — your Fontainian notions of intricacy and delicate patterns hold no sway here.
Navaratra: Complexity adds depth, yet raises barriers. Simplicity lacks grandeur, but flows unbound. If you wish to thrive here with your jewelry trade, ignore this at your peril.
Philpot: Hmm... You make an interesting point. I shall take note.
