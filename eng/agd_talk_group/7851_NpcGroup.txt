# Talk Group: Guseva (NpcGroup 7851)

## Talk 0

Guseva: There's nothing I can do now but wait...

## Talk 1

Traveler: What are you waiting for?
Guseva: I am waiting for the next Kristall shipment to arrive. I am waiting for things in Sretomorozsk to return to normal so that the next train will come and take me away from here.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Goodbye.
Guseva: See you.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Where are you going?
Guseva: To Glupov. I was heading there for the Fatui recruitment exam originally. Had the trains not stopped running, I might have already been accepted by now.
Guseva: But this is my home, and no matter how great the storms here are, I shall weather them alongside.

## Talk 4

Traveler: The station's back to normal now.
Guseva: Maybe. But Sretomorozsk isn't back to normal yet.
Guseva: This is my home, and I have to stay here to help. I cannot leave with peace of mind until I see for myself that everything is all right.
