## Talk 3

## Talk 4

Przybor: Someone like you, I bet you have an interesting job. Not like me, all I'm cut out for is a life on the seas. But I don't want to waste my whole life away sailing the oceans.

## Talk 5

Traveler: It sounds like you want to join the Fatui...

Przybor: After all, does anyone really want to be a sailor for the rest of their lives? Then again, being a sailor has its perks — you never know what sort of interesting people or things you're gonna find at your next port of call.

## Talk 6

Traveler: Do you know what the Fatui are shipping?
