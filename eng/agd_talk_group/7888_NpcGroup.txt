# Talk Group: Vyorskaya (NpcGroup 7888)

## Talk 0

Vyorskaya: The best season in this town is the season where no one comes around to the station.

## Talk 1

Traveler: "The best season"...?
Vyorskaya: Yes, the very best. I don't have to deal with one dreary thing after another when no one comes by the station.
Vyorskaya: But I get paid the same either way, and I know exactly when and how my day ends. You work like a horse, they don't give you a prize. You work less, nobody cares.
Vyorskaya: So obviously, fewer people is better. Fewer passengers at the station means less work. Less work equals less responsibility. Am I wrong?

## Talk 2

Traveler: Goodbye.
Vyorskaya: See you!

## Talk 3

Traveler: Well, that doesn't sound quite right to me...
Vyorskaya: I'd have agreed with you ten years ago, when I was that Shagova's age.
Vyorskaya: Shagova... That little fool. She thinks that she'll become someone if she works hard enough.
Vyorskaya: I remember the days when I was Sretomorozsk's Train Attendant of the Year for years running. Everyone kept saying that young Vyorskaya was bound to be promoted to Snezhnograd sooner or later.
Vyorskaya: But in the end? Every year, right here in this station, I would watch someone else go off to Snezhnograd instead. They'd either be a relative of the mayor's, or someone with a connection to the stationmaster...
Vyorskaya: That's when I knew. Forget fairy tales. Better to just live comfortably right here, right now, every day.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Of course!
Vyorskaya: So they say, great minds think alike. It seems you know the life of a working person well enough yourself.
Vyorskaya: That silly little girl... Have you met her yet? Look at that train attendant over there. Her name is Shagova. She actually thinks if she does enough, they'll notice her. Praise her. Even promote her.
Vyorskaya: Pbbft! Promote? She has no idea what is going on at all. Here, in this place, the more work you do, the more work you get. That's all.
