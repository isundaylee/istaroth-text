# Talk Group: Blyushko (NpcGroup 7416)

## Talk 0

Blyushko: If you need anything, come in, and we'll talk... But allow me to warn you that anything you break will be considered sold.

## Talk 1

Traveler: Let's see what you've got here...
Blyushko: Sorry, but we're still taking inventory of newly arrived goods and need some time to reorganize items on our shelves. Please, do come back at a later date.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Are your goods expensive?
Blyushko: Mostly not... but just as even sand can stack up into a tall tower, small losses can be harbingers for bankruptcy.
Blyushko: And most artworks are fragile things. Beautiful, yes, but fragile. If you have respect for their beauty, then do have a care.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Surely nothing's deliberately made to be fragile...
Blyushko: This is Sadov-Arbat, a venerable and respected establishment famous throughout Snezhnaya. We even get orders from dedicated personnel at the palace.
Blyushko: You won't find any of the charlatanry those gangs are so fond of here!

## Talk 4

Traveler: We'll leave you be, then...
Blyushko: We look forward to your future patronage... if there will indeed be a next time.
