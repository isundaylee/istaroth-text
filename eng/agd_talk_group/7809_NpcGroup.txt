# Talk Group: Baryatinsky (NpcGroup 7809)

## Talk 0

Baryatinsky: Ladies and gentlemen, for your safety, please avoid running or rushing through the vehicle. If the ride gets bumpy, please stay seated whenever possible.

## Talk 1

Traveler: You are...?
Baryatinsky: I'm the conductor of this train. It is my responsibility to maintain a comfortable environment for all passengers...
Baryatinsky: And, in extreme circumstances, to take immediate corrective action against anything that threatens the train's proper operation.

## Talk 2

Traveler: What if there's an accident?
Baryatinsky: If that happens, the first thing to do is stay calm.
Baryatinsky: Snezhnayan trains have been running across this frozen land for at least four hundred years. We've dealt with just about every situation imaginable, and every conductor is trained for emergencies.
Baryatinsky: If it comes to that, please trust our judgment.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Please excuse the interruption.
Baryatinsky: It's no trouble... Please let me know if you need anything.
