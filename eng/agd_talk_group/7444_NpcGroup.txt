# Talk Group: Lukyana (NpcGroup 7444)

## Talk 0

Lukyana: Discipline is the backbone and lifeblood of an army. Those who cannot watch their mouths in school will prove to be fodder in battle.

## Talk 1

Traveler: You're the instructor here, I presume?
Lukyana: That's right, I'm the current Head Instructor of the Royal Military Academy, Lukyana Vasilyevna Dragomirova, Officer Third Class.
Lukyana: As you can see, my duty is to forge these youngsters into blades to be wielded for our Tsaritsa, to protect our nation.
Lukyana: If you're not a student here, please don't stay for too long, or you may interfere with the normal flow of classes.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Tell me a bit about the Academy...
Lukyana: I'm no bard. If you want to hear stories, I suggest you hear them at the Podvorye Restaurant.
Lukyana: I can only tell you what sort of place this is. This was once a club for the nobility, which only fae and the children of high-ranking military officers could enroll in.
Lukyana: Only when Her Majesty took the throne did it start to have a quota for enrolling commoners. That said, prejudice and discrimination have proved tough to break down.
Lukyana: Even now, we still get some noble whelps, far too big for their britches, who need to be taught the meaning of "egalitarian discipline" from the comfort of solitary confinement.

## Talk 3

Traveler: I'll leave you to it, then.
Lukyana: Goodbye.

## Talk 4

Traveler: So, where do graduates from this place go?
Lukyana: The aristocrats usually inherit their family businesses afterward, while the vast majority of commoners join the Fatui.
Lukyana: For the latter group, this is the best way to climb the ladder and gain merit.
Lukyana: It is the highest honor to sacrifice themselves for Her Majesty. It's just... some recent Fatui policies, and the manner in which some Harbinger operate...
Lukyana: ...Must they really take these children, freshly out of their graduation uniforms, to replace those lost to meaningless sacrifices?
Lukyana: Sorry, I spoke out of turn. Just consider these the pointless ramblings of an old woman.

## Talk 5

Traveler: I shan't disturb you any longer.
Lukyana: See you.
