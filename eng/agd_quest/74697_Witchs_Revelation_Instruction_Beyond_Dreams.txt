# Witch's Revelation: Instruction Beyond Dreams

You encounter Yumemizuki Mizuki at Barbeloth's fortune-telling booth...


## Talk 1

(Talk to Yumemizuki Mizuki)

Paimon: Huh? Isn't that Mizuki and her two mini baku? Are they talking to Barbeloth already?
Yumemizuki Mizuki: #Traveler and Paimon...? Lady Barbeloth was just talking about you. I was wondering if we might cross paths.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: Hehe... It seems like fate has already arranged our meeting place.
Traveler: Are you here on holiday, Mizuki?
Yumemizuki Mizuki: Hmm... I suppose you could say that. If the prep work for this educational film about psychology I'm doing goes smoothly, I should indeed have time for some leisure. Though until then, I still need to focus on my work.

Option 1:

> Traveler: A film about psychology?

Option 2:

> Traveler: Is this a new form of therapy?

Paimon: Whoa, films can do that? That's amazing!
Yumemizuki Mizuki: Mmhmm, it's a new idea I came up with. Back when I was promoting Aisa Bathhouse, I took inspiration from Fontainian cinema and recorded a few variety show episodes.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: People seemed to love it. Not only did they watch every episode, they even remembered all the psychological concepts we showcased.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: So I thought, why not make a few films about mental disorders as a fun and educational way to explain basic psychological concepts.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: ...If I can raise everyone's basic awareness, perhaps people can develop a more objective understanding of what mental disorders are.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: ...Perhaps I can stop people from blindly dismissing these conditions as made-up nonsense, or treating them like some terrifying bogeyman.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: And maybe my future therapy sessions will go a little more smoothly...

Option 1:

> Traveler: You've clearly put a lot of thought into this...

Option 2:

> Traveler: You've really considered this thoroughly...

Yumemizuki Mizuki: That's right. After all, illnesses of the mind are invisible and hard to pin down. Trying to get a hold on them, or even just detecting their existence, is sometimes far from easy.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: But even I don't know how to pin them down to fit neatly into a script...
Yumemizuki Mizuki: I'm a bit embarrassed to admit it, but even though I've written quite a few papers about mental health, I'm completely stuck when it comes to writing a film script.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: Sometimes, I stay cooped up in my room all day, endlessly going over a few lines of dialogue or a few seconds of plot... Even then, I might come away with nothing to show at the end of it.

Option 1:

> Traveler: That sounds exhausting...

Option 2:

> Traveler: You need to relax...

Paimon: #Maybe you're just missing a little spark of inspiration? When Paimon and Traveler get stuck on one thing, we do something else, and then... *ding*! The answer just pops into our heads!
Yumemizuki Mizuki: Hmm, it seems like great minds think alike, Paimon. That's why I came here, to experience a form of divination quite unlike the fortune slips I'm used to.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: I'm hoping this mage's Lunar Arcana can bring me some fresh inspiration.
Barbeloth: Inspiration... Anya once said that she wanted to write a fairy tale for every card in the Lunar Arcana. But there were so many stories she wanted to write, and so little time... Heh...
Barbeloth: Since you're looking for inspiration, go ahead and draw a card, Miss Youkai. I hope that mage's unfinished fairy tale can lead you to the inspiration for your own story.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: Alright, Lady Barbeloth. But... most of what I know about divination relates to... the Grand Narukami Shrine. Fortune telling with cards is a first for me.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: If I do anything that breaks the proper ritual etiquette, please set me straight.
Barbeloth: Oh my, the reversed Moon...
Barbeloth: The upright and reversed meanings of the Moon are similar — "escapism," "illusion," "self-deception"...
Barbeloth: Like someone traveling beneath a dark moon, having lost their way forward but not knowing how to break free.

Option 1:

> Traveler: That doesn't sound good...

Option 2:

> Traveler: That sounds a little negative...

Paimon: Yeah, why does the meaning of this card sound like a warning...? Paimon's got a really bad feeling about this...
Barbeloth: However, since Miss Yumemizuki can still hear this prophecy, it means the situation is not yet too dire.
Barbeloth: With a little effort, you may be able to exit the haze that obscures your path ahead, and find what you seek...
Yumemizuki Mizuki: Escapism, illusion... and "self-deception"?
Barbeloth: Taking the card at face value, yes. To put it more plainly...
Barbeloth: You're running from an answer that's right before your eyes, searching far when you should be looking near.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: Are you saying there's someone who can review my script standing right in front of me?
Barbeloth: It's not limited to just the script... But how you interpret the meaning behind the card is entirely up to you.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: ...
Yumemizuki Mizuki: The answer is right in front of my eyes, yet I'm searching far when I should be looking near... I think I understand what you're getting at.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: Heh... When you put it like that, I really have been deceiving myself... Or rather, I couldn't help but "search far when I should be looking near."
Yumemizuki Mizuki: I thought about visiting Yae Publishing House and asking Guuji Yae for advice, but then...
Yumemizuki Mizuki: From a clinical perspective, I suppose this counts as "unconscious avoidance"...
Yumemizuki Mizuki: Thank you for your divination, Lady Barbeloth. You really helped me find inspiration after all.
Barbeloth: There's no need to thank me. I did nothing. You untangled the threads of fate yourself. I merely placed the loose ends in your hands.
Barbeloth: However, Guuji Yae is also someone swept along by fate.
Barbeloth: Intertwining threads often bring trouble. With that in mind, keeping your distance wouldn't necessarily be a bad thing.
Yumemizuki Mizuki: If I simply ask for some feedback on my script, surely that wouldn't bring fate into the mix, would it?
Barbeloth: As I said, how you interpret things is not up to me, Miss Yumemizuki. I cannot see every twist in your future from reading a single card.
Barbeloth: Who can perceive every obstacle on the path of fate before it trips them up?
Barbeloth: Sometimes, it's just like that little brat said...
Barbeloth: "...Fate is called as such, for it cannot be changed, nor can it be reversed. It can only but be accepted."
Yumemizuki Mizuki: ...
Barbeloth: Anyway, Miss Youkai, I look forward to watching your film. Hehehe...
