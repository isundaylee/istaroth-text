## Talk 1

Traveler: Have you been doing research all this time?

Paimon: Oh yeah, you mentioned "On Fungi Communities" earlier. It looks impressive... Like the kind of article that lays the foundation for the whole discipline...

Paimon: Are you going to become a researcher in the future, Haniyyah? Y'know, one of those masters who creates a whole new school of thought!?

Haniyyah: It's not that impressive. Researchers have been exploring these theories even before the very first Nilotpala Cup.

Haniyyah: I've only added a few modest additions to the theory by combining my predecessors' trial-and-error work with my own experiences with my Fungi friends.

Traveler: But your practical experience is unique.

Traveler: But you're the only one who can put many of these hypotheses to the test.

Paimon: That's true! If we're talking about who's spent the most time with Fungi in Teyvat, we'd be at the top of the list, right?

Paimon: Even those who know more theory than you can't match up to your real-world experience, Haniyyah!

Haniyyah: That's why I want to share these insights by writing a comprehensive guidebook. If I put my all into making it the best that it can be, I can help more people live in harmony with intelligent Fungi.

Haniyyah: Also, I really need to thank Souka...

Haniyyah: If it weren't for her daily guidance with vocabulary and syntax, I would never have been able to take the ideas in my mind and express them on paper.

Haniyyah: Besides, I still cook like I'm back in my mercenary days, so she usually handles the meals at camp.

Haniyyah: But after all this time, I still don't know how I should thank her... Other than paying her as much extra Mora as possible.

Paimon: Now that you mention it, you're right... She's been staying with you to help look after your Fungi friends since the first Nilotpala Cup, hasn't she?

Traveler: Perhaps this isn't something that can be measured in Mora...

Traveler: Could it be that Souka values something beyond money?

Haniyyah: Seems that just as I'm getting to know my Fungi friends, I should also spend some time getting to know this partner of mine better...

## Talk 2

Traveler: So, what's next for you?

Paimon: Paimon wants to know too! Besides spending more quality time with our Fungi friends, is there anything else you're working toward?

Haniyyah: Hmm... Besides "On Fungi Communities," I also have another work, "An Introduction to the Principles of Beast Taming," to revise.

Haniyyah: This book was my first attempt at writing, and the wording and grammar are pretty rough, so I only did a small test print — just thirty copies.

Haniyyah: Still, word reached a knight from Mondstadt somehow. They even went so far as to hire an adventurer to find our camp in the desert and buy a copy.

Haniyyah: I hear she wants to systematically train some raptors and use them as aerial assistants... I hope everything goes well for her.

Paimon: A buyer from Mondstadt? Looks like you're really making a name for yourself, Haniyyah!

Traveler: You were already somewhat well-known before, were you?

Traveler: Were you already a renowned trainer even before all this?

Haniyyah: My theory is that my former partners have been active in various regions and are doing quite well for themselves. As a result, word about me has begun to spread...

Haniyyah: At this rate, I might even receive invitations to return to my original line of work. Training Sumpter Beasts, for example, and those traditional competitive matches where you team up with animal partners.

Paimon: Whoa! So you mean... a more old-school version of Beast Tamer Tournaments?

Traveler: Perhaps we'll even have a chance to test our skills against each other?

Traveler: You never know. Maybe we'll enter a tournament too...

Haniyyah: Sure! If it's one of those classic matches, I'll bring my most faithful and loyal Sumpter Beast, Fluffy the Flower King.

Haniyyah: I usually let Fluffy roam free in the eastern desert. When the need arises, I meet up with him at a certain oasis. It's been our ritual for years now.

Haniyyah: Should I have the honor of meeting you on the field, I'll show you what I'm really made of. Don't expect any more smiles and small talk!

Paimon: Oh? Why's that?

Traveler: To keep things fair?

Traveler: For the thrill of the game?

Haniyyah: Fairness is a part of it. It can't seem as if we're holding back simply because we're friends. But more than that, I'm just eager to see what you're capable of now.

Haniyyah: To compete against the two of you would be the greatest honor and pleasure for any beast tamer. Of course I must face you with everything I have!

Traveler: Well then, let's have a good match when the time comes.

Paimon: In that case, we won't hold back if we wind up facing each other either! Let's give it everything we've got and have a great match!

## Talk 3

Traveler: About the Lil' Fungi Playground...

Paimon: Paimon's kinda curious too. Take these counters, for example. Besides providing a place for our friends to rest and have fun, they've gotta have some other use, right?

Haniyyah: Yes, they do. The foot traffic through Bayda Harbor is ideal, so I'm planning to use this place as a platform to help more people understand how gentle Fungi can be.

Haniyyah: Besides selling the finished Lil' Fungi's Fun-Tastic Fiesta tabletop game, those interested can also interact briefly with Kindlejoy and Blitzara under my guidance.

Haniyyah: Once I'm done with "On Fungi Communities," I'll be selling copies here too.

Haniyyah: In time, more people will start to see that you can trust some gentle, intelligent Fungi. They've got what it takes to be great partners, just like the Saurians in Natlan.

Haniyyah: Plus, when the time is right, if we ever host more beast tamer tournaments, this would be a great operational base.

Paimon: Ooh! So this place doubles as a spot for you to promote your theories and a support hub for future events.
