# Talk Group: Vidya (NpcGroup 7771)

## Talk 0

Vidya: There's still going to be an encore, right...? I don't think I can wait until my next holiday.

## Talk 1

Traveler: I think the show's already over.
Vidya: *sigh* I know. I do. But maybe they'll come back for one more?
Vidya: I mean, I've never seen anything like that before...
Vidya: You know, as a scholar, my life has always been about reasoning things through and proving them. I used to believe that was how everything should be approached...
Vidya: You find the problem, then you solve it. So I approached emotions the same way. Joy, fear, sorrow... I broke them down, sorted them out, and tried to explain them. But in the end, they all began to feel equally dry and lifeless.
Vidya: That's why emotion itself, and the relationship between emotion and morality, remained the one problem in my research I could never solve.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Your next holiday?
Vidya: If I want to leave Sumeru and go all the way to Snezhnaya for a performance, I need a break long enough to make the trip.
Vidya: It has to be approved by the Akademiya, and it has to be one that does not get derailed by research deadlines, evaluations, reviews, or whatever academic exchange comes up next.
Vidya: I just wish I could see a little more... even ten more minutes.
Vidya: *sigh* Let me sit here a little longer. There might still be an encore. Otherwise, I don't know when I'll ever get to sit in this theater again.

## Talk 3

Traveler: See you.
Vidya: Goodbye.

## Talk 4

Traveler: And how does that explain why you're staying?
Vidya: During that brief performance, people did not need to question where their laughter or tears came from, or whether those feelings were justified.
Vidya: All we had to do was follow the actors' songs and dances. Before reason had time to judge them, we already knew whose fate to cheer for, whose sorrow to weep over, and whose fear to share as if it were our own.
Vidya: Is this why Snezhnaya holds theater in such high regard? Maybe the whole point of it is to guide people's emotions and give those feelings somewhere to go. There is no need to analyze it or judge it.
Vidya: What a performance with so much to discuss... If I could see just a little more, even ten minutes, perhaps it would be enough to push my research forward.
Vidya: Maybe there is still an encore... Maybe if I stay a little longer, I will get those ten minutes after all...
