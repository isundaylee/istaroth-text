# Talk Group: Yaroslav (NpcGroup 7510)

## Talk 0

Yaroslav: Stay indoors and still travel the world — right here in Snezhnaya! We've got everything!

## Talk 1

Traveler: Everything?
Yaroslav: Everything! The sights and goods of nations across the world. You can find them all here.
Yaroslav: Inazuman novels, Sumeru research collections, Fontaine tea pastries, Natlan pigments...
Yaroslav: With just a small amount of Mora, what you gain here might even surpass what those traveling adventurers bring back!

## Talk 2

Traveler: Goodbye.
Yaroslav: Farewell.

## Talk 3

Traveler: No local Snezhnayan goods?
Yaroslav: Ah, I see you're not from around here, so let me be honest. What I really do is local business — for local people.
Yaroslav: On this vast land, most people spend their entire lives without ever crossing Snezhnaya's borders.
Yaroslav: But that doesn't mean they aren't curious about the outside world. It doesn't mean they don't long for something different.
Yaroslav: These little foreign curiosities — affordable, yet enough to weave a strange and distant dream...
Yaroslav: As they say, where there's demand, there's a market. Right?

## Talk 4

Traveler: I've actually been to those places. All of them.
Yaroslav: Then you are truly fortunate — a brave and wise adventurer, no doubt.
Yaroslav: But there are many who will never leave Snezhnaya in their entire lives.
Yaroslav: Perhaps what I sell are only ordinary trinkets, but for them, it may be the fastest way to glimpse a foreign world.

## Talk 5

Traveler: So how's business?
Yaroslav: To be honest, ever since Project Stuzha began, life has been hard for everyone...
Yaroslav: Forget buying souvenirs, people can barely afford necessities anymore.
Yaroslav: Before long, I may have to find another line of work.

## Talk 6

Traveler: How's business been?
Yaroslav: To be honest, ever since Project Stuzha began, life has been hard for everyone...
Yaroslav: The market's been rough, business too. I was already preparing to change careers...
Yaroslav: But wouldn't you know it — the Project was suspended not long ago!
Yaroslav: People are still stuck in their frugal habits, but I'm feeling fairly optimistic about the future.

## Talk 7

Traveler: Let's see what you've got...
Yaroslav: Certainly! Now, I got all these from outside... lots of them are "relics." The genuine article!
