# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 1792

## Talk 0

Ursula: As soon as I get this month's pay... I'm going to have a meal with Ematol. Then, I'm buying that limited-edition portable Commanders console... I really hope the price hasn't gone up again!

## Talk 1

Traveler: Why are you thinking about your salary while standing guard?

Ursula: Because I'm a workplace slacker... No, I am the Slacker Extraordinaire!

Ursula: Ever since we got back here to Dornman Port, things haven't been as busy as they were before. In fact, things have been pretty laid-back recently.

Ursula: It's not like when we were in Nod-Krai, when we barely had time to rest. If we weren't fighting monsters, we were digging trenches, building fortifications, cleaning our weapons, or hauling supplies...

Ursula: Now, whenever a knight goes on leave, I can volunteer to cover for them and earn myself some extra pay that way!

Traveler: How is this considered "slacking"?

Traveler: You're clearly putting in overtime.

Ursula: ...Right? No... I mean... Um... No?

Ursula: There's more money going into my pocket, so I'm the winner here... right?

Ursula: Besides, this slacker has other ways of earning herself more mora.

Ursula: The top three shooters at Ranged Company's monthly shoot also get a cash prize!

Ursula: And ever since they started the contest, this slacker has managed to win it every single time!

Traveler: But that's... still not "slacking," is it?

Traveler: I mean, you clearly got yourself a bonus for good performance!

Ursula: But working with everyone makes me happy, shooting makes me happy, cleaning my musket makes me happy, and eating those canned rations makes me happy...

Ursula: My life is full of things that make me happy, and I'm raking in the mora! So no matter how I look at it, I have the one-up over the Knights of Favonius.

Traveler: Are you saving up for something big? Or are you really being paid that little?

Ursula: Oh no, that's not it. It's because... Armored Assault is pretty popular. The price of the limited-edition Commanders console in galactic silver, which was produced in very small numbers, has also been skyrocketing over the years.

Ursula: If I'm not wrong, I'm actually in the salary tier that's just below the vice captain's...

Ursula: But even with my paycheck, those models are quite the expense. It's amazing to me how some folks can snap up a dozen of them at one go...

Traveler: Actually, it sounds like you're doing quite well.

Traveler: It sure doesn't sound like you need a raise anytime soon.

Ursula: I mean, apart from not being able to splurge on all the models and comics I want, I can pretty much buy anything!

Ursula: I've got quite a decent bit of mora left over to eat something delicious with Ematol every month!

Ursula: If only that Erminie would stop taking up so much of Ematol's time... I just need to get my gift ready for that gloomy Phonia.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Ematol?

Ursula: She's a good friend of mine from the Investigative Team! A first-class expert who always hooks me up with wonderful ammo!

Ursula: I got to know her when we were both at Support Company's clinic.

Ursula: When I first met her, she was covered in bandages from head to toe — she'd misjudged the safety distance for a controlled demolition and sent herself flying...

Ursula: And I was there after I'd botched the testing of high-pressure rounds for a new musket. I fired too many rounds in a single burst, the musket blew up, and a nasty fragment knocked out my front tooth...

Traveler: That sounds like quite the disaster...

Ursula: I suppose? It... didn't hurt that much though?

Ursula: She burst out laughing when she saw the state I was in, and I couldn't help but laugh along too. That got us chatting, and we became friends.

Ursula: I think we'd still be meeting up for meals every week if I hadn't been sent out with the expeditionary force!

Ursula: Ematol even said that she was going to get me some grenades that pack a serious punch. Nothing like those clunky, launcher-dependent ones that Fontaine's Special Security and Surveillance Patrol uses...

Ursula: These grenades fit right over the muzzle and are fired using a blank round. With this method, we get a much, much larger caliber and payload...

Ursula: The goal here is to be able to shatter a Mitachurl's shield with a single hit!

Ursula: Oddly, while Ematol is an expert on ammo, she knows nothing about muskets. I just hope she doesn't end up in the clinic again from one of her tests...

## Talk 3

Traveler: Good luck!

Ursula: Thank you, you too!
