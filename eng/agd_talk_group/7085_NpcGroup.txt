# Talk Group: Valehti (NpcGroup 7085)

## Talk 0

Valehti: Want to hear a secret? It won't come cheap. Though if you'd rather hear about how I battled a sea serpent... Well, I'll tell you that story for free.

## Talk 1

Traveler: What secret?
Valehti: I could tell you, but, well, rules are rules — you ought to show a little goodwill so I know you're legit.
Valehti: I'll cut to the chase — no haggling — 200,000 per piece of intel.

## Talk 2

Traveler: I'll take the story, please.
Valehti: Beneath the rolling waves of the boundless Forsaken Sea, there lurked a mighty beast, a colossal sea serpent that even Kratti — though troubled — was unable to best. In those days, youth was still mine to own, and I was famed throughout my homeland for my strength and valor.
Valehti: Slaying the serpent was an easy task... Ah, I can still recall the warmth of its blood washing down my face, like wine at a victory feast. From that day forward, I gained the ability to understand the whisperings of animals.
Valehti: I won't lie, much of my intel comes from their words — words only I can comprehend.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Maybe next time.
Valehti: Are you sure you want to turn your back on such a rare opportunity?

## Talk 4

Traveler: That's too steep for me.
Valehti: Ha! The price is right — if you're not willing to pay, it just means this secret isn't meant for you.

## Talk 5

Traveler: How would I know if it was the intel I needed?
Valehti: Ha! I see through your ruse. You're trying to trick me into revealing my intel for free. No Mora, no secret!

## Talk 6

Traveler: Are you trying to trick me?
Valehti: Of course not! Do you take me for one of those Frostmoon Schemers?

## Talk 7

Traveler: (*Jahoda* appears to have something on her mind...)
Valehti: Well, well, well! If it isn't Jahoda! Come, come. Want to hear about—
Jahoda: No, I don't.
Valehti: But I haven't even said anything yet! I'm not talking about sea serpents or Amrita this time, it's about—
Jahoda: No, I don't want to hear about it.
Jahoda: My purse is shut tight, Valehti. So save your breath.
Valehti: Tch... You really don't know a good thing when you see it. But hey, no worries... I'm sure someone out there has a better eye...
