## Talk 3

(Enjoy teatime)

Lauma: ...
Marionette: ...
Marionette: ...So, how have the Frostmoon Scions been faring lately?
Lauma: Quite well. The return of the Moon Goddess has strengthened our resolve to walk our given path.
Marionette: Really? Well, that's good to hear.
Marionette: ...
Lauma: ...
Marionette: Hah... I suppose we really do have nothing to talk about.
Lauma: I'm afraid that... is because there's far too much history between the Frostmoon Scions and the Fatui.
Lauma: Speaking purely for myself, I'm grateful for your efforts in protecting Nod-Krai and welcoming back the Moon Goddess, Miss Sandrone. But as for your associates...
Marionette: I understand. For various reasons, we failed to establish friendly relations — or even mutually beneficial ones.
Marionette: But the Fatui no longer seek to take anything from the Frostmoon Scions, and I have reminded my subordinates not to make things difficult.
Marionette: From where I stand, seeing as we're neighbors, even if we can't coexist in perfect harmony, it would be nice if we could at least treat each other with mutual respect.
Lauma: I'm afraid that will take time...
Marionette: Undoubtedly. But we can start by taking one small step at a time. Would you like to try this herbal tea I prepared?
Lauma: Oh? Herbal tea...?
Lauma: Hmm... Such rich flavor. Did you brew this the Frostmoon Scions' way? Most outsiders I've met don't like our tea. How did you...
Marionette: By learning. And showing respect.
Lauma: Hah... Not a bad first step.
Marionette: I hope it suits your tastes.
Lauma: I like it a lot.
Lauma: Amicable exchanges over hot pots of tea have always been a great way to strike up friendly relations.
Lauma: I have a feeling this time will be no exception.
