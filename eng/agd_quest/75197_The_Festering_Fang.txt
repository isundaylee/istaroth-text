# The Festering Fang


## Talk 1

Unknown Role (None): Ah, I was hoping you'd swing by. Yes, about those Dragonteeth... Oh, I see you've already finished gathering them up.

Unknown Role (None): When you were off exploring, I ended up hitting a slight snag over here... No matter what I hit this Dragontooth with, I simply cannot take so much as a sliver off of it.

Unknown Role (None): I've never seen anything like this before, so I had someone talk to Mr. Albedo to see how we should deal with these materials.

Unknown Role (None): And so, if you look over yonder, you'll see that Mr. Albedo had Miss Sucrose send over a Runestone. It's right on the Alchemist's Crafting Bench.

Unknown Role (None): A Runestone? What's that got to do with our Dragonteeth?

Unknown Role (None): Well, according to what Miss Sucrose relayed to us from Mr. Albedo, we need to give the material some special treatment before we can use it to forge weapons.

Unknown Role (None): One of said methods is to have one Dragontooth absorb dragon-related "vitality," then used the vitalized Dragontooth to treat other Dragonteeth.

Unknown Role (None): As for what Miss Sucrose said about the Runestone... Ah, yes, she said it was "a relic left to the Knights of Favonius by a wandering troupe that helped Mondstadt rebel against the old nobility a thousand years ago."

Unknown Role (None): The flautist of that troupe once fought against a monster known as "Bakunawa" in the Mare Jivari. This Runestone was carved from its body...

Unknown Role (None): Eh? Wait a sec, if Paimon's memory isn't failing her, didn't Citlali tell us that the Mare Jivari was created by Sanhaj's battle with the Abyss... five hundred years ago?

Unknown Role (None): Are we sure the troupe's story is from a thousand years back?

Unknown Role (None): Haha, how could it be mistaken? That wandering troupe's story of heroism has long since been recorded in the "History of Kings and Clans."

Unknown Role (None): Anyway, Mr. Albedo said that this Runestone contains "infinite vitality," similar to that of a dragon.

Unknown Role (None): All you need to do is use the Crafting Bench to absorb the vitality of the Runestone with the Dragontooth, then we can get to forging our weapons.

Unknown Role (None): That sounds impressive... as expected of Albedo...

Unknown Role (None): We'll have to thank him later.

Unknown Role (None): #Yeah! If not for him, we'd be totally stuck... Anyway, Traveler, let's try absorbing the Runestone's power!

Unknown Role (None): Alrighty, I'll handle it.

Unknown Role (None): Haha, that's great! Here — you take this Dragontooth, and I'll leave that whole side of things to you.

Unknown Role (None): Alchemy's truly something else, huh... How does one even absorb "vitality" from a thousand year-old stone?
