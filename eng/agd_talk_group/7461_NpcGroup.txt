# Talk Group: Sereda (NpcGroup 7461)

## Talk 0

Sereda: Found some cheap, tasty ingredients again today. Time to make a few extra dishes for the lads!

## Talk 1

Traveler: What's on the menu?
Sereda: Well, between you and me, I found some damp rye and potatoes frozen hard as rocks. Only one-fifth the usual price.
Sereda: For a unit with a limited food budget, the most important things are "filling stomachs" and "not poisoning anyone."
Sereda: But a combat chef's duty is to take the cheapest, most ordinary ingredients and turn them into meals that satisfy the soldiers.
Sereda: So the staples I buy may not be the finest quality. The priority is quantity above all else.
Sereda: Whatever funds are left go toward ingredients that improve the flavor, like well-seasoned cured meat and rimecurrant.
Sereda: Lately, I've been trying to make a new kind of cured-meat bread. Ideally, it should be simple, tasty, and easy for soldiers stationed on the front lines to eat over long periods of time...

## Talk 2

Traveler: Could I get two portions, too?
Sereda: If I were still working at my old little restaurant, I'd be sure to cook you up a few of my best dishes.
Sereda: But right now, I have to make sure the lads in the army are fed first.
Sereda: Once I retire, please do come by my little restaurant again!

## Talk 3

Traveler: Congratulations!
Sereda: Thank you. I hope you find ingredients you're happy with, too, and get to enjoy the tastiest meals around!
