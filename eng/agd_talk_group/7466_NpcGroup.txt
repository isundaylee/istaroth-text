# Talk Group: Laktionov (NpcGroup 7466)

## Talk 0

Laktionov: The latest, most explosive news. Get yourself a copy!

## Talk 1

Traveler: How explosive are we talking?
Laktionov: Like imported fireworks bursting open, decorating the whole night sky with brilliant colors...
Laktionov: Choose the right kind of fireworks and set them in just the right places, and it is like filling the sky with colorful lights!

## Talk 2

Traveler: Do you have any calmer news...?
Laktionov: Sorry, then you will have to go to Katia. I don't have plain, boring newspapers here.
Laktionov: Er... What I mean is, every piece of news is interesting to me personally.
Laktionov: You have such a distinguished bearing, such elegant speech, so unlike everyone else...
Laktionov: If you find the newspaper uninteresting, just tell me, and I will pass your suggestions along to the editor!

## Talk 3

Traveler: I'll buy one next time.
Laktionov: Then... I will save you a copy every day, and wait for you to come buy it.

## Talk 4

Traveler: You make it sound like you know a lot about fireworks...
Laktionov: I do! My father once spent a fortune to take me out to set off some truly incredible Liyue fireworks!
Laktionov: But later on, I kept arguing with him, and we never did anything like that together again...
Laktionov: I was a disappointment. I never got into an art school with any real future, so I suppose he had every reason to beat me up...
Laktionov: Ahem. Got a bit off topic there. Anyway, just trust me when I say the papers I sell are packed with good stuff!
