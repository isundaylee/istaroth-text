## Talk 0

Zamorov: Who's the face of the theater? Why, the answer is obvious: the incomparable Vodyanitsa, of course!
Zamorov: Every time Lady Vodyanitsa takes the stage, the cheers are so deafening they nearly bring the roof down. Even the chandeliers are in danger of shattering!
Zamorov: Just look across the entire Korolevskiy Troupe — is there a single performer more beloved than she is?
Taisiya: Your reasoning is laughably simplistic. Those cheers and screams merely reflect fans imitating their favorite actress. They prove nothing about her popularity.
Taisiya: Odette's admirers, on the other hand, greet her arrival with the same grace and refinement she embodies — with dignified applause.
Taisiya: Such elegance is perfectly in keeping with the troupe's distinguished image. And image, after all, is the most important criterion when deciding who serves as its face.
Taisiya: The answer should therefore be self-evident.
Zamorov: Your thinking is hopelessly provincial. I'm not sure this conversation can continue.
Taisiya: No matter. I don't believe there's anything left for us to discuss, either.
