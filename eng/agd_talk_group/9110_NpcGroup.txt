# Talk Group: Iansan - Serenitea Pot (NpcGroup 9110)

## Talk 0

Iansan: Hello there. So this is where you live...
Iansan: Mind if I take a quick stroll around? You know me — I like to keep moving.

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Iansan: I like it a lot. The air here is crisp and fresh, which is good for both body and mind.
Iansan: Although, I didn't see a dedicated training area... Want me to help you set up a gym? I can plan it out and get the equipment for you.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Sure.
> Iansan: Alright, then I have some thinking to do.
> Iansan: First, we should tackle the equipment, and this is where it's important to take a lot of care. You wouldn't believe how many injuries start with poor equipment choice.
> Iansan: Oh, how about this? Give me some time, and I'll ask a friend to make you a custom set.

Option 2:

> Traveler: Eh, adventuring keeps me in good enough shape.
> Iansan: Very fair... Everyone has different approaches to physical fitness.
> Iansan: Some people prefer the great outdoors to a gym. For a traveler like you, hiking and running from place to place are great kinds of exercise.


## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to have a chat with me?
Iansan: Hmm...
Traveler: What's wrong? Are you worried about something?
Iansan: Ah... Sorry. I was just thinking about the curriculum for my next batch of students.
Traveler: Curriculum?
Iansan: Yeah, their training curriculum.
Iansan: Physical fitness is big thing in the Collective of Plenty. As the head coach of Fitness Club, I'm in charge of the newcomers.
Traveler: You can't just reuse the curriculum from previous years?
Iansan: Ah, you think the same exercises work for everyone? That's what most people think when they start out.
Iansan: Let me ask you this... What do you think is the most important thing when it comes to fitness?

Option 1:

> Traveler: Exercise.
> Iansan: Let's use that as an example, then. A lot of people think all exercises are created equal — as long as you're doing something to keep active, you'll achieve your fitness goals.
> Iansan: But in reality, everyone starts from a different baseline. So, I design regimens that account for different circumstances.
> Iansan: If a student is on the heavier side, I usually ease them into cardio exercises like running to lower their body weight.
> Iansan: If a student is very lean, I usually start them off with resistance training to build muscle.
> Iansan: Lumping everyone into the same regimen is just asking for injuries.

Option 2:

> Traveler: Nutrition.
> Iansan: Now, that's a whole other can of worms. But, at the most basic level, nutrition plans need to be tailored from student to student, as well.
> Iansan: For some students, it's about gaining weight — eating enough calories to keep up with the physical demands of training. That's something I always try to impart on my students — the number on the scale is not a measure of fitness.
> Iansan: For those who are at a good fitness level, it's all about balance. A lack of diligence about what you put in your body is a sure fire way to ruin all your progress.
> Iansan: So, I usually design customized nutrition plans for every student.

Iansan: As a result, a new batch of students means designing tailored curriculum to meet their needs.
Traveler: Wow, being a fitness coach sounds like a lot of work...
Iansan: Well, when you like what you do, it's not work at all!
Iansan: My Ancient Name means "strength." So, it feels really connected to my profession. Training is a means to gain strength, but you only get out what you put in. There are no shortcuts.
Iansan: With every student I teach, I gain a deeper understanding of my Ancient Name.
Iansan: By the way, do you have any fitness goals of your own? I can craft a training plan for you.

Option 1:

> Traveler: I think I'm good.
> Iansan: But, fitness is a journey, not a destination. You have a great foundation, which is never a bad thing, but don't get complacent, or it might not stay that way!

Option 2:

> Traveler: Please take me on, Coach.
> Iansan: Haha, welcome to the club. As far as the training plan goes... let's start with the nutrition side of things. It's the biggest hurdle for a lot of my students.


## Talk 3

Traveler: Anything you want to do?
Iansan: I like to meditate sometimes! I learned it from the Masters of the Night-Wind. It's a nice mental work-out.
Traveler: Is that considered a part of fitness training, too?
Iansan: Hmm... The best answer to that... is a story.
Iansan: Many, many years ago, there lived a great warrior in the Collective of Plenty. He loved to train, and loved showing off his strength even more.
Iansan: He started with weightlifting competitions. Then, he progressed to wrestling tatankasaurs, and finally, he challenged a Long-Necked Rhino.
Iansan: To everyone's surprise, he bested every challenge.
Iansan: People recognized him as the strongest warrior in Natlan, but he still wasn't satisfied. So, he came up with a crazy plan.
Iansan: Pushing a giant boulder up the sacred volcano and using it to seal off the crater... proving him capable of besting the might of Tollan in the process.
Iansan: He stopped at nothing to accomplish his goal, including ingesting dangerous medications.
Iansan: Finally, he made an attempt, and began pushing a mountain-sized boulder up the slope of the volcano.
Iansan: But, just as he made it half-way, the volcano erupted. He lost his footing, and the boulder fell and crushed him to death.
Iansan: Ironically, only a boulder of such enormous size could have bested him. Similarly, only a man of his strength could have placed himself in such a precarious position.
Iansan: In the end, it was his own desire to prove his strength that led to his downfall.
Traveler: Wow, I never knew...
Iansan: So, training isn't just about getting stronger. It's about control and discipline.
Iansan: That's why the mental side of training is so important. It means you're not a slave to your own power.
Traveler: Sounds like you're a master in that area, too.
Iansan: ...Actually, compared to the newcomers, it's harder for me to keep that desire in check sometimes.
Iansan: There was a time when I really liked pushing my limits. I always wanted to do that one extra push-up or bench press, and every time I got a personal best, I was ecstatic...
Iansan: Now that I look back on it, I feel like that was my own boulder and volcano.
Iansan: So, as a friend, I have a favor to ask — if I ever succumb to that mindless drive again, pull me back, okay?
Iansan: I know you have what it takes.
Traveler: You have my word.
Iansan: Thank you. Then, let's close our eyes and meditate together. Oh, and don't try and fake it, okay? I can always tell.
