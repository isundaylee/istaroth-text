# Talk Dialog

Brest: I can't help feeling like it's not fast enough. It would be great if we could modify things to speed up the experience for our guests. Maybe if we installed a void generator on the front and some sort of propulsion device on the back...
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Are you planning on firing our guests out like cannonballs?
        Brest: Of course! This is supposed to be a thrilling attraction, after all! The faster the ride, the more exciting the experience!
        Brest: That said, what with this being an underwater attraction, you should really be comparing things to torpedoes rather than cannonballs...
        Brest: Obviously, this is an amusement park, so safety is paramount. That's why I want to provide reliable protection for every guest — something along the lines of what the clockwork meka are armored in should do.
        Brest: Not to mention a sufficiently thick and cushioned buffer layer to absorb unforeseen impacts.
        Brest: Hmm... Something seems off about this. In order to meet my design criteria, these suits are going to have to be sturdy enough to knock out a Hunter's Ray...
    Option 2:
        Traveler: Have you considered the cost?
        Brest: Now that you mention it, the ticket price is quite cheap. If we want to rescale these suits for commercial use, the R&D budget alone...

        Brest: On second thoughts... Covering production costs by selling amusement park equipment to law enforcement agencies... It feels like we've got this all backwards somehow.
    Option 3:
        Traveler: How about I leave you to figure out the details?
        Brest: Hmm... I'll report back to you when I've reached a conclusion! You're the boss, after all. Your approval is non-negotiable!
