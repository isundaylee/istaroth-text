Unknown Role (None): The Consilium Sapientium?

Unknown Role (None): Trust in the wisdom of the elderly is a tradition here. Since ancient times, all affairs in the town have been assessed by a committee of its elders.

Unknown Role (None): And of these elders, the most trustworthy would be selected as "Doge."

Unknown Role (None): And that is me, old and unskilled though I may be.

Unknown Role (None): Though given the role of overseer, I must say that there is little for me to oversee here. We live simple lives and have simple customs. All we do, we do by tradition.

## Talk 3

Unknown Role (None): About Petrichor's customs...

Unknown Role (None): Petrichor is a romantic, idyllic place. People respect custom and are customarily polite and friendly to outside guests.

Unknown Role (None): Of course, some of us go a little overboard, and as a result may seem almost... wary, perhaps.

Unknown Role (None): But do forgive us. Petrichor is far from the Court of Fontaine, and people here have hardly ever left the town.

Unknown Role (None): Just as a stone thrown into an ancient well can stir up ripples, people always fear that outlanders may disturb our peace.

Unknown Role (None): So please, do not hold it against them!

## Talk 4

Unknown Role (None): About Petrichor's history...

Unknown Role (None): Haha! Well, there's much to tell. Petrichor's got a history longer than the Court of Fontaine, after all — so long that ten operas wouldn't be enough to get through it all.

Unknown Role (None): Five hundred years ago, this place was still governed by the de Petrichor family, and their lineage had long ruled here.

Unknown Role (None): That had been the case ever since the first Duke appointed by Egeria, and they were, one and all, just and benevolent leaders who guided the town to ever-increasing prosperity.

Unknown Role (None): Alas, the final patrono of the family was transferred to be mayor of Poisson — there, he lost his life, and there did the family tree wither.

Unknown Role (None): For a time after, it seemed as though the town would become some abandoned fishing village... At least until after our first Doge Ruggiero established the Consilium Sapientium and restored our fortunes.

Unknown Role (None): The townspeople at that time believed that the town fell into ruin because of our relations with the outside world grew too close, destroying our traditions.

Unknown Role (None): That said, our youngsters believe differently nowadays. That fellow Foscari's been advocating that it's time for change, too.
