Unknown Role (None): Hmm... In the past, small animals or kuuhenki would help deliver broken objects to an artisan's doorstep for me.

Unknown Role (None): All I had to do was wait for the artisan to find it. Of course, sometimes, that would take a few days.

Unknown Role (None): Yeah, just leave it to us. Who knows, we might even be able to get this done today.

Unknown Role (None): Yep, that clears it up for sure...
