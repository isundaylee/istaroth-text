# Talk Group: Bazhenov (NpcGroup 7486)

## Talk 0

Bazhenov: When it comes to Snezhnaya's safety and security, even the smallest error is unacceptable...

## Talk 1

Traveler: What are you doing?
Bazhenov: I'm carrying out a security inspection, traveler. Snezhnograd Central Station is the lifeline of the city, and our city is the very heart of Snezhnaya itself.
Bazhenov: Look at the area I'm responsible for, the exit gates. They're one of the station's most important checkpoints, wouldn't you say?
Bazhenov: For the safety and future of Snezhnaya, we must identify all possible risks and suspicious persons at the earliest, and report these at once.

## Talk 2

Traveler: See you.
Bazhenov: Goodbye, traveler.

## Talk 3

Traveler: But it looks safe enough here...
Bazhenov: That is what it means to prevent trouble before it happens, traveler.
Bazhenov: Avalanches can happen to you when you least expect it. One moment, you're on a peaceful snow-covered mountain, and the very next, you're running for your life. The same goes for tsunamis — they can rise all of a sudden out of the calmest waters.
Bazhenov: The calmer a place appears, the more likely it is that hidden currents are moving beneath the surface, with danger lurking everywhere. That is why we must remain vigilant at all times.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Suspicious?
Bazhenov: Just think about it: what if someone is smuggling Kristall? What if some spy tries to enter Snezhnograd from here to gather information on Her Majesty's whereabouts?
Bazhenov: What if someone tries to come into the city to scalp theater tickets and disrupt the market? Or what if foreigners arrive hoping to join the Transport Bureau, only to take all the places meant for Snezhnayans?
Bazhenov: If we allow them into Snezhnograd through the gates, there's no telling how many crises they might bring upon Snezhnaya...
Bazhenov: Oh... There are far too many possibilities. I mustn't dwell on them. I must focus on my duties.

## Talk 5

Traveler: Is all that part of your job too?
Bazhenov: Now, now... Even if I'm only required to guide passengers out of the station, I'm still a citizen of Snezhnaya. Isn't protecting our homeland's security part of our duty as well?
Bazhenov: I'll have to stop here, since I must keep my eyes on the exit gates at all times. Please excuse me.
