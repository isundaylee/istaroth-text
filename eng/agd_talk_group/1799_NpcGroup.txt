# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 1799

## Talk 0

Ematol: The new explosives are much more stable now... As long as we can lower production costs and increase consistency, we'll be good.

## Talk 1

Traveler: What are you doing?

Ematol: Ah! It's the Honorary Knight! Hello!

Ematol: Investigative Team Weapons Officer Ematol reporting! My new model of explosive has completed prototype testing!

Ematol: Based on current evaluations, its ideal detonation velocity has reached approximately one-fifth the level of the Spark Knight's Jumpty Dumpty.

Ematol: Once mass-produced, its overall performance is expected to improve further...

Ematol: ...Provided, of course, that the power of Spark Knight's explosives remains within the range of my previous observations...

Traveler: Wait... isn't that contradictory?

Traveler: Why is the power one-fifth...

Traveler: But the overall performance only one-tenth?

Ematol: Because the greater the power, the higher the cost. And mass-produced explosives have to account for price, manufacturability, and safety during transport and handling.

Ematol: In practice, the explosives we use are rarely composed of a single agent with the highest detonation velocity. They require desensitizers and stabilizers mixed in...

Ematol: Which affects processing difficulty, production speed, yield, and cost efficiency. All of which factor into the final performance rating.

Ematol: So higher power doesn't necessarily mean higher overall performance. Every component must be carefully balanced.

Traveler: What a strict assessment...

Traveler: Such high standards...

Ematol: Well, it's not about complexity or harsh standards. Anyone who wants to defeat monsters would pursue greater destructive force, wouldn't they?

Ematol: According to the Grand Master, and intelligence gathered from our allies in Natlan...

Ematol: That Fatui Harbinger, The Captain... the one who could casually destroy a Ruin Guard using nothing but the Spark Knight's crayon... He...

Ematol: He can throw the equivalent of ten knights' worth of carried explosives with a single swing of one hand from two hundred paces away, wiping out entire swarms of monsters!

Ematol: Right now, it's impossible to even imagine reaching that level of destructive power. What firearms will we need to develop to...

Ematol: The pursuit of combat power has no end... We have to keep pushing forward.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Never mind the explosives for a second. How have you been?

Ematol: Whoa... The Honorary Knight's worried about me? I—It's, uh, an honor!

Ematol: Don't worry! Phonia, Dustball, and I are all well-fed and well-rested every day...

Ematol: Ursula's been stationed in Dornman Port too, so we get to enjoy big meals together all the time!

Ematol: The only thing I miss is watching the Spark Knight's spectacular fish-blasting sessions... just a little...

Ematol: And aside from the occasional trip back to Mondstadt for development work and academic meetings, there's hardly anything to complain about!

Ematol: Right now, I'm the Investigative Team's public face here at Dornman Port, so I have to stay sharp and work hard.

Ematol: For my personal interests, Dornman Port is also a great place to be...

Ematol: Here, I get to work closely with the Ranged Company to combine explosives and muskets...

Ematol: A lot of ideas I used to only sketch might finally take shape and become reality. It's so exciting!
