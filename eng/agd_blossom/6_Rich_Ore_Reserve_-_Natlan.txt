# Rich Ore Reserve - Natlan

## A recently formed ore deposit containing an abundance of ores. For someone looking to strengthen their weapon, discovering this place would be like hitting the jackpot.

### Talk 0

Alom: Looking for intel on where to find ores around this neck of the woods, huh? I know some places where you can often find Condessence Crystals, but I can't leave right now. Are you able to gather them yourself?
Alom: I'll mark any such location on your map so you can go there and check things out. Be careful though.

### Talk 1

Ahoeitu: The guards have reported seeing some ores nearby, and it sounds like it's more of those Condessence Crystals that contain phlogiston... Not exactly headline news though.
Ahoeitu: If only we had someone from the Children of Echoes here, we could have consulted them about the quality... Though as I see it, they're all pretty much the same anyway. Every kind of material has its use, after all.
Ahoeitu: Anyway, if you're in need of ore, you can go mine some here.

### Talk 2

Yagbeu: Do rocks count? Just now, I heard someone mention that there's some good ore nearby.
Yagbeu: I don't know much about rocks though. If you're interested, go take a look.

### Talk 3

Apoxpalon: A few of my regulars have been kind enough to mention seeing some Condessence Crystals nearby. The phlogiston must be quite active in those areas.
Apoxpalon: I'm still nowhere near to running out myself though, so I thought I'd just leave them for someone else who needed them more than I did. They're probably around about... here. If you could use them, go check it out.

### Talk 4

Michica: One of my colleagues came across some newly formed Condessence Crystals on the way over here. Luckily, he was on the ball too, or he would've tripped over them.
Michica: Ores are very important resources, you know... They can be used to make lots of different things. If you have some spare time to go mine some, I'm sure they'd come in handy. They're not far off — I'll show you where to look.

### Talk 5

Utsil: Thanks to the Qucusaurs' protection, everything's going great! The Riders did espy some reflective objects from their vantage point — ore, if I had to guess.
Utsil: By the way, did you know that gem-related cards have some rather intriguing uses... Sorry, bit off-topic. If you need Condessence Crystals or something, you can just head over here.

### Talk 6

Tepano: I heard someone mention that some Condessence Crystals had emerged out of nowhere. Our first thought was that it might be Ley Line troubles, so a whole bunch of people rushed over to check things out.
Tepano: As it turned out, it was just some ordinary ore. Not that having a place to replenish resources is a bad thing, of course. If you wanna have a look, it should be in... this direction.

### Talk 7

Pahua: Let me think... Hmm, now that I think about it, when the volcano erupted before, it ejected a bunch of Condessence Crystals from deep within the earth.
Pahua: I'll mark the location on your map and you can check it out if you're interested. As far as you're concerned, I imagine digging up minerals counts as a productive training session.

### Talk 8

"Muchih": Anything of note? Well, I guess you should be careful on the beach so you don't step on seashells with your bare feet or get pinched by crabs...
"Muchih": Oh, right, Muchih accidentally trod on some sharp chunks of ore a little further down, and ended up cutting his feet.
"Muchih": Someone said they were called Condessence Crystals or something, and that they were worth more than crabs and shells. Made Muchih pretty happy, that. As long as you promise to watch your step, I'll mark out their location for you.

### Talk 9

Alom: You're looking for intel on where to find ores around this neck of the woods, huh? I know a few spots, but I can't get away right now. I've marked them on your map — why don't you give them a try yourself?
