Albedo: Of course, my family could scarcely be considered "normal." So, by that logic, that type of relationship holds little meaning.
Albedo: At the same time, Sebas did look after me quite a bit back in the day, helping me tidy up various vials and jars... He even modeled for my sketches at times.
