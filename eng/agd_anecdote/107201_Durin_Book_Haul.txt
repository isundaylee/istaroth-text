# Durin: Book Haul

In Nasha Town, you run into Durin as he's asking Jahoda for help with purchasing some books. Thanks to Jahoda's generous assistance, Durin manages to snag the tomes he's looking for at a sizeable discount...

## Talk 0

Jahoda: Okay, let's go with this one, this one... and this one. These are all pretty old, huh... Hey, we're buying so many. You can give us a discount, right?
Jahoda: And throw those two at the back into the bundle too. C'monnn, you've put them out for ages, and nobody's bought them anyway! We'll give them a home at half-off, hehe.
Durin: Thank you, Jahoda...
Jahoda: Eh, no biggie. It's just a few fairy tale books. Easy-peasy! Plus, with me around, you can be sure you're not getting ripped off.
Jahoda: But, dude, seriously, what are you gonna do with all these? Read 'em yourself?
Durin: Mm-hmm! I also want to bring them to Hat Guy. He probably hasn't read these books either.
Jahoda: Alright, you two really get along, huh. These books are all packed — here, take 'em. I've got some things to take care of, so I'll head off now.
Durin: Mm. Thank you, Jahoda.
Jahoda: Thanking me once is enough... *cough* Anyway, I'm going. Catch you next time.
