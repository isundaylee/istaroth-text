## Talk 3

(Enjoy teatime)

Navia: Ah! Miss Columbina, Miss Sandrone! Thank you for taking the time to join our Tabletop Troupe!
Marionette: The pleasure's all ours. We haven't really played this before, so we'll be counting on you to show us the ropes.
Clorinde: Don't worry, there's no pressure. The most important thing is that everyone has fun.
The Damselette: That's great! I'm really looking forward to this.
Clorinde: Then let me briefly explain the story...
Clorinde ("Game Master"): "This is an era where vampires and humans coexist. Our story occurs in a city shared by the two races..."
Clorinde ("Game Master"): "While not openly hostile, the relationship between humans and vampires is far from harmonious. Now, a murder in the dead of night has completely shattered the delicate balance..."
The Damselette: Vampires?
Navia: They're a race that survives by drinking human blood. They're a common trope in fantasy novels. Have you read any stories like this before, Miss Columbina?
The Damselette: Hmm, I think I may have heard of them. So what do we do in this story?
Clorinde: First, everyone needs to decide what character they want to be. You can choose any identity and profession you like, provided it fits the setting.
The Damselette: What character do I want to be...
Clorinde: If you're having trouble deciding, you can team up with someone else and let them think of a character for you.
Marionette: Oh? You mean... we can let someone else create our backstories?
Navia: Oh, that sounds like fun! Let's all do that! I'll leave my character sheet to you, Clorinde!
The Damselette: Then you can decide my character, Sandrone, and I'll think up the perfect role for you.
Marionette: Hah... Works for me. I wasn't sure what profession to pick anyway.
Navia: Ooh, is everyone finished? Hehe, I can't wait to see what mine says...
Navia: "I'm a merchant who plies her trade in a city where vampires and humans coexist..."
Navia: ...Wait a minute. Game Master, this character sheet is suspiciously similar to my last one... Even the stats are the same! You just copied the whole thing!
Clorinde: No matter what I give you, you'll still find some weird way to derail the story. Besides, I think this type of shrewd, boisterous character suits you right down to the ground.
Navia: ...Hey!
The Damselette: Haha. Let me see who I am...
The Damselette: "I'm a big, bad vampire. During the day, I like to sleep at home, only going outside to take strolls in the garden at night when the moon is out."
The Damselette: "I like to sing, and I use my voice to lure in my prey. No one who's seen into my eyes has ever lived to see the sunrise."
The Damselette: Hmm... Is this how you see me, Sandrone?
Marionette: Hehe, don't you think it suits you? Plus, it's a fascinating character.
The Damselette: Alright, but I still want to try my best to be a slightly... kinder-hearted vampire.
Marionette: Whatever you think is best. Right, let's see mine...
Marionette: "I'm an elegant, noble lady who likes to tinker with inventions in my room. I go to sleep very late, and keep a highly irregular schedule..."
Marionette: Hey! What sort of person introduces themselves like this!?
Marionette: Hmph... "But I'm also a vampire hunter. When someone in the city is in danger, I bravely use my inventions to save them..."
Marionette: ...Huh? This is nothing like me at all!
The Damselette: You don't think so? I think it's exactly like you.
Navia: But based on these introductions, it seems like Miss Columbina and Miss Sandrone's characters should be sworn enemies!
Clorinde ("Game Master"): Hmm, well it certainly seems like this story is going to throw up some fascinating developments.
Navia: Oh! I can't wait!
Marionette: Hah... I feel like this story is gonna throw up a lot of headaches...
The Damselette: You're worried how this'll play out, aren't you, Sandrone? Is it because I'm an evil, bloodsucking vampire?
Marionette: Hah! The only thing I'm worried about is missing my chance to set your character straight!
