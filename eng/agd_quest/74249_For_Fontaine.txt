## Talk 3

(Enjoy teatime)

Furina: I feel simply awful leaving all the work to you again, Miss Guillotin.
Marionette: It's no trouble. This is the least I can do after your recent support during the Fontinalia Film Festival.
Furina: Oh, please! The actors were the true stars of the show.
Marionette: Don't you plan to return to performing yourself, Miss Furina?
Furina: Well... Never say never... But, uh... right now, I prefer working behind the scenes.
Marionette: Truth be told, when I still lived in Fontaine, even I knew who you were — the universally beloved superstar, Furina.
Furina: Ugh, please don't! Whatever you do, don't say you "grew up watching my performances"... It makes me feel so awkward...
Marionette: Oh, you've no need to worry there. I just wanted to say that when I left Fontaine, I had the honor of seeing you as you were back then.
Marionette: But seeing you again after all this time, I think I prefer the way you carry yourself now.
Furina: Huh...? The way I carry myself now?
Marionette: Yes. You used to look so tense on stage, but these days you seem a lot more relaxed.
Furina: Hehe... Thank you for saying so. I actually am enjoying life a lot more these days.
Furina: And what about you, Miss Guillotin? Are you content with your current lifestyle? Compared to when you departed Fontaine, I mean.
Marionette: Everything's going well, though I suppose that's mostly down to realigning my state of mind. In truth, I've always liked Fontaine, and it's always felt like home.
Marionette: I'm not saying it's better than other nations, but it holds so many memories. Life here can be as calm as still water, but it can also be filled with novel changes.
Marionette: I've recently learned to see the brighter side of life. I suppose people in other places view their homelands in much the same way.
Furina: Wow... How beautifully put, Miss Guillotin.
Furina: This might be a slight breach of tea party etiquette, but... I propose a toast. To Fontaine!
Marionette: Haha... To our homeland.
