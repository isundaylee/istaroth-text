# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 4497

## Talk 0

Souka: Hey there, big shots! Any orders for me? Novelist and up-and-coming mercenary Souka is at your command!
Paimon: You know, it sometimes surprises Paimon how Souka's been getting more and more energetic and motivated ever since she started looking after the Fungi friends with Haniyyah.

## Talk 1

Traveler: How's the novel business going?
Souka: Haha! The prose for my novella, Labyrinth of Legend: A Hero's Journey, was actually pretty well-received...
Souka: But when Aratani asked me if I planned to keep it going as a series, I thought about it and decided that I wanted to focus my energy on the works related to my Fungi friends, so I turned him down...
Souka: So, barring any unforeseen circumstances, "using pictures and a broadcasting device to turn my novel into an audio-visual, three-dimensional reading experience"...
Souka: Due to insufficient funding, this project was scaled back heavily. In the end, all that was left were the text and illustrations, making it no different from any other ordinary novel.
Paimon: Aw, that's a shame...
Souka: It's no big deal! That's just how it goes with new-generation projects these days.
Souka: The plan was made to be so ideal, but when the time to find artists and composers came, I ran into walls everywhere... Then there's also that claymore they call a "budget" hanging over my head!
Souka: Having that claymore drop on me early was actually for the best. It kept me from getting carried away with unrealistic fantasies.
Souka: The creative process is essentially just a series of collisions... Sometimes, we collide into walls! Thud after thud after thud... but one must learn to embrace the small joys along the way.
Paimon: Uh... Paimon isn't sure if she should comfort you or call you an optimist.
Souka: Anyway, with the bad news out of the way, I have some truly good news to share~
Souka: I've finished my latest work, "The Fungi Clan"! It's already with the editors for reviewing and proofreading.
Souka: In a little over half a year, it should be ready for printing. I'll give you each a copy then!
Traveler: You went with this title in the end?
Traveler: I figured you'd go for something more complex.
Souka: I won't lie, I really wanted to go with that naming style that's all the rage nowadays. The one where you take a long sentence and insist on making it the title.
Souka: However, many light novels that attract audiences with quirky titles also feature rather peculiar settings, with plot twists and positive feedback so abundant the story is saturated with them.
Souka: Authors who can keep a story racing forward and consistently give their audience a sense of satisfaction are certainly impressive. But not every work has to be like that...
Souka: If all authors just aim for the so-called "optimal solution" in their creations, wouldn't it become a kind of... cliche? Sooner or later, the audience is bound to grow weary of it.
Paimon: That... makes sense. Reading one or two stories with the same formula's more than enough.
Paimon: If everyone writes this way, it might end up doing more harm than good.
Traveler: You need a mix of fast and slow to keep things balanced.
Traveler: A wealth of different styles keeps things interesting.
Souka: In any case, while writing, I've realized that the bond Haniyyah and I share with our Fungi friends, and the adventures we had together, feel much more traditional and slow-paced...
Souka: So, returning to a simple, modest title actually fits the vibe of our story much better. I just hope that when the time comes, the readers it attracts will take their time to savor the narrative!

## Talk 2

Traveler: Up-and-coming mercenary? How did that happen?
Souka: Hooah! I've been with Haniyyah for so long that I've picked up all her combat moves!
Souka: Not only that, but I've actually started taking on mercenary orders in between my writing to help make ends meet.
Souka: Sure, a legendary beast would probably send me flying in a single hit. But chasing off a few hilichurls, scorpions or vultures? I can handle that!
Paimon: Paimon never thought things would take this turn when she first saw you in Haniyyah's clothes...
Paimon: Is this, uh, normal for a light novel author? Are you sure you'll be fine?
Souka: Of course I'm doing this right!
Souka: Tagging along with Haniyyah to share her food, drinks, and even her Fungi friends, embarking on adventures in foreign lands that I once thought of as distant frontiers...
Souka: Even when I'm just sitting still in camp, there's a steady stream of new things to see and hear. The inspiration to write just flows toward me like a mountain spring...
Souka: Could I get any luckier as a light novel author? It feels wrong to just sit back and enjoy all of this without paying her my fair share!
Souka: I've been setting aside a portion of my royalties and wages over the last few years, and I'm going to give it all to her when the time comes!

## Talk 3

Paimon: That's right. You always seem to be in such high spirits, so... just don't forget to eat well and get enough sleep.
Souka: Understood! That goes for both of you big shots too!
