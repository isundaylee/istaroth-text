# The Underwater Salvage Commandments

Freminet seems to have something to tell us...


## Talk 1

(Talk to Freminet)

Paimon: Freminet? You look exhausted! You haven't been working yourself too hard, have you?
Freminet: Uhm...
Paimon: Hey! Don't you go hiding inside your diving helmet! We're your friends. You'll feel a lot better if you let it all out.
Freminet: ...*sigh* I guess you're right.
Freminet: I bumped into a real piece of work while diving just now.
Paimon: A real piece of work?
Freminet: Yeah... How can I explain it... According to Fontainian law, if an owner of sunken cargo accepts an insurance payout, the cargo becomes unclaimed property.
Freminet: Because of this, some people see diving as a way to get rich. They salvage things others have lost, and sell them for Mora back on dry land...
Freminet: This line of work doesn't require much skill, which means there are plenty of divers who are... well, the sort who only care about money.
Freminet: They often resort to barbaric means to drive away fish and Blubberbeasts, just to gain access to valuable salvage... It's largely because of people like them that I don't pick up certain commissions.
Paimon: So that's what's bothering you... Paimon can really sympathize with how you feel.
Freminet: Just now, while I was diving, I ran into someone driving away the wildlife. I mustered up the courage and managed to stop them, but it ended with them saying some pretty unpleasant things.
Paimon: That's awful...

Option 1:

> Traveler: You did the right thing!

Option 2:

> Traveler: We can stop them for you next time!

Freminet: Thanks for the kind offer, but... even though it's difficult and leaves me feeling drained, I still want to stand up for what's right and try to make a change myself.
Freminet: I love swimming underwater, seeing the patterns of Beryl Conches, feeling the breath of Romaritime Flowers...
Freminet: When the underwater world accepts you, it responds with kindness.
Freminet: I believe that's how we should interact with nature, rather than just selfishly taking whatever we want.
Paimon: Wow, Freminet, Paimon thinks everything you just said was great! See? When you have something you really want to say or do, doesn't it feel much better to just get it off your chest?
Freminet: Uhm...
Paimon: Freminet?
Freminet: I'm sorry... I'm still a little bit shy. But don't worry... I—I'll keep working on it.
Freminet: Oh, I almost forgot... Here's the latest underwater survey data. Please take it, I'm sure you'll find it helpful.
