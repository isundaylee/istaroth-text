## Talk 3

(Enjoy teatime)

Marionette: To be honest, I'm still in shock.
Marionette: You know, back then, both Alain and I thought you'd already left this world.
Mary-Ann: Hmm... If we had woken up earlier, maybe we could have visited you and Alain. But, I suppose that's fate.
Ann: Ms. Mary-Ann...
Mary-Ann: Fate works in mysterious ways. Who would have thought that Ann and I would see a film poster featuring your portrait during our travels?
Marionette: So that's what made you think to contact me?
Ann: Precisely!
Mary-Ann: Ah... The aroma of coffee. It really takes me back... Hehe, thank you for inviting us to your tea party, Miss Sandrone.
Mary-Ann: Your garden is so elegantly arranged. It's perfect for a lady of such grace.
Marionette: This is the home Alain and I once shared. I feel it's only right that I maintain its beauty.
Mary-Ann: See? You're already so much better at this than my brother.
Ann: Alain never even decorated his room. He couldn't deal with pretty things.
Mary-Ann: That's right, and Rene was no different. Jakob and I were the only ones who could properly spruce up a room — and prepare a tea party.
Marionette: So he was like that even when he was little? I always thought it was due to his age...
Mary-Ann: He was always that way. He may have been book smart, but he was a bit of a klutz in other ways.
Mary-Ann: Maybe that's why he was the only one of us who chose painful reality over the illusion of a sweet dream.
Marionette: Huh. I don't think he regretted that choice.
Ann: No, because that was his wish as a hero.
Ann: The regret is ours. His wish should never have stood alone.
Mary-Ann: What about you, Miss Sandrone? Is there something you wish for?
Marionette: Alain once asked me that exact same question.
Mary-Ann: Hmm? So he asked you that, too?
Mary-Ann: My brother must have matured a lot. He never would have asked something like that when I knew him.
Marionette: "Wish" is such a hollow word. Can you ever have whatever you want? Hah, if that were the case, there would be no bad in the world.
Marionette: I'm doing what I want to do, what I believe to be right. I don't know if this counts as a wish, but it makes me happy.
Mary-Ann: Hmm, now that is a wonderful thing. I envy you.
Mary-Ann: Then do you... resent Alain? Because he created you... in Mary-Ann's image.
Marionette: Never. He always made it clear that I'm my own person — unique and distinct — and that I should choose my own life. So that's exactly what I did.
Ann: Haha, whether it's stories or food, as long as you're happy with your choices, then they're the right choices for you, Sandrone.
Mary-Ann: Exactly. So now, let's just quietly enjoy the food you've so carefully prepared for us.
