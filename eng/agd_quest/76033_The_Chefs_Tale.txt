# The Chef's Tale


## Talk 4

Food-Obsessed Phantom: Hey, newbie. You can't be making food like that.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: We're making rations for the Ratniki on the island, so we've gotta cook it to their tastes.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: These dishes won't do at all. First, your heat control's terrible, and second, this stuff doesn't suit our diet. That's gotta change.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: You've still got a lot to learn — I suggest starting from our most basic dishes, like Nod-Krai Hot Dogs. Come back and show me once you're done.

## Talk 6

Food-Obsessed Phantom: Mmm, the sausage is nicely grilled.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: You'd make a half-decent sous chef if you were working in a restaurant outside, I'd say.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: But we're making meals for every Ratnik on this island. So it's not about how fancy the food is, but whether it's plentiful enough.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: Make the bread thicker. There's not a ton of vegetables right now, and you could also add some meats apart from sausages.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: You still need more practice! Come back next time and make some Berries and Fried Meat.

## Talk 9

Food-Obsessed Phantom: Mm. The meat's been sliced into sizable chunks, then fried perfectly after that. Not bad.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: But let me remind you. When making fried meat, you must consider your side dishes.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: It's easy to get stuffed from eating too much meat. That's why the berries are there.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: Here on this island, we're in charge of everyone's dietary health, so a balance must be struck between vegetables and meat.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: This dish doesn't have enough such veggies on the side. We can't have that here.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: You need to practice more. Come back next time and whip up some Lakkaberry Madame.

## Talk 12

Food-Obsessed Phantom: The chicken's flavorful, and there are sufficient side dishes. You tryin' to make it tough for me to nitpick, huh?
Food-Obsessed Phantom: ...Heh. Whatever. Not that I was ever looking to find fault with you newbies specifically.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: I'm just a chef. I just do my job, and I want to do it well.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: And to that end, I made all kinds of preparations for the Wild Hunt's arrival.
Food-Obsessed Phantom: But it's a pity... I couldn't send them the finished dishes in time... The food wound up spilled... spilled...
