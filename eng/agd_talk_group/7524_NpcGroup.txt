# Talk Group: Zybina (NpcGroup 7524)

## Talk 0

Zybina: *Sigh* I can't believe the President... the old sir... is gone. And — why am I doing so much work by myself!?

## Talk 1

Traveler: You don't look very happy...
Zybina: Tell me, who's happy at work? ...But yes, I've been feeling down. The old sir, our former President... he passed away so suddenly.
Zybina: I'd been working under him ever since I first started here. Once in a while, when things went sideways at work, he'd actually step in himself to clear up the mess.
Zybina: *Sniffle* I always thought he'd still be here long after I'm gone, what with him being a fae and all...

## Talk 2

Traveler: Tell me more about this "President" of yours...
Zybina: Well, "former President" now, to be exact. I mean, he's gone... I just keep calling him the President out of plain habit.
Zybina: Old man Izmaylov lived for a real long time. Sometimes, he'd even tell us stories about how Snezhnaya used to be.
Zybina: ...Sadly, we won't be hearing those stories anymore.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Can't you get anyone to help you?
Zybina: We did have this "technical expert" with us before... And troubleshooting and problem-fixing was a lot smoother and quicker when he was around.
Zybina: But that gentleman must've been sent somewhere else, since we don't see him much around here anymore. I'm the only one left around here to handle these technical issues now.
