# Those Strange and Intriguing Questions


## Talk 1

Fadaweh: Atashye! I've tried my best to remain calm, but these reports you keep handing in are useless!

Fadaweh: Just look at the things you write! "Possibly," "probably," "roughly"... How am I supposed to build a mathematical model based on uncertainties!?

Atashye: Th—Th—That's... I don't have enough time! Not to mention, I'm from Kshahrewar! How am I supposed to know how to measure the impacts on living organisms? T—T—Take Fungi as an example...

Atashye: Wh—Wh—What am I supposed to do with Fungi? Do you want me to stick my hand in one's mouth and let it bite me so I can write down something like "Hmm, that is a surprisingly powerful bite"?

Atashye: Darwish, you're from the Amurta Darshan, back me up here, will you?

Darwish: Haha, if you really want me to get involved, I'd have to start by saying that your grasp of basic biology is shockingly poor — surely everyone knows that most Fungi don't have mouths...

Fadaweh: Ha!

Darwish: However, Fadaweh, we don't require an exceedingly precise model this time. Our research isn't aimed at those stuffy old scholars at the Akademiya...

Darwish: The purpose of this little project is to provide knowledge to children. It's supposed to be engaging. A little imagination is hardly unwarranted.

Fadaweh: O—Oh... I see. Well, if you say so...

Atashye: Hmph! Honestly, Fadaweh. The way you speak to Darwish... You're a whole different person from when you talk to me!

Darwish: Alright, alright, no need to argue. I've pretty much finished everything I was working on, and I've reviewed Atashye's conclusions.

Darwish: Besides, the data can be adjusted once the test has concluded. That's the whole point of testing.

Fadaweh: A—Ah, of course, Darwish... Clear-headed and organized as always! Unlike a certain someone...

Atashye: Indeed, indeed... And no, I'm not asking who that "certain someone" is.

Darwish: Can you two please knock it off? We've got final preparations to make!

Fadaweh: A—As you say!

Atashye: And I'll go check if the paint's dried...

Darwish: Speaking of the test... and test subjects... I don't suppose you two would be interested in helping out?

Darwish: I mean, you've been standing there watching us for a while now.

Paimon: Huh? Us?

Darwish: Yes, you. Do you want to take part in our test? You'll get to eat all the fruit you want!

Traveler: Are you asking us to test new varieties of fruit?

Darwish: Not quite... Hmm. How best to explain...

Traveler: Sorry, we're only interested in watching those two argue...

Paimon: Don't say that! You make it sound like we're a couple of passing weirdos.

Paimon: Maybe we should hear them out before deciding? Paimon wants to learn more about the fresh fru— Ahem, about this project...

Darwish: Such an inquisitive mind! I'm sure your travels have been blessed by luck.

Darwish: Now, let me introduce our research here. Hmm, how best to explain...

Darwish: How about this — I'll start by asking you a question... Let's see... "Who would win in a fight between a sliding Stretchy Pyro Fungus and a Rishboland Tiger?"

Traveler: The sliding Stretchy Pyro Fungus...

Paimon: You sure? Paimon feels like the Rishboland Tiger would have the advantage. Y'know, with those long, sharp claws...

Paimon: That's a very peculiar question...

Traveler: The Rishboland Tiger, obviously!

Paimon: Aha! Paimon would've gone for the tiger, too!

Paimon: That's a very peculiar question...

Traveler: A sliding Rishboland Tiger would win!

Paimon: Not an option! What's a sliding Rishboland Tiger anyway?

Paimon: Who asks crazy questions like that?

Darwish: Wait... there's more. "Who's stronger, a Floating Anemo Fungus or an Anemo Slime?" "Who are the better fighters, the Eremites or Natlan's Saurian Warriors? What about the Kairagi?"...

Darwish: These are the sort of questions that children always ask when we teach them.

Paimon: Little minds love asking those sorts of questions!

Darwish: Haha, but child-like curiosity is the foundation of learning! They "want to know"... and that's why we're doing all this!

Darwish: Observe — the "Companion Caper Chronicles," based on genuine real-world deductions! The children can build their own simulated adventure teams, then use them to battle each other!

Darwish: Atashye is researching the effects of diet and environment on all kinds of creatures, while Fadaweh is constructing the "simulated development" model.

Traveler: Simulated development?

Darwish: Yes, precisely! I mean, we cannot simply let children freely interact with real-world Fungi, Eremites, and such...

Darwish: So, what do you say? If you can spare the time, come take part in the test — you'll be helping with my research and gaining valuable academic experience all at once!

Paimon: #Hmm, that does sound good, doesn't it, Traveler?

Traveler: Alright then! "Wise Old Paimon" could do with some academic experience...

Paimon: Hmph! Don't you make fun of Paimon! Paimon's very smart, she'll have you know — and soon she'll be even smarter! Let's go do some research!

Darwish: #You're more than welcome to help out, Traveler. Of course, so is Paimon — a sage in the making if I ever saw one!

Darwish: Just let me know when you want to give it a go, and I'll run you through our "simulated development" rules.
