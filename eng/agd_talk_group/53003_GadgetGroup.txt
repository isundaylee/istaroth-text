# Talk Group: GadgetGroup - 53003

## Talk 0

Lightkeepers' Long Table: This looks like a long table used by the *Lightkeepers* during their banquets.

## Talk 1

Flins: (This grand mead hall is located at the very top level of Piramida. It is here that banquets are often held following hard-won victories.)
Flins: (It is both a celebration of the renewed triumph of the lights of Nod-Krai over the long night, and a remembrance for comrades who shall no longer greet the dawn with us.)
Flins: (While the banquet's general form can be traced back to the Snezhnayan court of old, the passage of time has long since transformed its substance beyond recognition.)
Flins: (The only thing that has remained unchanged is the custom of offering the first pour of the feast to the dearly departed.)
Flins: (Life is short and fleeting, and only death is eternal.)

## Talk 2

Illuga: (When I was young, I used to think Piramida was never more alive than during the banquets at the great mead hall.)
Illuga: (I wasn't a Lightkeeper then — just helping out with everyone else in the kitchen. The real appeal of a banquet, to me, was getting food we didn't usually get to eat otherwise.)
Illuga: (That's why I could never understand back then why the older members at the table would always burst into tears after a few drinks.)
Illuga: (It wasn't until I became a Ratnik myself that I started weeping harder than anyone else...)
Illuga: (Our comrades' sacrifices paved the way for us, and only by eating our fill can we find the strength to carry on. This, I suppose, is the true meaning of these banquets.)
