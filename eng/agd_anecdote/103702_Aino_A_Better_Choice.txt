# Aino: A Better Choice...

Aino's troubles remain unresolved...

Returning the fawn to the wild might be the better choice, however...

## Talk 0

Aino: Little deerie! You gotta eat your breakfast, or you're never gonna grow up big and strong!
Ineffa: ...
Ineffa: Aino. There is still Lakkaberry residue hanging from the corner of its mouth.
Aino: Eh? What?
Ineffa: ...Hmm. Its recovery is progressing smoothly. In a few more days, we can ask Miss Lauma to send it back to its herd.
Aino: ...
Ineffa: Aino. I am sure you know that it deserves to go back home instead of staying with us.
Aino: But...
Ineffa: Did the Lady Moonchanter from the Frostmoon Scions not say as much when she came to treat the deer?
Ineffa: Its family might be worried about it. Once it has recovered, it would be best to send it home.
Aino: I know! But it's... it's not well yet...
Rimehorn Deer: ...
Aino: Does your leg still hurt? Do you... Do you wanna go home?
Ineffa: ...
Ineffa: Aino, didn't the Lady Moonchanter promise us that she would come and pick up the deer?
Ineffa: When the time comes, let's ask her to listen to what the deer has to say again.

## Talk 2

Traveler: *(Perhaps Lauma can help them...)*
Lauma: This little one has recovered well, thanks to your diligent care. When I return, I'll ask around in advance.
Lauma: If fortune smiles on me and I manage to locate its herd, then the next time I come, it will be to send this fawn home.
Aino: But... But...
Lauma: I understand. You cannot bear to see it leave. But this little Rimehorn Deer has its own family, and its father, mother, and friends must surely be worried sick.
Lauma: Once it is well, I believe it would be best to have it reunite with its own.
Aino: I know! But it's... it's not well yet...
Rimehorn Deer: ...
Aino: Does your leg still hurt? Do you... Do you wanna go home?
Lauma: It says, "It doesn't hurt anymore. But can you brush my fur again?"
Aino: Okay! Let me grab my comb, I just asked Ineffa to make a new one yesterday!
Aino: If you like it, you can take it back with you. To your family.
Aino: ...Wait, huh? Where'd I put it? Lemme go back home and dig around.
Lauma: Then I shall take my leave. Once again, I thank you for taking such good care of the little one. I came in a rush today, so next time, I shall prepare some gifts.
Ineffa: Do not force yourself to go out of your way for gifts, Miss Lauma. Aino loves the deer, and I do too. It is only natural for us to care for it.
Ineffa: Besides, Aino and I are happy to have the chance to talk to it.
Lauma: Is that so? Haha, well, the Rimehorn Deer is very fond of you too. I'm sure it considers both of you its true friends.
Lauma: I believe this is a friendship that will not fade, even if you are worlds apart.
