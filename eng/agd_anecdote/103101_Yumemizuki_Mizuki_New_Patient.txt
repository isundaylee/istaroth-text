# Yumemizuki Mizuki: New Patient

In Inazuma, the Aisa Bathhouse is welcoming a new client...

At her teachers' insistence, Layla arrives at the Aisa Bathhouse. While doing her best to relax, she also seeks the help of Yumemizuki Mizuki. However, Layla's condition seems to only be getting worse...
Perhaps Yumemizuki Mizuki has another solution...
