## Talk 1

Anselm: I was the captain of the 6th Company before I was sent out on expedition. Now, I assist the Grand Master with the expeditionary force's administrative affairs.
Anselm: ...I am also in charge of looking for the Grand Master when he goes missing.

## Talk 4

Anselm: But, as the Grand Master once said, the Knights' responsibilities are the same no matter where we are: aid the distressed and help the poor, punish the wicked and champion the good.

## Talk 5
