# Talk Group: Stepanyan (NpcGroup 7463)

## Talk 0

Stepanyan: I must continue tempering my will until I become a reliable company commander...
Stepanyan: Hm? Oh, it's you... Glad to meet you again.

## Talk 1

Traveler: Your will? Company commander?
Stepanyan: Yes. Recently, my superiors put me in charge of all company affairs, and my duties became much more complicated overnight.
Stepanyan: Handling all the tedious, drawn-out paperwork and keeping a unit running smoothly is fundamentally different from fighting on the front lines.
Stepanyan: Combat skills have become secondary. What matters more now is overall awareness, and the ability to schedule and coordinate tasks.
Stepanyan: Ahh, I still prefer fighting on the front lines...
Stepanyan: However, to become a proper war machine, I must be capable in every respect.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Do your best.
Stepanyan: Thank you for the encouragement. I will do my best every day.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Have we met before?
Stepanyan: Yes. I was once deployed near the Experimental Design Bureau in Nod-Krai, and I had the honor of exchanging a few blows with you. You defeated me without breaking a sweat, of course.
Stepanyan: Naturally, it's to be expected that you don't remember me.
Stepanyan: After all, at least a hundred Fatui soldiers who earned the Fireblade Shock Trooper qualification badge have fallen at your hands.
Stepanyan: You may simply think of me as an ordinary soldier, the kind you might find anywhere on the roadside.
