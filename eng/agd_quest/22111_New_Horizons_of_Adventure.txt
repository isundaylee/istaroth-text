# New Horizons of Adventure


## Talk 1

Unknown Role (None): Any new Commissions?

Unknown Role (None): Greetings, Adventurer. The tales of your deeds are now known to all in Mondstadt.

Unknown Role (None): Continue in this fashion and you are destined to become one of the great Adventurers of our age.

Unknown Role (None): Got anything for me to do?

Unknown Role (None): If you wish to become a great Adventurer, there comes a time when you must move beyond the places you know.

Unknown Role (None): Now you have amassed such a wealth of adventuring experience, perhaps it is time for you to explore somewhere new.

Unknown Role (None): But Mondstadt is all I know...

Unknown Role (None): That is because your adventures this far have all been limited to the Mondstadt area.

Unknown Role (None): But all that is about to change. Soon, you will be able to venture into the world beyond.

Unknown Role (None): Take this letter and deliver it to Liyue Katheryne.

Unknown Role (None): ...There's a Katheryne in Liyue too?

Unknown Role (None): Yes. I'm sure you will have no trouble finding her.

Unknown Role (None): Should I call you "Mondstadt Katheryne" in the future, then?

Unknown Role (None): If it helps advance your adventuring career, call me whatever you like.

Unknown Role (None): Anyway, once Liyue Katheryne has this letter, she will be your point of contact there for all your adventuring needs.

Unknown Role (None): Of course, whenever you are back in Mondstadt I will still be delighted to be of service.

Unknown Role (None): I would ask that you refrain from opening the letter en route. If anything were to fall out, it could cause complications.

Unknown Role (None): Adventurer! The time has come for you to take the next step towards the stars and the abyss. Ad astra abyssosque!

## Talk 2

Unknown Role (None): I have a letter for you.

Unknown Role (None): Ad astra abyssosque! Welcome to the Adventurers' Guild.

Unknown Role (None): I suspect this is for you...

Unknown Role (None): A letter? For me? Oh... So, you're an Adventurer from Mondstadt?

Unknown Role (None): What's this — a Dandelion? Why is there a Dandelion in the envelope?

Unknown Role (None): Adventurer, did you place a Dandelion in this envelope to try and prove you are from Mondstadt?

Unknown Role (None): There's really no need. There is more than enough information contained in this letter to establish your identity and background.

Unknown Role (None): It wasn't me.

Unknown Role (None): Not that it really matters, of course.

Unknown Role (None): I bet it was Katheryne...

Unknown Role (None): Katheryne? ...Oh, you must mean Mondstadt Katheryne.

Unknown Role (None): Why did she put a Dandelion in with the letter? Very odd... A Dandelion isn't much use to an Adventurer, now, is it?

Unknown Role (None): Not that it really matters, of course.

Unknown Role (None): This letter from the Mondstadt Adventurers' Guild says that you are a highly experienced Adventurer.

Unknown Role (None): If that's true, I'm sure you are ready to take on Commissions from the Liyue Adventurers' Guild.

Unknown Role (None): A whole new world of wild adventure awaits you. Ad astra abyssosque!
