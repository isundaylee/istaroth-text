# They Who Abandoned the Past

Katheryne: Haha... Very well then. Please wait a moment.

Traveler: Taking commissions again?

## Talk 3

Kaleva: Okay, okay, you win!

Jahoda: See? If you'd just played along earlier, you wouldn't have ended up in this mess. Now, take the Mora and go find yourself a decent doctor.

Kaleva: Hmph, is this your way of showing pity?

Jahoda: Save your breath and just take the Mora. If you'd rather chuck it in the water or hire thugs to jump me, that's your call.

Jahoda: One thing's for sure, though — going hungry and getting beaten up both suck. Don't make things worse for yourself, Kaleva.

Kaleva: ...

## Talk 4

Ihanti: *panting*...

Ihanti: Who's there? I don't have any Mora, nothing at all... If you're here for my life, just... go ahead and take it...

Jahoda: I—I've brought your stuff back, Ihanti.

Ihanti: ...Jahoda, is that you? I can hear your voice, but I can't see clearly anymore.

Jahoda: It's me, Ihanti, and I'm standing right here in front of you. Why didn't you come find me? You look... terrible.

Ihanti: Even a starving stray doesn't wander into a rich man's city... Not after all the beatings it's taken.

Jahoda: I'm not that kind of person, and Nef— I mean, Boss Nef isn't either.

Ihanti: All the more reason why I couldn't come find you. If folks got wind that you're the type to lend a hand, they'd come banging on your door day after day.

Jahoda: No one would be crazy enough to pull a stunt like that with the Boss.

Ihanti: But you're always out there, secretly doing good, only to be stabbed in the back by the very people you helped.

Jahoda: Hey. I've learned a lot from Boss Nef, y'know. No one's gonna get me good like that, ever again.

Ihanti: ...Ah, you just can't stop yourself from watching out for other people, can you, Jahoda?

Jahoda: ...That's my business, and no one else's.

Ihanti: Heh... Maybe that's why the boss thinks so highly of you.

Jahoda: Maybe it's the only reason I'm even still alive.

Ihanti: Hahaha—hurk *coughing*... You're right, Jahoda. You're right...

Jahoda: ...Anyway. If there's something I can do for you, Ihanti, now's the time to tell me.

Ihanti: Still the same as ever, huh... What if this was all just a big scam, or I was luring you into some kind of trap?

Jahoda: You wouldn't do that. Not after how long we've known each other...

Ihanti: And that's enough to win your trust?

Jahoda: Come on, look at the state you're in. How could you possibly threaten me?

Ihanti: Ha! Finally, we're getting somewhere. I guess saving a venomous snake that can barely open its mouth is pretty safe, all things considered... *cough*

Ihanti: Do me a favor, then — roll up the painting in that bag and slip it into my pocket. I haven't the strength to pick it up myself.

Ihanti: I painted this with my wife and daughter. It's the only thing I still care about in the whole wide world.

Ihanti: As far as I'm concerned, nothing else matters anymore.

Jahoda: Heh, the old you would be in stitches hearing you say that.

Ihanti: Hah... and what happened to those things I once treasured above all else? They could save neither my wife, nor my life...

Ihanti: But maybe that's just the fate of a thief. Everything we steal always gets stolen back, and we wind up empty-handed again eventually.

Ihanti: That pendant I put up for the commission was all I had left... Hah, though I guess I can't really call it "mine."

Jahoda: That was the first piece of jewelry you ever stole. You must have told me the story a dozen times — how you even pulled it off, and the look on the rich guy's face when he found out.

Ihanti: Yeah. Anyway, it's yours now.

Jahoda: Who did it belong to?

Ihanti: A Snezhnayan called Holomov. He used to live in Nod-Krai, but he's probably run home to Snezhnaya by now. Don't tell me you're thinking of—

Jahoda: I'm not a thief anymore, and I'm not about to start hanging onto stolen goods either. This pendant should go back to its rightful owner.

Ihanti: Haha... hahaha... You really are something else, Jahoda.

Ihanti: The boss was right... Someone like you could never... *cough* hack it as a thief... *cough*

Ihanti: *coughing*...

Jahoda: Enough, stop wasting your breath. I'll head back and find you a doctor.

Ihanti: Forget it. Just let me go the way I'm meant to.

Jahoda: But—

Ihanti: Jahoda, you know the boss's code.

Jahoda: "Grant rest to those who seek it."

Ihanti: You know what I'm talking about, so leave me in peace.

Jahoda: ...

Jahoda: Fine.

Ihanti: I'm glad it was you who came to help me, Jahoda.

Ihanti: Sorry I don't have any treasure to give you. But if you want, you can have this doll.

Jahoda: Wait, but... I thought I told you to—

Ihanti: Don't worry, I made this one myself. The ones you put together are long gone — I burned every last one of them, just like you told me.

Ihanti: Hah. Not too shabby, huh? Especially for someone who was already on the brink of going blind...

Ihanti: Take it, Jahoda. Consider it my final gift.

Jahoda: I'll come back to see you.

Ihanti: Go now. Farewell, Jahoda.

Ihanti: Thank you.
