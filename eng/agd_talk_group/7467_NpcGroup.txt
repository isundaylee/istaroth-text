# Talk Group: Iliana (NpcGroup 7467)

## Talk 0

Iliana: Please mind the distance between the platform and the train! And please be careful with your belongings... Do not drop anything into the gap...

## Talk 1

Traveler: What happens if something falls in?
Iliana: That would be a problem. Best case, we would have to retrieve it before the train departs...

## Talk 2

Traveler: Who are you?
Iliana: I'm a newly appointed Transport Bureau Safety Officer. My job is to ensure that every train can operate safely.
Iliana: But I've just taken up the post, so there is still a great deal for me to learn!

## Talk 3

Traveler: See you.
Iliana: Goodbye. And please be careful with your belongings!

## Talk 4

Traveler: What if I can't get it out?
Iliana: Then you'll have to hope that the item does not get damaged as the train leaves the station... If it is damaged, the passenger will have to bear the loss themselves.
Iliana: If it's not damaged, Transport Bureau staff will help retrieve it from beside the tracks.

## Talk 5

Traveler: You go down there to get it?
Iliana: Of course. We can't ask the passengers to climb down there themselves.
Iliana: Making sure passengers can safely get back their belongings is part of a safety officer's duty, after all.
