## Talk 1

Odette: A newspaper, please.
Katia: Certainly, Miss Odette. Here you are.
Katia: Today's paper has some pretty interesting stories. It should keep you entertained for a while.
Katia: And it's thick enough that if you decide to take a nap on the train, it'll do a decent job of keeping the light out, too.
Odette: Then I suppose it's sturdy enough for paper folding as well?
Katia: Of course. A paper airplane made from it would fly quite a long way.
Odette: Sounds perfect. Thank you, Katia. I'll make good use of it.
