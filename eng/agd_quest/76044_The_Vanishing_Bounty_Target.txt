## Talk 2

Unknown Role (None): Details on the targets are up on the board. I can fill you in if needed, but don't bother haggling over the bounties.

Unknown Role (None): About the bounty targets...

Unknown Role (None): There are four targets on the board, but we only have leads for three. The first one's wanted for fraud...

Unknown Role (None): ...Stop, stop, stop! Save me the spiel for the brand new treasure hunters — I could reel it off as well as you. You know who I'm here about.

Unknown Role (None): The Curatorium wants to get involved?

Unknown Role (None): That depends on how the Boss is feeling. Hmm, how did we arrive at you asking questions?

Unknown Role (None): Anyway, seems to me the real reason Crackjaw's not been found is the lack of leads. Look at the board. You haven't even said what he looks like.

Unknown Role (None): Well, if these treasure hunters set off without even knowing who they're looking for, well, they're hardly a match for someone like Hark. I don't want some novices wasting their time, much less throwing away their lives.

Unknown Role (None): Fair point. Apart from the scar on his chin, what else can you tell me about him? Preferably something no one else knows?

Unknown Role (None): Something no one else knows... You're looking for physical characteristics? Like, say, if he had some marks on his back?

Unknown Role (None): That's exactly what I'm looking for! But how do you know so much about him?

Unknown Role (None): I don't know squat. I never said he actually has any marks... Besides, even if he does, how are you going to see them?

Unknown Role (None): W—Well, yeah, sorta... *sigh* So, there's nothing you can tell me? We've always worked well together — can't you give me anything to work with?

Unknown Role (None): After Hark revealed his true nature here in Nasha Town, he fled to the north of Lempo Isle and picked up his old life of crime.

Unknown Role (None): That's old news. Everyone and their dog knows that.

Unknown Role (None): No one's reported seeing him recently. What if he went further north? I could maybe track him to Piramida, but what if he's made it all the way to Snezhnograd?

Unknown Role (None): (Nothing but ice and snow up there. I'm not sure if my arm would function in the cold...)

Unknown Role (None): He hasn't. He's still on Lempo Isle — I just don't know where. You can consider that "something no one else knows."

Unknown Role (None): Huh, how can you be so certain?

Unknown Role (None): Now that's a trade secret — you wanna know, you'll have to pay.

Unknown Role (None): Hah, stingy! Still, a hulk of a man with a scar on his face... Even on Lempo Isle, they're not hard to find. How'm I supposed to know which one's Hark?

Unknown Role (None): Well, seeing as how we "work together so well," I'll give you some free advice. Don't get too caught up in looking for a scar.

Unknown Role (None): ...Are you saying, he's found some way to hide the scar on his chin?

Unknown Role (None): I guess that makes sense. If everyone knows you as Crackjaw, walking around with no visible scar on your face would be a pretty good way to hide...

Unknown Role (None): In that case, I just need to find someone who's deliberately hiding their jawline, right? It's a lead, at least... Alright, if I find anything new, I'll come back and fill you in.

Unknown Role (None): I'll wait with baited breath...
