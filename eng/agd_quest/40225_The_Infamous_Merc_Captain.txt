# The Infamous Merc Captain

Jahoda reminds you that the person you're about to meet on the giant ship is an infamous merc captain who sails the high seas. You had best act with caution...


## Talk 1

Paimon: Wait, is this...?
Traveler: The Alcor?
Beidou: Actually, this is the second ship in our fleet. The Alcor's back home, patrolling Guyun Stone Forest.
Paimon: Beidou! The infamous merc captain who sails the high seas!
Beidou: Uh, yeah, haha. We've met, remember? What's with all the pomp and circumstance?
Paimon: It's a long story...
Beidou: Well, no sense standin' around. Take a seat. Tell me all about it. I'll have my men roll out the welcome mat.
Beidou: Ah, I see. So, you know the head honcho of the Curatorium, huh... Your network of connections is somethin' else, that's for sure.

Beidou: A while ago, Ningguang revamped the Guizhong Ballista. The beauty you see here is one of her new and improved models.
Beidou: It has insane firepower and comes complete with remote guidance for accurate striking even at long distances. I just know people are gonna be fighting to get their hands on it.

Beidou: Haha, don't worry. Compared to the model the Millelith's using, this one might as well be an antique.
Paimon: ...Nefer wasn't kidding when she said "no restrictions," huh...
Traveler: But, who's gonna buy something like this?
Yelan: Oh, I'm sure someone will bite. Buyers from all over Teyvat will be in attendance, after all.
Paimon: ...Yelan?

Paimon: No worries. You here for the expo too, Yelan?
Yelan: No, I'm just here for work. Captain Beidou was kind enough to lend me a ride. So, no need to register me with the expo.
Yelan: #Anyway, it's been a while, Traveler. If you get the chance, be sure to pass along my regards to the head of the Curatorium.
Traveler: What kind of work?
Yelan: The... sensitive kind.
Paimon: Sounds like our cue to stop asking questions, doesn't it...?
Traveler: One hundred percent.
Yelan: Well, I'll be off, then. Once I'm done with this, drinks are on me.
Beidou: About time! I've been itching to see Nod-Krai from the bottom of a bottle, haha.
Paimon: Alright, that's it for registration. Follow us, and we'll take you to the venue.
