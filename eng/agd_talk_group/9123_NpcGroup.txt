# Talk Group: Durin - Serenitea Pot (NpcGroup 9123)

## Talk 0

Durin: Is this your lair... Sorry, your home?
Durin: Such a huge world hidden inside such a tiny teapot. It's just like how Simulanka is contained within a book.

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Durin: Well, though this place is just as new and unusual...
Durin: It's different from when I first arrived in Sumeru or Mondstadt. I don't feel that mix of excitement and unease. I feel safe and comfortable here.
Traveler: That's good to hear. If you need anything, just let me know!
Durin: Thank you, but... but this place already has everything. I don't need anything more.
Durin: If anything, there's too much. Some things will be wasted on me.
Durin: For example, the soft bed that you prepared for me. Perhaps it could go to another guest who needs it?
Durin: And don't worry, I haven't used it.
Durin: I'll fold the bedding and put it back in the closet. A very capable maid taught me how to do it, and I've been looking for a chance to practice.
Traveler: Oh? Was the bed not comfortable?
Durin: The bed is fine. It's just that I'm more used to sleeping in confined spaces. I feel safer that way.
Durin: I've already found a few suitable places. The small sofa. And inside the wardrobe.
Durin: And since there aren't any cats here to come and scratch me because I've taken their favorite spot, every night is a good night's sleep.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Uh-oh... I actually do have cats.

> Traveler: Don't worry, my cats don't scare easily.
> Durin: They're more than welcome!
> Durin: Starting tonight, I'll be sure to leave a little space before I go to sleep.

Option 2:

> Durin: Will you draw one for me, like Albedo? W—Would that be too much trouble?
> Traveler: I'm sorry, I'm not that good at alchemy.
> Durin: I've never had a lair like this before... I'll treasure it.
> Durin: Please, allow me to help you!

Option 3:

> Durin: Oh... In that case, I'll let them have first pick and then I can find somewhere else. I wouldn't want to scare 'em...
> Traveler: They'll probably try to curl up beside you.
> Durin: They're more than welcome!
> Durin: Starting tonight, I'll be sure to leave a little space before I go to sleep.

Option 4:

> Traveler: I'll make you your own little "lair"!
> Durin: Will you draw one for me, like Albedo? W—Would that be too much trouble?
> Traveler: But I can make one myself, no sweat!
> Durin: I've never had a lair like this before... I'll treasure it.
> Durin: Please, allow me to help you!


## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to have a chat with me?
Durin: Of course I would! Opportunities such as this are rare.
Durin: Based on my observations, it seems adventurers have very little free time... And you're probably busier than most.
Traveler: And how did you come by these observations?
Durin: I like to stroll the streets of Mondstadt in my spare time, listening to people talk and watching people as they go about their day.
Durin: Passing by the Adventurers' Guild, I've noticed that adventurers always run up to the counter and stop for only a moment to talk before hurrying on their way once more.
Durin: Come to think of it, I suppose everyone has something they want or need to do.
Durin: Albedo and Sucrose often get so caught up in their research that they forget to eat and sleep.
Durin: As for Master Jean, well, need I say more? And then there are others who are busy practicing their swordwork, or singing, or even drinking and making merry...
Durin: Compared to them, I sometimes feel like only my form is human, and I'm still out of place...
Durin: Do you think I should find something to keep myself busy too?
Traveler: Do you have any life goals?
Durin: Well, there are many things I'm interested in, but I wouldn't go so far as to say they're my "life goals."
Durin: At least for me, I don't think you can call something your "life"-anything unless it's some kind of incredible achievement, right?
Durin: The heroes in most stories all have momentous, earth-shattering lifetime accomplishments.
Traveler: You shouldn't compare yourself to others. Just be yourself.
Durin: But... I want to grow a little faster too, to become someone everyone accepts.
Durin: After all, the fact that my own "story" has come this far is the result of a lot of people's hard work.
Durin: If the "ending" is anticlimactic, if it doesn't have some sort of positive impact on this world, then I'll have let everyone down...
Durin: I'm sure Mother would want to see me become someone remarkable, too...

Option 1:

> Traveler: I think she'd want to see a happy you.

Option 2:

> Traveler: As long as you're happy, you won't have let anyone down.

Durin: ...
Durin: I think I'm starting to understand what you mean. Thank you.
Durin: Uhm... I've still got a lot of difficult questions floating around my head. I still need to spend a lot of time thinking them over...
Durin: Being busy thinking... Maybe that's the best kind of busy for me.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Is there anything you'd like to do?
Durin: While taking a stroll nearby, I discovered a relatively empty space, with no major buildings nearby or things on the ground.
Durin: May I practice my fighting skills there?
Durin: I've recently gained fresh insights into harnessing the power within me, and I'd like to put in some more practice.
Traveler: Of course you can! Want me to join you?
Durin: Huh? You'd be willing to train with me?
Durin: But... be careful, alright? I don't often get the chance to train in a safe, isolated environment... I wanna try unleashing my full power.
Durin: Doing so might be dangerous... Are you sure you want to be there?
Traveler: What's that? Are you questioning my abilities?
Durin: Of course not!
Durin: It's just... I'm still not sure where my limits are, and if I lose control...
Durin: Fine, the truth is... I'm scared of making a fool of myself in front of you.
Durin: That's a normal thing to worry about, right? Humans seem to care a lot about how other people see them...

Option 1:

> Traveler: I once saw a great toy dragon crying and wailing...

Option 2:

> Traveler: He tried to hit me through his tears... and missed.

Durin: Alright, that's enough! You can stop there!
Durin: Just... take care of me, okay?
Durin: I must admit, whether in real battle or in training, I feel so much safer when you're around.
Durin: Before I came here, Hat Guy sent me a letter telling me that that would be the case.
Traveler: Really? What else did he say?
Durin: He also said, "If you don't want trouble, then stay out of other people's business."
Durin: But it's not like he follows his own advice there, so I don't plan to either.
Traveler: Haha, spot on!
Durin: Besides, I doubt you could avoid meddling even if you tried.
Traveler: Me? How are we on me now?
Durin: My friends in Mondstadt told me all about your adventures. It sounds like you like meddling in other people's affairs even more than Hat Guy.
Durin: Mother once wrote in the narration, "Sticking one's nose into other people's beeswax is exactly what a hero does."
Durin: The way I see it, you're this world's hero.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Good morning, Durin.
Durin: Good morning. I just went to check on that little bird... Or should I say, big bird?
Durin: She wakes up so early. What does she eat for breakfast?
Durin: Does she like to eat bread or...? What about grain?

## Talk 5

Traveler: Good night, Durin.
Durin: If you're having trouble sleeping, just wake me up.
Durin: Mother read me so many bedtime stories, I can tell them to you.

## Talk 9

Durin: This place reminds me of the study Miss Lisa set up for me when I was learning to read and write.
Durin: She said that human writing needs to be appreciated slowly and with a calm mind, so it's important to shut out all noise. When I was in that study, I felt like I could sit and read forever.
Durin: Ah, honestly, I'm still learning. Would it be alright with you if I come here whenever I want to read?

## Talk 10

Durin: This place reminds me of when I first arrived in Nod-Krai...
Durin: It is a mysterious place. Sometimes the rain flies up toward the sky, and the flowers and plants by the roadside shy away from me... Everything there is so different compared to Mondstadt.
Durin: But going to faraway places is like reading an unfamiliar story. Such wondrous surprises are exactly the kind of experience that only a new book can provide.
