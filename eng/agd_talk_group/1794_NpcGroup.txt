# Talk Group: Glenfidia (NpcGroup 1794)

## Talk 0

Glenfidia: Take your time to go through whatever you need... But check with your captain or the Liaison Officer first. Then list everything clearly, one by one.

## Talk 1

Traveler: You look like you're really busy?
Glenfidia: I'm doing alright. Thanks for asking!
Glenfidia: I'm currently managing Logistics Company's supply operations at Dornman Port on behalf of Captain Hertha.
Glenfidia: Although I've been learning the ropes from Captain Hertha for a while now, now that I'm handling things on my own, I'm thinking I could really use a second pair of hands... or three.
Glenfidia: Makes me wonder if Captain Hertha actually hit the ground running doing this back in the day...
Traveler: What kind of supplies do you deal with?
Glenfidia: Logistics Company primarily supplies combat gear, consumables, and general provisions...
Glenfidia: Food and drink are classified under the general provisions category. We've divided these into two main types: the traditional dry stores and the new canned rations that are sourced through a tender by Logistics Company.
Glenfidia: The dry stores come packed in wooden crates. They consist mainly of hardtack, jerky, and dried beans. If you don't want to chew on these all day long, you'd better throw them all into a pot and make a soup or stew outta them.
Glenfidia: The canned rations come in several different menus. Personally, I'd go with the meat stew, mashed potatoes, and Valberry jam combo.
Glenfidia: You've got to make sure you have enough potable water or your standard-issue water purification agents on hand though. Otherwise, you'd be drinking brine...

## Talk 2

Traveler: So, when I need something, how do I go about putting in a request?
Glenfidia: #Allow me to explain, Commander Traveler.
Glenfidia: First, the knight should identify the item or items they're in need of. Once that's done, they have to fill out a request form with their name, unit, company, and service number.
Glenfidia: Next, and this is the most important part, they have to take this form to their Company Captain, or at least the Liaison Officer, to get a sign-off on the request. Only when we see the sign-off do we then issue the requested supplies.
Glenfidia: Typically, individual requests do not come to me directly. Instead, they're consolidated at the unit level and then passed on to me.
Glenfidia: But every now and then, someone tries to cut corners and bypass the system. That's a real headache for me.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Maybe they had some sort of emergency?

Option 2:

> Traveler: Maybe they didn't want to wait too long?

Glenfidia: I get that. In the field, sticking strictly to established procedures is too rigid and can cause one to miss out on windows of opportunity.
Glenfidia: But now that we're back at Dornman Port, and things are a lot less frantic down here, we should really try to stick to the standard procedures.
