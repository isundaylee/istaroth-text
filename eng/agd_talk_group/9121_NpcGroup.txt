## Talk 0

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Aino: It's great! I got to talk to Tubby for the whole day!
Aino: Her full name is even harder to remember than the "Boom and Blast Kuuvahki Energy Cannon." Something to do with rain, I think? I don't get it at all...
Traveler: She's one of the adepti from Liyue.
Aino: Oh right, yeah... Another group that can't be explained in terms of science and mechanics...
Aino: That seems to be a recurring theme ever since leaving Nod-Krai...
Aino: Well, anyway, Tubby told me building things here is super easy. All you need is a blueprint.
Aino: So, I decided to get started on my next machine. I had the design schematic, tools, and components all ready to go, but then she said none of it was useable...
Traveler: Yeah, we use adeptal arts instead...
Aino: See? So, it's not easy at all! It's nothing like what I'm used to! I guess that means no tinkering for me...

Option 1:

> Traveler: It's okay. Just tell me what you're thinking.

Option 2:

> Traveler: And I'll see it done.

Aino: *sigh*... Okay!
Aino: I'm a little sad I won't get to build anything, but as a guest here, I guess it's better not to overstep...
Aino: But, uh... could we maybe get a krumkake-maker?
Aino: Then, at least, we'll have something to snack on together!

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to have a chat with me?
Aino: Sure! But, um... Could you help me find my precision machinery hex nut first?
Traveler: Where did you last see it?
Aino: Hehe... No idea.
Traveler: Then, when did you last use it?
Aino: Hm, when was it? Earlier today? Or maybe yesterday...? Sorry, when I get busy, I tend to lose track of time...
Traveler: Well, what do you usually do when you lose something?
Aino: Oh, that's easy. Whenever my room gets too messy and I can't find something, I just call out, "INEFFA!"
Aino: It's the fastest way to fix the situation! Even if it means walking right into a lecture...
Aino: Huh, but you don't have somebody like that to help you. How do you survive?

Option 1:

> Traveler: It's just a matter of staying organized...

Option 2:

> Traveler: After a while, it becomes a habit...

Aino: But, if I had to spend time on cleaning after pouring countless hours into making stuff, there'd be no time left over for snacks!

Option 1:

> Traveler: Okay, so snacks are your main concern...

Option 2:

> Traveler: I think I understand Ineffa a whole lot better now...

Aino: What!? No, no, no! We need to be a united front! Like... a snack alliance!
Aino: Ah, I get it. You've forgotten how good a delicious, crumbly cookie can be, haven't you? Well, you're in luck, 'cause I've got extra! Here, we can—
Aino: Hey! My hex nut! It was in my sleeve the whole time!

## Talk 3

Traveler: Anything you'd like to do?
Aino: Yeah! I wanna give our krumkake machine a new look!
Aino: It's just a bit plain right now. I think we can make it stand out a lot more!
Aino: What shape do you like? We could do... a robot? Ooh, or a little duck could be cute!
Aino: #Oh, but I've got the name down already! Traveler's Special Sizzling Krumkake Contraption!
Traveler: How would the robot work?
Aino: Well, it would have a robot arm to do the cooking, and an oven in its chest.
Aino: Then, once you hear the sizzling sound, the krumkakes burst from the oven in the chest and the robot's mouth shoots out sugar to dust them with!
Traveler: Okay, what about the duck?
Aino: Well, first you put the ingredients in the duck's back, and then you press the switch on its wings to activate it.
Aino: Then, you wait for the sizzling sound, and... Ta da! You have a whole batch of krumkakes that you can take from the duck's mouth, one after the other!

Option 1:

> Traveler: Huh, both sound like good options...
> Aino: If you can't decide, why don't we just make both?
> Aino: The construction should be pretty simple... And, if there's one thing I know, it's how to execute a vision!

Option 2:

> Traveler: Wow, fun!
> Aino: Hehe, you see the vision! I knew you would like it!
> Aino: Then, let's make both! A machine like this deserves to be in the hands of someone who understands its value!

Aino: One small hiccup, though... Taking this machine apart still won't give me the components I need. Looks like I gotta make a quick stop at home...
Aino: But, I'll be super quick! So, wait for me, okay? You promise?
