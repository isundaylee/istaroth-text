# Talk Group: Alyosha - Serenitea Pot (NpcGroup 9135)

## Talk 0

Alyosha: I had no idea Liyue's adeptal arts had such practical uses...
Alyosha: Are these landscapes really "carved"? Not even the master artisans in Svetloledovka possess such skill.

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Alyosha: There's no such thing as "getting used to a place" for me, I'm afraid.
Alyosha: Anyone who gets picky with the roof over their heads would never survive out in the snowfields.
Alyosha: But I'd say that out of all the shelters I've seen, this is definitely the best one. Thanks for having me.

Option 1:

Alyosha: I was working part-time at the theater troupe, so I used Odette's connections to score a gig as a night watchman.
Alyosha: Once everyone had left the theater, I'd take Tugarin to the most expensive VIP box and sleep soundly until dawn.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to chat?
Alyosha: Sure. Speaking of which, I've encountered some pretty strange things recently, and I'd love to get your opinion on them.
Alyosha: I really like Genius Invokation TCG, but The Rokot is so full of experts, I always lose more than I win.
Alyosha: But whenever those experts who beat me play against someone else, they usually lose terribly... So, am I somehow draining my opponents' luck?

Option 1:

> Traveler: Could it be that...

Option 2:

> Traveler: You gave them a false sense of confidence?

Alyosha: You mean... they beat me too easily and then underestimated their opponents?
Alyosha: How could that be... I carefully organize my deck and put a lot of thinking into my strategy for every match. Anyone who's beaten me must be a really formidable card player...
Alyosha: The only possible alternative is that... my skills were never that good to begin with. But surely that can't be true...?
Traveler: Alyosha, I...
Alyosha: It's alright. I've played enough games now to get a sense for what the answer might be...
Alyosha: But I just can't let it go. I'll play anyone, anywhere, whenever I get the chance.
Traveler: Why do you like Genius Invokation TCG so much, Alyosha?
Alyosha: ...Since joining the Rokot, it's practically been my only form of entertainment.
Alyosha: The more pragmatic a person is, the harder they work, spending all day on serious matters... People like that hardly have a life of their own.
Alyosha: So, when I discovered Genius Invokation TCG, something I could use to pass the time, I was hooked almost instantly.
Alyosha: But perhaps that's for the best. I believe Snezhnaya will change one day. The Rokot won't need to exist forever.
Alyosha: And when the day comes that I can truly relax, at least I'll have a hobby to pass the time.
Traveler: When that time comes, let's play a few rounds together.
Alyosha: Sure!

## Talk 3

Traveler: Anything you'd like to do?
Alyosha: I'd like to set up a workbench here too, so I can maintain my hunting rifle whenever I'm here.
Traveler: Do firearms really need to be maintained that often?
Alyosha: Of course.
Alyosha: Haven't you heard? They say that weapons have "emotions" too. Regular maintenance keeps them happy, which can be a matter of life and death in critical moments.

Option 1:

> Traveler: S—Seriously?

Option 2:

> Traveler: Weirdly, I actually think I've heard that saying before...

Alyosha: In that case, let me share my personal experience.
Alyosha: One time, I went to take out a band of highwaymen, but I was a beat too slow, and their lookout aimed his gun at me first.
Alyosha: But what do you know? His gun jammed, so I just shot and took him down.
Alyosha: While cleaning up the scene, I made a point to check his gun. The poor maintenance showed. Way too much dust in the chamber, and the bolt was a bit misaligned.
Alyosha: All in all, not what I'd call a happy gun.
Traveler: Surely there's a more rational explanation?
Alyosha: Yeah, Mitya told me the same thing, and then started throwing around technical terms like gunpowder combustion efficiency and mechanical reliability.
Alyosha: Way too complicated. I prefer the idea that weapons have "emotions."

## Talk 4

Alyosha: #Morning, Traveler. Come to think of it, I rarely sleep this soundly.
Alyosha: I suppose that's how you know we're not out in Snezhnaya's wildernesses anymore. No wild beasts prowling at night, no bandits striking under the cover of darkness...
Alyosha: After living like that for so long, I really don't know if this is good or bad.
