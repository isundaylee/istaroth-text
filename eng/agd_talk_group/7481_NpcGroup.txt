# Talk Group: Mamontov (NpcGroup 7481)

## Talk 0

Mamontov: Is it my turn yet? How much longer is this going to take? Am I going to miss the train?

## Talk 1

Traveler: Who are you?
Mamontov: Huh? You recognize me? Are you one of my readers? Wow, I didn't realize I was getting so famous...

## Talk 2

Traveler: Looks like you'll be waiting for a while more.
Mamontov: Yeah, looks like it. There was a young couple arguing here earlier — they were really going at it. The attendants didn't even lift a finger... Though I suppose there wasn't much they could do.
Mamontov: After that, the two of them just walked off in opposite directions. The child still here must be theirs. The poor thing must have a difficult home life.
Mamontov: Still, if the ticket clerk takes any longer with him, I'm going to be the one in trouble. Standing in line for long just to miss my train... That'd be so infuriating.

## Talk 3

Traveler: I'll leave you to it, then.
Mamontov: Your train's here already? Well, safe travels.

## Talk 4

Traveler: What reader?
Mamontov: A reader of "Vestnik," naturally... Ah. Except the paper has never once printed my picture, has it? Right... My mistake. I appear to have promoted myself a little too soon.
Mamontov: Truth be told, I write a little column for "Vestnik." My editor says it's been rather popular with readers lately, and, well, I got carried away, thinking fame had finally found me.
Mamontov: You know, I don't really get it. My serious train features, the ones I research and fact-check properly, keep getting rejected. Meanwhile, the half-true little train anecdotes I write off the cuff turn out to be the hit.
