Enghiene: *sigh* Whenever I think about the new recruits making the same mistakes Turenna made when she was young... It just makes me even more determined to make them practice more. Failure is the mother of success, after all!

Option 1:

> Traveler: What's up with Turenna and the newbies?
> Enghiene: *sigh* They spent far too long obsessing over equipment prep and individual combat skills.
> Enghiene: Experimenting with musket properties, honing marksmanship, looking for better uniforms, headgear, and vests... It's not wrong to pay attention to these, but once it comes at the cost of everything else...
> Enghiene: Every member of the Maison Gardiennage values teamwork. Aside from Captain Chevreuse, who's in a class all of her own, a lone musketeer cannot hope to handle complex tasks singlehandedly.
> Enghiene: For example, no matter how well a musketeer has honed their marksmanship, or how good their equipment, they can only attack in one direction when breaking into a room. That leaves them open to retaliation from every other angle...
> Enghiene: On the other hand, if you coordinate with your squad members, each one of you can cover a different area of a room during an assault. Even if your guns aren't the best, your rate of success is dramatically increased.
> Enghiene: Turenna enjoys providing long-distance support, but the logic is the same. A sniper who works alone will quickly be spotted by the enemy, and they'll fan out to avoid her line of sight.
> Enghiene: Worse, if they return suppressive fire, she'll be outnumbered and outgunned. And that's not even getting into how difficult it'd be to get out alive...
> Enghiene: Now that Turenna's gotten some real field experience, she'll get her priorities straight. It'll only be a matter of time until the rest of the new blood follows suit.
