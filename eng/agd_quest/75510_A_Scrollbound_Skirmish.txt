# A Scrollbound Skirmish

Little Pinecone: Although the Masked Warrior can't fly about like the Peacemaker, all she has to do to win is time her charges right!

Flammabomb Wood Figure: You blithering idiot! You can't just think of how fast she can run in a straight line — if she can't turn or hit anything, it won't make any difference!

Vigee: That's enough, children. We can have a civilized discussion, can't we?

Flammabomb Wood Figure: We're being civilized...! It's just, well, when we talk about great warriors, we get pumped up!

Little Pinecone: Don'cha worry none, Miss Vigee! We might be at war, but we're gonna fight with reason and evidence!

Flammabomb Wood Figure: ...Oho! I just thought of a new strategy. I challenge you again!

Little Pinecone: Rematch, rematch!

Traveler: ...Sure is lively here.

Traveler: What's going on?

Vigee: A new customer! Hello there!

Vigee: Judging by your attire, you're a traveler, aren't you? We've got that in common!

Vigee: Do you want to try out this interactive woven scroll I've created with the children?

Traveler: "Interactive woven scroll"?

Traveler: A scroll that moves on its own?

Vigee: Simply put, it's a game that combines Liyue's paper theater, Inazuma's comics, and Fontaine's film technology.

Vigee: It's inspired by our local heroes, and adds the concept of "crossover challenges" to create a novel story.

Vigee: Give it a try! Who knows, it might be a new experience for you.

Traveler: With pleasure!

Vigee: Wonderful! Have a great time!
