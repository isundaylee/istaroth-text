Traveler: I'll be paid for this gig, I presume?

Nikita: Rest assured. Though our strength may have waned, we Lightkeepers never fail to reward those who assist us.

Paimon: In other words, we've gotta go to the Pillar of Embla, find the legendary "Silver Lampwick," and bring it back here...

Paimon: Eh? But isn't the relic being kept by the fae? How are we supposed to find and retrieve it?

Nikita: According to the legend, following the kuuvahki as it guides you upward will lead you to her. She was once an ally of the Torchforger, so I believe she will understand if you explain the situation to her.

Nikita: But if you do obtain the "Silver Lampwick," please give it to Illuga.

Nikita: You've already seen him. He's the young leader of that force, and thus, and the one who needs this relic most right now...

Nikita: No, and in any case, we at HQ have never failed to send you check-in notices either. You have the kid to thank for that. He's the closest to you of us Lightkeepers. Always presses me about how you're doing.

Nikita: Yes. I need him, and I trust him greatly as well...
