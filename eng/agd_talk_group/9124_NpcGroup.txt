# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 9124

## Talk 0

Jahoda: Wow, this place is just like the one in that story Boss Nef told me.
Jahoda: A treasure, hidden in the middle of the desert, that can only be opened with a password...
Jahoda: Hehe, it's just a shame I came here alone. I'm no "master thief." I need a crew...

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Jahoda: I'm eating well, sleeping well, and living really well. What's not to like?
Traveler: Then why do you have dark circles under your eyes...
Jahoda: Eh? Ehhhh? Is it really that obvious?
Jahoda: Fine, you got me. I hardly slept a wink last night. I was tossing and turning for ages before I finally managed to nod off.
Jahoda: It's just... Everything here's way too nice! The bed's so soft, the blanket's so light, and it's all so peaceful...

Option 1:

> Traveler: T—Too nice?

Option 2:

> Traveler: That's why you couldn't sleep!?

Jahoda: Yeah... That's just how I am. The cozier the place, the less comfortable I get. Makes it impossible to relax and get some real rest.
Jahoda: Even if I try and force myself to sleep, the second I get close, my eyes always snap back open...
Jahoda: Then I either jump up and look around or slide my hand under the pillow to see if anything's there. It's instinctive.
Jahoda: Of course, most of the time, I'm alone and there's nothing under my pillow... So I can lie back down, bathed in a cold sweat, and start to savor the feeling of insomnia.
Jahoda: When I first arrived at the Curatorium, it was pretty serious. Eventually, I had to sneak out and lie on the lobby couch to get any sleep at all...
Jahoda: And when I woke up, there was Nef, petting Ashru and smiling at me.
Jahoda: Ugh, it almost scared me to death...
Traveler: Used to life here yet?
Jahoda: Uhh, not really... But Nef bought me a bunch of... Uh, I can't remember what they're called, but they're some kind of special mat from Inazuma...
Jahoda: She had them laid out on the floor for me, said I could try sleeping on top of 'em if I ever had trouble sleeping. And that that way, at least I wouldn't get cold.
Jahoda: Hehe, Nef's so good to me. That's why she's the nicest, loveliest, bestest boss in the whole world!
Traveler: I'll get you a new bed then.
Jahoda: I'm fine, really. I've already mastered the art of getting used to a new bed, just give me some time. No need to fuss.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to have a chat with me?
Jahoda: No prob. What did you want to talk about? Or should I say, what do you want me to teach you?
Jahoda: Lockpicking? Diving? Trap-setting? Mixing laxatives? Or maybe even... some sleight of hand? I can teach it all, you just say the word.

Option 1:

> Traveler: Where did you learn all that?

Option 2:

> Traveler: Was Nefer the one who taught you?

Jahoda: Oh, those are just a few little tricks I picked up along the way... Boss Nef had nothing to do with it. She's got her area of expertise, and I've got mine.
Jahoda: Sure, she's an absolute legend when it comes to the big stuff. Totally unstoppable, crazy resourceful and smart, always ten steps ahead...
Jahoda: But once she's dealt with the high-level stuff, someone's gotta tie off all the loose ends. That's my time to shine.
Jahoda: Hehe, after all, behind every great boss is a reliable assistant!
Jahoda: You don't get to work in the Curatorium of Secrets without a few special skills — even Ashru knows how to wave customers in and show them where to sit.
Jahoda: Still, I don't really know what Nef's thinking, having me as the only full-time employee in the whole place.
Jahoda: When I first started, I figured we were just understaffed.
Jahoda: Turns out the real heavy hitters in the Curatorium are all lurking in the shadows.
Jahoda: All Nef has to do is snap her fingers and poof! They appear, and then...

Option 1:

> Traveler: Do they all have cool-sounding code names?

Option 2:

> Traveler: Are there power rankings and leaderboards?

Jahoda: Yeah, totally! So you've read that light novel too?
Jahoda: Too bad the Curatorium doesn't have anything like that. Truth is, I really am the only employee.
Jahoda: *sigh* Boss Nef, O great Boss Nef! Why won't you hire more help? Every day, I'm running all over the place doing errands, and when there's too much, you still have to step in yourself...
Jahoda: You might not be tired, but I sure am...
Jahoda: #Hey, Traveler! You're pretty awesome, why don't you come work at the Curatorium? Nef would so...
Jahoda: Oh... no, that's not right...
Traveler: What's not right now?
Jahoda: Knowing Nef, she'd probably say:
Jahoda: #"Our long-established partnership with Traveler is working perfectly well as-is. Why disturb the status quo? Formalizing our collaboration would simply create superfluous admin."

Option 1:

> Traveler: You know her really well.

Option 2:

> Traveler: You sound exactly like her!

Jahoda: It's easy when you spend as much time with her as I do. Still... I—I really wish she'd employ someone else! C'mon, Boss Nef, pretty pleaaase?
