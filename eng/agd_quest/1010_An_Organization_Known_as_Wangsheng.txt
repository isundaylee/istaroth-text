## Talk 7

Unknown Role (None): Allow me to introduce Mr. Zhongli, consultant to an organization known as Wangsheng... and a trusted associate of the Fatui.

Unknown Role (None): Associate of the Fatui?

Unknown Role (None): Wangsheng?

Unknown Role (None): Indeed. Wangsheng's line of work can be... sensitive at times. Let's just say they understand when discretion is needed.

Unknown Role (None): And we, the Fatui, have always been glad to do business with friends who walk in the shadows.

Unknown Role (None): W—Walk in the shadows?

Unknown Role (None): It is an honor to meet you. I have heard tell of you from Mondstadt.

Unknown Role (None): Discretion... shadows... Ah! Is Wangsheng some kind of business involving... "dealing" with people?

Unknown Role (None): Indeed, it is as you have guessed.

Unknown Role (None): Ahh—!

Unknown Role (None): The Wangsheng Funeral Parlor organizes burials. We ensure that those who pass on do so in peace.

Unknown Role (None): Eh?

Unknown Role (None): Haha, did you think he was some sort of hired killer?

Unknown Role (None): The Fatui calls many such people friends, but the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor does not dabble in such business... well, ostensibly.

Unknown Role (None): Ostensibly...

Unknown Role (None): Well, they are still— Ah, I shouldn't say too much. In any case, I brought you to meet Mr. Zhongli because...

Unknown Role (None): Because I can bring you to see Rex Lapis' vessel.

Unknown Role (None): What!?

Unknown Role (None): Ha, don't be so surprised. Sure, the Geo Archon's body has been squirreled away by order of the Tianquan Ningguang... but first, let's hear what Mr. Zhongli has to say, shall we?

Unknown Role (None): Rex Lapis may be the Prime of Adepti, but he is ultimately an adeptus. Many adepti have left us over the millennia — this is the inexorable trend.

Unknown Role (None): The times have changed — you must have felt it too when you were at Jueyun Karst.

Unknown Role (None): That's true. The adepti are staying away from Liyue.

Unknown Role (None): The Geo Archon is also an adeptus?

Unknown Role (None): Archons go by many names. The God of Contracts, the God of Commerce, the Warrior God, Morax, Rex Lapis... is the idea that he also has the title of Adeptus so strange?

Unknown Role (None): As you have seen, the time of the adepti is ending, and the time of mankind is slowly dawning.

Unknown Role (None): In years past, Liyue's tradition was that a huge memorial service be held to mark the passing of every adeptus. But this time, the Qixing have made no attempt whatsoever to respect this tradition. It is sacrilege.

Unknown Role (None): Well, they do have a deicide to deal with...

Unknown Role (None): Yeah, the killer hasn't even been caught yet.

Unknown Role (None): Deicide or not, the concern of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor is this: When the ritual to receive this god is so kingly, it is all the more egregious for his final send-off to go unattended to.

Unknown Role (None): Traveler, Childe has told me a lot about you. Since you have had dealings with the Anemo Archon, could I ask you to help me prepare the Geo Archon's last rites?

Unknown Role (None): I'm going to have to think about it...

Unknown Role (None): #Well... if you really think about it, Traveler, we don't have any other choice...

Unknown Role (None): Indeed, no amount of consideration can change this outcome.

Unknown Role (None): I accept your invitation...

Unknown Role (None): A wise decision.

Unknown Role (None): Childe says too much.

Unknown Role (None): Oh? In our line of work, this means that you want to silence me... How interesting. Draw your sword. We shall—

Unknown Role (None): Just kidding. Let's continue, shall we?

Unknown Role (None): The Tianquan, Ningguang, has forbidden anyone from accessing Rex Lapis' vessel... Which of course you would need to access if you are to achieve your goal of meeting all of The Seven.

Unknown Role (None): Precisely. Only by participating in the Rite of Parting will you be able to see the form of Rex Lapis again.

Unknown Role (None): Looks like there's no other choice.

Unknown Role (None): If we are agreed, come with me. We will speak of the details as we walk.

Unknown Role (None): (Fade to black. Exit Zhongli.)

## Talk 8

Unknown Role (None): After having experienced the land of the absentee archon, Traveler, how does it feel to know that our archon and adepti are here all around you in Liyue?

Unknown Role (None): I prefer the atmosphere in Mondstadt.

Unknown Role (None): I see, so you're that sort of person. It's not a bad thing.

Unknown Role (None): But I suppose you have yet to experience the substance of Liyue's three thousand and seven hundred years of divinity.

Unknown Role (None): Liyue is the better place.

Unknown Role (None): Indeed. The weight of three thousand and seven hundred years' worth of history runs deepest in the true divinity of Liyue.

Unknown Role (None): Organizing the Rite of Parting should prove to be an enlightening part of your travels.

Unknown Role (None): Liyue is the most prosperous of the seven nations, defended by deities and ruled by the Qixing. As such, the diplomatic maneuverings of the Fatui have gained no purchase here.

Unknown Role (None): Ningguang of the Qixing has always been on her guard against the Fatui. That is in all likelihood why Childe wants to make use of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor's connections.

Unknown Role (None): Huh... what would Childe get out of us doing the Rite of Parting anyway?

Unknown Role (None): I neither know nor do I wish to know. As far as I am concerned, the Fatui are merely financial sponsors. I only wish for Liyue's traditions to endure.

Unknown Role (None): (Zhongli hands the player a bag of money)

Unknown Role (None): These are the advance funds that Childe has provided. If you use them up, you can go to him to apply for any subsequent funding.

Unknown Role (None): Wow...

Unknown Role (None): Well then, let us be off. The first step in our preparations shall be to obtain some prize *Noctilucous Jade* worthy of a deity.
