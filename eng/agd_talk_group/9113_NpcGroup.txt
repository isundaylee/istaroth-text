# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 9113

## Talk 0

Ifa: So... you have this whole pad all to yourself?
Ifa: That's impressive. You could raise... probably ten saurians here.

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Ifa: It's pretty great.
Ifa: There's fresh air, plenty of space, it's comfortable and cozy... Pets and saurians would feel right at home here.
Traveler: A true vet — always thinking about animals!
Ifa: Heh, everyone's got their own focus. I'm sure you could teach me a thing or two about adventuring, for instance.
Ifa: Or maybe... how to save the world, nation by nation?
Traveler: That sounds like a compliment...?
Ifa: Of course. You helped Natlan in its hour of need. People like you deserve all the praise they can get.
Ifa: From what I see, you have a lot of experience surviving in the wild, and you have a real zest for life in general.
Ifa: In other words, you're exactly the kind of person I wanna be friends with.
Traveler: Oh yeah? Why is that?
Ifa: Well, people with a zest for life are usually proactive about keeping their homes clean and tidy, and they don't tend to cause trouble everywhere they go.
Ifa: It makes you much less of a liability, bro.
Traveler: Sounds like... you see some of your other friends as liabilities?
Ifa: Huh? Was it that obvious? Oh well...
Traveler: Is Ororon one of those friends?
Ifa: Oh — no, he's fine. He leads a pretty benign life. For one thing, you can't hope to grow good crops if you're too out of control.
Ifa: I was actually talking about my saurian companions. I guess I also think of them as friends.
Ifa: A saurian in a bad mood is a lot more dangerous than a human. They'll rampage through your home like a storm if you're not careful.
Ifa: If that happens, all you can do in the moment is try to get them to calm down. Settling the score... that has to wait for another time.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to have a chat with me?
Ifa: Sure thing.
Ifa: You picked a good moment — I actually have some snacks on me. You want some?
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Are they for feeding to saurians?
    Option 2:
        Traveler: Are they your emergency rations?
Ifa: They're for whoever wants them. Me, you, or the saurians. It never hurts to carry some food with you.
Ifa: Try this — it's some jerky that I grilled myself. It's really good, even if I do say so myself.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Nice and crispy... it's delicious!
        Ifa: Yeah? I'm glad you like it. I also do other kinds with different seasonings. I'll bring a bigger variety next time we meet.
    Option 2:
        Traveler: Thank you, but... this is a little too chewy for me...
        Ifa: Ah, whoops... I forgot, your teeth aren't nearly as sharp as a saurian's...
        Ifa: Sorry about that. Let's try something else, then... I've also got some little cookies?
        Traveler: Mm, these are really crispy!
        Ifa: Heh, hopefully a little easier on your teeth.
Ifa: Uh, you can keep this bag of jerky, by the way. Next time you need to ingratiate yourself with a saurian, feed them some of this. It'll help.
Ifa: Dunno if you have any immediate need for it, but... Anyway, hold onto it for now.
Traveler: Thanks, Ifa! This is so thoughtful of you...
Ifa: Aw, I'm glad you think so.
Ifa: To tell you the truth, I was pretty all over the place as a kid. I only got my act together after becoming a vet.
Ifa: Tragedy is unavoidable in life. But everything passes in time.
Ifa: Whatever difficulties you're facing in your own life, I wish you the very best in getting through them.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Anything you'd like to do?
Ifa: I'd like to take a look around, see what you've done with this place. Maybe I'll pick up a thing or two about furnishing and decorating.
Ifa: I like how you've arranged some of your furnishings — it's fun and practical. I'm actually thinking about redecorating my own yard at the moment.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Be my guest! Feel free to look around and ask any questions.
        Ifa: Haha, you don't charge tuition fees, right?
    Option 2:
        Traveler: Well, I do like to think of this place as my magnum opus.
        Ifa: Even more reason for me to take notes.
Traveler: Maybe you can teach me something in return?
Ifa: Teach you something, huh? Hm... Lemme think...
Ifa: I think I'd be hard-pressed to teach a seasoned adventurer like you any new tricks.
Ifa: But if I had to pick something... Okay, try this hand gesture.
Traveler: Isn't that just a beckoning gesture?
Ifa: Yup. It might look simple, but the angle and the frequency of repetitions are key.
Ifa: Not too fast, not too slow, just calm and gentle, with a sense of rhythm. That way, they can feel your energy.
Ifa: If you get it right, you'll have cats, dogs, and saurians coming up to say hi to you. They see it as a sign of friendship.
Traveler: Today I learned!
Ifa: Useful little trick, huh?
Traveler: How about whistling? Does that work too?
Ifa: I wouldn't recommend it. Maybe for smaller animals, but with most saurians, whistling can anger them.
Ifa: Saurians can have big egos, and they're pretty intelligent creatures too. They won't appreciate being controlled or condescended to.
Ifa: Fundamentally, dealing with saurians is no different from dealing with people. Honesty and respect goes a long way.
