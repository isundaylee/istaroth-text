# Talk Dialog

Flins: #Hello there, Traveler. Fancy running into you here.

Flins: I was just out patrolling the area, cleaning up a spot of Wild Hunt unpleasantness, and now here you are.

Flins: This is quite a ways from town, and the area's teeming with monsters and the Wild Hunt. If you need me to escort you back, I'd be more than happy to oblige.

Traveler: There's no need for that...

Traveler: I have a few things to take care of first.

Flins: Ah, I see. In that case, I'd better not distract you.

Traveler: Don't worry, I'm quite tough.

Traveler: I don't encounter danger — danger encounters me!

Flins: Hehe, well said — and I have witnessed your skills already.

Flins: But it would be rude not to make the offer, having found you alone in the wilderness.

Flins: Anyway, I'd best be going. If you do find yourself in need of help, you know where to find me. I'll always be ready to answer when you come knocking on my door.
