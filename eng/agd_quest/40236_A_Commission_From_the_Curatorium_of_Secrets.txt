# A Commission From the Curatorium of Secrets


## Talk 3

Paimon: Hello? Anyone here?

Nefer: Ah, you got here even earlier than I expected. That should give us just enough time to go over some details before the opening ceremony.

Traveler: So, what did you need us to do?

Nefer: How about a cup of tea first? I'm sure it was a long trip.

Nefer: #Now tell me, Traveler and Paimon... Have you heard of the "Nod-Krai Trade Expo"?

Paimon: Uh, nope...

Nefer: A long time ago, back before Nasha Town even had that name, this place was essentially one giant black market.

Nefer: With no administrative oversight, trade that was prohibited elsewhere was fair game here. So, this place began to attract business from all over Teyvat.

Nefer: Over time, more and more people settled here, and what was once a black market became what is now Nasha Town.

Nefer: The spirit of free trade has been a local staple ever since. Hence, the "Nod-Krai Trade Expo" was born.

Paimon: So... is it like a business convention?

Nefer: In a sense. It's an annual tradition once the shipping routes open up. The expo offers a chance to trade without restrictions.

Nefer: In other words, it's the biggest "legal" black market in all of Teyvat.

Traveler: So, is that why you asked me to come?

Nefer: Yes. The Voynich Guild is organizing the event, and that comes with a lot of responsibilities.

Nefer: Normally, I would let Jahoda handle most of the work. But this year, it might prove too much for her to manage on her own.

Nefer: Something tells me we'll have a lot of big names in attendance this time. Working with VIP clients takes someone with the right experience and connections.

Nefer: Hence why I think Jahoda might need a helping hand.

Paimon: #Oh, so that's where Traveler comes in!

Nefer: Exactly. I was hoping you could help her with participant coordination.

Traveler: It's nice of you to look out for Jahoda.

Nefer: I don't know if I'd put it quite like that. I'm just interested in playing to people's strengths.

Nefer: In addition, I think you'll find the compensation to be quite to your satisfaction. So, are you in?

Traveler: Yeah, should be a piece of cake.

Nefer: Confident, I like it. This is exactly why I chose everyone's favorite globe-trotting hero for this job.

Traveler: Sure. But what's the catch?

Nefer: Hah, good instincts.

Nefer: I do have one special request, as the one offering this commission.

Nefer: In principle, the Nod-Krai Trade Expo is a platform for commerce with no restrictions. That said, the Guild does have one line that is not to be crossed.

Nefer: I'll need you to help make sure all participants are clear on this unspoken rule. As long as everyone complies, the expo should go off without a hitch.

Traveler: So what's the unspoken rule?

Nefer: No violence at the expo. The Guild has pledged to ensure the safety of all sellers and buyers in attendance.

Paimon: Violence? Whoa, that wasn't even on Paimon's radar... Just because it's legal doesn't change the fact that this is still a black market, huh...

Nefer: If that line is crossed, the event will descend into chaos.

Traveler: Understood.

Nefer: Of course, mediating such situations might prove complicated. So, if you ever want my judgment on the matter, just come find me.

Nefer: The expo is being held on the shoreline to the southeast of Nasha Town. Jahoda is working on setting up the booths as we speak. She'll be able to provide details on the participants as well.

Nefer: Alright, I suppose I'll leave you to it, then.

Paimon: Yep, we've got this!
