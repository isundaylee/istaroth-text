# Challenger: Series II

## Hydro Hunter

Defeat every type of Hydro Mimic that an Oceanid can summon.

## Dip, Duck, Dive, Dodge, Defeat

Defeat an Oceanid without being hit by water bombs left behind by certain Hydro Mimics.

## ...Well, That Was Strange

Defeat the Unusual Hilichurl 1 time.

## ...Well, That Was Strange

Defeat the Unusual Hilichurl 20 times.

## ...Well, That Was Strange

Defeat the Unusual Hilichurl 50 times.

## Extreme Gardening

Paralyze a Cryo Regisvine by attacking its corolla.

## Gardener Extraordinaire

Paralyze a Pyro Regisvine by attacking its corolla.

## Geronimo!

Hit an opponent with a Plunging Attack after plunging for more than 5 seconds.

## Vicious Circle

Unleash 5 Elemental Bursts within 15 seconds.

## Shield Me From the World

Have a single character be protected by 3 different types of shield at once.
