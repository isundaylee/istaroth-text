# Talk Group: Antioch (NpcGroup 7445)

## Talk 0

Antioch: Back in the day, we never had all these strange faces all over the place. That was the real golden age for Snezhnograd...

## Talk 1

Traveler: Golden age?
Antioch: That's right, those were the days when everything was the color of honey... The young 'uns these days will never know a thing about it, and they'll never get it either.
Antioch: Back in those days, folks thought of nothing but the future of our nation. Nobody who came to Snezhnograd was here for their own personal gain.
Antioch: But just look at the square now, young 'un. Look at all these young pups who don't know the first thing about dedication and sacrifice. They're either here for the Mora or trying to climb some kind of ladder.
Antioch: They pour into Snezhnograd on the train from all over the place, just to squeeze a profit out of the locals and foreign tourists... Which one of 'em actually even gives a hoot about the glory of Snezhnaya?
