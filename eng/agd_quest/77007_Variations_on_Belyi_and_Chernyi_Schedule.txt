## Talk 1

(Head to the designated location)

Odette: Whew...
Paimon: That's the last of the monsters around here, right?
Paimon: You okay, Odette? You fought extra hard today...
Odette: I'm fine. It's just...
Odette: ...
Traveler: You don't have to talk about it if you don't want to.
Odette: No, I'm just trying to find the right words.
Odette: This is where I used to practice dancing when I was little.
Paimon: Out in the wild?
Odette: Well, most of the time, it was in a studio full of mirrors.
Odette: But, every so often, I would practice outside. I love dancing in nature. It reminds me of the true essence of the art form — celebrating the beauty in this world.
Paimon: Ohh! Is that why you took on this mission? Because you wanted to keep this spot peaceful?
Odette: That's part of the reason.
Traveler: And the other part?
Odette: It's... because of the Black Snow Swan.
Paimon: The ballet?
Odette: Yes. I practiced it a lot when I was little, but I didn't get to perform it on stage in the end. That was a big disappointment for a long time.
Paimon: It's about a snow swan whose feathers are a different color than everyone else, right?
Odette: That's right. I felt so sorry for the black snow swan back then. It suffered such an unfair fate just because of how it was born.
Odette: It deserved to live a normal life alongside its companions, but it couldn't...
Odette: Actually, when I was little, the black snow swan was my least favorite role in the ballet. I didn't want to be anything like her...
Odette: But fate always has a way of playing tricks on us. The more I wanted to be a dazzling, white snow swan onstage, the closer my real life came to the black swan.
Traveler: (She must be talking about her life as a member of both the Fatui and the Rokot...)
Odette: I have a much greater appreciation for the character now.
Odette: But thinking about what I felt as a child helps me reflect on how far I've come. I hoped it might help me better interpret the character in the ballet itself.
Paimon: So that's why you thought of coming here...
Traveler: So, did it help?
Odette: Hm... do you know how the Black Snow Swan's story ends?
Traveler: No, I don't...
Odette: She stops trying to fit in with the flock and lives on proudly in solitude.
Odette: After that, she flies higher than any other snow swan. In the end, the pure white clouds embrace her, and she goes to a place no white-feathered snow swan could ever reach.
Paimon: That's a beautiful ending.
Odette: Yes. Solitary, yet beautiful.
Paimon: Well, after hearing all that, Paimon thinks you can pull it off for sure! We'll be cheering for you!
Odette: Thank you...
Odette: But before that, I should focus on the work I'm doing for the Fatui.
