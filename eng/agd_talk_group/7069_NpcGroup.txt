# Talk Group: Gorchakova (NpcGroup 7069)

## Talk 0

Gorchakova: Wow, this place sure is different from Snezhnaya!

## Talk 1

Traveler: Really? How so?
Gorchakova: This place has a little more... how should I put it, natural vitality? It's not covered by snow, ice, and permafrost, and there's plenty of fruit growing here that looks just like Midsommar Berries. These things are rare in Snezhnaya.
Gorchakova: But, and this may sound a little strange, I still prefer Snezhnograd in the midst of a snowstorm! That's the only time you can truly feel the warmth of "Kresnik's Torch"!
Gorchakova: Of course, I'm probably biased — it's only natural to have a preference for your hometown.

## Talk 2

Traveler: All the more reason to explore.
Gorchakova: Of course! I just saw some traveling merchants from Natlan — a right lively bunch, to be sure! I'll have to go over and talk to them, and who knows, maybe we can do business?
