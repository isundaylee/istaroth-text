# Challenger: Series VIII

## The Distance of the Moon

Complete 11 Plunging Attacks in a single Gravity Reduction Field, hitting an Experimental Field Generator.

## Gravity Front

Defeat an Experimental Field Generator without being hit by a "Gravity Ripple."

## Shackle Me, That I Might Willingly Be Destroyed

During the climax of "Icewind Suite: Dirge of Coppelia," use Coppelius's attack to make all the Dirge's whirlwinds undergo Elemental Conversion.

## Crabs for the Crab Throne

Defeat the Emperor of Fire and Iron while weakened.

## Collezione di Sabbia

Break the Xenomare Pearl directly without destroying the Resonant Coral Orb while the Millennial Pearl Seahorse is guiding Hoarthunder.

## Fulgura Frango

Defeat the Millennial Pearl Seahorse while it is attempting to re-construct a Xenomare Pearl.
