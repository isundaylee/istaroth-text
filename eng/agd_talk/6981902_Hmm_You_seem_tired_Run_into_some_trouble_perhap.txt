# Talk Dialog

Kaedehara Kazuha: Hmm? You seem tired. Run into some trouble, perhaps?
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Nothing but the usual minor problems one runs into while on the road.
        Kaedehara Kazuha: Such strength... Still, it is best not to push yourself too hard.
        Kaedehara Kazuha: Take a short break today. There are leaves nearby — I'd be happy to play you a tune.
        Kaedehara Kazuha: May its melody accompany you — from where the wind blows, and into the distant future.
    Option 2:
        Traveler: My recent travels have been rather tiring...
        Kaedehara Kazuha: I understand. Though we may think ourselves accustomed to a life of wandering, there will always be times when we cannot help but sigh.
        Kaedehara Kazuha: Of course, the day may come when we miss our days as wayfarers, even should we find a place to settle down. No life has ever been lived in perfect bliss.
        Traveler: Do you also get tired, Kazuha?
        Kaedehara Kazuha: Of course. Not all the paths I've tread were smooth going.
        Kaedehara Kazuha: When a bad mood strikes, troubles soon follow in their twos and threes, with nostalgia not far behind... A wanderer yearning for home is like a bird pining for the old forest.
        Kaedehara Kazuha: But once these troubles pass, the journey continues once more. Having experienced this again and again, I've come to realize something—
        Kaedehara Kazuha: The world is vast, and setbacks inevitable, but a clear heart fears not inclement weather.
