# Duelist: Series III

## "The Peak"

Defeat Local Legend: The Peak.

## "The Peak"

Defeat The Peak in its weakened state.

## "The Peak"

During the course of completing a single challenge, do not let The Peak unleash its Saurian Stomp.

## "Battlegoat" and "Ironclaw"

Defeat Local Legends: Battlegoat and Ironclaw.

## "Battlegoat" and "Ironclaw"

During the course of completing a single challenge, have Battlegoat's charge collide with Ironclaw's guard stance 2 times.

## "Battlegoat" and "Ironclaw"

Defeat Battlegoat and Ironclaw at the same time by having Battlegoat's charge collide with Ironclaw's guard stance.

## "Infinitesimal"

Defeat Local Legend: Infinitesimal.

## "Infinitesimal"

During the course of completing a single challenge, avoid getting hit by Infinitesimal's Earthshaker Strike.

## "Infinitesimal"

Defeat Infinitesimal when it first enters a weakened state.

## "Reji"

Defeat Local Legend: Reji.

## "Reji"

During the course of completing a single challenge, have your characters be protected by a shield when being attacked by Reji, and the shield must not be destroyed.

## "Reji"

During the course of completing a single challenge, deflect 4 bullets in a row within a short period of time.

## "Tupu's Lushness"

Defeat Local Legend: Tupu's Lushness.

## "Tupu's Lushness"

During the course of completing a single challenge, no characters were downed.

## "Tupu's Lushness"

During the course of completing a single challenge, do not let the Electro Ward created by Tupu's Lushness reach more than half of its maximum value.
