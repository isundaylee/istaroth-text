# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 9119

## Talk 0

Lauma: What a pleasant place this is, and there's such a nice scent in the breeze... Living here must be wonderful.
Lauma: Thank you, truly, for inviting me to this place.

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Lauma: I like it very much.
Lauma: The air is clean, the ventilation is good, and the number of plants around here is just right.
Lauma: I'm sure the animals would love it here. They would say, "We are certain only a kind soul could create a cozy place like this."
Traveler: The forest in your home gives me the same sort of vibe.
Lauma: Does it now? I'm happy to hear that.
Lauma: I know that in the eyes of many ordinary fellows, the Frostmoon Scions are rather unique... We care more about scents, temperature, and environment than most.
Lauma: Indeed, we are closer to nature and animals than others, but to fashion it another way, this also means that our senses are sharper.
Lauma: Hence, our decision to accept you as a friend is also a sort of... proof, shall we say.
Lauma: To seek advantage and avoid harm is the nature of all living things. That we gravitate to you is proof that you are someone who makes others feel very safe.
Lauma: And that's something very important. Please never forget it.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to have a chat with me?
Lauma: Of course. Would you like me to think of a topic?
Lauma: Hmm... However, I am not exactly the sort of person that can carry a conversation... I believe that much about myself.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Hardly! You're very good at talking to others, Lauma.
        Lauma: I'm sorry to disappoint you... It is because of my duties that I must speak those words of comfort and encouragement.
        Lauma: Words carry a wondrous power, so people tend to say whatever is appropriate to a conversation. I am no different.
    Option 2:
        Traveler: It's alright. I'm not much of a talker either.
        Lauma: Oh, hardly... I've seen the way you talk to others. You're doing just fine.
        Lauma: Although Paimon often speaks in your stead, I find your words often fascinating. I've been paying special attention to them, you know.
Lauma: Now that we're on the subject, I suppose I would like to become a more proficient communicator...
Traveler: You can always learn from your friends.
Lauma: ...Ah, yes. Ms. Nefer, Mr. Varka, and Mr. Flins come to mind.
Lauma: They are all clever and wise individuals. Having come from different backgrounds, their ways of speech also differ.
Lauma: Meeting them has shown me the extent of my abilities. I still have much to learn.
Traveler: But maybe there's a lot they can learn from you in return.
Lauma: Hmm... You're right, I suppose there might be.
Lauma: At least in terms of communicating with animals, I know I definitely have the upper hand.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Anything you'd like to do?
Lauma: There are, but perhaps not right now.
Lauma: There are some beautiful forests in Nod-Krai that rarely see visitors, so I like to go to such places and rest when I am free.
Lauma: Perhaps it's because of the extraordinary blood that flows through my veins... I am always concerned with matters such as the cleanliness of the water and the gentleness of the breeze.
Lauma: And that is also why I have less time to truly relax compared to others.
Lauma: Only in places such as those can I truly forget my frustrations. Soaking in the water and daydreaming... Why, sometimes I lose focus, and slip into my six-limbed form...
Traveler: Six limbs? That would be a rare sight indeed.
Lauma: Hehe, indeed. Life is easier with more limbs to work with...
Lauma: Ah, do forgive me. We seem to have talked ourselves onto a rather unorthodox tangent.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: It's alright. Unorthodox is interesting!
        Lauma: Is that so? What a relief.
        Lauma: It truly pays to befriend those who have seen much of the world.
    Option 2:
        Traveler: Does it feel weird to have six limbs?
        Lauma: No, I'm used to it by now. Besides, sprinting with four legs simply means an advantage in speed. Once you experience it, it's hard to go back to anything else.
        Traveler: No wonder you sometimes just forge ahead without looking back...
        Lauma: Yes, it is as you say. I have made quite the habit out of sprinting around.
