# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 9120

## Talk 0

Flins: Your abode is both comfortable and homely, and the decor within and without is most rich in style. It exudes the fine tastes of its host.
Flins: #For inviting me here, you have my sincerest gratitude, Traveler.

## Talk 1

Traveler: How do you like it here?
Flins: Your concern is most touching. Fret not, for I am enjoying myself.
Flins: Every conceivable need is met here — a testament to the host's meticulous thoughtfulness.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: You're too kind.
        Flins: Hardly. I came here empty-handed, bearing not even a gift.
        Flins: But you treated me to dishes you prepared, and so I offered you sincere compliments in return. It's only polite.
    Option 2:
        Traveler: Let me know if anything is lacking.
        Flins: Your home is rich in storage, fitting for an adventurer as well-traveled as yourself. I have no doubt that these nooks hold treasures aplenty.
        Flins: If you have a moment, I would love for us to view the spoils of your journey together.
Flins: But aside from this desire, born out of mere curiosity, all my other material needs are met.
Traveler: You're very welcome!
Flins: You are much too kind. If you truly wished for me to nitpick, then I suppose I might offer some critical suggestions regarding the allocation of accommodations here.
Flins: If convenient, perhaps I could request for a room that is quieter, and in a more secluded spot?
Flins: Accustomed as I am to living alone in a cemetery for many years, a gloomy environment is in fact more comfortable for me.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Would you like to have a chat with me?
Flins: I certainly would. What is on your mind today?
Traveler: I can't remember what I wanted to talk about...
Flins: That is well. We can discuss any matter you please. Death, freedom, meaning, and solitude... or what we shall have to eat later.
Flins: And yet — perhaps I understand this state of yours.
Flins: When people deliberately seek conversation with others, it's... it is easy for their efforts to be in vain.
Flins: If we wish to speak, we should not hesitate. Whatever thoughts linger in your heart, say them, and do so decisively.
Flins: No matter how trivial these thoughts may seem, what matters is that they are said and heard.
Flins: For instance, if you see an insect on your way out today, or if you plan to go fishing later.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: I thought of something. Why do you keep your hair long, Flins?
        Flins: You mean, why do I not cut it short and neat, like my colleagues? Why this look, of all choices?
        Flins: ...I see, I did not expect you to take an interest in my appearance. Hmm, yes, knowing this has helped me understand you better.
        Flins: Have you heard the saying? "One's appearance is a reflection of their innermost thoughts and experiences."
        Flins: There was a time when my long, smooth hair was a symbol of my status. It was a habit for me, back then...
        Flins: I suppose this will suffice as an answer to your question.
    Option 2:
        Traveler: I thought of something. Do you know anything about Snezhnograd, Flins?
        Flins: I recall that you intend to visit Snezhnograd in the future. It is indeed necessary to gather relevant information before your departure.
        Flins: ...Regrettably, my memories of it are hazy, but what remains tells me that it was a beautiful place.
        Flins: The brilliant night sky, the never-ending blizzard... It seems that nowadays, it's become rather industrialized.
        Flins: Some fae still live there. Perhaps they have gotten used to the biting cold, and are no longer able to survive in a warm place.
        Flins: Hmm... I wonder what Snezhnograd looks like now...

## Talk 3

Traveler: Anything you'd like to do?
Flins: I... I have been sleepless of late.
Traveler: Is the bedding here not to your tastes?
Flins: Oh, no, please don't misunderstand. The beds here are absolutely delightful, far more than I require.
Flins: Besides, for someone of my particular habitation arrangements, I have never lost sleep on account of the outside environment.
Traveler: Then...?
Flins: Lately, I've been longing for that time, long ago, when I slept alone in absolute darkness.
Flins: It was a slumber without dreams, without consciousness, without joy, and without pain.
Flins: It was a strange sensation. An absolute state of peace and emptiness.
Flins: Perhaps it is difficult for most people to understand, but I was immersed in that slumber for a long time indeed.
Flins: Compared to it, the dreamful sleep that normal humans experience every night feels almost... too noisy.
Traveler: I don't suppose you want to...
Flins: ...To extinguish myself again? Hehehe, of course not.
Flins: Thinking about my past experiences is merely a way for me to review my current judgments and position.
Flins: To yearn for peace, and to forsake it for a new purpose — these paths do not conflict.
Flins: I hope my prattling on and on does not bore you.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: I'm not bored at all.
        Flins: Then all is well. The fact I can share these trivial thoughts with you is proof of our friendship.
    Option 2:
        Traveler: I don't really understand...
        Flins: That is fine. There is always a difference between two individuals, and to fully bridge that gap of understanding is a nigh-insurmountable task.
        Flins: But it is precisely because we think differently that our conversations are meaningful.

## Talk 4

Flins: Good morning to you too. I wish you a smooth journey ahead, and the fulfillment of all that you may desire.
