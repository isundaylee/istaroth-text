# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 1844

## Talk 0

Istoria: #What is it, Traveler?

## Talk 1

Traveler: Tell me about this place.

Istoria: This is the Temple of Space.

Istoria: The gods had meant for it to be an exhibition hall for the conservation of fallen civilizations...

Istoria: But the sisters of that one great god... had other ideas. And so they put whatever they thought interesting in here, willy-nilly.

Istoria: This is why the collection here can appear rather eclectic.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Tell me about your era.
Istoria: My era?
Istoria: You mean the "mortal paradise" that I do not even dare imagine in my post-hoc recollections...
Istoria: From Hyperborea in the north to Lang-Gan in the south, Delphi, and this place, what used to be Arcadia...
Istoria: What do you want to know? What we ate, what we thought, or what we made?
Istoria: It's not that I do not wish to tell you everything, one by one. It's just that once you hear me out, you'll probably think us merely children of some intellect.
Istoria: We ate the same things you do. We were able to think about matters far removed from reality only because the priests and angels always gave us the answers to the practical issues.
Istoria: We created playthings, mere trifles, that amused us, because nothing we made would match the exalted means the heavens had already bestowed upon us.
Istoria: It's like we were children doing something that pleased our parents.
Istoria: A fellow named Bosch drew a piece called "The Mortal Paradise" based on what I told him. He captured the feeling exactly — the very feeling.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Tell me about the secrets that have been buried by time.
Istoria: Where do I begin? You have to be more specific — that'd also help me recall.
Istoria: Or perhaps there's a subject you have questions or are curious about.
Istoria: Like the relationship between mass and energy, or the definition of certain cosmic laws in bounded sectors...

## Talk 4

Traveler: I'll see you around, Istoria.

Istoria: Yes, see you around. And I mean that literally of course, since I'll always be here.
