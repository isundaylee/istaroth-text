# Talk Group: Golitsyn (NpcGroup 7766)

## Talk 0

Golitsyn: I suggest you proceed with greater caution, young friend. The harsh winter shows no mercy to the naive.

## Talk 1

Traveler: You are...?
Golitsyn: Andrei Dmitrievich Golitsyn. Second-rank noble and Active Privy Councillor of the Snezhnograd Veche.
Golitsyn: As you can see, I am no different from the mayor, only an old man doing his duty in Her Majesty's name.
Golitsyn: For five hundred years, generation after generation, the Golitsyn family has served Snezhnaya. Our loyalty has always been to Snezhnaya, and to Snezhnaya alone. I trust you understand.

## Talk 2

Traveler: I shall take my leave.
Golitsyn: May Her Majesty's glory endure. Farewell.

## Talk 3

Traveler: About the mayor...
Golitsyn: You mean Lord Rooster. The Golitsyn family is honored to stand with him in the Veche.
Golitsyn: After all, like him, our house owes its standing today to the grace of Lady Ioanna Ivanovna.
Golitsyn: His project concerns the future of Snezhnaya. Yet there are always impatient people who believe their reckless ideals are reason enough to challenge order.
Golitsyn: But order is life. To throw aside steady progress in the name of change is no different from taking off your coat and walking into the blizzard ahead.

## Talk 4

Traveler: About the mayor...
Golitsyn: You mean Lord Rooster. The Golitsyn family is honored to stand with him in the Veche.
Golitsyn: After all, like him, our house owes its standing today to the grace of Lady Ioanna Ivanovna.
Golitsyn: Regrettably, there are always impatient people who believe their reckless ideals are reason enough to challenge the order the mayor has built.
Golitsyn: But order is life. To throw aside steady progress in the name of change is no different from taking off your coat and walking into the blizzard ahead.

## Talk 5

Traveler: About the project...
Golitsyn: You wish to know about the project. Very good.
Golitsyn: To create a miracle capable of shaking the very laws of the world, one must endure suffering beyond what ordinary people can bear.
Golitsyn: Fae and humans alike, in this long winter, are fuel for Her Majesty's flame. That is their place, and the only place they can have.
Golitsyn: Regrettably, some naive commoners — and even certain nobles — still fail to understand the worth of such a noble sacrifice.
Golitsyn: There are always those who believe they can flee the blizzard, or wait for it to pass, and then live out their days in peace and comfort.
Golitsyn: Laugh if you must, but even my own daughter, Dunya — my poor Dunyasha Andreyevna Golitsyna...
Golitsyn: She has turned away from her family duties and gone off to a frontier town like Nasha Town. There, she is trying to live like the common folk, running some peculiar little general store.
Golitsyn: I will not stop her. Once she has spent what little pocket money she has, she will realize where she belongs and return to the family hearth.
Golitsyn: Until Project Stuzha is complete, I shall simply indulge this little excursion of hers as an extended holiday in the countryside.

## Talk 6

Traveler: About the project...
Golitsyn: You have likely heard by now. By Her Majesty's boundless mercy, Project Stuzha has been brought to a halt.
Golitsyn: You should know, when the project was first proposed, we all had our reservations — every one of us, without exception.
Golitsyn: After all, if it had continued, how would those already living in poverty have managed? Those poor, unfortunate souls in Zolnyy... How were they supposed to carry on?
Golitsyn: Fortunately, Her Majesty's wisdom prevailed, and she ordered the project halted in time.
Golitsyn: As for the few tax officials who acted with undue severity, deliberately distorted Her Majesty's benevolent policies, and stirred resentment among the people, we have already punished them according to regulation. They have been removed from office and sent into exile.
Golitsyn: It is deeply regrettable that we did not discover the matter sooner. Alas... our compatriots in Zolnyy should never have had to suffer such grievances. Our hearts ache whenever we think of it.
Golitsyn: But please, rest assured. In the name of Her Majesty's glory, order and justice shall be upheld.
