Unknown Role (None): Now that's rich — Treasure Hoarders calling us thieves!

Unknown Role (None): Why are you looking at me like that? Of course I didn't steal this sword.

Unknown Role (None): Not long ago, the Knights caught a band of Treasure Hoarders outside the city. The goods they were smuggling were also impounded.

Unknown Role (None): This sword was one of those items. We believed it to be stolen plunder, but no one came to claim it, nor could we find out where it came from, so it languished in our stores.

Unknown Role (None): I noticed it quite by chance while in our storehouse. To tell you the truth, such an old sword would see little use outside of alchemy.

Unknown Role (None): Eh? You claimed it for yourself, just like that?

Unknown Role (None): So... then what happened? You just took it with you?

Unknown Role (None): Indeed, and I initially intended to perform some experiments on it, but I unexpectedly discovered its true origins in the process.

Unknown Role (None): This ancient-looking weapon once passed through the hands of a now-deceased blacksmith. It is a legendary magical sword.

Unknown Role (None): A magical sword!?

Unknown Role (None): It is said that the smith vanished not long after creating the weapon, with the weapon subsequently becoming lost to time.

Unknown Role (None): Working backwards from its eventual fate as plunder, one can guess that it was then stolen by the Treasure Hoarders, where it remained in obscurity till recently.

Unknown Role (None): The blacksmith who made this weapon... disappeared!? That's kinda spooky.

Unknown Role (None): No wonder it's cursed!

Unknown Role (None): Exactly.

Unknown Role (None): To think that humans could make such a weapon...

Unknown Role (None): Do you know the story of *Durin* and this mountain?

Unknown Role (None): *Durin* was the black dragon who menaced Mondstadt, before finally being defeated by the combined efforts of *Dvalin* and the *Anemo Archon Barbatos*.

Unknown Role (None): After a fierce battle, the vanquished Durin crashed to ruin here, falling into the thick snow.

Unknown Role (None): Snow gathers atop this mountain and never melts, which is a most curious phenomenon. Which leads one to wonder — was Durin's fall here purely by chance, or was it intent?

Unknown Role (None): Could it really have been intent?

Unknown Role (None): Perhaps this place was chosen as a gravesite precisely so that the snow's power could seal the corrosive toxin coming from Durin's body.

Unknown Role (None): It seems plausible that Barbatos would think of such a method.

Unknown Role (None): Huh... Was Barbatos such a capable god?

Unknown Role (None): Well, these are just my postulations. I don't have any evidence. But what's for certain is that this mountain and Durin are deeply intertwined.

Unknown Role (None): In fact, I believe that the very sword in your hand has Durin's remains in it.

Unknown Role (None): A dragon's remains!?

Unknown Role (None): Indeed. The dragon's eyes, claws, and scales, ground into dust before being used to coat the blade.

Unknown Role (None): In this way, Durin's corruption and venom entered into the sword, and became the source of its power.

Unknown Role (None): This is very advanced craftsmanship. I presume that having successfully forged the weapon, the smith must have tried it out themselves in their joy.

Unknown Role (None): But using this sword in battle would have allowed the corruption to seep through the blade's handle, and into their bodies.

Unknown Role (None): Ordinary mortals cannot withstand such power. The blacksmith must then have fled, driven mad by the curse, before meeting their end in some unknown place.

Unknown Role (None): Eek...
