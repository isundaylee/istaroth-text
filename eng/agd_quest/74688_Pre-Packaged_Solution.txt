# Pre-Packaged Solution

Frejus: No problem! A paper I once read mentioned that you can suppress the flash by installing a device at the end of the barrel. It helps lower visual fatigue when firing for long periods.

Chenee: And you don't have to run off to dangerous areas and battle monsters and the like every time you want to test a toaster.
Frejus: Hmm, you make a good point. Maybe I should try switching my focus...

Chenee: You...

Chenee: That way, you can combine all these machines together and sell them as a "Pre-Packaged Solution"...
Frejus: Oh! That's not a bad idea! Solving all our customers' problems with one complete package... That's definitely something worth looking into!
Paimon: It's like they just need to start chatting and the ideas come flowing out...

Traveler: You shouldn't eat just anything, Paimon.
