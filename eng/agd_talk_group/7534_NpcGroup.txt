# Talk Group: Apraksina (NpcGroup 7534)

## Talk 0

Apraksina: What is it, Bogatov? What are you waiting for?
Bogatov: Do I... really have to go with you?
Apraksina: Of course!
Bogatov: But I'll never be able to return if I take the boat to Nod-Krai with you like that. My father will surely disown me...
Apraksina: What in the world are you talking about? Things like family name and wealth stopped meaning a thing the very moment we decided to run away together. Or do you still intend to carry those to your grave?
Bogatov: I... just don't know how we're going to survive without them...
Apraksina: Goodness me! Just what use are those fangs and claws of yours? Or has time stripped you of the weapons of your ancestors, and left you with nothing but a soft, fuzzy coat?
Apraksina: You were the one who swore at the opera house that you'd take me to the sea to find freedom. And now you're the one thinking of backing out!?
Bogatov: Maybe there's another way. Maybe I can still persuade my mother... I just think that we shouldn't be so reckless...
Apraksina: *Sigh* Maybe I was wrong about you. I thought I could count on you to break me out of the cage. But I guess that was just wishful thinking.
Bogatov: I'm sorry... Would you hate me if I cannot leave Snezhnaya with you?
Apraksina: Of course not, Bogatov. I won't have the time to hate you. Even if I have to do it by myself, I will get on that boat and go where I truly wish to go.
Bogatov: Apraksina... you have always been so much more decisive than I. Honestly, between the two of us, you resemble my great ancestor King Miscislav far more than I ever could. I only wish... I wish I could be even half as courageous as you are...
