# Iansan: Training Solutions

Iansan arrives to make medicine for some tribespeople who have hurt themselves while training.
Free weights can be dangerous, so it's important to increase the load slowly.

## Talk 0

Iansan: One... Two... Should need to make a few more...
Traveler: What are you making, Iansan?
Iansan: Some topical injury ointments. One of our tribesmen — the idiot — put way too much weight on the barbell for his squats. He lost control and threw out his back.
Iansan: He's just lucky others reacted so fast. If they hadn't managed to rush in to stabilize the barbell...
Traveler: Th—That could have gone horridly...
Iansan: *sigh* I gave him a detailed, clear training plan, and specifically warned him multiple times to have me spot him if he wanted to push himself with heavier weights.
Iansan: But no, the fool got all full of himself, had to try on his own, and refused to listen to everyone else.
Iansan: Now he's stuck with a busted back. Even my special ointments can't cure him overnight.
Iansan: What a pain... Now I'll have to go and give them another safety lecture in a few days...
