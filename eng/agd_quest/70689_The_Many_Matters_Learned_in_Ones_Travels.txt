# The Many Matters Learned in One's Travels

Katheryne of the Adventurers' Guild seems to be looking for you...


## Talk 1

(Talk to Katheryne)

Traveler: *Any news?*
Katheryne: #Ah, Traveler, you've come at just the right time — I have something for you.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: A letter for help? Or a clue to some treasure?
    Option 2:
        Traveler: An escort commission, or a search and rescue?
Katheryne: ...You're certainly very familiar with the kind of work we do, but I'm afraid this has nothing to do with any of the above commissions.
Katheryne: Please, take this brand-new Adventurers' Guild notebook.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: But I already have the Adventurer Handbook...
    Option 2:
        Traveler: Why are you giving me a new one?
Katheryne: The Adventurer Handbook is for recording details of your completed commissions and intel on all sorts of monsters.
Katheryne: Whereas this notebook is for recording the marvelous people and wonderful events you encounter on your travels.
    Option 1:
        Traveler: Oh...
    Option 2:
        Traveler: That actually sounds kind of useful.
Katheryne: I hope it will be of help to you. Anyway, that's all for now, my "commission" is complete.
Katheryne: #Safe travels, Traveler. Ad astra abyssosque!
