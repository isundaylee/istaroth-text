# Teyvat Fishing Guide: Series I

## Amateurs Hammer Nails Into Hooks

Catch your first fish.

## "Do you need a fishtank to go with that?"

Catch your first Ornamental Fish.

## Yon Mirror'd Moon, Broken

Catch a fish that only comes out at night for the first time.

## Ding Ding Ding!

Catch fish successfully 100 times.

## Ding Ding Ding!

Catch fish successfully 500 times.

## Ding Ding Ding!

Catch fish successfully 2,000 times.

## "Call Me Ishmael."

Catch one fish in another player's world.

## Fishy Motive

Buy a fishing rod from the Fishing Association.

## Into the Waters

Successfully make 20 Bait.

## A Right Proper Angler

Unlock 20 fish Archive entries.

## Intermission

Catch a scattered page of a book while fishing in Inazuma.

## Stabilizer

Catch fish successfully 10 times while always staying inside the Ideal Tension Zone.
