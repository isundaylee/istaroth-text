# Talk Group: Dobronravov (NpcGroup 7495)

## Talk 0

Dobronravov: My dear lady, the way you speak and the taste you show say a great deal about your upbringing... You have no idea how moved I am.
Dobronravov: I never thought I'd meet someone in Snezhnaya today who appreciates the poetry of the Court of Pale Star as much as I do — and who still thinks so deeply about existence, the soul, and solitude...
Svetlana: Sir, how could I not feel the same? Out of all the people I've met, you seem deeper, more complex, and more genuine than anyone.
Svetlana: And when you said you were tired of the same old cliches in Vestnik's literary section... all I could think was what a shame it is that we only met today, by chance, on this train!
Dobronravov: Yes, brief as our meeting has been, I can only feel grateful for it, my lady. To think that two kindred spirits should both call Snezhnograd home...
Dobronravov: Earlier, you mentioned the Korolevskiy Troupe. As someone who also has a deep love for the arts, I wondered whether I might have the honor of accompanying you to a performance someday...
Svetlana: It would be my pleasure, sir! So many people go to the theater just because it's fashionable, yet they don't know how to appreciate the tension and beauty in every moment of a ballet.
Dobronravov: My lady, your understanding is as deep as ever. The crowd may cheer at every high note, but they fail to grasp the philosophy and meaning behind opera, the highest form of art...
Svetlana: Hold on, sir! You called opera the highest form of art, and that's where I have to disagree. Surely you weren't inviting me to see an opera rather than a ballet?
Dobronravov: Ballet has its charms, certainly, but opera is the troupe's finest jewel. Wait, do you truly place dance above song...?
Svetlana: How awful! I can't believe your views on something so important could be so ordinary. How did I ever think you were deep and special?
Dobronravov: I could say the same, my lady. I praised your taste before, but clearly I misjudged the depth of your soul. I thought I had finally found someone who might save me from my loneliness...
Svetlana: Say no more, sir. This is where we part ways. Farewell!
Dobronravov: See you!
