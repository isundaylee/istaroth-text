# Talk Group: Aksakov (NpcGroup 7583)

Aksakov: Don't mind me... I won't be around much longer anyway.

## Talk 1

Traveler: What happened to you?

## Talk 2

Traveler: Is there anyone looking after you?
Aksakov: Don't worry about it. Who'd want a nagging old man weighing him down anyway? It's not like he's my own flesh and blood...

## Talk 3

Aksakov: He should've just let me freeze to death in my old house. At least there'd be no need to bother with a coffin. The place was good enough for me — not like I've got anyone to leave it to anyway.

## Talk 4

Traveler: Would you like me to go get Alyosha?

Aksakov: Dragged me all the way here just because he insisted I might live a few days longer...
