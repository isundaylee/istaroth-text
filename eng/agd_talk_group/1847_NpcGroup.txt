# Talk Group: NpcGroup - 1847

## Talk 0

Amucivari: Glory to the blazing sun. May the fiery winds and rains of flame from every direction scour your enemies from the earth, noble friend.

## Talk 1

Traveler: Who are you?

Amucivari: I am Amucivari, born in the first Year of the Seven Reeds after the Blazing Sun's return to flame. Once I served as a Recording Officer in the court of the glorious Holy Sov{RUBY#[D]Ti'sakhuun}ereign, His Ma{RUBY#[D]Kalomte}jesty.

Amucivari: At the time, I was pursuing a traitor, one who had abandoned the Alliance's oath and forgotten the teachings of the blazing sun. Yet in the end, it was I who became trapped here.

Amucivari: My ancestors once shared a cup with the true god. And I — no more than a shadow cast by the blazing sun upon this humble earth — offer you my greeting.

## Talk 2

Traveler: Tell me about your Alliance.

Amucivari: The blazing sun blessed our great Alliance and set us upon our purpose: To scour the world with flame. To burn away every impurity, every wicked thing that dares stand between humankind and its rightful destiny.

Amucivari: Yes, I know what the traitors of later ages claim. They shattered the Alliance, raised mortals up as new suns, and branded the Guardian of the{RUBY#[D]Yajaw K'ahk'} Sacred Flame as a tyrant. Let them speak.

Amucivari: The blazing sun has never died. One day the true god will return. Fire will sweep across this corrupted age, and the earth itself will be made pure again. And when that day comes, those trapped here will not perish... they will be reborn into the hope that awaits them.

## Talk 3

Traveler: What are you studying?

Amucivari: Creations of the northern tribes. Ingenious things. These metal instruments can perform music entirely on their own. No attendants required, only the ingenuity of their makers.

Amucivari: Still, I cannot help thinking such devices deserve a higher purpose. Imagine them spreading the wisdom of the Alliance, or the enlightened teachings of the glorious Holy Sov{RUBY#[D]Ti'sakhuun}ereign, rather than serving as mere instruments of music.

Amucivari: Sadly, Mr. Istoria will not permit me to modify the originals. So I have begun trying to recreate them from the ground up.

Amucivari: I am no craftsman. Lady Ixquieh, who sacrificed herself for the Alliance, possessed far greater skill than I do. My progress has been slow... Even so, I believe that determination can carry a person through anything.
