Varka: A camp always needs to have one sober soul to guard their drunk comrades from things that go bump in the night. Those have been my standing orders since this expedition started.

Varka: Rotten luck, eh? Just so happens that tonight, it's my turn to be that sober soul.

Varka: ...So, on this night when they're all drunk, I can't be.

Traveler: Was it really luck?

Traveler: On the very day the Dandelion Wine was due to arrive?

Varka: Who knows? Maybe fate just gets a kick out of messing with us? If it'd come one day later, maybe I'd have been down there guzzling wine to my heart's content.

Varka: But if it had come but one day later, maybe you'd have passed the camp by. Missed the feast. Never even had this chance to talk.

Varka: Nobody can say what choices lead to what outcomes until the time comes.

Varka: Just like when I decided to lead this expedition out of Mondstadt... never imagined I'd make it this far.

Traveler: The reason you launched the expedition... was it to deal with the Wild Hunt?

Varka: ...That's how it turned out, aye. But when we marched, all I "knew" was this: An Abyssal storm was brewing up north, one that threatened to scour all of Teyvat.

Varka: It took us some time to mobilize, but where would the enemy appear, and what form would they take?

Varka: ...That was all a complete mystery to us when we set out. All we could do was gather intel as we marched, preparing for every contingency.

Traveler: That's all you knew, and you still launched the expedition?

Varka: Ha! Quick study, aren't you? Alas, the enemy won't sit around twiddling their thumbs while we finish preparing.

Varka: Every hour we hesitated, the Abyss was devouring the lives of countless people. The sooner we marched, the sooner we could change their fates.

Varka: —That was all the intel we needed to launch this "expedition."

Traveler: No wonder you're the Knight of Boreas...

Traveler: That's the Knights of Favonius for you...

Varka: None of that formality, "Honorary Knight"! And you're no different. Your journey's also changed many people's fates.

Varka: Be it Mondstadt back then, Dvalin... or Nod-Krai now, the expeditionary force... even my fate, too.

Traveler: ...Am I really that amazing?

Varka: ...Hah, who's to say? Fate's fickle and hard to fathom, after all. But as long as you're alive, there's hope.

Varka: The wine we didn't drink tonight, we can have tomorrow — the lads'll save my share. As long as we're still breathing, tomorrow'll bring more wine and laughter!

Varka: So, cheers! To tomorrow! To hope!
