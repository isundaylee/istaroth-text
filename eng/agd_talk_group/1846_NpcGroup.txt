## Talk 1

Traveler: Could you tell me about the era you lived in?

Cyrstin: Hehe, compared to the ages the others speak of, mine was rather uneventful.

Cyrstin: Remuria and the Lochknight existed only in plays, and the intricate machines of later centuries had yet to appear for another thousand years.

Cyrstin: So if you were hoping I could share some exciting story from my era, I fear it may sound rather dull.

Cyrstin: As for me, Cyrstin de Panserhe, I was merely the daughter of a rather ordinary viscount. Even in my own time, our house could hardly stand beside great families like the de Petrichors or the Randolphs.

## Talk 3

Traveler: About these exhibits...

Cyrstin: Miss Vindia — the "Madam Snow-Winged Goose" — had never even heard of picture books. In her era, pigments seemed to be used only for military purposes.
