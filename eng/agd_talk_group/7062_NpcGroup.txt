# Talk Group: Pentti (NpcGroup 7062)

## Talk 0

Pentti: It's so noisy here. Back in my day, we'd... *sigh*

## Talk 1

Traveler: What was it like in your day?
Pentti: Back in my day, everything at The Flagship was topmast quality! Exquisite decor, wonderful food, enthusiastic staff!
Pentti: But look at it now! And look at the clientele! *sigh* Noisy, rowdy, loud... Flagship my foot. More like a beached skiff!

## Talk 2

Traveler: Maybe it's time to let the past go...
Pentti: Huh? You wouldn't be saying that if you knew what it was like! A night in this place was a tenth of the price it is now, and the service was ten times better!
Pentti: Ah, forget it. What's the point in talking about all this with a whipper-snapper like you? It's not like you were there to see it.

## Talk 3

Traveler: Sorry, I'll try to tread more lightly.
Pentti: I wasn't talking about you. If that rowdy lot had half the manners you do, this place would be a whole lot nicer...

## Talk 4

Traveler: Are you saying Nasha Town is noisier than it used to be?
Pentti: Ha! If this is the crowd we're attracting nowadays, I'd rather Nasha Town went back to being a sleepy little market town! Where did this madhouse of shops spring up from, anyway?
Pentti: Back in the day, the only thing the market sold was seafood fresh off the boat. No fancy seasoning required — just whack it in some hot water and let the flavor stay with you all day...
Pentti: *sigh* But those times are gone now. It's enough to make you want to cry...
