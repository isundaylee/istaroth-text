# Talk Group: Koch (NpcGroup 17040)

## Talk 0

Koch: Honorary Knight! Koch the gunsmith greets you. May your range estimates be precise, and your ballistics ideal.

Option 1:

> Koch: And you are...
> Koch: I'm a member of the Support Company, normally stationed at Dornman Port. This time I've been sent over to maintain the equipment used in the wargame.
> Koch: Before muskets were issued in bulk, I worked quite a bit with the Cavalry Company's bow and crossbow shooters in addition to the Ranged Company — so you could call me an old friend of Horken and Hedwig...
> Koch: And this time, the moment word came down about the joint wargame, Hedwig went and named me directly to lead the gunsmith team.
> Koch: Suits me just fine — gives me a chance to really get a feel for the new Fontainian muskets!

Option 2:

> Traveler: About the other workings of the "observation grenade"...
> Koch: Let me guess — you're going to ask: How does it change direction?
> Koch: Ha! The principle isn't actually that complicated. The grenade body uses a low-energy smokeless powder, so its speed isn't anything dramatic.
> Koch: Pair that with the adhesive devices around the observation mechanism, and on impact it sticks to the target — then the side-mounted tracer at the rear ignites, sending out a hit-confirmation signal.
> Koch: The remaining space in the warhead is packed with directional charges. Once it's been remotely guided to point at the next target, igniting those propellants sends it flying onward.
> Koch: And so, in a wargame, the "observation grenade" can perform a limited number of redirections.
> Koch: Which fits the wargame's theme perfectly — "with a finite number of shots, achieve the most flawless trajectory."

Option 3:

> Traveler: I'll come back to chat with you in a bit.
> Koch: Understood. I'm here on standby anytime.
