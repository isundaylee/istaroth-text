(Talk to the treasure hunters)

Marja: Silly cat... where on earth are you— Oh! You found it!

Linnea: Good thing we found it in time. It's just a little weak now, but if we'd been any later... who knows what might've happened.
Jukka: G—Goodness... That's dangerous...
Marja: Hmph, can't blame anyone but yourself for crawling in there, silly cat.
Linnea: Well, it's not really the cat's fault. These Twinleaf Pitcher Plants give off a special scent. Humans aren't affected, but insects and other little creatures might get drawn in.
Linnea: That's how the plants catch their prey. There's an unusual number of pitcher plants here, small animals are affected by their presence too.
Marja: Oh... I see. Anyway, having this many of these plants grow here is definitely not a good sign, right?
Linnea: That's right. If we leave them alone, they could cause some real trouble...
Marja: Alright, then we'll handle the weeding. Jukka, come help too!
Marja: These plants made our silly cat suffer so much. We have to return the favor.

Marja: Phew... we're finally done. Time to head back to Nasha Town. Thanks a lot, miss, for helping us find this silly cat. If it weren't for your help, who knows how long it would've been trapped.
