# Backup Plans

Aino's working on a backup plan to save her friends...


## Talk 1

(Head to the testing platform)

Aino: Hmm... Should I continue my research or not?
Paimon: What's this?
Aino: Zip-Zap Spy-Bot Number 1!
Paimon: Zip-Zap what now?
Ineffa: To be precise, this is a testing platform for Zip-Zap Spy-Bot Number 1, the latest stealth robot of Aino's design.
Ineffa: She recently purchased an abandoned cleaning bot from the black market. After modifying it, she created the Zip-Zap Spy-Bot Number 1 you see here today.
Ineffa: This platform has simulated several hostile environments to test the robot's ability to maintain stealth under pressure.
Paimon: Why's Aino making this stealth robot again?
Ineffa: After the theft of Krum-Clank 70% Sweet, Aino made up her mind, and declared: "No matter how powerful the foe, we have to rescue our friends!"
Ineffa: Then she quickly completed the draft of Zip-Zap Spy-Bot Number 1 and started its development.
Ineffa: According to her plan, once the development is complete, it should be able to infiltrate even the heavily guarded Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau with ease to rescue our friends.
Paimon: That sounds pretty awesome!
Ineffa: But Aino soon realized that perfecting the plan would take far too long — so she wanted to abandon the idea. As per her calculations, it would be much faster to just turn all of Nod-Krai upside down.
Aino: I've made up my mind! Since we're taking things nice, chill, and easy, I'll just keep working on Zip-Zap Spy-Bot Number 1! It'll definitely help us save our friends!
Ineffa: In that case, could we ask you two to assist with the tests?
Ineffa: Based on past statistics, having data provided by other testers as reference will increase Aino's development speed by at least 30%.
Paimon: Count Paimon in! This sounds like fun!
Traveler: I'll help too.
Ineffa: Then I thank you both on Aino's behalf. I will grant you access permissions to the platform — from now on, you can use it for any testing you need to conduct.
